# PEQ (Programme de l'expérience québécoise)

## Must cover
- Intake window 2026-07-02 to 2026-10-31 (closed); watch for a new window; status of reported 2025-11-19 suspension

## Current rules
- **CLOSED to new applications since 2026-10-31.** "La période de réception des demandes ... qui était ouverte depuis le 2 juillet 2026, est fermée depuis le 31 octobre 2026." No date yet for another window (page updated 2026-10-29). Quebec's reinstatement notice says new periods may open before the program ends on 2028-07-02.
- After 2026-10-31, only requests to add or remove family members are accepted on existing files.
- For: temporary foreign workers with Quebec experience; foreign graduates of Quebec programs. French level and stream criteria for the 2026 window were not read this pass.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-11-19: PEQ abolished (H: Quebec government notice)
- 2026-07-02 to 2028-07-02: PEQ reinstated, with intake by periods (H: Quebec government notice). Selection conditions are those in place at the suspension (graduates) and abolition (workers)
- 2026-07-02 to **2026-10-31**: first intake window, for those with a Quebec diploma or qualifying work experience at the 2025-11-19 abolition (H)
- **Correction:** the first window ended 2026-10-31. Quebec says it plans further intake periods before the program ends in July 2028, with no dates yet (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- **Do not file or promise PEQ now.** Redirect to PSTQ Stream 1 or 2 (Quebec experience/diploma strongly helps Stream 2).
- Clients who filed in the window: keep work permits valid (the bridging OWP needs a CSQ or AOR) and watch the PSTQ priority rules for expiring permits.

## Common refusal reasons
- n/a while closed (applications outside window not accepted)

## Re-verify before advising
- PEQ windows (any new window after 2026-10-31)
- PEQ French level and criteria (subpages)
