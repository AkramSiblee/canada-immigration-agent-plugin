# French language and Bill 96

## Must cover
- Language rules, government communications, impacts on files

## Current rules
- **Since 2023-06-01** the Quebec civil administration serves "exclusively in French" unless an exception applies.
- **Immigrants:** other languages allowed for integration services during the **first 6 months after arrival** in Quebec.
- Other exceptions: Indigenous peoples; services outside Quebec; tourism; health/safety/justice situations; persons who communicated only in English before 2021-05-13; persons eligible for English instruction (**not** temporary foreign workers or foreign students).
- **Francisation Québec**: the single access point for French courses (16+, in Quebec or planning to settle).

### French in immigration files
- PSTQ: Stream 1 oral 7 / written 5; Stream 2 oral 5; spouse oral 4 (see pstq.md).
- Work CAQ after 3 yrs: oral 4 (from 2028-12-17).
- PEQ: closed; French criteria not reviewed.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-12-17: French oral 4 for long-stay temporary workers (phased) (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- After 6 months, MIFI correspondence will be in French. Ensure clients (or you) can respond on time. Translation delays are not an excuse.
- Documents submitted to MIFI in languages other than French/English need certified translation.

## Common refusal reasons
- French level not met; missed deadlines on French correspondence

## Re-verify before advising
- PEQ French levels; Francisation Québec course offer
