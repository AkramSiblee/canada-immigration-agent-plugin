# Quebec business immigration: Investor, Entrepreneur, Self-Employed

## Must cover
- Intake status, requirements, French, investment/deposit, stay in Quebec, fees, federal stage

## Current rules
### Status (quebec.ca pages 2026-11-09 / 2026-11-10) (H)
- **All three programs accept applications "at any time", no maximum number.** No Arrima/EOI step; paper application mailed to MII (formerly MIFI) in one envelope.
- Low admissions: 2026 Quebec plan, business immigrants about 500 (plan shows 450-550 and 500-600; M). Demand for the Investor Program is very low (secondary: 5 applications in 2025, M).
- **French: oral level 7** (Échelle québécoise) for all three, since **2024-01-01** (proof needed even for native speakers: TCF/TEF variants, DELF/DALF, or accepted diploma; results under 2 yrs).
- All: secondary diploma (or 1+ yr vocational/college); values attestation within 60 days of MII (formerly MIFI) request; excluded sectors (payday/cheque/pledge lending, sex industry; plus real estate for entrepreneur and self-employed).
- After the CSQ: PR application to IRCC (admissibility, medicals, security). IRCC fees in fee-schedule.md.

### Investor Program (PIIQ) (pages 2026-11-09)
- **Net worth CAD $2,000,000** (lawfully acquired; gifts in last 6 months excluded; may be shared with spouse).
- **2 yrs management experience** in the last 5.
- Through a financial intermediary, within 120 days of MII (formerly MIFI) request: **$1,000,000 for 5 years** (guaranteed by Quebec, returned **without interest**) **+ $200,000 non-refundable contribution** to Investissement Québec Immigrants Investisseurs.
- Notice of intent to select -> **apply for a work permit within 6 months** -> within 2 yrs of the permit, **stay 12 months in Quebec** (at least 6 by the applicant; 6 may be the spouse's) -> send proof -> CSQ.
- **2026 fee $18,241** principal; spouse/children $0 (adjusted each Jan 1).

### Entrepreneur Program (stream pages 2026-11-10)
| Stream | Key conditions |
|---|---|
| **1 Innovative business** | Profile A start-up (up to 3 partners) or Profile B (already in Quebec, 2-yr stay). **10%+** equity; **service offer from a recognized innovation-support organization**; business plan. No net worth figure stated |
| **2 Business start-up** | **2 yrs** business management in last 5; **net worth $600,000**; **25%+** equity; start business within 2 yrs of the work permit; spend **$300,000 in CMM (Montréal) / $150,000 outside**; business registered 1+ yr. Already-started profile: net worth $300,000, 1 yr operating + 2-yr stay |
| **3 Takeover** | Target business **5+ yrs** old, not acquired by selectees in last 5 yrs; **net worth $600,000**; control; spend **$300,000 CMM / $150,000 outside**; takeover-support organization offer; succession plan; bid within 2 yrs. Acquired-company profile: net worth $300,000 + 2-yr stay |
- Self-sufficiency for 3 months after PR (amounts: RIQ Schedule C, 3-month table; 2026 = $1,979 for one adult, NPI 2025-001) (H).
- Net worth (Streams 2 and 3): **gifts ("donations") received in the 6 months before the application don't count**; may be shared with an included spouse; applicant and included spouse must both prove lawful origin and accrual. Stream 1 page states no net worth figure or gift rule (pages 2026-11-10 / 2026-11-19) (H).
- **2026 fee: $1,272 principal; $201 spouse and each child**, all three streams (stream "applying" pages, Streams 1 and 3 updated 2026-11-10, Stream 2 2026-01-01; MIFI 2026 fee table: "Entrepreneur and self-employed worker" $1,246 -> $1,272; family member $197 -> $201). Non-refundable; indexed each Jan 1 (H).
- Fee legal basis: fees under the Québec Immigration Act (CQLR c. I-0.2.1), indexed under s.83.3 Financial Administration Act. RIQ (c. I-0.2.1, r.3) s.112 indexes the **financial schedules** B, C, D (2.05% for 2026, NPI 2025-001), not the fees. LégisQuébec returned 403 on 2026-11-20, so the RIQ fee provisions themselves were not read (OPEN, low impact).

### Entrepreneur: when the conditions are met (Stream 2 conditions page, updated 2026-11-19) (H)
- **Before the CSQ, not after.** Sequence: application -> notice of intent to select -> **work permit** -> start the business **within 2 years of the work permit** -> prove start-up -> CSQ.
- To get the CSQ: business registered **1+ year** when proof is sent; **$300,000 (CMM) / $150,000 (outside)** spent on start-up or operations; **25%+** of original capital.
- **After the CSQ:** only the federal PR application (IRCC decides admission) and the 3-month self-sufficiency undertaking from the date of PR. The "after the CSQ" pages for Streams 1, 2 and 3 (updated 2026-01-08 / 2026-11-10) list **no business-operation, reporting or CSQ-cancellation conditions** after selection (H for absence on those pages).
- Wording conflict (Quebec pages): Stream 2 conditions say the work permit comes with the **notice of intent to select**; the "after the CSQ" pages say the **C-60 support letter** comes "with your acceptance letter and CSQ" for those not yet in Quebec. Treat the notice-of-intent route as the main path and confirm with MIFI which letter to file with IRCC.

### Self-Employed Worker Program (pages 2026-06-25 / 2026-11-10)
- **2 yrs self-employed** in own profession in last 5; regulated professions need Quebec authorization or equivalence (Qualifications Québec).
- **Net worth $100,000**; deposit **$50,000 (CMM) / $25,000 (outside)** at a Quebec institution on MII (formerly MIFI) request.
- Must practise the profession in Quebec; 3-month self-sufficiency.
- **2026 fee $1,272** principal, **$201** spouse and each child.

## Work permits before PR
- Entrepreneurs and self-employed with a CSQ or a MII (formerly MIFI) support letter: **C60** (R205(a)); may apply before the CSQ is issued; up to **3 years** in Quebec (IRCC C60 page 2026-04-16) (H). Detail: 05-temporary-residence/work-permits-lmia-exempt.md.
- Quebec "after the CSQ" pages (all 3 Entrepreneur streams, 2026-11-10) name the code: LMIA-exempt work permit **"C-60"**; the Stream 1 page calls it an open work permit (H).
- **Investor: code T10, R204(c)** ("Quebec investors with notice of intent to select", from 2022-12-15; IRCC exemption codes list modified 2026-09-23) (H). Not C60.
  - Apply within **6 months** of the notice of intent; later = refused. Open, **Quebec only**; up to **36 months** or passport expiry (IRCC investor "About" page modified 2026-03-18; Quebec cases program instructions 2024-05-01) (H).
  - Spouse/children: open permits under **C41/C46**, restricted to Quebec (program instructions 2024-05-01) (H).
  - **One extension only, up to 2 years**, needs a valid **CSQ** (Investor Program), living in Quebec, and no PR application yet or one not yet past completeness. Form IMM 5710, job title "T10 Quebec Investors" (extend page modified 2026-03-05) (H).
- Self-employed (Quebec CSQ): C60 per the IRCC codes list row "Provincial business candidates or Quebec self-employed applicants with a CSQ" (H).

## Consultant pitfalls
- **French 7 oral is the real barrier.** Test early; most investors fail here, not on funds.
- Investor: the $200,000 is a **cost**, not an investment; the $1M returns with no interest after 5 years.
- **Intent to live in Quebec** is required. Using a Quebec CSQ to settle elsewhere invites misrepresentation findings (IRPA s.40) and is a known IRCC concern.
- Source of funds is assessed by MII (formerly MIFI) and again by IRCC; document the full chain.
- Entrepreneur Streams 1 and 3 need a support organization's offer: line it up before filing.
- Federal business programs are paused or closed (01-federal-economic/business-and-self-employed.md); Quebec is currently the only open federal-level business PR route in Canada besides PNP entrepreneur streams.
- The firm should not advise on investment products themselves (financial intermediary's role).

## Common refusal reasons
- French level not proven; net worth or lawful origin not shown; management experience not qualifying; business plan not credible; stay-in-Quebec condition unmet
