# Refugees, protected persons, asylum seekers and H&C - Quebec

## Must cover
- CSQ for protected persons, H&C CSQ, collective sponsorship status, asylum-seeker services, roles

## Roles (roles page, 2025-07-14) (H)
- **Canada (IRB/IRCC):** decides refugee protection, H&C approval in principle, admissibility, PR.
- **Quebec (MII, formerly MIFI):** selects (CSQ) refugees/protected persons living in Quebec, refugees abroad destined to Quebec, and H&C cases approved in principle; runs collective sponsorship; funds settlement services.

## Protected persons living in Quebec -> PR (page 2026-11-09) (H)
- On a positive IRB/IRCC decision, do **two steps at the same time**:
  1. **Quebec permanent selection (CSQ):** form **A-0520-DF** (French only), whole family included, signed, mailed with photocopies to: Direction du regroupement familial et de l'enregistrement, MII, 204-1200 boulevard Saint-Laurent, Montréal (Québec) H2X 0C9.
  2. **IRCC PR application** (protected persons and Convention refugees). No need to wait for the CSQ to file it.
- **Fee:** none for the CSQ ("free").
- **Timing:** 25-60 days; MII processing applications received 2026-10-18; "exceptionally high volume".
- **CSQ validity:** 24 months, or until IRCC decides if PR is in process.
- **Family abroad / not recognized:** CSQ later, after IRCC's letter asking for it is sent to MII.
- **Work:** work permit still needed (renew with IRCC until PR).
- **Health:** IFHP ends **90 days after the positive decision** -> apply to **RAMQ** once the CSQ issues. Missing this leaves the family uninsured.

## H&C cases destined to Quebec (page 2026-11-12) (H)
- Requires IRCC **approval in principle** first. IRCC sends the file to MII; MII **emails** an "Invitation to Apply for Permanent Selection" with form and document list. Do not contact MII before that.
- Free. Mail to the same Direction address. Family abroad named but not included.
- Quebec assesses: "a particularly distressful situation", capacity to integrate, serious harm if returned/negative impacts if not allowed to stay.
- CSQ valid 24 months; IRCC then finishes medical/security/criminality. See `06-protection-humanitarian/humanitarian-compassionate.md`.

## Refugees abroad (H)
- Must be Convention refugees or in the country of asylum class.
- **Government-assisted refugees** destined to Quebec are selected by Quebec.
- **Collective sponsorship (parrainage collectif)** - Quebec's private sponsorship (groups of 2-5 natural persons or legal persons/organizations; financial capacity required): **intake of undertaking applications suspended until 2029-12-31** (quebec.ca collective sponsorship pages and "Décisions de gestion des demandes" table, read 2026-11-20). An earlier 2026-2029 consultation document said 2027-12-31 - the current pages say 2029. Undertakings already filed that meet conditions will be examined.
- Quebec residents cannot use the federal PSR program (Groups of Five / community sponsors are federal, outside Quebec) (M). Federal G5/CS pause: see `06-protection-humanitarian/resettlement-and-private-sponsorship.md`.

## Asylum seekers living in Quebec (page 2026-11-04) (H)
- Eligible once the claim is submitted to Canada and they live in Quebec:
  - temporary shelter; **last-resort financial assistance** (apply online, MTESS); low-cost childcare and drop-in activities (listed on page 2026-11-04); employment services; French courses (no allowance); legal aid; **PRAIDA** social/health services (514-484-7878 ext. 64500); **IFHP** health coverage.
- Work permit: federal (IRCC) rules; see `06-protection-humanitarian/refugee-claims-inland.md`.
- Quebec has asked Ottawa to redistribute claimants and cut volumes (political; not a legal rule) (M). No Quebec rule found that limits services as of 2026-11-20.

## Consultant pitfalls
- File the A-0520-DF **immediately** after the positive decision; IFHP's 90-day end date runs regardless.
- Only one paper application per family; incomplete files are returned (French only).
- Client moving from Quebec to another province before PR: CSQ/Quebec destination must be dealt with; tell IRCC (A40 risk if intent misstated).
- Do not promise Quebec collective sponsorship to Quebec groups before 2030.

## Re-verify before advising
- MII processing date for A-0520-DF (changes weekly).
- Collective sponsorship suspension end date (2029-12-31 vs 2027-12-31 in older document).
- Asylum-seeker childcare access (subject of 2025-2026 political debate).
