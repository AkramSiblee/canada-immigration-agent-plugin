# Temporary foreign workers in Quebec

## Must cover
- Joint LMIA, Quebec temporary selection (CAQ), fees, wage threshold, facilitated list, Montréal/Laval freeze, CMA 6% rule, French at renewal, IMP

## Three authorizations for an LMIA job (H)
1. **LMIA** (ESDC) **and** Quebec's assessment - filed **at the same time** to Service Canada and the MII (formerly MIFI), for jobs over 30 consecutive days. In **French**; MII copy in the provincial format. Unnamed LMIAs go to MII via **Arrima** (ESDC Quebec page, 2026-07-24).
2. **Quebec temporary selection (CAQ for work)** - worker applies to MII after the LMIA/Quebec approval. quebec.ca now calls this the "demande de sélection temporaire" (M on naming).
3. **Work permit** (IRCC).
- **IMP (LMIA-exempt) jobs:** no LMIA and, in general, no Quebec CAQ; see `05-temporary-residence/work-permits-lmia-exempt.md` (M - confirm on the quebec.ca IMP page for the specific code).
- CSQ holders with a valid work permit extending with the current employer, renewing with a new Quebec employer, or PGWP/IEC holders: Quebec LMIA exemption (ESDC Quebec page) (H). The "from 2026-03-13" start date listed in `overview-and-plan.md` and `work-permits-lmia-exempt.md` is not on the ESDC page (re-verify which IRCC instrument carries it).

## Fees (2026, indexed every 1 January) (H)
- Quebec temporary selection (worker): **$233**, payable by worker or employer (quebec.ca, from 2026-01-01).
- Federal: LMIA $1,000 per position; work permit $155; biometrics $85 (`fee-schedule.md`).

## Wage threshold - Quebec (ESDC median-wage page, 2026-10-07) (H)
- **$36.00/h** for LMIAs received from **2026-07-17** (was $34.62). At or above -> high-wage stream; below -> low-wage stream.

## Low-wage limits in Quebec (H)
- **Montréal and Laval economic regions:** low-wage LMIAs **not processed until 2026-12-31** (ESDC Quebec page). Applies to facilitated-process files and renewals. Excluded sectors include NAICS 23 (construction), 311 (food manufacturing), 6111, 62 (health/social), certain caregiver positions; NAICS 6244 from 2026-01-09. **No 2027 extension or early end announced as of 2026-11-20** (re-checked: ESDC Quebec page still "Until December 31, 2026", page details 2026-07-24; ESDC refusal page 2026-10-27 only points to the Quebec page; quebec.ca TFWP page 2025-12-17 silent; no MII release) (H for absence on official pages). The PQ government plans to cut TFWP to about 40,000 (platform, M), so an extension is possible; nothing official yet.
- **Federal CMA 6% rule:** low-wage LMIAs refused in CMAs with unemployment ≥6%. **Montréal CMA 7.2%** for 2026-10-09 to 2027-01-07 -> refusal applies; Québec City 4.3% -> processed. Next table 2027-01-08 (refusal page, 2026-10-27) (H).
- **Low-wage cap:** 10% of workforce at the location (20% for NAICS 23, 311, 622, 623, some in-home caregivers) (refusal page) (H).
- Net effect: low-wage jobs on Montréal island / Laval are blocked twice through 2026; after 2026-12-31 the CMA rule still blocks them while Montréal CMA ≥6%.

## Facilitated processing (H)
- MII list of occupations eligible for simplified processing (no recruitment proof), French only, updated **2026-02-24** (cut back in 2025-2026; secondary HR Reporter). Check the list for the NOC before filing.

## French at renewal (H)
- **Oral French level 4** (Quebec scale) required for a work CAQ once the worker has **3 years** of work stay in Quebec; checked on applications sent from **2028-12-17** (rule in force 2025-12-17 with a 3-year transition; quebec.ca news 2025-11-19).
- Exempt: agricultural workers; employees of foreign-state offices; recognized INGO employees.
- Plan now: workers arriving in 2026 hit this at their first renewal after 3 years.

## Consultant pitfalls
- File the LMIA with **both** ESDC and MII, in French; a missing MII copy delays or voids the file.
- Do not file Montréal/Laval low-wage LMIAs before 2027 without checking the ESDC page on 2027-01-01 and the CMA table on 2027-01-08.
- Address on the LMIA = work location; moving staff to Laval/Montréal sites after approval breaches conditions.
- PEQ closed 2026-10-31; PR route for Quebec workers is PSTQ (`pstq.md`).

## Re-verify before advising
- Montréal/Laval measure after 2026-12-31 (ESDC Quebec page, MII news).
- CMA unemployment table 2027-01-08.
- 2027 CAQ/temporary selection fee (2027-01-01).
- Quebec CAQ need for IMP codes (quebec.ca IMP page).
