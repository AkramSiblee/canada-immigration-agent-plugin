# PSTQ (Skilled Worker Selection Program)

## Must cover
- Streams, points, invitation criteria, eligibility, fees

## Current rules
### Streams (by main NOC / FEER)
| Stream | Who | Experience | Education | French (Échelle québécoise) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1, highly qualified | FEER 0-2 | 1 yr full-time in last 5 | Diploma leading to a profession, at least 1 yr (Quebec: 900 h / 30 credits) | **Oral 7, written 5**; spouse oral 4 |
| 2, intermediate/manual | FEER 3-5 | **2 yrs in last 5, at least 1 in Quebec** | At least DES or 1-yr program | **Oral 5**; spouse oral 4 |
| 3, regulated professions | Profession on the Ministère list | — | Licence or recognition (at most 5 yrs old), or 1 yr after invitation to get it | FEER 0-2: oral 7 / written 5; FEER 3-5: oral 5 |
| 4, exceptional talent | Achievement on list or partner opinion | 3 yrs in last 5 | — | Not stated |
- All: 18+; intent to settle in Quebec; **Financial Self-Sufficiency Contract**; **Attestation d'apprentissage des valeurs démocratiques et québécoises** (all adults).

### Arrima invitations
- Invitations by stream, in exercises with minimum scores, sometimes targeting Quebec diplomas or NOC lists.
- 2026 Stream 1 rounds (verbatim): Jan 29: 1,094 (min 674-782); Feb 26: 907 (627-741); Mar 19-20: 893 (588-718); Apr 30: 983 (571-716); Jun 4: 1,094 (from 677).
- 2026 Stream 2 rounds (verbatim): Jan 29: 683; Feb 26: 495; Mar 19-20: 509; Apr 30: 506; Jun 4: 756.
- Later rounds (Jul 3, Jul 30, Aug 27, Sep 24) were summary-only and **smaller** (Stream 1 about 74-105). Verify on the page (M).
- Accept within **30 days**. Apply within **60 days** of accepting (Stream 3 without recognition: 1 yr).

### Fees and processing
- **$940** principal; **$201** spouse; **$201** per child (since 2026-01-01; non-refundable).
- From 2026-05-13: priority to applicants whose work permits are expired or expiring. Applications from Feb-Mar 2026 under review (2026-10-13).

### After selection
- CSQ, then federal PR application (Quebec skilled worker class; fee $1,590 incl. RPRF).
- **Federal stage (IRCC pages 2026-08-04):** PR Portal, mandatory online since 2022-09-23; police certificates required. Digital forms IMM 0008, IMM 5669, IMM 5406, IMM 5562; PDFs IMM 5690 checklist and IMM 0008 Schedule 5 (intent to reside in Quebec). Guide Q7000. Representatives cannot open the portal account or sign for the client. Biometrics for every PR application, ages 14 to 79. A CSQ stays valid until IRCC decides, even if expired; MIFI no longer renews CSQs (since 2018).
- **Work permit with a CSQ (page 2026-10-21):** Quebec stopped issuing IMP+ invitation letters (those received before 2025-01-16 are still processed). Otherwise: bridging open work permit once PR is filed, or employer-specific permit before filing.
- CSQ holders (CSQ 24 months old or less) abroad may get an OWP under **IMP+**. In Quebec: LMIA-exempt permit or bridging OWP after AOR.

## Consultant pitfalls
- Stream 2 needs **12 months in Quebec**. Workers outside Quebec can't qualify.
- French must be proven with an approved test at the required **oral and written** levels. The spouse's oral 4 is also required.
- The values attestation is mandatory for each adult. Missing it means refusal.
- Arrima profile errors (main NOC, experience dates) can invalidate the invitation. That is a misrepresentation risk under both Quebec and federal law.

## Common refusal reasons
- French level not proven; experience not in main NOC; diploma below the stream minimum; missing attestation/contract
