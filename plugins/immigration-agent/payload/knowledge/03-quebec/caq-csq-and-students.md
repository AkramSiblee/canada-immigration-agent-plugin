# CAQ, CSQ and students in Quebec

## Must cover
- CAQ for study/work, CSQ for PR, processing

## Current rules
### CAQ for studies
- Fee **$135** (from 2026-01-01; indexed each Jan 1). Decision in **25 business days** (80%).
- Arrima application; fee paid within 1 hour; documents within 15 days, uploaded by the applicant only (not the representative). CAQ then serves as the attestation for the federal study permit (quebec.ca, updated 2026-11-10) (H).
- **2026 living-cost amounts (students, per year):** 1 person $24,617; 2 $34,814; 3 $42,638; 4 $49,234; 5 $55,045; 6 $60,299; each extra $5,254 (quebec.ca, 2026-01-01) (H). Plus tuition and travel; these replace IRCC's table for Quebec.
- **Cap on CAQ applications per institution** (Decree 1488-2025, dated 2025-12-10, published in the Gazette officielle 2025-12-17; replaces 155-2025 as amended by 632-2025), in **two periods: 2025-12-17 to 2026-12-16, then 2026-12-17 to 2027-12-16** (vocational, college, university) (quebec.ca news 67762, 2025-12-17, read 2026-11-20) (H). Revised for some institutions by order of 2026-07-22 (decree number not on the page). Renewals for the same program, level and institution are exempt. Compared with 2024: private vocational -35%, non-protected AEC -75%, DEC/university roughly unchanged.
  - **Second period (from 2026-12-17): no new decree.** The same Decree 1488-2025 already sets it. Quebec news 67771 (2025-12-17) gives the **same maximum for each of the two periods**: university network **54,702**; college **22,606** (AEC 6,644, DEC 15,915, other 47); vocational **27,915** (H for the 2025-12 figures). The 2026-07-22 revision may have changed some institutions' figures; no revised totals are published in page text. Sector sum 105,223 is our arithmetic, not an official total.
  - 124,760 = the ceiling of the **earlier** decree 155-2025 (2025-02-26 to 2026-02-26), per secondary sources (M). Not a current figure: don't quote it.
  - Places freed up are reallocated on selected Thursdays at 2 p.m. (quarterly calendar). Institution tracking tables: PDF https://cdn-contenu.quebec.ca/cdn-contenu/immigration/publications/fr/TAB_SuivisDemandes_EtudiantsEtrangers.pdf (French only). **Read 2026-11-20 (text extracted, H):** table "Version du 16 novembre 2026", 15 pages, per-institution maximum plus a "Nombre maximal atteint" Oui/Non column; at that date only **1** listed line showed the maximum reached (114 lines 'Non'). Examples: Université de Montréal 7,403; Université Laval 7,124; UQAM 6,523; Concordia 4,448; McGill 4,361. The table does not label a second period; treat figures as the current-period caps and re-check after 2026-12-17. Check the institution's line before advising.
  - quebec.ca temporary selection (studies) page: last updated **2026-11-18** (read 2026-11-20). No 2027 figures in page text. See 14-policy-tracker/announced-for-2027.md.
- Cap exemptions: same program and institution; asylum seekers; dependent children of workers/students; health refresher training; certain scholarships; protocol certificate holders; transfers after an institution closes.
- Institution events: TAQ (2026-07-28) suspended the licence non-renewal of Collège supérieur de Montréal / Sherbrooke pending decision. Herzing Montréal may continue Computing Support to 2027-05-07.
- The CAQ replaces the PAL for federal study permits.

### CAQ for work (TFWP)
- Required with an LMIA in Quebec (joint process). Quebec now calls it the temporary selection application; fee **$233** in 2026, payable by worker or employer (quebec.ca, H). Detail: `temporary-workers-quebec.md`.
- **New French requirement**: CAQ renewals after a **3-year** work stay in Quebec need **oral French level 4**. Verification applies to applications from **2028-12-17** (transition). Exempt: agricultural workers, foreign-state offices, recognized INGO staff.

### CSQ
- Issued after PSTQ (or PEQ) selection. Then federal PR. A CSQ 24 months old or less gives IMP+ OWP eligibility (see pstq.md). No separate CSQ fee: the permanent selection fee is **$940** principal, **$201** spouse, **$201** per child (2026, indexed 1 January; PSTQ page 2026-01-01) (H).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-12-17: study CAQ cap per institution (to 2027-12-16) (H)
- 2025-12-17: French oral 4 for work CAQ after 3 yrs (verification from 2028-12-17) (H)
- 2026-01-01: study CAQ fee $135 (H)
- 2026-07-22: caps revised (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Check the institution's remaining CAQ cap **before** the client pays deposits.
- Private college licence disputes (TAQ) put programs at risk. Avoid institutions under review.
- Plan French early for workers who will renew after 3 years.

## Common refusal reasons
- CAQ: institution cap reached; insufficient funds (Quebec amounts); incoherent study plan

- Cap table (each institution); 2027 fees (from 2027-01-01) (Quebec student funds amounts filled 2026-11-17)
