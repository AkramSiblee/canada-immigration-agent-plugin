# Quebec family sponsorship (regroupement familial)

## Must cover
- Two-step process, intake caps 2026-2028, intake schedule, undertaking terms, financial capacity, fees, timing, integration plan

## How it works (H)
1. **IRCC first:** sponsor files the federal sponsorship (family class / spouse-in-Canada class). IRCC assesses sponsor eligibility (federal bars apply: see `04-family/sponsor-eligibility-and-bars.md`) and issues an **eligibility letter or acknowledgment of receipt**.
2. **Quebec second:** sponsor files the **undertaking application** with the **MII** (Ministère de l'Immigration et de l'Intégration - formerly MIFI). Quebec assesses the undertaking and financial capacity and issues the **CSQ** for the sponsored person.
3. IRCC finishes admissibility (medical, criminality, security) and issues PR.
- Sponsor must be a citizen/PR, **resident in Quebec**, 18+. No last-resort social assistance (except for age/disability). Criminal-record and support-payment conditions apply.
- **Mail only**, one application per envelope. No in-person filing. No Arrima for sponsorship.

## Intake caps: 2026-07-02 to 2028-06-30 (rules page, updated 2026-11-11) (H)
- **15,700** undertaking applications in total:
  - **13,300** spouse / common-law / conjugal partner
  - **2,400** parents, grandparents, and other listed relatives
- Each category closes separately when its cap is reached. Late applications are returned unprocessed, without fees.
- **Exempt from caps and schedule:** dependent child (under 22, or 22+ dependent due to a physical/mental condition; not married/common-law), minor child being adopted, orphaned minor sibling/niece/nephew/grandchild, adding dependants to an existing undertaking.
- Whether either cap has been reached: not published (re-verify).

## Intake schedule (non-exempt cases) (H)
File only from the date shown, based on the date of the IRCC eligibility letter / AOR:
| May file from | IRCC letter dated no later than |
|---|---|
| 2026-11-01 | 2025-06-30 |
| 2027-01-01 | 2025-08-31 |
| 2027-03-01 | 2025-10-31 |
- Filing early = "inadmissible and returned".
- MII stated it is reviewing spouse applications received 2026-11-01. **Effect: a 2026 IRCC letter will not reach the Quebec stage for many months** - set client expectations.

## Undertaking terms (Quebec, pages updated 2026-11-03) (H)
| Sponsored person | Quebec term | Federal (outside QC) |
|---|---|---|
| Spouse / partner | **3 years** | 3 years |
| Dependent child under 16 | **min. 10 years or to age 18, whichever is longer** | 10 yrs or to age 25, whichever first |
| Dependent child 16+ | **min. 3 years or to age 25, whichever is longer** | 10 yrs or to age 25, whichever first |
| Parents / grandparents | **10 years** | 20 years |
| Other relatives | **10 years** | 10 years |
- Cannot be cancelled after PR is granted (marriage breakdown does not end it).
- Sponsor must complete a **welcome and integration plan** for sponsored persons aged 18-55 (since 2023-11-23) and help them learn French. No French test for the sponsor.

## Financial capacity (calculating-income page, 2026 amounts) (H)
- **Required** when sponsoring: parents/grandparents; another relative; an orphaned minor; a dependent child who has a dependent child; a spouse whose dependent child has a dependent child.
- **Not required** for a spouse/partner (or dependent child) in the ordinary case.
- Income: Canadian-source, earned in the **12 months before** the application, and expected to continue through the term. (No 3-year income test as in federal PGP.)
- **Table A (sponsor's family unit), 2026:** 1 person $29,642; 2 $40,015; 3 $49,401; 4 $56,819; 5 $63,237; +$6,418 each additional.
- **Table B** adds an amount per sponsored person (e.g. 1 adult: $21,682; 2 adults: $31,796; 0 adults/1 minor: $10,261).
- Amounts change each **1 January** - re-check from 2027-01-01.

## Fees and timing (2026) (H)
- **$335** for the first sponsored person; **$135** each additional. Non-refundable. Wrong amount -> whole file returned.
- Plus federal IRCC fees (`11-forms-fees-processing/fee-schedule.md`).
- MII: "Most undertaking applications are processed within 3 months" once complete. Overall Quebec-destined family files are much slower than outside Quebec (IRCC: PGP about 60 months QC vs 26 outside; spouse QC 34/33 months) (`processing-times.md`, H).

## Consultant pitfalls
- Moving to Quebec after IRCC approval, or claiming non-Quebec residence to avoid caps: misrepresentation risk (A40) if intent is false.
- Spouses: Quebec cap + schedule now control timing. Consider status options in the meantime (spousal OWP for spouse-in-Canada class; visitor status).
- Federal PGP invitations (MI89 pause 2026) still gate parents; Quebec cap is a second gate.
- Use the current MII forms only (PDF, mailed). Check the envelope date rule.
