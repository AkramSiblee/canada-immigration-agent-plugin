# Quebec overview and immigration plan

## Must cover
- Canada-Quebec Accord roles (MIFI selects, IRCC admissibility)
- 2026-2029 plan: 45,000 PR/yr, study permit caps, French targets, LMIA suspensions

## Current rules
### Roles (Canada-Québec Accord, 1991)
- **Quebec (MIFI)** selects economic immigrants (CSQ) and sets income/undertaking terms for family sponsorship. Its CAQ is needed for study and work.
- **IRCC** decides admissibility (medical, criminal, security) and issues PR and permits. Family class eligibility is federal; the undertaking is signed with Quebec.
- **No PNP** and no Express Entry selection for Quebec.

### Plan
- **2026-2029 orientations** (tabled 2025-11-06): about **45,000 PR admissions a year**; by end-2029 at most **65,000 TFWP** and **110,000 student** permit holders in Quebec (Plan d'immigration du Québec 2026, MIFI PDF, read 2026-11-20) (H).
- **2026 plan (official Tableau 4), regular admissions 43,000-47,000:** economic 27,500-30,100 (skilled workers 27,050-29,500; business 450-550); family 9,600-10,400; refugees and similar 5,500-6,000 (selected abroad 700-900, incl. government-assisted 400-500 and sponsored 300-400; recognized in Quebec 4,800-5,100); other 400-500. Targets: about 64% economic, about 76% knowing French, about 54% already in Quebec, about 67% from Quebec selection (H). Temporary: 84,900-124,200 TFWP + student permits in 2026 (H).
- Federal Levels 2027-2029 list Quebec economic as **"TBD"**, awaiting Quebec's fall 2026 plan (H). **No 2027 Quebec plan published as of 2026-11-20** (MIFI planning page last updated 2025-11-06; no news release found) (H for absence).
- French: high share of French-speaking admissions targeted; PSTQ French minimums enforce this (see pstq.md).

### Temporary workers
- **Montréal and Laval low-wage LMIA suspension**: in place to **2026-12-31** (ESDC Quebec page 2026-07-24, H). Excluded sectors listed in `temporary-workers-quebec.md`. **No 2027 extension announced as of 2026-11-20** (H for absence).
- Federal 6% CMA rule also applies to Quebec CMAs.
- Quebec LMIA exemption for certain extension workers from 2026-03-13 (IRCC).
- Work CAQ renewals after 3 yrs need oral French 4 (from 2028-12-17).

### Programs status (2026-11-07)
- PSTQ: open (Arrima invitations). PEQ: **closed since 2026-10-31**. Quebec business programs (Investor, Entrepreneur, Self-Employed): **open, no cap** (quebec.ca 2026-11-09/10); see business-immigration.md.

- Detail files: family-sponsorship-quebec.md, temporary-workers-quebec.md, refugees-protected-persons-quebec.md. The ministry is now the MII (Ministère de l'Immigration et de l'Intégration).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-11-06: Quebec 2026-2029 orientations: about 45,000 PR/yr; 2026 plan 43,000-47,000 (H)
- 2025-11: Montréal/Laval low-wage freeze extended to 2026-12-31 (H, ESDC)
- 2026-10-31: PEQ window closed (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- For a Quebec-bound client, PNP/EE strategies don't apply. Use PSTQ (PEQ closed 2026-10-31), or a federal route with genuine intent to live outside Quebec.
- Montréal/Laval low-wage LMIAs are not processed through 2026-12-31. Check the status before filing in January 2027.
