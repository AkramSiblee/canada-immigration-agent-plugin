# Quebec overview and immigration plan

## Must cover
- Canada-Quebec Accord roles (MII (formerly MIFI) selects, IRCC admissibility)
- 2026-2029 plan: 45,000 PR/yr, study permit caps, French targets, LMIA suspensions

## Current rules
### Roles (Canada-Québec Accord, 1991)
- **Quebec (MII (formerly MIFI))** selects economic immigrants (CSQ) and sets income/undertaking terms for family sponsorship. Its CAQ is needed for study and work.
- **IRCC** decides admissibility (medical, criminal, security) and issues PR and permits. Family class eligibility is federal; the undertaking is signed with Quebec.
- **No PNP** and no Express Entry selection for Quebec.

### Plan
- **2026-2029 orientations** (tabled 2025-11-06): about **45,000 PR admissions a year**; by end-2029 at most **65,000 TFWP** and **110,000 student** permit holders in Quebec (Plan d'immigration du Québec 2026, MIFI PDF, read 2026-11-20) (H).
- **2026 plan (official Tableau 4), regular admissions 43,000-47,000:** economic 27,500-30,100 (skilled workers 27,050-29,500; business 450-550); family 9,600-10,400; refugees and similar 5,500-6,000 (selected abroad 700-900, incl. government-assisted 400-500 and sponsored 300-400; recognized in Quebec 4,800-5,100); other 400-500. Targets: about 64% economic, about 76% knowing French, about 54% already in Quebec, about 67% from Quebec selection (H). Temporary: 84,900-124,200 TFWP + student permits in 2026 (H).
- Federal Levels 2027-2029 list Quebec economic as **"TBD"**, awaiting Quebec's fall 2026 plan (H). **No 2027 Quebec plan tabled as of 2026-11-20**: MII publications page (updated 2026-10-23) lists the 2026 plan as the latest; planning page last updated 2025-11-06; no quebec.ca release found (H for absence). Minister Gentilcore said on 2026-11-10 she will present a plan "dans les prochaines semaines" in line with PQ commitments (secondary, Journal de Sherbrooke 2026-11-11) (M). No 2027 targets by category exist yet.

### New government (2026 election) - proposals, not law
- **2026-10-05** election: Parti Québécois won (minority, about 59 of 127 seats; secondary) (M). Cabinet sworn in **2026-10-21**; **Catherine Gentilcore** minister of Immigration and Integration; ministry renamed **MII** on **2026-10-22** (quebec.ca release 72771) (H).
- **PQ platform (presented 2026-08-29):** permanent admissions cut to about **35,000/yr**; **moratorium on permanent economic immigration from abroad**, kept until temporary immigration is halved, with economic PR drawn only from temporary residents already in Quebec; TFWP about 40,000 and students about 50,000 (secondary: Fragomen 2026-10-12, IMI Daily 2026-10-07; PQ platform) (M).
- **Status 2026-11-20: announcement/platform only.** No bill, regulation, Gazette officielle notice or ministerial order found; no PSTQ suspension announced; the 2026-2029 orientations and 2026 plan remain the official framework (Fragomen; immigration.ca 2026-10-26; absence on quebec.ca) (M). It may be carried out through the 2027 plan and PSTQ invitation choices rather than a statute. **Do not tell clients abroad that the PSTQ is closed; do not promise it stays open in 2027.**
- French: high share of French-speaking admissions targeted; PSTQ French minimums enforce this (see pstq.md).

### Temporary workers
- **Montréal and Laval low-wage LMIA suspension**: in place to **2026-12-31** (ESDC Quebec page 2026-07-24, re-read 2026-11-20, H). Excluded sectors listed in `temporary-workers-quebec.md`. **No 2027 extension or end announced as of 2026-11-20** (ESDC Quebec and refusal pages; quebec.ca TFWP page; no MII release) (H for absence).
- Federal 6% CMA rule also applies to Quebec CMAs.
- Quebec LMIA exemption for certain extension workers from 2026-03-13 (IRCC).
- Work CAQ renewals after 3 yrs need oral French 4 (from 2028-12-17).

### Programs status (2026-11-20)
- PSTQ: open (Arrima profiles); last invitation round 2026-09-24, none since (pstq.md). PEQ: **closed since 2026-10-31**. Quebec business programs (Investor, Entrepreneur, Self-Employed): **open, no cap** (quebec.ca 2026-11-09/10); see business-immigration.md.

- Detail files: family-sponsorship-quebec.md, temporary-workers-quebec.md, refugees-protected-persons-quebec.md. The ministry is now the MII (Ministère de l'Immigration et de l'Intégration).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-11-06: Quebec 2026-2029 orientations: about 45,000 PR/yr; 2026 plan 43,000-47,000 (H)
- 2025-11: Montréal/Laval low-wage freeze extended to 2026-12-31 (H, ESDC)
- 2026-10-05: PQ elected; 2026-10-21 cabinet; 2026-10-22 MIFI renamed MII (H)
- 2026-10-31: PEQ window closed (H)
- 2026-11-10: minister promises 2027 plan "in coming weeks"; not tabled as of 2026-11-20 (M)

## Consultant pitfalls
- For a Quebec-bound client, PNP/EE strategies don't apply. Use PSTQ (PEQ closed 2026-10-31), or a federal route with genuine intent to live outside Quebec.
- Montréal/Laval low-wage LMIAs are not processed through 2026-12-31. Check the status before filing in January 2027.
