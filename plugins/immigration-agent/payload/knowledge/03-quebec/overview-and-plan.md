# Quebec overview and immigration plan

## Must cover
- Canada-Quebec Accord roles (MIFI selects, IRCC admissibility)
- 2026-2029 plan: 45,000 PR/yr, study permit caps, French targets, LMIA suspensions

## Current rules
### Roles (Canada-Québec Accord, 1991)
- **Quebec (MIFI)** selects economic immigrants (CSQ) and sets income/undertaking terms for family sponsorship. Its CAQ is needed for study and work.
- **IRCC** decides admissibility (medical, criminal, security) and issues PR and permits. Family class eligibility is federal; the undertaking is signed with Quebec.
- **No PNP** and no Express Entry selection for Quebec.

### Plan
- **2026-2029 multi-year plan** (published Nov 2025): about **45,000 PR/yr** (baseline, M); economic about 28,800/yr (secondary) (M).
- Federal Levels 2027-2029 list Quebec economic as **"TBD"**, awaiting Quebec's fall 2026 plan (H). **No 2027 Quebec plan found as of 2026-11-07.**
- French: high share of French-speaking admissions targeted; PSTQ French minimums enforce this (see pstq.md).

### Temporary workers
- **Montréal and Laval low-wage LMIA suspension**: in place to **2026-12-31** (secondary; listed on the ESDC refusal page). Excludes crop/animal production, construction, food manufacturing and other listed sectors. **No 2027 extension found** (M).
- Federal 6% CMA rule also applies to Quebec CMAs.
- Quebec LMIA exemption for certain extension workers from 2026-03-13 (IRCC).
- Work CAQ renewals after 3 yrs need oral French 4 (from 2028-12-17).

### Programs status (2026-11-07)
- PSTQ: open (Arrima invitations). PEQ: **closed since 2026-10-31**. Quebec business programs: status not confirmed (MIFI pages not retrieved); figures in 01-federal-economic/business-and-self-employed.md. Never present as open.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- Quebec plan: 45,000 PR/yr (M)
- 2025-11: Montréal/Laval low-wage freeze extended to 2026-12-31 (M)
- 2026-10-31: PEQ window closed (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- For a Quebec-bound client, PNP/EE strategies don't apply. Use PSTQ (PEQ closed 2026-10-31), or a federal route with genuine intent to live outside Quebec.
- Montréal/Laval low-wage LMIAs are not processed through 2026-12-31. Check the status before filing in January 2027.
