# Decision tree: skilled worker to PR

## Must cover
- Quebec / PNP / EE / category / high-wage / pilots

## Current rules
```
1. Intends to live in Quebec?
   YES -> PSTQ (Stream 1 FEER 0-2 + oral 7/written 5; Stream 2 FEER 3-5 + 12 mo Quebec + oral 5)
          PEQ CLOSED since 2026-10-31. No EE/PNP.
   NO  -> 2
2. Meets FSW, CEC or FST?
   NO  -> 6 (PNP base, pilots, work permit first)
   YES -> 3
3. Estimate CRS (labour market integration factor since 2026-11-03).
   - High-wage NOC job offer (no LMIA needed) adds 50/70/100 points
   - High-wage Canadian experience adds 20/40/70 points
   - French NCLC 7 x4: +25/50 and French category draws (cut-offs 382-435 in 2026)
4. Category fit? (12 months in one listed NOC within 3 yrs)
   French | Healthcare | Trades | Transport | Physicians/Senior managers/Researchers (CWE in Canada) | STEM/Education (no 2026 draws)
5. CRS likely below the CEC/category cut-off? -> PNP EE stream (+600). See 02-pnp/comparison-table.md
6. PNP base / pilots:
   - Job offer in ON (Workforce Priority stream), BC (Skilled Worker), AB (Opportunity, Rural Renewal), SK, MB, Atlantic (AIP), RCI/FCI communities?
   - TEER 4-5: ON Workforce Priority TEER 4-5 pathway; AB; YT Critical Impact (2026 intakes closed); AIP (TEER 0-4 only; no TEER 5)
   - Manitoba regional initiatives (see 02-pnp/manitoba.md). Morden (age 21-45): needs a Morden job or offer, unless physician, RN or French teacher on its current priority list. Several others closed in Nov 2026 (H, 2026-11-08)
   - Community routes: match the NOC to each community's current list (01-federal-economic/rural-franco-communities.md; 02-pnp/regional-community-streams.md). AB Rural Renewal from abroad: TEER 0-3 only. ON REDI communities
7. None fits -> temporary route: LMIA / IMP work permit, or study, then CEC / PNP later.
   Check the 2027 workers cohort (provinces identify via PNP; no application).
```
- Always screen inadmissibility (08) and misrepresentation risk first.
