# Decision tree: protection

## Must cover
- C-12 bars, PRRA, H&C, referral

## Current rules
```
1. Unaccompanied minor? -> exempt from 1-yr/14-day bars. Refer to counsel.
2. Prior claim in Canada or elsewhere (s.101(1)(c)-(d))? -> ineligible -> PRRA path
3. Entered between ports from the US?
   - under 14 days -> STCA applies unless s.159.5 exception (family, docs, minor...) -> return to US
   - 14+ days -> s.101(1)(b.2) ineligible
4. First entry after 2020-06-24 and claim more than 1 yr after first entry
   (claims started after 2025-06-02)? -> s.101(1)(b.1) ineligible
5. Eligible -> online claim; complete within 60 days (+30); IRB referral; work permit
6. RPD refuses -> RAD appeal: file 15 days / perfect 30 days from written reasons (IRPR s.159.91) unless no RAD access (IRPA s.110(2)); then JR (lawyer)
7. Ineligible -> conditional removal order (enforceable after 7 days);
   CBSA says at the interview if PRRA available -> apply in 15 days (in person) / 22 (mail)
8. Also consider: H&C (no pending claim; 12-month bar after IRB decision; BIOC/medical exceptions),
   TRP, family sponsorship, other status
9. REFER TO LAWYER: retroactive-bar challenges, Federal Court JR/stays, s.34/35 issues
```
