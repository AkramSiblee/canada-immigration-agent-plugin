# Advertising and conflicts

## Must cover
- Code ss.44-46, informed consent for joint files

## Current rules
### Advertising (Code s.44)
- Display the **name as registered** with the College. Written ads must include the **internet address of the online public register** (s.44(1)).
- Prohibited (s.44(2)): **false, misleading or inaccurate** representations; **guarantees of success**; implying a relationship with the Government of Canada or a province.
- **Client endorsements (s.45):** allowed only if actually given by a client or former client, true and accurate, and reviewed and approved for public use by that client **in writing**.
- **College name, logo or marks (s.46):** only as the College permits.

### Conflicts (Code ss.15-18)
- Disclose any conflict **in writing** and get the client's **free and informed consent in writing** (s.15(1)). Some conflicts need reasonable grounds to believe the licensee can advise without compromise (s.15(2)).
- **Non-consentable** (s.16): lending to or borrowing from a client; intimate personal relationship with a client (until 1 yr after completion).
- Employment and student-recruitment conflicts (s.17-18): disclose recruiter or education-agent commissions.
- **Joint files** (e.g. sponsor and applicant, employer and worker): treat as a conflict. Get written disclosure and consent from each, and explain what happens if their interests diverge (M: "joint retainer" isn't a defined Code term).

## Consultant pitfalls
- "Guaranteed visa" or "100% success" claims are prohibited. So are Canada flag or government-style logos suggesting affiliation.
- Commissions from schools or employers must be disclosed to clients (s.17-18).
- Social media posts are advertising. The same rules apply.
- Keep each client's written approval on file before using a testimonial, including on Google or social media.
