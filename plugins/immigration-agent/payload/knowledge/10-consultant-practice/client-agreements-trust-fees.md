# Client agreements, trust, fees

## Must cover
- Required terms, trust rules, refunds

## Current rules
### Consultation agreement (Code s.23)
- **In writing, before the initial consultation**. Includes the licensee's name and registration number and the **consultation fee**.

### Service agreement (Code s.24)
- In writing, **before** providing services. Check whether the client already has another authorized representative (s.24(2)(c)).
- Required content s.24(3)(a)-(z), incl.:
  - parties and registration number
  - scope of services
  - **fee estimate** and hourly rate or fixed fee (i)
  - disbursements
  - **payment terms and interest** (l)
  - **advance payments and refund policy** (m)
  - confidentiality (q)
  - **complaint procedure** (r)
  - termination
  - **incapacity plan** (y)
- Amendments in writing (s.24(5)).

### Fees (s.31)
- Fair and reasonable. Disbursements at actual cost (s.31(2)).
- Going over the agreement's estimate or fixed amount, or a new disbursement: tell the client and get their **written agreement** first (s.31(3)). No work that needlessly increases fees (s.31(4)).

### Client (trust) account (Code s.32)
- Advance payments go into a **client account at a College-approved institution**. Give a receipt showing the advance (s.32(b)). No non-client funds. **Separate records per client**. Use funds only for the purpose paid (s.32(e)).
- **Invoice before withdrawal** (s.32(f)); withdraw the invoiced amount **within 30 days of the invoice** (s.32(g)).

### Invoices and receipts (s.33)
- Invoice only **after** the services or disbursements are provided, with a **full description** (s.33(1), (2)).
- A receipt for a payment must show **which invoice** it relates to (s.33(3)).

### CICC Client Account Regulation 2021-001 (approved 2021-11-17)
- **Deposit within 14 calendar days** of receipt. An electronic transfer counts as deposited when the institution gives notice (4.1(a), (b)).
- **Model 1**: all client money into the client account. **Model 2**: only unearned and unbilled money. Choose one and apply it for the **whole audit year** (4.2).
- No client money may be held before a client account exists (3.2). A licensee who holds no client money can file an **annual exemption declaration** (3.3).
- Notify the College within **15 days** of opening or closing a client account (4.5).
- **Never hold money the client needs to qualify for a program** (e.g. proof of funds) (5.1(b)). Firm money only for a reasonable amount of bank charges (5.1(a)).
- Withdraw only for: disbursements with written permission; invoiced disbursements or fees; a transfer on written direction to another licensee, lawyer or notary; money deposited in error; refunds (6.1).
- **Never withdraw more than is held for that client** (6.3). Withdrawals by cheque or electronic transfer only, never to cash or bearer (6.4, 6.5).
- Interest goes to the licensee, unless the client asked for their own account (7).
- Keep enough on deposit to meet all obligations at all times (8.1).
- **Reconcile every month**, within **90 days** of month-end. Keep each reconciliation, the bank statements and a list of money held for each client for **6 years** at the principal business address (9.1).
- Penalties: a written warning first, then $100 per incident; suspension or revocation if not corrected within 30 days (11.1).
- Report an error in trust funds to the Registrar **within 30 days** of discovering it (Code s.39(f)).

### Termination (s.34-36)
- Reasonable notice and good reason. Mandatory termination in s.35(1).
- **Within 30 days** of completion or termination: return documents, accounting, final invoice, **refund excess trust funds** (s.36(1)). Transfer the file within **10 business days** on request, even with payments outstanding (s.36(3)); up to 30 days if delay is beyond your control, with notice (s.36(4)).
- On early termination, also tell IRCC or other bodies you no longer represent the client, within 10 days if they have no procedure (s.36(2)(c)).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- SOR/2026-68 (2026-07-15) adds Registrar powers and compensation fund mechanics (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- No retainer, or a retainer signed after work starts: Code breach.
- Never pay operating expenses from the client account. Withdraw only after invoicing.
- The refund policy must be explicit for each stage (e.g. if IRCC refuses or returns the file).
- Use the client-agreement-drafter skill for compliant templates.

## Common refusal reasons
- n/a (complaint triggers: no agreement, unclear fees, refusal to refund)
