# CICC framework and Code of Professional Conduct

## Must cover
- CICC Act, Code SOR/2022-128, SOR/2026-68 regulations (in force 2026-07-15), penalty framework

## Current rules
### CICC Act (S.C. 2019, c.29, s.292; current to 2026-10-19)
- College, Board, **Complaints Committee** and **Discipline Committee** (s.29); Registrar issues licences and keeps the register (s.30(3)); register public and searchable (s.31(1)).
- Licence on meeting by-law eligibility for the class (s.33). **Professional liability insurance mandatory** (s.42(1)).
- Code made by the Minister (s.43). Breach = professional misconduct or incompetence (s.44).
- Offences (s.79): indictment up to **$50,000** / 2 yrs; summary up to $10,000 / 6 months.
- By-laws (s.80) set: compensation fund, fees, licence classes, CPD, insurance, record keeping, fee disputes, pro bono. Any class allowing IRB representation needs tribunal training (s.80(3)).

### Code of Professional Conduct (SOR/2022-128), key duties
- Integrity, honour, no discrediting conduct (s.4); loyalty (s.5); honesty and candour (s.6); no assisting fraud (s.12); no inducements (s.13); originals returned (s.14).
- **Conflicts** (s.15-18): written disclosure + written informed consent. **Non-consentable**: loans to/from clients; intimate relationships (to 1 yr after completion) (s.16).
- **Competence** (s.19-22): within competence and licence scope; decline or get help from an authorized person, with written disclosure (s.20); deadlines and written status updates (s.22).
- **Agreements** (s.23-24): written consultation agreement before the consultation; written service agreement before services (see client-agreements-trust-fees.md).
- **Futile applications** (s.27): written opinion + client acknowledgement.
- Confidentiality indefinitely (s.28). Fees fair and reasonable (s.31). **Client account** (s.32). Invoices (s.33). Termination (s.34-36). Office administration and records per by-laws (s.37). **Supervise assistants** (s.38). Report to Registrar within **30 days** (bankruptcy, charges, trust errors) (s.39). Respond to College (s.40). Report other licensees' misconduct (s.42). **Advertising** (s.44).

### CICC Regulations (SOR/2026-68)
- Registered **2026-04-16**. In force **2026-07-15** (90th day, s.57(1)). **s.22 register content in force 2027-04-16** (s.57(2)). Gazette publication 2026-05-06.
- Compensation Fund Committee (s.13-15): payments case by case, only on a Discipline finding of a **dishonest act**.
- Registrar measures (s.28-29): conditions, caution (on file up to 2 yrs), reprimand, CPD, mentoring, **monetary penalty per by-law schedule**, suspension, revocation.
- **Discipline Committee penalties (verified 2026-11-07, Justice Laws):** CICC Act s.69(3): conditions, suspension (max prescribed period), revocation, penalty (max prescribed amount). **SOR/2026-68 s.38(2): suspension up to 2 years. s.38(3): penalty under Act s.69(3)(d) up to $50,000.** **s.38(1)(b): additional actions:** reimburse College costs (i) and designated persons' costs (ii); reimburse client fees and disbursements (iii); **additional monetary penalty up to $30,000** (or, for a dishonest act causing client financial loss, an amount equal to the fees and disbursements that client paid) (iv); freeze funds and trust money (v); mandatory CPD (vi); any other public-protection action (vii). Registrar measures (s.29) follow a by-law schedule (separate) (H).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- SOR/2026-68 in force 2026-07-15 (H) - in-force date conflict resolved
- 2026-10-23: IRCC AMPs on unauthorized representation and misrepresentation (SOR/2026-220/221) also bind licensees (H)
- **Correction (2026-11-07):** earlier "by-law-determined / don't quote $30k or $50k" was wrong. Both figures are in SOR/2026-68 s.38: $50,000 (Act penalty) and $30,000 (additional penalty) (H).

## Consultant pitfalls
- Code duties are personal. Supervising staff doesn't shift responsibility (s.38).
- Report criminal charges, bankruptcy and trust errors within 30 days (s.39).
- AMPs under IRPA/Citizenship Act regulations apply in addition to CICC discipline.
