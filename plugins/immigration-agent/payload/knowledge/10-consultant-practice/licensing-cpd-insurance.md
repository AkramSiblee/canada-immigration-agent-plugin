# Licensing, CPD, insurance

## Must cover
- RCIC / RCIC-IRB / RISIA, 16 CPD hours, renewal, liability insurance

## Current rules
**CICC regulations read via browser 2026-11-07 (College-made 2021 series, still posted as current).**
- **Classes** (set by by-law; CICC Act s.80(1)(n)):
  - **RCIC**: IRPA and Citizenship Act matters before IRCC/CBSA/ESDC
  - **RCIC-IRB**: also appears before the IRB. The Act requires tribunal training for any IRB-eligible class (s.80(3)) (H)
  - **RISIA**: international student advisers; student-related advice only; no IRPA/Citizenship representation (s.83(7)(k)) (H)
- **Entry**: graduate diploma / Entry-to-Practice Exam, then licensing steps (secondary).

### Verified from CICC regulations (2026-11-07)
- **CPD (CPD Regulation for RCICs 2021-003, s.4):** **16 CPD hours per annual term**, of which **1 hour on professionalism and/or the Code**. Applies inside and outside Canada. Complete by **11:59 p.m. on the last day of the College fiscal year** (term July 1 to June 30). Suspended and conditional licensees also owe 16 h. Leave: 1 h reduction per month on leave. New licensees: 1 h per remaining month; licensed in Apr-Jun owe none that term. Non-completion brings fines/penalties (s.15). Subject areas: foundational knowledge, case management, legal research, business management, IRB and tribunals, professionalism, cultural competence, communication/advocacy, critical thinking.
- **Professional liability insurance (PLI Regulation 2021-001):** RCICs **must enrol in the College's PLI Group Plan** (ENCON; broker Smith, Petrie, Carr & Scott), even with employer coverage and while on leave (unless excused by the Registrar). **$1,000,000 per claim/per policy period**; **$500 deductible**; $5,000,000 group aggregate; policy term July 1 to July 1. **RISIAs**: provide a certificate of comparable employer coverage.
- **Annual fee (Fees Regulation 2021-001, effective 2021-11-23):** RCIC **$1,809.25** (single payment July 1 or 4 instalments July 1 / Oct 1 / Jan 1 / Apr 1); RISIA **$913** (July 1). **$100 late fee** after 30 days. Plus taxes. Prorated for new licensees. Payment plans available (default not cured in 15 days leads to suspension). Check the current invoice; amounts may since be updated (M on current amount).
- **IRB class training:** required by CICC Act s.80(3) for any class allowed to appear before the IRB.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- None found since the 2021 College regulations (2026-11-07)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Practising before the IRB without the RCIC-IRB class breaches licence scope (Code s.19).
- Keep CPD proof for random audits (retention period not stated in CPD Regulation 2021-003; keep at least 6 years in line with the client-file rule).
- Lapsed insurance = unauthorized practice risk.
