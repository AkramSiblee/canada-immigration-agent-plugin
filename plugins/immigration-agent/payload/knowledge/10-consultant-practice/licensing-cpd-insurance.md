# Licensing, CPD, insurance

## Must cover
- Becoming an RCIC: eligibility, diploma, Entry-to-Practice Exam (EPE), licence application, good character, new-licensee mentoring
- Licence classes L1-L5, RCIC-IRB, RISIA
- 16 CPD hours, renewal, liability insurance, annual fee

## Licence classes (By-law 2021-2 s.10.1, s.10.7) (H)
| Class | Name | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | RCIC Conditional Practice | First licence for anyone licensed after 2022-07-01 |
| L2 | RCIC Restricted Practice | Full IRCC/CBSA/ESDC/Citizenship practice; **no IRB** |
| L3 | RCIC-IRB Unrestricted Practice | Also appears before all 4 IRB divisions |
| L4 | RISIA Conditional Practice | Study-related advice only, inside the hiring school |
| L5 | RISIA Unrestricted Practice | Same scope as L4, after mentoring |
- CICC Act s.80(3): any IRB-eligible class requires tribunal training. s.83(7)(k): RISIAs cannot represent anyone.

## How to become an RCIC (step by step)
**1. Qualifying education (H)**
- Only two accredited programs: **Queen's University Graduate Diploma in Immigration and Citizenship Law** (English, online) or the **Université de Montréal D.E.S.S. en réglementation canadienne et québécoise de l'immigration** (French). The old Immigration Practitioner Programs (IPPs) are **no longer accredited**. (college-ic.ca, how-do-i-become-an-rcic-)
- Queen's admission (immigrationdiploma.queenslaw.ca, read 2026-11-20):
  - A bachelor's degree with a B average. Below a B, two other routes: (a) some university study plus 5+ years of relevant work, or (b) a qualifying route with two undergraduate courses graded A.
  - English test: **IELTS Academic 7.0 in every band**, or CAEL 70 in every component, or PTE Academic 70 in every component. Results must be **no older than 2 years**. Exemptions: native English speaker; 4+ years of full-time university study taught in English; or Queen's ESLA 850 with an A.
  - No residency or status requirement to be admitted.
  - Format: online, 9 courses, full-time in under 12 months or part-time over 24-36 months. Starts in September and January. Fall 2027 applications run 2026-11-01 to 2027-05-01.
  - **Tuition $17,567.12** total (as of 2026-09-01; subject to Board approval). $400 deposit.
- **Language:** CICC's current pages set **no separate CLB/NCLC licensing test**. The language threshold now comes from program admission. A "CLB 9 after the EPE" rule appears only in a **2018 ICCRC guide**, so do not quote it as current. (H for its absence on current CICC pages; M for the Queen's figures, which are the program's own rules.)

**2. EPE qualification application (H)**
- Must be **18 or older**. The diploma must be **completed within 3 years of the intended EPE date**.
- Apply on the College Portal (Initial Licence tab) with valid colour government photo ID and a **Certification of ID form** signed by a guarantor. The College checks your education directly with your school. The **$75 fee is non-refundable**.
- Use ID with 6+ months of validity left. If your ID expires before you pass, you must reapply and pay $75 again.

**3. EPE registration and exam (H)**
- **Format:** closed-book, online with a remote proctor, **135 multiple-choice questions** (standalone and case-based; 10 are unscored), **3 hours**. RISIA EPE: 2 hours.
- **Blueprint:** foundational knowledge 17.6%, case management 19.2%, legal research 8.8%, business management 12.8%, IRB and tribunals 8.8%, professionalism 8.8%, cultural competence 6.4%, plus communication, counselling and advocacy.
- **Scoring:** pass/fail only. The pass mark is set for each sitting by the Bookmark method. Results arrive in about 7 weeks.
- **Fees:** exam $425 for the first attempt and $325 for each re-write. No-show or ID problem: $150. Deferral: see the conflict below. Compassionate waiver: $75. Plus taxes.
- **Attempts:** maximum **4**. Every attempt must fall **within 3 years of completing the diploma**. The regulation also says to re-write within 1 year of the first attempt and pass within 18 months of it. Once you start the exam it counts as an attempt; a deferral or no-show does not. You must write your first attempt within 1 year of registering. After a 4th fail: wait at least 1 year and meet the admission rules then in force; the Registrar may require more education.
- **Sittings:** 2026-07-08 and 2026-10-21 have passed. **Next: 2027-03-10.** Deadlines: qualification **2027-02-03**, registration and accommodation requests **2027-02-17**, deferral 2027-03-05. Results about 2027-04-28. No later 2027 date is posted yet.

**4. Licence application after passing (H)**
- You must **apply within 120 calendar days of passing** or the result expires (RISIA: 182 days). After applying, you have **182 more days to upload supporting documents**.
- Documents:
  - **Police certificate** for every jurisdiction where you lived more than 6 months since age 18. Canadian residents need an **RCMP certified criminal record check with fingerprints**, issued within 1 year. Non-residents need a national police certificate with fingerprints from their country.
  - Photo ID
  - **Business Name Selection Form** or a letter of employment
- Admission criteria (By-law s.10.2): **18+; not insolvent or an undischarged bankrupt; not incompetent**; education, exam and other requirements met; fee paid; **character references**; agree to the Code and the Act; **good character**.
- **Good character** (Good Character and Good Conduct Regulation 2021-001):
  - You must give a **sworn statutory declaration**.
  - Red flags (s.6.1): pending charges or warrants; unpardoned convictions; civil fraud findings; discipline by any regulator; academic misconduct; **current insolvency, a proposal to creditors or wage garnishment**; **past unauthorized paid representation (IRPA s.91)**; insurance claims paid out.
  - A record does not automatically bar you. The Registrar weighs candour, how long ago it happened, rehabilitation and similar factors. **Non-disclosure is the real risk.**
  - The Registrar may refuse, or grant a licence with conditions. Refusals can be appealed to the Registrar Appeal Committee.
- Insurance enrolment and the prorated annual fee apply from licensing (see below). The CICC pages did not show a separate licence application fee. Details are emailed after you pass (not found; re-verify).
- **No Canadian citizenship, PR or residency requirement** appears in the By-law or on the CICC pages. Non-residents are expected, since police certificates from abroad are accepted. (H for absence.)

**5. After licensing: new-licensee obligations (H)**
- **New-Licensee Mentoring Program** is mandatory for every L1 and L3 licensee licensed after 2022-07-01:
  - Complete it **within 12 months** of licensing (By-law s.10.7(d); Mentoring Policy 2024-003).
  - Fee **$665** (non-refundable); re-registration $532; $150 admin fee for an incomplete registration.
  - Online and in a group: a practical-experience period, then assessments.
  - **2 attempts.** Failure can lead to suspension, then revocation.
  - Current intake: 2026-10-27 to 2027-03-28.
- **L1 to L2:** at least **6 months as an L1**, plus completing mentoring, plus good standing (s.10.3).
- **Graduate Diploma route straight to L3 (RCIC-IRB):** graduates of the Queen's diploma or the UdeM DESS who pass the EPE are **issued an L3 licence and exempt from the specialization pathway** (RCIC-IRB Policy 2024-003 s.3.1). They must complete mentoring within 12 months to **keep** L3 (Mentoring Policy).
  - Conflict: By-law s.10.4(b) describes L3 as coming after 6 months as an L1. The Policy says diploma graduates are "issued" L3. Follow the Policy and the Mentoring Policy wording, and confirm the class shown on the Letter of Authority.
- **Practice Management Education (PME Regulation 2021-001, still posted):** one mandatory PME course within 3 months of licensing and the rest before the 1st anniversary. Failure can lead to suspension, then revocation. PME does **not** count as CPD. Whether PME is now delivered through the mentoring program is not stated (M).

## RCIC-IRB (Class L3) for existing L2 licensees (H)
- Eligibility: good standing, plus an approved pathway, plus passing the **Specialization Exam** (Policy 2024-003, approved 2024-06-20; revision date 2026-06-20).
- The only open pathway is the **Education pathway**: 5 courses (Foundations of the IRB, ID, IAD, RPD, RAD). **Fee $1,000**. The PLAR and hybrid pathways were discontinued in 2023.
- Specialization Exam: up to 4 attempts; starting the exam counts as an attempt. After 4 fails the Registrar may require more education.
- **Dual licensees:** a lawyer (any provincial law society) or an Ontario paralegal (LSO) may appear before the IRB under that **other** licence. To appear under the RCIC licence, they must take the pathway and the exam. They must tell clients which licence they are acting under.
- Practising before the IRB without L3 has been a restricted-practice breach since 2023-07-01.

## RISIA route (H)
- 18+; complete CBIE's **ISIEP**; pass the RISIA EPE (2 hours); apply within 182 days of passing; RCMP check with fingerprints plus a letter of employment from the school (DLI).
- Scope: advice only on study permits, TRVs, and how they connect to other programs. **No representation and no completing forms.** Advice only while employed by that school. They must notify the College if they leave.
- L4 to L5: at least 12 months as an L4, plus mentoring (By-law s.10.6).

## Who doesn't need a CICC licence (IRPA s.91(2); see scope-and-representation.md)
- Lawyers and paralegals in good standing with a provincial law society, and Quebec notaries (Chambre des notaires du Québec). Unpaid representatives. Paid representation by anyone else is an IRPA s.91 offence, and it also counts against good character (Regulation s.6.1(k)).

## Ongoing licence requirements (verified 2026-11-07)
- **CPD (CPD Regulation for RCICs 2021-003, s.4):**
  - **16 hours per term** (July 1 to June 30), including **1 hour on professionalism or the Code**. Due by 11:59 p.m. on June 30.
  - Suspended and conditional licensees owe the full 16 hours too. Leave reduces the total by 1 hour per month on leave.
  - New licensees owe 1 hour per remaining month; anyone licensed in April-June owes none for that term.
  - Fines apply for non-completion (s.15). Acting as a mentor can earn CPD hours (Mentoring Policy s.5).
- **Professional liability insurance (PLI Regulation 2021-001):**
  - Mandatory **College group plan** (ENCON; broker Smith, Petrie, Carr & Scott), required even with employer coverage or while on leave unless the Registrar excuses you.
  - **$1M per claim**, **$500 deductible**, $5M group aggregate; term runs July 1 to July 1.
  - RISIAs instead give a certificate of comparable employer coverage.
- **Annual fee (Fees Regulation 2021-001; only version posted as of 2026-11-20):**
  - RCIC **$1,809.25** (paid July 1, or in 4 instalments); RISIA **$913**. Plus taxes.
  - $100 late fee after 30 days. Prorated for new licensees.
  - Confirm the current invoice amount (M on the current figure).

## Consultant pitfalls
- Anyone studying through a non-accredited "RCIC course" can't write the EPE. Only Queen's and UdeM qualify.
- The 3-year clock from finishing the diploma covers **all 4 attempts**. Late starters lose attempts.
- Missing the 120-day licence application window after passing means the result expires.
- Not disclosing a past charge, insolvency or unauthorized practice is worse than the incident itself (Regulation s.7.1(a), (f)).
- Missing the 12-month mentoring deadline leads to suspension, then revocation.
- Practising before the IRB without L3 breaches licence scope (Code s.19).
- Keep CPD proof for audits; keep it at least 6 years, in line with the file-retention rule.
- Lapsed insurance creates an unauthorized-practice risk.

## Common refusal reasons
- Licensing refusal on good-character grounds, especially non-disclosure. EPE withdrawal through missed time limits.
