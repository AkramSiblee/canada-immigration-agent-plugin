# Complaints and discipline

## Must cover
- Intake, Complaints Committee, Discipline Committee, penalties, compensation fund

## Current rules
- **Anyone may complain** (CICC Act s.45). Referral, then investigation (s.48, s.50).
- **Complaints Committee** (s.57): dismiss; require the licensee to appear and be **cautioned**; dispute resolution (with consent); or refer to discipline.
- **Discipline Committee** (s.58 onward): hearing; findings of professional misconduct or incompetence; penalties under Act s.69(3) and SOR/2026-68 s.38 (see below) (H).
- **Registrar measures** (SOR/2026-68 s.28-29): conditions, caution (on file up to 2 yrs), reprimand, CPD, mentoring, **monetary penalty per by-law schedule**, suspension, revocation. Interim suspension powers (Act s.38). Federal Court enforcement (s.39.1). Temporary administration of a practice (s.73.1).
- **Compensation fund** (by-laws; regs s.13-15): pays clients harmed by a licensee's **dishonest act** (Discipline finding required). Amount case by case.
- **Offences** (Act s.79): up to $50,000 / 2 yrs on indictment.
- **Discipline Committee penalties (verified 2026-11-07, Justice Laws):** CICC Act s.69(3): conditions, suspension (max prescribed period), revocation, penalty (max prescribed amount). **SOR/2026-68 s.38(2): suspension up to 2 years. s.38(3): penalty under Act s.69(3)(d) up to $50,000.** **s.38(1)(b): additional actions:** reimburse College costs (i) and designated persons' costs (ii); reimburse client fees and disbursements (iii); **additional monetary penalty up to $30,000** (or, for a dishonest act causing client financial loss, an amount equal to the fees and disbursements that client paid) (iv); freeze funds and trust money (v); mandatory CPD (vi); any other public-protection action (vii). Registrar measures (s.29) follow a by-law schedule (separate) (H).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- SOR/2026-68 in force 2026-07-15: Registrar measures; compensation fund mechanics (H)
- **Correction (2026-11-07):** earlier "by-law-determined / don't quote $30k or $50k" was wrong. Both figures are in SOR/2026-68 s.38: $50,000 (Act penalty) and $30,000 (additional penalty) (H).

## Consultant pitfalls
- Respond to College requests promptly (Code s.40). Non-response is itself misconduct.
- Keep complete files and trust records. They are the main evidence in complaints.
- Use the cicc-compliance-check skill before risky practice decisions.
