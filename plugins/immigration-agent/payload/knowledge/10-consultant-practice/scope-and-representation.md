# Scope and representation

## Must cover
- What RCICs can/cannot do; IMM 5476; ghost consultants; s.91 IRPA

## Current rules
### Who may represent or advise for consideration
- **IRPA s.91(1)**: no one may knowingly represent or advise for consideration (or offer to) on an EOI or IRPA proceeding/application, **except** (s.91(2)) lawyers/notaries; other law society members (incl. paralegals); **CICC members in good standing**; supervised students-at-law (s.91(3)); Crown-agreement entities (s.91(4)).
- **Citizenship Act s.21.1**: same structure for citizenship matters.
- **Penalties for unauthorized representation**: IRPA s.91(9): up to **$200,000 and/or 2 yrs** (indictment); $40,000 and/or 6 months (summary). Plus **AMPs**: IRPR Part 19.2 (SOR/2026-220) and SOR/2026-221 (citizenship), baseline **$5,000** per violation, names published (from 2026-10-23).
- Unpaid help (family, friends) is not "for consideration", but must be declared on the IMM 5476.

### RCIC limits
- **No superior court proceedings** (s.91(10)), so **no Federal Court** JR or stays. **Refer to a lawyer.**
- IRB appearances only with the **RCIC-IRB** class.
- Stay within competence (Code s.19). No legal advice outside immigration/citizenship (e.g. criminal defence, family law, tax). **Refer** (CLAUDE.md rule 4).
- Escalate: Federal Court, criminal equivalency, retroactive asylum bars, multigenerational C-3, citizenship revocation actions.

### IMM 5476 (Use of a Representative)
- Required to appoint a paid or unpaid representative with IRCC. Paid representatives must give their CICC/law society ID. Withdraw it on termination (M: form not re-read).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026-10-23: AMPs for unauthorized paid representation (IRPA and Citizenship regs) (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- **Ghost consultants:** flag files where an unlicensed agent prepared earlier applications (CLAUDE.md rule 5). Get GCMS, review for misrepresentation, and advise the client of AMP and s.40 exposure.
- Don't partner with or take referrals from unlicensed "agents" who charge clients. That is facilitating s.91 breaches (Code s.12).
- Overseas recruiters and education agents giving immigration advice for a fee are covered by s.91 if advising on IRPA matters.

## Common refusal reasons
- Representative undeclared or unauthorized; application returned

- IMM 5476 current version
