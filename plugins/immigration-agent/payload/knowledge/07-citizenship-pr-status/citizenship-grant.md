# Citizenship grant (s.5)

## Must cover
- 1,095 days, what doesn't count, taxes, language, test, oath, minors, s.5(4), prohibitions, how to apply, urgent processing, refusal and JR, fees, processing
- Not here: citizenship by descent, s.5(5) stateless, s.5.1 adoption detail (see citizenship-proof-bill-c3.md); revocation, renunciation, resumption, SOR/2026-221 AMPs (see loss-of-status.md)

## Current rules

### Adult grant, s.5(1)
| Requirement | Rule |
|---|---|
| Status | PR, no unfulfilled PR conditions (s.5(1)(c)); **not under a removal order** or s.20 declaration (s.5(1)(f)) |
| Physical presence | **1,095 days in the 5 years before signing** (s.5(1)(c)(i)), incl. **at least 730 days as PR** (IRCC; follows from the 365-day pre-PR cap) |
| Pre-PR credit | Each day as TR or protected person = **½ day, max 365** (s.5(1.001)(a)) |
| Crown service abroad | Days abroad as Crown servant (CAF, federal or provincial public service) or living with one count in full (s.5(1.01)-(1.03)). Not locally engaged staff. Paper application + CIT 0177 |
| Taxes | Filed for **3 of the 5** taxation years (s.5(1)(c)(iii)) |
| Language (18-54) | Adequate knowledge, s.5(1)(d); IRCC standard **CLB/NCLC 4 speaking and listening** |
| Knowledge test (18-54) | s.5(1)(e); taken after applying |
| Oath | Adults and minors **14-17**; under 14 don't take it (IRCC who page) |
| 55+ | Exempt from language and test (age on signing date) |
| Waivers | s.5(3) compassionate: language, test, oath (and minor presence) |

### Time that does NOT count (s.21)
- Under a **probation order** (s.21(a)), **paroled inmate** (s.21(b)), **served a term of imprisonment** (s.21(c)), under any enactment in force in Canada.
- **Conditional sentence orders: do not count**, and are a prohibition while being served. Source: IRCC program delivery update 2017-06-21 (Bill C-6). The Act does not say "conditional sentence"; it is caught as a term of imprisonment. The current IRCC eligibility page lists only prison, probation and parole.
- **Conditional discharge**: probation time may count if completed successfully (IRCC).
- Completed youth sentences (YCJA/YOA) need not be declared (IRCC).
- **After a removal order is in force**: PR status is lost (IRPA s.46(1)(c)), so no PR days, and s.5(1)(f) bars the grant.
- **Pending s.44 report or admissibility hearing**: not an automatic bar. Processing may be **suspended** (s.13.1(a)-(b)) and a judge can't decide until the removal question is settled (s.14(1.1)). IRCC: you can apply while under review, but processing may be suspended.

### Minors
| Route | Who applies | Requirements | Form |
|---|---|---|---|
| **s.5(2)** | Parent who is a citizen or **applying at the same time**, or legal guardian | PR, under 18 at signing, no prohibition. **No physical presence, tax, language or test** | **CIT 0003** (2026-02) + checklist CIT 0008 |
| s.5(1) minor | Person with custody or legal authority (need not be a citizen) | 1,095 days incl. 730 of the **minor's own** PR days; taxes if any | Online, or paper per IRCC minor 5(1) page |
- Minor 14+ countersigns (s.5(1.04); Regs s.4). Oath at 14-17 (waivable, e.g. disability).
- No one to apply: minor may apply alone using the waiver box (online, with approval).
- Fee **$100** per minor (processing only; no right of citizenship fee, no refund on abandonment).

### Discretionary and special grants
- **s.5(4)**: Minister may direct a grant to any person to alleviate **statelessness** or **special and unusual hardship**, or to reward **services of exceptional value to Canada**. Fully discretionary, rare; ask for it in writing with evidence. No separate fee on the fee list.
- **s.5(5)** stateless child born abroad to a citizen (under 23, 1,095 days in 4 years): see citizenship-proof-bill-c3.md. Fee **$123** (adult, right of citizenship only).
- **s.5.1** adoption, Part 1 **CIT 0010** (parent's citizenship), then Part 2 **CIT 0012** after Part 1 approval and final adoption order. Rules and the s.5.1(4) bar for post-2025-12-15 adoptions are in citizenship-proof-bill-c3.md. s.5.1 fees are not shown on the fee list (unverified).

### Prohibitions, s.22 (grant under s.5(1), (2), (4), 11(1), and oath)
| Para. | Plain language |
|---|---|
| (1)(a) | Now on **probation, parole or serving imprisonment** in Canada |
| (1)(a.1)-(a.2) | Serving a sentence abroad |
| (1)(b) | **Charged, on trial, or appealing** a Citizenship Act offence or any federal indictable offence (not Contraventions Act) |
| (1)(b.1) | Charged abroad with an offence indictable in Canada (waivable, s.22(1.1)) |
| (1)(c)-(d) | War crimes or crimes against humanity: under investigation, charged, or convicted |
| (1)(e) | No authorization to return after removal (IRPA s.52(1)) |
| (1)(e.1) | **Misrepresents or withholds material circumstances** in this application |
| (1)(e.2) | Barred under (e.1) **in the 5 years** before applying |
| (1)(f) | Citizenship revoked for fraud (s.10(1), 10.1(3), former 10(1)(a)) **in the 10 years** before applying |
| (2) | Canadian indictable or Citizenship Act conviction **in the 4 years** before applying, or between application and oath (subject to Criminal Records Act) |
| (3) | Same for foreign convictions, **even if pardoned** |
| (4) | Conviction as PR for treason, terrorism (5+ yrs), spying, listed NDA offences, or serving in a force in armed conflict with Canada: **permanent** bar (Minister may lift, s.22(5)) |
| (6) | No oath if the person never met, or no longer meets, the requirements |

### Language proof (18-54), IRCC steps 1-4
1. **Diploma, certificate or transcript** from secondary or post-secondary study in English or French, in or outside Canada (certified translation if needed).
2. **Third-party test**, any date, expired results OK (page 2026-09-03):
   - CELPIP-G 4+ (after 2014-04-01); CELPIP General-LS (Help Centre)
   - **IELTS General Training** L 4.5 / S 4.0 (Academic **not** accepted)
   - **PTE Core** L 28 / S 42
   - TEF Canada, TEFAQ B1+; TCF Canada, TCFQ B1+; DELF B1+; DALF any; TEF IRN (Help Centre)
3. **LINC/CLIC** CLB 4+. Certificate if completed on or after 2012-11-01; tick box if Jan 2008-Oct 2012.
4. **Provincial programs**: BC ELSA; SK EAL; MB CLB report; NS PENS; ON Adult Non-Credit (Dec 2013+); QC MIFI Bulletin or MEES Relevé.
- Missing or unreadable proof means the **whole application is returned**. Waiver request is the alternative.

### Citizenship test (IRCC, pages 2026-03-31)
- **Online by default**, webcam-proctored, alone, computer or tablet (no phone, no VPN). In-person (paper or oral) or Microsoft Teams for accommodation.
- **20 questions**, multiple choice or true/false, **45 minutes**, pass **15/20 (75%)**. Guide: *Discover Canada*.
- **3 attempts within the 30-day test period.**
- **Need not be in Canada** for the online test (IRCC online-test page).
- Fail all 3: **hearing with a citizenship official**, 30-90 min. Oral test (20 questions, pass 15), residence questions, language check (up to 9 questions, pass 6). Fail the hearing and the application is **refused**; reapply and pay again.
- Pass: possible interview for identity, original documents, eligibility, then ceremony.

### How to apply
- **Online is the default.** The applicant creates their own account, fills it out, signs and submits. **The representative must not use the client's login.**
- **Paper is required** if (a) the presence count uses Crown-servant time abroad, or (b) a **representative completes and submits** the application. Paper must be signed and mailed **within 90 days** of signing (IRCC), to CPC Sydney.
- Forms: **CIT 0002 Adults, version 2026-02** ("Last updated February 2026"; PDF path `.../cit0002/01-02-2026/cit0002e.pdf`); CIT 0007 checklist **2026-10**; IMM 5476 for representatives.
- **Physical presence calculation**: required document. The signature date must equal the calculator's "Application date". IRCC's adult checklist page does not say "printout" (see Re-verify).
- Also needed: passport pages for the full 5 years, 2 IDs, 2 photos, fee receipt, language proof (18-54), police certificates if requested.

### Fees (fee list modified 2026-09-21)
- Adult **$653** ($530 processing + **$123** right of citizenship, since 2026-03-31). Minor **$100**. Stateless adult born to a Canadian parent **$123**.
- The right of citizenship fee is **refunded** to adults if refused or abandoned. Everything is refunded if withdrawn before processing starts.

### Urgent processing (IRCC page 2025-10-27)
- **Exceptional cases only**, e.g. citizenship needed **to apply for a job or keep one**; travel for a **death or serious illness** in the family when no passport is available from the current nationality; a **successful Federal Court decision** on a previous application. The program delivery instructions also list attending a Canadian school.
- Before applying: paper package, envelope marked "Request Urgent Processing – Grant of Citizenship", explanation and proof. After applying: IRCC web form. **Not guaranteed.**

### Oath and ceremony
- **Virtual (video) or in person.** Format can be changed **once**. Reschedule within 30 days of the ceremony date. The first reschedule gets a **final invitation**.
- Miss 2 ceremonies, or an unreasonable second request: **abandonment** (s.13.2). Adults get the $123 refunded; a new application is needed.
- Virtual: sign the Oath/Affirmation form **on the same day**; e-certificate in the IRCC Portal within about 5 business days of receipt.
- **Self-administered (online click) oath: not in force.** Pre-published in Canada Gazette I on 2023-02-25. No final regulation found. Ceremony pages (2026-10-08) do not mention it (M on proposal status).

### Decision, refusal, review
- Decided by a citizenship officer (Minister). Presence doubts may be **referred to a citizenship judge**, who decides within 60 days with written reasons and notice of JR rights (s.14).
- **No appeal to the IRB.** Remedy is **Federal Court JR with leave** (s.22.1(1)). File and serve **within 30 days** of being notified, or of otherwise learning of the decision. A judge may extend for special reasons (s.22.1(2)). The hearing is held within 90 days of leave. FCA appeal only on a **certified question** (s.22.2). Refer JR to a lawyer (CLAUDE.md rule 4).
- Usually better: **reapply** once the shortfall is fixed.

- Inventory: **327,125** grant applications not finalized; **217,960** new citizens since 2026-01-01 (data 2026-08-31; page modified 2026-10-20) (H).

## Consultant pitfalls
- **Don't log in as the client.** If you complete and submit, file on **paper** with IMM 5476. **Ghost-consultant red flag:** unlicensed preparers using client accounts (s.21.1 offence; SOR/2026-221 AMPs).
- Use the **physical presence calculator**. Match its application date to the signature date. Cross-check every trip against CBSA entry/exit data and passport stamps. **Undeclared trips are a misrepresentation risk (s.22(1)(e.1): 5-year bar; s.10 revocation).**
- Exclude probation, parole, imprisonment and **conditional sentence** days (s.21). Check court dispositions before counting.
- **Pending charges** (s.22(1)(b)) and convictions in the last 4 years (s.22(2)-(3)), **foreign ones too, even if pardoned**: screen before filing.
- s.44 report or admissibility hearing pending: expect suspension (s.13.1). The grant is barred only once a removal order is made (s.5(1)(f)).
- Count tax years **filed**, not tax owed.
- Language proof missing or not in an accepted form means the whole application is returned. IELTS Academic is refused.
- Age cut-offs (18, 55, 14) apply on the **signature date**.
- Client abroad: the online test can be taken outside Canada, but the ceremony can't be missed twice.

## Common refusal reasons
- Presence shortfall or inconsistent travel history
- Tax years not filed
- Failed 3 tests and the hearing, or language not shown
- s.22 prohibitions
- Misrepresentation (s.22(1)(e.1))
- Abandonment: missed test, interview or ceremony, or no reply to requests (s.13.2)
