# PR card, PRTD, residency obligation

## Must cover
- 730 days, exceptions, appeals, fees

## Current rules
### Residency obligation (IRPA s.28)
- **730 days in every 5-year period** (not continuous).
- Days abroad that count (s.28(2)(a)):
  - (ii) accompanying a **Canadian citizen** spouse, partner or parent
  - (iii) **employed full-time by a Canadian business** or the federal/provincial public service
  - (iv) accompanying a PR spouse, partner or parent employed as in (iii)
- Assessment (s.28(2)(b)): PR for less than 5 yrs, "will be able to meet" it by the 5th anniversary; 5+ yrs, the **last 5 years** before examination.
- **H&C** can overcome a breach (s.28(2)(c)), including the best interests of a child.
- PR status is **not** lost when the card expires. It's lost only on a final determination (s.46(1)(b)).

### Documents
- **PR card**: $50 (fee list); first card free. Expired card ≠ loss of status, but needed to board flights to Canada.
- **PRTD**: **$50**; single entry; processed with priority. The applicant must show compliance with RO (or H&C/s.28(2)(a) exceptions).
- **One form for both**: IMM 5444 (09-2024) covers the PR card and the PRTD, filed mainly in the Permanent Residence Portal. The old PRTD form **IMM 5524 is no longer used** (IRCC PRTD page and Guide 5529, read 2026-11-10) (H).

### Appeals
- **IAD appeal** of an RO decision made outside Canada (IRPA **s.63(4)**). IAD may allow on H&C (s.67(1)(c)).
- Deadline: **60 days** after receiving the decision (IAD Rules 2022 r.16(c)) (H). Port of entry: a PR may also face a s.44 report and an IAD appeal of the removal order (s.63(3)).
- Processing (IRCC, 2026-11-04): new PR card 29 days; renewal 46 days (H).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- None to s.28 found. IRPA last amended 2026-03-26 (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Clients abroad with a shortfall: **don't** fly in on a PR card without assessing the RO. The CBSA can write a report at the POE. Compare a PRTD application (with H&C) vs entering by land.
- Work for a Canadian business abroad must be a **genuine assignment** with a temporary posting abroad. A remote job for a Canadian company while living abroad permanently often fails (M).
- "Accompanying" a citizen spouse requires actually residing together abroad.
- Voluntary renunciation of PR (IMM 5782 / guide 5781) is useful to get a visitor eTA/TRV. Explain the irreversible effect.

## Common refusal reasons
- Days short with no qualifying exception; H&C insufficient (weak establishment or ties, no compelling reasons for absence, delay in returning)
