# Loss of status (PR and citizenship)

## Must cover
- Renunciation, revocation, PR loss routes

## Current rules
### PR loss (IRPA s.46(1))
- (a) becoming a citizen
- (b) **final determination** of a residency obligation breach (decided outside Canada)
- (c) removal order in force
- (c.1) **cessation** of refugee protection (s.108(2))
- (d) refugee decision vacated
- (e) approved **voluntary renunciation**. Then a temporary resident for 6 months if in Canada (not at a POE) (s.46(1.1)).

### Citizenship renunciation (Citizenship Act s.9)
- Must hold or be about to hold another citizenship; adult; capable; **not reside in Canada**; no s.20 security declaration. Waivers of capacity or residence on compassionate grounds.
- Fee **$100**. **Free** for those who became citizens automatically under the 2009/2015/2025 changes.

### Resumption (s.11)
- Must have become a PR since losing citizenship; **365 days** physical presence in the 2 years before applying; taxes filed for the prior year; no bars (s.10 order, s.10.1, s.20, removal order).
- Fee $530 adult / $100 minor (fee list).

### Revocation (s.10-10.7)
- Grounds: citizenship obtained, retained, renounced or resumed by **false representation, fraud or knowingly concealing material circumstances** (balance of probabilities).
- Written notice. **60 days** for representations; the person may ask for a Minister's decision. Otherwise referred to **Federal Court** for a declaration (s.10.1). s.10.2 presumption where PR was obtained by fraud. s.10.5 combined inadmissibility (creates a removal order). FCA appeal only on a certified question.

### AMPs for representatives (SOR/2026-221, in force 2026-10-23)
- Applies to citizenship matters: **unauthorized paid representation** (s.36), baseline **$5,000**; inducing misrepresentation, fraud or false information (s.37), **$15,000** each; failure to produce documents, $10,000.
- Preliminary notice (30 days for submissions), notice of violation, review within 30 days, payment within 30 days. **Names published.**

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026-10-23: SOR/2026-221 citizenship AMPs for representation misconduct (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- **Revocation and Federal Court: refer to a lawyer.** RCICs can help with the s.10(3.1) written representations only if within scope. Confirm in 10-consultant-practice/scope-and-representation.md.
- Advise against PR renunciation for clients who may want to return. Re-immigration is not guaranteed.
- Citizenship renunciation requires **non-residence** in Canada.
- SOR/2026-221 exposes **consultants** too (e.g. false information). Keep a full verification trail.

## Common refusal reasons
- Renunciation: still residing in Canada; no other citizenship. Resumption: 365 days not met.

## Re-verify before advising
- PR renunciation form number (IMM 5782 vs guide 5781)
