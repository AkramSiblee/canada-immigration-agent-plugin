## Must cover

## Current rules
**Always download the form from the IRCC page on the day of filing.** Old versions are returned. Dates below are as listed on the IRCC forms page (2026-11-03). Rows marked * came from summary, not verbatim. Rows that name the form file were read from the version printed inside the official PDF. ## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026: citizenship forms (CIT 0002 2026-02, CIT 0007 2026-10); refugee forms 2026-09 (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Validated PDF forms must be validated (barcode) before upload.
- Sign where required. Digital signature rules vary by form.

## Common refusal reasons
- Outdated form version; unsigned; unvalidated barcode page

## Re-verify before advising
- Each form's edition on the day of filing
