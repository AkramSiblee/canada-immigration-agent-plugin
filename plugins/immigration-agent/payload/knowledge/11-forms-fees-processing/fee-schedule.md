## Current rules
The fee that applies is the one in force on the **date the application is received** ("received on or after" rule in IRCC notices).

### ESDC (not IRCC)
- **LMIA processing fee: $1,000 per position.** Not refundable on withdrawal or a negative LMIA. Cannot be recovered from the worker. Exempt: primary agriculture and specified NOCs; certain in-home caregivers (medical-needs families).
- Recognized Employer Pilot: $1,000/position; REP ends 2026-12-31.

## Consultant pitfalls
- **RPRF trap**: it can be paid up front or later, but PR isn't finalized until it's paid. A dependent child pays no RPRF; an accompanying spouse does.
- **Date rule**: fees are set by date received. A file submitted on or after an increase date with old fees is incomplete and may be returned. Top up promptly if IRCC requests it.
- A family-class dependent child pays $180, not $270. The economic child fee is $270.
- Open work permit = $155 + $100 holder fee. Spousal OWP applicants often forget the $100.
- Restoration requires both the restoration fee and the new permit fee.
- Biometrics family cap applies only when applying together at the same time.
- LMIA fee is the employer's cost. Charging it to a worker breaches TFWP rules and can mean penalties or bans.

## Common refusal reasons
- Incomplete fee (wrong class rate, missing open work permit holder fee, missing biometrics), leading to return as incomplete (R10/R12)
- Old fee paid after an increase date
