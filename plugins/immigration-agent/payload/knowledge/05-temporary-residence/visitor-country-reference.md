# Visitor visa: reference by country

## Must cover
- For each country: visa need, local visa office instructions, processing time, application centres, medical exam trigger

## Current rules
**How to read this table**
- **Visa need goes by nationality of the passport.** Local instructions, processing time and centres go by **where the person applies from**. A Bangladeshi living in Dubai uses the UAE row for those columns.
- **Local instructions** come from the "Where are you applying from?" list on the IRCC apply page. "None" means IRCC shows no local instructions. Each form is listed below. Download it on the day of filing: `https://ircc.canada.ca/english/pdf/kits/forms/imm####e.pdf`.
- **Processing times** are IRCC data, "lastupdated" November 4, 2026. They change weekly. Check the IRCC tool on the day you advise.
- **Medical** "yes" means the country triggers an exam **only if** the stay is over 6 months and the person lived or travelled there 6+ months in a row in the year before entry. Super Visa and public-health jobs always need one.
- **No centre listed:** the person can use any other centre (IRCC). Russia: centres have not accepted passports or documents since 2026-01-28, so choose one outside Russia.

## Consultant pitfalls
- The general IRCC page says at least 6 months of bank details. Local forms differ (3, 4, 6 or 12 months). Follow the longer of the two where practical.
- Family form conflict: the apply page lists IMM 5645, while many local forms say IMM 5707. Follow the local form and the portal checklist.
- Confirm where the client will apply from. It changes the local form and the processing time.
- Territories (Aruba, Puerto Rico and others) have local rows, but visa need depends on the passport held.

## Common refusal reasons
- Missing a document the local form requires. See `visitor-visa-documents.md` and `12-decision-trees/visitor-refusal.md`.

## Re-verify before advising
- Processing time on the IRCC tool on the day of advice
- Local form edition on the day of filing
- Centre services (IRCC says they may change on short notice)
