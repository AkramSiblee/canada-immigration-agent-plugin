# Global Skills Strategy (GSS)

## Must cover
- 2-week processing, short-term work permit exemptions, Global Talent Stream link, Dedicated Service Channel

## Current rules
### 1. Two-week processing (IRCC page 2026-07-30) (H)
- **Target, not guarantee:** eligible, complete applications processed within 2 weeks. IRCC won't confirm GSS handling; contacting IRCC doesn't speed it up.
- **Eligible:** apply **online from outside Canada** for an **employer-specific** permit where:
  - LMIA-exempt job in **TEER 0 or 1**: need the contract, **offer of employment number**, **exemption code** and **proof the compliance fee was paid**; or
  - LMIA-required job with a positive **Global Talent Stream** LMIA (decision letter).
- **Family:** spouse/children applying for visitor visa, work or study permit **at the same time**.
- **Biometrics within 2 weeks of the BIL** (fee from $85), or GSS is lost. Valid biometrics on file are reused.
- **Excluded:** in-Canada applications (except some doctors/health workers under separate rules), paper, incomplete, untranslated documents, **IEC**, **open work permits**.

### 2. Short-term work permit exemptions (page 2025-09-10) (H)
| Who | Length | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Highly skilled worker, TEER 0 or 1 | 15 consecutive days | once every 6 months |
| Highly skilled worker, TEER 0 or 1 | 30 consecutive days | once every 12 months |
| Researcher at a publicly funded degree-granting institution or affiliated research institute | 120 days | once every 12 months |
- Choose one TEER option per period. Wait 6 months before reusing the 15-day, 12 months for the 30/120-day.
- At entry the worker needs a **visitor record marked "authorized to work"** (no SIN or payroll without it). Visa/eTA still needed.
- R186 general exemptions (business visitors etc.): work-permit-process-and-status.md.

### 3. Global Talent Stream (ESDC page 2026-10-05) (H)
- LMIA in **10 business days**, then 2-week permit (80% of the time). **$1,000 per position**, non-refundable, can't be recovered from the worker.
- **Category A** (designated referral partner): wage at least **$38.46/h ($80,000/yr)** for first 2 positions per year; beyond 2: **$72.11/h ($150,000/yr)**. Mandatory benefit: job creation.
- **Category B** (Global Talent Occupations List): occupation wage floors (e.g. NOC 22310 $86,000; NOC 22220 $85,000: from page summary, M). Mandatory benefit: skills and training investment.
- Labour Market Benefits Plan: mandatory benefit + **at least 2 complementary benefits**.
- Wage floors update with Job Bank: **fall 2026 update on 2026-11-18**. Re-check after that date.
- More detail and EMP5713: work-permits-lmia.md.

### 4. Dedicated Service Channel (page 2026-10-21) (H)
- For employers making a **significant investment in Canada** and universities with publicly funded research chairs. Must be **referred by a referral partner**; then DSC contacts the employer. Email promotion@cic.gc.ca.
- **Does not** process applications or give LMIA exemptions.

## Consultant pitfalls
- TEER 2-3 LMIA-exempt jobs get **no** 2-week processing: set client timelines from processing-times.md.
- Missing the 2-week biometrics window silently drops GSS. Book the VAC appointment the day the BIL arrives.
- Spouse filing later loses GSS; file the family together.
- The 15/30-day exemption is per period, not per trip: track entries. Repeated use can look like ongoing work in Canada.
- GTS Category B wage floors change on 2026-11-18; recheck any LMIA prepared before then.

## Common refusal reasons
- Not GSS-eligible (not TEER 0/1, in-Canada, missing fee proof); short-term exemption used too soon; GTS LMBP commitments unmet (compliance)

## Re-verify before advising
- GTS wage floors after 2026-11-18
