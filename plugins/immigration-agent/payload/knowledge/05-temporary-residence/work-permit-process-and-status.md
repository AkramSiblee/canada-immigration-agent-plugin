# Work permits: applying, status, changing jobs, exemptions

## Must cover
- Where to apply, who needs no permit, extensions and maintained status, changing or losing a job, restoration, worker rights, non-compliant employers

## Current rules
Program rules: `work-permits-lmia.md`, `work-permits-lmia-exempt.md`, `open-work-permits.md`, `iec.md`, `pgwp.md`.

### Where to apply
**All applicants:** enough money for themselves and family and to return home (IRCC points to low-income cut-offs as a general guide); required documents. **Possible refusal:** medical or criminal inadmissibility, security, misrepresentation, an employer marked "ineligible" on the non-compliant list, or an employer regularly offering striptease, erotic dance, escort services or erotic massages.

| Where | Who |
|---|---|
| **Outside Canada** | Default. IMM 1295, passport copy, 1 photo, portal checklist, local visa office documents. Must show they will leave before the permit expires |
| **Inside Canada, only if** | Valid work or study permit; spouse, partner or parent holds a valid work permit; person, spouse, partner or parent holds a valid study permit; PGWP-eligible with a valid study permit; waiting on PR under spouse in Canada or overseas family class; Convention refugee or protected person; legally working without a permit and now needs one for a different job (not business visitors); refugee claimant; TRP valid 6+ months; CUSMA trader, investor, transferee or professional; Quebec PSTQ applicant |
| **Port of entry, only if** | Visa-exempt, and has the positive LMIA or offer of employment number, or is eligible for an open work permit |
| **Cannot use the port of entry** | Needs a visitor visa; IEC (unless a US citizen or PR); SAWP; PGWP; leaving and re-entering from the US or Saint-Pierre and Miquelon just to access services, with limited exceptions (see `flagpoling-biometrics-medicals.md`) |

### At the border
- Passport valid longer than the planned stay; visa if needed; **port of entry letter of introduction**; valid medical results on the day of entry; supporting documents.
- Upfront medical before leaving avoids medical conditions on the permit. Conditions can be removed later with an exam.
- Check the printed conditions: type of work, employer, location, validity, "Must leave Canada by" date.

### Who can work without a work permit (R186 and related)
Many still need a visa or eTA, and conditions apply.

| Group | Key condition |
|---|---|
| **Short-term high-skilled (GSS)** | TEER 0 or 1; up to **15 consecutive days once every 6 months, or 30 consecutive days once every 12 months** |
| **Short-term researcher (GSS)** | Public degree-granting institution or affiliate; 120 days, once every 12 months |
| Examiners and evaluators, public speakers, convention organizers | See IRCC page for each |
| Business visitors | See `transit-and-visit-purposes.md` |
| Crew | Foreign-owned and registered vehicles mainly in international transport |
| Emergency service providers, aviation inspectors and investigators, expert witnesses, military | See IRCC page for each |
| Foreign representatives and family | Family: accredited spouse or child, "D" or "I" on counterfoil and ID, letter of no objection from Global Affairs Canada |
| Athletes and coaches | Foreign team or Canadian amateur team. A Canadian professional team needs a different route |
| Judges, referees, advertising shoots (foreign-financed), news and film crews not entering the labour market | See IRCC page for each |
| Short-term performing artists | Limited period, not ongoing employment with a Canadian group, not making film, TV or radio |
| Health care students | Clinical clerkship, training is the main goal, regulator's written approval where needed, under 4 months, medical exam passed |
| Students | On or off campus under study permit conditions. Recent DLI graduates who applied for a work permit |
| **Religious leaders** | Leader of the congregation only. **Religious workers need an employer-specific permit** (no LMIA; Employer Portal offer) |
- **GSS workers need a visitor record marked "authorized to work"** to get a SIN and be paid. Get it on arrival.

### Extending or changing conditions
- Apply to extend or change: job extended; change in pay or responsibilities; different job; new employer; location; removal of medical conditions; gender identifier (IRM 0002).
- Quebec PSTQ applicants: employer-specific permit up to 12 months (public policy to 2026-12-31; see `open-work-permits.md`).

### Maintained status
- Applied before expiry: can stay and work under the **same conditions** while in Canada, until a decision.
- **Letter WP-EXT** (online, except PGWP): proof of authorization. Shows an expiry 365 days from receipt, but the person can keep working past that date until a decision. Not given if applied after expiry or for a first permit in Canada.
- Employer-specific: same employer, job and location. Applying to change job or employer: must stay with the original employer until approved, unless work authorization is granted.
- Usually **cannot renew a driver's licence or health card**. SIN expires with the permit, can keep working on it, and it can be updated only after the new permit.

- **Pending extension plus a new job offer (read 2026-11-09):** the pending extension doesn't cover the new job. A new in-Canada employer-specific application plus PPCHANGEWORK2020 is the route; the attestation covers a permit "held". For how two pending applications interact, see "Two pending applications at once" below. Don't withdraw the first without review, and file the second before the original expiry where possible. Open permit: can usually change.
- Travel with an extension pending: Help Centre 190 adds that the officer may ask for proof of funds; urgent processing can be requested (page 2026-07-17).

### Changing job or employer (page 2026-10-08)
- **Open permit:** can change at any time.
- **Employer-specific:** new permit needed. **Can start early under the public policy for changing employment:**
  1. Apply for the new employer-specific permit in Canada.
  2. Use the IRCC web form, under "Other", with the **priority code PPCHANGEWORK2020** and the attestation text on the IRCC page.
  3. Wait for the work authorization email before starting.
- Available to employer-specific permit holders and people authorized to work without a permit, **not** business visitors or GSS short-term workers. IEC participants: check IEC rules first.

- **Authorization timing:** IRCC should answer the web form in about 10 to 15 days; the email is not a work permit (page 2026-10-08).
- **Work location (Help Centre 1667 and 1378, 2026-11-03, read 2026-11-09):** location under Conditions or Remarks: apply to change before working elsewhere. Location only under Additional Information: anywhere in Canada, unless the open permit is tied to a nomination or CSQ. A new permit for a changed employer, occupation or restricted location usually needs a new offer.
- **Consequence note (IRPR 200(3)(e)(ii)):** the 6-month bar doesn't apply where work was unauthorized only because of breaching 185(a) or 185(b)(i)-(iii) conditions. A41 non-compliance and removal risk remain.

### Losing a job (page 2026-10-08)
- **Employer-specific:** must stop working for anyone. Leave by the "Must leave by" date unless they have a new offer or another program.
- To stay working: new offer, apply to change conditions, stay in Canada, wait for approval or a work authorization email.
- To stay otherwise: visitor record (no work), or study route.
- Open permit: job loss doesn't affect the permit.

- **Layoff with an offer and no LMIA (read 2026-11-09):** public policy (signed 2025-03-04) names laid-off workers and requires valid TR status, IRPR 199(a) or (b), a filed application and a request by electronic means. Filing without an LMIA is limited to permits expiring within 2 weeks with a complete LMIA already filed (code CPTS2026; page 2026-10-14); IRCC won't decide until the LMIA arrives. Whether interim authorization issues on that filing isn't stated. A person who falls back to visitor status generally loses in-Canada work permit filing under R199.

### Travel while an extension is pending (page 2026-07-17)
- Renewal approved while away: may re-enter as a worker.
- Still processing: may re-enter **as a visitor and cannot work** until approved. Some can apply at a port of entry.
- A work permit is not a travel document. Check visa or eTA.

### Restoration (page 2026-08-28)
- Status ends on the "Must leave Canada by" date if later than the permit expiry. Status is lost if it expires before applying, or conditions aren't respected.
- **Must stop working.** Restore the original permit and status first, then apply for a different one.
- Family members each apply and pay separately; no family rate.
- 90-day limit and fees: `trp-restoration-maintained-status.md`.
- **Eligibility, exact list (restore page 2026-08-28; read 2026-11-17, H):** "apply within 90 days of losing your status"; "met the requirements for your stay (for example, you didn't work illegally)"; "met all the conditions listed on your expired permit before it expired"; "are not a temporary resident permit (TRP) holder".
- **Support-letter exemption wording:** "If you're a foreign worker with a letter of support from a participating province or territory" you're exempt from the 90-day limit, the stay requirements and the prior-conditions requirement. **The page names no province**; it links out to the list (current: Manitoba only, see below).
- **Refusal:** "If your application is refused, you'll have to leave Canada." Work: "Normally, you aren't allowed to work until your status has been restored and your new work permit issued."
- **Visitor-record fallback, before expiry (not on the restore page):** apply to extend as a visitor, **$100 per person** ("Extend your stay as a visitor - per person"; fee list modified 2026-11-10; no family rate). Work: "You can keep working only until your work permit expires. You cannot continue to work while waiting for a decision on your visitor application." (expired-permit page 2026-07-17). https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/extend/expired-permit.html

### Restoration plus a new permit in one application (restore page 2026-08-28; read 2026-11-17)
- **One online IMM 5710 application.** Q3: tick **"Restore my status as a worker"** plus the new permit choice. Upload a letter explaining the overstay under Client information > Optional documents. Guide 5553. Paper only for disability or system failure.
- **Fees:** restoration $246.25 + work permit $155 (+ $100 open work permit holder fee if open; + $85 biometrics if needed). Worker total $401.25 (closed) or $501.25 (open), before biometrics. **The portal does not charge the $155 when restoration is combined:** pay it separately and upload the receipt in Client information. Each family member applies and pays.
- **Order of assessment:** the officer restores the original status and authorization first, then processes the new permit. Study example on the page: expired worker restoring and getting a study permit in one application.
- **What the officer assesses (IRPR s.182):** for restoration, that the person kept to the **initial requirements of the status being restored** and breached no other condition (unauthorized work or study defeats it). The new permit is then assessed on **its own** requirements (R200 for a work permit). Restoring as a worker while seeking a different permit: both tests apply. Secondary sources (CILA) report IRCC limits restoration to the **last status and authorization held** (M).
- **PGWP:** restore as a **student**, not a worker. Apply online for the PGWP within 90 days of study permit expiry, pay $255 PGWP + the restoration fee **on the IRCC payment page** and attach the receipt. No separate restoration application. No work until the PGWP is approved (PGWP apply page 2026-08-28).
- **BOWP:** eligible if the person "is eligible to restore your status and get a work permit" (BOWP page 2026-10-16). Same combined IMM 5710 route.
- **No work (or study) while pending**, even with a combined application (s.183(1)(b)-(c); restore page). The new permit must issue first. No WP-EXT letter.
- If refused: must leave Canada. Once 90 days have passed, only a TRP or leaving remain.

### Provincial support letter exception to the 90-day rule (read 2026-11-17)
- **Only current policy: Manitoba Workforce Transition Bridge** temporary public policy (effective 2026-07-06; instructions page 2026-08-19; program update 2026-08-05; eligibility page 2026-08-18).
  - Who: Manitoba PNP candidates with a **Manitoba support letter issued on or before 2025-12-31** confirming placement in the MPNP **EOI pool**; letter of employment from a current Manitoba employer; work permit valid on 2024-05-07, since expired, or s.186(u) authorization. Not new candidates; not people already nominated (secondary, M). Manitoba may issue up to 2,700 letters (secondary, M).
  - Status: valid status, maintained status, or **restore when applying**. Expired-permit cohort: **exempt from the 90-day restoration deadline** in s.182 and from the stay and pre-expiry condition requirements.
  - Apply online: "Applying for an open work permit under an active public policy or pilot program announced by IRCC"; code **MBEOI2024** in both the job title and duties boxes.
  - Permit: open, Manitoba only ("Manitoba NES"), **2 years** or passport expiry, **no further extension**.
  - **Expiry: applications must be received before 2027-12-31.** Later applications, including renewals, are refused.
  - Fees on the instructions page: $155 + restoration "$229" + $85 biometrics. **Conflict:** the $229 restoration fee is out of date. The fee list shows $246.25 since 2025-12-01. Pay the fee-list amount. Re-checked 2026-11-20: instructions page still 2026-08-19 with "$229" and no $100 OWP holder fee listed; fee list (2026-11-10) $246.25 (H). Whether the $100 holder fee applies isn't stated on the instructions page (unknown; check the portal fee screen).
- **Expired:** the 2024 PNP-candidate policy and its Alberta, Manitoba and Yukon extension (instructions page 2026-08-19). The restore page's generic "participating province or territory" text is not a separate open policy.
- **No other province or territory has a current support-letter work permit policy** on IRCC's instructions page as of 2026-11-17. Quebec PSTQ applicants use a different policy and are not exempt from restoration (see `open-work-permits.md`).

### Two pending applications at once (maintained status instructions 2025-05-28; read 2026-11-17)
- Examples: an extension and a PGWP, or an LMIA permit and a BOWP. **Officers process both** and don't administratively cancel the outstanding one.
- **Both filed before status expired:** if the first is refused, maintained status continues while the second is processed.
- **Second filed after the original expiry** (while on maintained status from the first): the second gives no maintained status (instructions, H). If the first is refused, the person is out of status and must restore within 90 days if eligible. Secondary sources say the second application is then refused (CIC News, Fragomen; M).
- **Work while both are pending:** s.186(u) work continues only under the **old permit's conditions**. Neither application adds new work authorization until approved.
- **Withdrawing:** if no other extension is pending, the authorized stay ends on the withdrawal date. Don't withdraw one without checking the filing date of the other.
- **Which decision governs:** not addressed in the instructions. A refusal of one doesn't cancel an approval of the other. If one is approved first, the person holds that permit; what happens to maintained status under the other is unclear. **Unknown; treat case by case.**

### LMIA validity vs work permit validity
- **LMIA validity: up to 6 months** from issuance (all streams except SAWP; ESDC can issue shorter). The work permit application must be **submitted before** the LMIA validity date. Processing time after that doesn't matter. Late applications are refused (ESDC expiry page 2026-04-14; IRCC instructions 2026-10-28).
- **LMIA "duration of employment"** is separate from the validity date. Officers "must not issue work permits for a longer validity than the duration of employment specified in the LMIA". The permit is also limited by the **passport (and biometrics) expiry** (IRCC instructions 2026-10-28; POE apply page 2026-11-16).
- Overseas: Valid To is generally calculated from approval for the full LMIA duration. Officers may add a remark so CBSA issues the full duration from the date of entry.
- **Shortened by the passport:** extend under the **same LMIA** after the passport is renewed, even past the LMIA validity date, within the original duration. On maintained status with the same employer, the extension runs from the old permit's expiry date.
- **Permanent-duration LMIA:** the work permit is capped at **2 years**.
- **Maximum durations ESDC can approve:**
  - High-wage: **up to 3 years**, longer only in exceptional circumstances with an adequate rationale (high-wage page 2026-10-07).
  - Low-wage: **up to 2 years for LMIAs submitted on or after 2026-11-06**, up from 1 year (ESDC release 2026-11-06). **Conflict (re-checked 2026-11-20):** the low-wage page (still 2026-10-07) says "a maximum of 1 year"; the low-wage requirements page (2026-11-10) states no maximum; the PR LMIA page (2026-06-09) still says 1 year. The release governs for new applications (H for all texts).
  - Duration can be changed only while the LMIA is pending, not after a positive decision.
- **Concurrent processing:** program update 2026-08-21: "applications eligible for concurrent processing without a labour market impact assessment (LMIA)" "will not receive a decision until 90 days has elapsed from the date the application was received" (grace period was 60). **Re-checked 2026-11-20: the 90 days is still only in the update notice.** The instructions page it links to (validity/duration PDI, 2026-10-28) says IRCC holds the file "until there is a decision on the LMIA" and gives code CPTS2026, with no day count. Client pages (extend/apply 2026-10-14; employer extend page 2026-10-14) and Help Centre 701 (2026-11-03) give only the 2-week rule and no 60/90 days (H for all texts). Practice: tell the employer the LMIA should be decided within 90 days of IRCC's receipt.

### PR-only and dual-intent LMIAs (ESDC pages 2026-06-09, 2026-09-18, 2026-10-27)
- **PR-only:** "a position in support of a permanent residency application only (no application for work permit)". **No processing fee.** Exempt from the transition plan, the low-wage cap, and the refusal to process in CMAs at 6%+ unemployment.
- **Dual intent:** supports both a PR visa and a work permit. **$1,000 per position**, not refundable. Transition plan required for high-wage positions since 2024-09-26.
- Offer must fit FSW (TEER 0-3), FST (TEER 2-3, up to 2 employers) or CEC: full-time 30+ hours, at least 1 year, non-seasonal (FSW/CEC). Excluded: embassies, ineligible employers, businesses under 1 year old, Quebec destinations.
- **After Express Entry MI 2026-1 (ITAs from 2026-11-03):** no LMIA is needed for FSW arranged employment or the FST job offer, and an LMIA earns **no extra CRS** (see `01-federal-economic/crs-scoring.md`). ESDC's PR LMIA pages (latest 2026-09-18) **still offer the PR-only stream** and don't mention the change.
- **Remaining uses (derived, M):** ITAs issued before 2026-11-03 under the old rules; a work permit to actually start work (dual intent, unless LMIA-exempt). A PR-only LMIA now has little Express Entry value. Check PNP stream rules before using one for a PNP.
- **Unknown:** whether ESDC will retire or change the PR-only stream after MI 2026-1.

### Work permits for PR applicants (IRCC menu 2026-01-09)
Bridging open work permit; spouse in Canada; TR to PR (to 2026-12-31); AIP 2-year employer permit; RCIP and FCIP 2-year employer permit; Quebec CSQ holders; Start-up Visa (closed to new applicants; extensions only); Agri-Food family permits (pilot closed 2025-05-14; pending files only).

### Fee exemptions (page 2025-09-25)
- **Employer-specific, exempt from processing and compliance fees:** Fulbright, Roosevelt Campobello, cultural agreements with listed countries, family of reciprocal military and accredited missions, IDRC, AECL, NRC, NSERC, research chairs, unpaid charitable or religious workers. US government personnel: processing fee only.
- **Open:** refugee claimants who need to work (both fees).

### Worker rights (page 2026-03-31)
- Employer must pay for work and keep the workplace safe, and **can't take the passport or work permit**.
- Employer-specific permit: signed employment agreement required. Keep a copy.
- Provincial labour standards apply to most jobs; federal law to federal government, banks, interprovincial transport, telecom.
- Contacting labour offices needs no permission and cannot lead to punishment or deportation.
- Housing is required only for SAWP. Room and board deductions are limited in most provinces.

### Non-compliant employers (IRCC page 2026-11-06; names load live, so check the page itself)
- Outcomes: monetary penalty, a hiring ban, or both.
- Employers must keep records for **6 years** from the worker's first day.
- **Check the list before any job offer.** An "ineligible" employer can't be used.

## Consultant pitfalls
- Don't let a client start with a new employer until approved or the authorization email arrives.
- Leaving Canada during maintained status removes work authorization on return.
- Religious "workers" and "leaders" are different. Many clients need a permit.
- GSS workers leaving the airport without the visitor record can't get a SIN.
- Warn clients never to pay for a job offer or LMIA, and never to surrender their passport.

## Common refusal reasons
- Ineligible employer; funds; intent to leave; misrepresentation; applying at the port of entry when not eligible
