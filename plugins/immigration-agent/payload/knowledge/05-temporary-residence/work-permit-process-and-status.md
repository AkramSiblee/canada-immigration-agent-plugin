# Work permits: applying, status, changing jobs, exemptions

## Must cover
- Where to apply, who needs no permit, extensions and maintained status, changing or losing a job, restoration, worker rights, non-compliant employers

## Current rules
Program rules: `work-permits-lmia.md`, `work-permits-lmia-exempt.md`, `open-work-permits.md`, `iec.md`, `pgwp.md`.

### Where to apply
**All applicants:** enough money for themselves and family and to return home (IRCC points to low-income cut-offs as a general guide); required documents. **Possible refusal:** medical or criminal inadmissibility, security, misrepresentation, an employer marked "ineligible" on the non-compliant list, or an employer regularly offering striptease, erotic dance, escort services or erotic massages.

| Where | Who |
|---|---|
| **Outside Canada** | Default. IMM 1295, passport copy, 1 photo, portal checklist, local visa office documents. Must show they will leave before the permit expires |
| **Inside Canada, only if** | Valid work or study permit; spouse, partner or parent holds a valid work permit; person, spouse, partner or parent holds a valid study permit; PGWP-eligible with a valid study permit; waiting on PR under spouse in Canada or overseas family class; Convention refugee or protected person; legally working without a permit and now needs one for a different job (not business visitors); refugee claimant; TRP valid 6+ months; CUSMA trader, investor, transferee or professional; Quebec PSTQ applicant |
| **Port of entry, only if** | Visa-exempt, and has the positive LMIA or offer of employment number, or is eligible for an open work permit |
| **Cannot use the port of entry** | Needs a visitor visa; IEC (unless a US citizen or PR); SAWP; PGWP; leaving and re-entering from the US or Saint-Pierre and Miquelon just to access services, with limited exceptions (see `flagpoling-biometrics-medicals.md`) |

### At the border
- Passport valid longer than the planned stay; visa if needed; **port of entry letter of introduction**; valid medical results on the day of entry; supporting documents.
- Upfront medical before leaving avoids medical conditions on the permit. Conditions can be removed later with an exam.
- Check the printed conditions: type of work, employer, location, validity, "Must leave Canada by" date.

### Who can work without a work permit (R186 and related)
Many still need a visa or eTA, and conditions apply.

| Group | Key condition |
|---|---|
| **Short-term high-skilled (GSS)** | TEER 0 or 1; up to **15 consecutive days once every 6 months, or 30 consecutive days once every 12 months** |
| **Short-term researcher (GSS)** | Public degree-granting institution or affiliate; 120 days, once every 12 months |
| Examiners and evaluators, public speakers, convention organizers | See IRCC page for each |
| Business visitors | See `transit-and-visit-purposes.md` |
| Crew | Foreign-owned and registered vehicles mainly in international transport |
| Emergency service providers, aviation inspectors and investigators, expert witnesses, military | See IRCC page for each |
| Foreign representatives and family | Family: accredited spouse or child, "D" or "I" on counterfoil and ID, letter of no objection from Global Affairs Canada |
| Athletes and coaches | Foreign team or Canadian amateur team. A Canadian professional team needs a different route |
| Judges, referees, advertising shoots (foreign-financed), news and film crews not entering the labour market | See IRCC page for each |
| Short-term performing artists | Limited period, not ongoing employment with a Canadian group, not making film, TV or radio |
| Health care students | Clinical clerkship, training is the main goal, regulator's written approval where needed, under 4 months, medical exam passed |
| Students | On or off campus under study permit conditions. Recent DLI graduates who applied for a work permit |
| **Religious leaders** | Leader of the congregation only. **Religious workers need an employer-specific permit** (no LMIA; Employer Portal offer) |
- **GSS workers need a visitor record marked "authorized to work"** to get a SIN and be paid. Get it on arrival.

### Extending or changing conditions
- Apply to extend or change: job extended; change in pay or responsibilities; different job; new employer; location; removal of medical conditions; gender identifier (IRM 0002).
- Quebec PSTQ applicants: employer-specific permit up to 12 months (public policy to 2026-12-31; see `open-work-permits.md`).

### Maintained status
- Applied before expiry: can stay and work under the **same conditions** while in Canada, until a decision.
- **Letter WP-EXT** (online, except PGWP): proof of authorization. Shows an expiry 365 days from receipt, but the person can keep working past that date until a decision. Not given if applied after expiry or for a first permit in Canada.
- Employer-specific: same employer, job and location. Applying to change job or employer: must stay with the original employer until approved, unless work authorization is granted.
- Usually **cannot renew a driver's licence or health card**. SIN expires with the permit, can keep working on it, and it can be updated only after the new permit.

- **Pending extension plus a new job offer (read 2026-11-09):** the pending extension doesn't cover the new job. A new in-Canada employer-specific application plus PPCHANGEWORK2020 is the route; the attestation covers a permit "held". IRCC's pages don't address how the two pending applications interact, so don't withdraw the first without review, and file the second before the original expiry where possible. Open permit: can usually change.
- Travel with an extension pending: Help Centre 190 adds that the officer may ask for proof of funds; urgent processing can be requested (page 2026-07-17).

### Changing job or employer (page 2026-10-08)
- **Open permit:** can change at any time.
- **Employer-specific:** new permit needed. **Can start early under the public policy for changing employment:**
  1. Apply for the new employer-specific permit in Canada.
  2. Use the IRCC web form, under "Other", with the **priority code PPCHANGEWORK2020** and the attestation text on the IRCC page.
  3. Wait for the work authorization email before starting.
- Available to employer-specific permit holders and people authorized to work without a permit, **not** business visitors or GSS short-term workers. IEC participants: check IEC rules first.

- **Authorization timing:** IRCC should answer the web form in about 10 to 15 days; the email is not a work permit (page 2026-10-08).
- **Work location (Help Centre 1667 and 1378, 2026-11-03, read 2026-11-09):** location under Conditions or Remarks: apply to change before working elsewhere. Location only under Additional Information: anywhere in Canada, unless the open permit is tied to a nomination or CSQ. A new permit for a changed employer, occupation or restricted location usually needs a new offer.
- **Consequence note (IRPR 200(3)(e)(ii)):** the 6-month bar doesn't apply where work was unauthorized only because of breaching 185(a) or 185(b)(i)-(iii) conditions. A41 non-compliance and removal risk remain.

### Losing a job (page 2026-10-08)
- **Employer-specific:** must stop working for anyone. Leave by the "Must leave by" date unless they have a new offer or another program.
- To stay working: new offer, apply to change conditions, stay in Canada, wait for approval or a work authorization email.
- To stay otherwise: visitor record (no work), or study route.
- Open permit: job loss doesn't affect the permit.

- **Layoff with an offer and no LMIA (read 2026-11-09):** public policy (signed 2025-03-04) names laid-off workers and requires valid TR status, IRPR 199(a) or (b), a filed application and a request by electronic means. Filing without an LMIA is limited to permits expiring within 2 weeks with a complete LMIA already filed (code CPTS2026; page 2026-10-14); IRCC won't decide until the LMIA arrives. Whether interim authorization issues on that filing isn't stated. A person who falls back to visitor status generally loses in-Canada work permit filing under R199.

### Travel while an extension is pending (page 2026-07-17)
- Renewal approved while away: may re-enter as a worker.
- Still processing: may re-enter **as a visitor and cannot work** until approved. Some can apply at a port of entry.
- A work permit is not a travel document. Check visa or eTA.

### Restoration (page 2026-08-28)
- Status ends on the "Must leave Canada by" date if later than the permit expiry. Status is lost if it expires before applying, or conditions aren't respected.
- **Must stop working.** Restore the original permit and status first, then apply for a different one.
- Family members each apply and pay separately; no family rate.
- 90-day limit and fees: `trp-restoration-maintained-status.md`.

### Work permits for PR applicants (IRCC menu 2026-01-09)
Bridging open work permit; spouse in Canada; TR to PR (to 2026-12-31); AIP 2-year employer permit; RCIP and FCIP 2-year employer permit; Quebec CSQ holders; Start-up Visa (closed to new applicants); Agri-Food family permits.

### Fee exemptions (page 2025-09-25)
- **Employer-specific, exempt from processing and compliance fees:** Fulbright, Roosevelt Campobello, cultural agreements with listed countries, family of reciprocal military and accredited missions, IDRC, AECL, NRC, NSERC, research chairs, unpaid charitable or religious workers. US government personnel: processing fee only.
- **Open:** refugee claimants who need to work (both fees).

### Worker rights (page 2026-03-31)
- Employer must pay for work and keep the workplace safe, and **can't take the passport or work permit**.
- Employer-specific permit: signed employment agreement required. Keep a copy.
- Provincial labour standards apply to most jobs; federal law to federal government, banks, interprovincial transport, telecom.
- Contacting labour offices needs no permission and cannot lead to punishment or deportation.
- Housing is required only for SAWP. Room and board deductions are limited in most provinces.

### Non-compliant employers (IRCC page 2026-11-06; names load live, so check the page itself)
- Outcomes: monetary penalty, a hiring ban, or both.
- Employers must keep records for **6 years** from the worker's first day.
- **Check the list before any job offer.** An "ineligible" employer can't be used.

## Consultant pitfalls
- Don't let a client start with a new employer until approved or the authorization email arrives.
- Leaving Canada during maintained status removes work authorization on return.
- Religious "workers" and "leaders" are different. Many clients need a permit.
- GSS workers leaving the airport without the visitor record can't get a SIN.
- Warn clients never to pay for a job offer or LMIA, and never to surrender their passport.

## Common refusal reasons
- Ineligible employer; funds; intent to leave; misrepresentation; applying at the port of entry when not eligible
