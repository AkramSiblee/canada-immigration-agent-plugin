# TRP, restoration, maintained status

## Must cover
- A24 TRP, s.182 restoration, s.183(5) maintained status, fees

## Current rules
### Maintained status (IRPR s.181, s.183(5)-(6))
- Apply to extend **before** the authorized stay ends (s.181(1)). If no decision by expiry, the stay is **extended until decision**; the person "retains their status" with the same conditions (s.183(6)).
- **Work while waiting** (s.186(u)): may keep working if they applied for a new work permit before expiry and keep complying with the old conditions (except the date).
- PGWP applicants: s.186(w) allows work while the PGWP is pending, if they met the s.186(v) student-work criteria.
- Leaving Canada ends maintained status. Re-entry is as a visitor, without work authorization from the pending application.

### Restoration (IRPR s.182)
- Apply **within 90 days** after losing status for breaching the period of stay or specified conditions (s.185(a), (b)(i)-(iii), (c)).
- Officer "shall restore" if the initial requirements are met, no other conditions were breached, and no s.22.1 declaration applies. Students who fail the s.220.1(1) condition can't be restored (s.182(2)).
- **No work or study while restoration is pending** (s.183(1)(b)-(c)).
- Fees: restoration **$246.25** + new permit: worker $401.25 total; student $396.25; visitor $246.25 (since 2025-12-01).

### TRP (IRPA s.24)
- For a person who is **inadmissible or doesn't meet requirements**, if the officer finds it "justified in the circumstances". Can be cancelled at any time. Officers follow Minister's instructions (s.24(3)).
- Bars: failed refugee claimants can't request one for **12 months** (s.24(4)). Designated foreign nationals: 5 yrs (s.24(5)).
- Fee **$246.25** (since 2025-12-01).
- **PR via permit holder class** (IRPR s.64-65): continuous TRP holders become eligible after **3 years** (or **5 years** for criminality/serious criminality grounds) (M: Division 4 not re-read this pass).
- No fixed validity in s.24. The officer sets validity per case.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-12-01: restoration and TRP fees $239.75 rose to $246.25 (H)
- 2026-11-03: s.183(4)(c.1) stay ends 10 yrs after latest biometrics for certain persons (IRPR) (M on effective date)

## Consultant pitfalls
- **Maintained status only exists if the extension was filed before expiry.** Keep proof of submission time and date.
- A refusal of an extension during maintained status puts the client out of status immediately. The 90-day restoration clock starts then.
- Restoration applicants must **stop working** at once. Continuing work is unauthorized work, which brings A41 inadmissibility risk.
- A TRP needs compelling reasons that outweigh the risk. A criminal TRP is not a substitute for rehabilitation where that is available (see 08-inadmissibility).
- Once the 90 days pass, restoration is impossible. Only a TRP or leaving Canada remain.

## Common refusal reasons
- Restoration: other conditions breached (unauthorized work/study); initial requirements not met (funds, enrolment)
- TRP: need not compelling; risk too high; incomplete rehabilitation evidence
