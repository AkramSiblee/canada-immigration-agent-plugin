# Study permits: applying, conditions, extensions, minors

## Must cover
- Where to apply, arrival, conditions, on-campus and co-op work, changing schools, extending and restoring, travel, minors, exemptions, graduate and Francophone routes

## Current rules
Requirements, PAL/TAL, cap, funds and off-campus work are in `study-permits.md`. PGWP is in `pgwp.md`.

### Who doesn't need a study permit (IRPR s.188)
- Family or private staff of an accredited foreign representative
- Members of designated armed forces under the Visiting Forces Act
- A course or program of **6 months or less**, finished within the authorized stay (may still apply before entering)
- An Indian under the Act
- Work permit holders may study up to 6 months under a 2026 public policy, to 2027-12-31 (see `study-permits.md`)
- **Construction apprentices:** valid work permit, eligible construction occupation, apprenticeship agreement registered with the province or territory (temporary measure)
- **IRCC conflict:** the "do you need a study permit" page (2026-09-09) still describes a worker measure limited to permits or letters issued on or before 2023-06-07, while public policy 2026-2 (2026-09-09) covers current work permit holders for up to 6 months. Confirm on the public policy before advising
- A minor without a study permit must still have valid visitor status. On reaching the age of majority (18 or 19), they must apply for a study permit to keep studying

### Where to apply
- **Online is mandatory** in or outside Canada, unless a disability prevents it or the person holds a document for non-national residents, refugees or stateless persons.
- **Outside Canada:** the default. Use the local visa office instructions. A visa or eTA is issued automatically with the permit, with no separate fee.
- **Inside Canada, only if currently in Canada and:** the person, spouse or parent holds a valid study or work permit; minor in primary or secondary school; exchange or visiting student; completed a short course required for DLI acceptance; person, spouse or dependent child has a TRP valid 6+ months; sponsored PR applicant, if eligible; person, spouse or dependent child under an unenforceable removal order; family of an athlete on a Canadian team, media, clergy, military on duty or accredited representative; refugee claimant or family.
- **At the port of entry, only:** US citizens, US permanent residents, residents of Greenland or Saint-Pierre and Miquelon.

### After approval and arrival
- Outside Canada: a **port of entry letter of introduction**, which is not the permit. The border officer prints the permit.
- **At the border:** passport; letter of introduction; copy of valid LOA; visa or eTA; proof of funds; any documents the visa office asked for.
- **Medical exam must be valid on the day of entry** (12 months), even if the letter of introduction is still valid.
- Get a passport stamp if the school needs proof of entry.
- **No work on or off campus until studies begin.**
- Deferring the start: DLI approval and an updated LOA. Upload it if still in process, or show it at the border with a valid letter of introduction.
- **Changing DLI after approval while outside Canada:** new application, new LOA, all fees again.
- Biometrics: 30 days from the instruction letter.

### Study permit conditions
- **Must:** be enrolled at a DLI; actively pursue studies (full or part-time each semester, excluding scheduled breaks, and making progress); take no authorized leave over **150 days**; apply to extend the permit to change post-secondary schools; stop studying if requirements are no longer met; leave when the permit expires.
- **Other conditions may be printed:** level of study, work authorization, medical reporting, travel in Canada, date to stop studying.
- **Authorized leave:** medical or pregnancy, family emergency, death or serious illness of family, other leave the school authorizes, school closure or strike, changing schools, deferred start. No notice to IRCC needed, but proof may be requested. **No work during leave**, except up to 150 days during a strike closure if otherwise authorized.
- **IRCC checks** may be random or for cause. It can ask for enrolment letters, transcripts, leave dates and medical proof. IRCC may email from an address ending in @cic.gc.ca asking for confirmation by a set date.
- **Non-compliance:** may be asked to leave, and **may have to wait 6 months** before applying for a new study permit, visitor visa or work permit in Canada. Future applications can be affected.
- **Exempt from the DLI and active-study proof:** refugee claimants and refugees and family; accredited representatives and family; foreign armed forces and family; suddenly impoverished permit holders; exchange students; certain US government staff; family of certain work or study permit holders in Canada. All other conditions still apply.

### Validity
- Usually program length plus 90 days.
- **Finished early:** permit stops being valid **90 days after completion**, whatever the printed date. The 90 days start on the school's first notice (email, letter or transcript) or receipt of the credential.
- Cannot run past the passport expiry.

### On-campus work
- **Requires:** full-time at a DLI (final-semester part-time allowed if full-time before); post-secondary, or Quebec secondary-level vocational; valid permit or extension filed before expiry; work condition printed on the permit; SIN.
- **No hour limit**, in term or during breaks.
- **On campus** means all buildings at the campus where the student studies. Off-campus locations are allowed only for a teaching or research assistant, on work strictly tied to a research grant, at an associated library, hospital or research facility.
- **Employers:** school, faculty member, student organization, private business or contractor on campus, or the student's own business physically on campus.
- **Must stop:** no longer full-time (except final semester), permit expired, authorized leave, or between schools and not studying.
- Missing condition on the permit: request an amendment, at no fee, before applying for a SIN. Public-health settings need a medical exam.

### Student work placements (page 2026-05-01)
- **Post-secondary: no co-op work permit since 2026-04-01.** Must be required by the program; DLI letter; full-time; work condition on the permit; placement **50% or less** of the program. No weekly hour limit. Any employer accepted by the school's program.
- **Secondary students still need a co-op work permit**, which has no fee.
- **Not eligible:** ESL or FSL, general interest, courses preparing for another program, courses outside Canada.
- Existing co-op permits stay valid for their whole term.

### Changing schools or programs (page 2026-06-10)
| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Different school, post-secondary (same or different level), or changing schools for prerequisite courses | **Extend the study permit first.** Include a letter explaining the change and a new PAL/TAL unless exempt. New school must be a DLI |
| Joint program with a single credential; changing schools within primary or secondary; moving from primary to secondary | Can keep studying on the current permit if valid and no condition prevents it |
| Secondary to post-secondary | New study permit by extension. Can continue while waiting, even with a "primary or secondary" condition |
| Same school, new program or level | May need an extension and possibly a new PAL/TAL. Can study while waiting; stop at once if refused |
| To Quebec | CAQ attestation, with few exceptions. Vocational centre to vocational centre: new CAQ only |
| While the application is in progress | Send new LOA and PAL/TAL through the web form |
- **Start before approval only if:** no exit from Canada since the new LOA; still meeting conditions; and the old DLI closed, ended the program, was suspended or lost designation. Otherwise get the permit before finishing at the current school.
- **Faster processing: 60 days** for a change to a new post-secondary DLI, if the online question is answered "Yes".
- **Unreported change:** old school reports non-enrolment. Permit may become invalid or be cancelled, and future applications are at risk.
- **DLI loses designation or is suspended:** can study until the permit expires. A designation loss blocks extensions at that school. A suspended DLI allows extension only to finish the current program. Can change DLI and start right after applying.

### Extending or restoring (pages 2024-08-09)
- Apply **at least 30 days before expiry**, in the IRCC secure account, even if another account was used before. Minors: at least 90 days.
- Post-secondary: upload the LOA or letter of enrolment. The DLI must verify it; if it doesn't by the deadline, the application is returned and refunded.
- Applied before expiry: can keep studying under the same conditions while in Canada. **Maintained status doesn't let the person renew a health card or driver's licence or get a SIN card.**
- **Extension timing and passport (read 2026-11-09):** IRCC says apply at least 30 days before expiry and Help Centre 475 recommends more; IRPR 189 only requires filing before expiry. Permit can't run past the passport; with a new passport in Canada the student can apply again (Help Centre 1504). Same DLI and level: no new PAL. **Restoration needs a new PAL even at the same DLI.** Work continues if the student still qualifies and applied before expiry (work pages 2026-04-15, 05-01). Study extension estimate 53 days (2026-11-04). Conflict: Help Centre 475 still shows a $200 restoration fee; the fee list shows $246.25 ($396.25 with the permit). Use the fee list.
- **Permit expired, or conditions changed without authorization, or conditions breached:** status lost. Cannot study until restored and the new permit is received.
- **Restore:** select "Restore my status", explain why, pay the restoration fee ($396.25 with the permit, see `11-forms-fees-processing/fee-schedule.md`). Family members apply and pay separately. TRP applicants do not get maintained status.

### Travel outside Canada (page 2026-08-28)
- Re-entry needs a valid permit or approval; valid passport; attending a DLI; valid visa or eTA; other entry requirements.
- **Expired visa or eTA:** may return without one if visiting only the US or Saint-Pierre and Miquelon and returning within the authorized stay.
- **Extension pending:** can't study until approved. May be allowed to enter if otherwise admissible.
- **Medical:** a new exam may be needed to return if the country requires one and the exam has expired.
- A study permit is not a visa. See `transit-and-visit-purposes.md` for a new visa from inside Canada.

### Minors (page 2025-12-18)
- **Age of majority:** 18 in AB, MB, ON, PE, QC, SK; 19 in BC, NB, NL, NT, NS, NU, YT.
- **Under 17:** must come with a parent or guardian or have a **custodian** (Canadian citizen or PR). Optional at 17+, but an officer can ask for one.
- **IMM 5646 Custodian Declaration (2023-01):** page 1 signed by the custodian and notarized in Canada; page 2 signed by parents or guardians and notarized in their home country.
- **Study permit needed (primary or secondary):** foreign national coming with a visitor parent, or a parent with a study or work permit, or unaccompanied; or in Canada with a visitor parent.
- **Not needed:** parent is a citizen or PR; refugees or claimants; no status; in Canada with a parent holding a work or study permit, or in Canada with neither parent present (permit still recommended).
- Six months or less: no permit needed. Six months or more: apply before entering. LOA generally needed, but not outside Canada when coming with a parent applying for a study or work permit.
- Validity: primary 1 year, renewable; secondary up to 4 years. **Permit expired: 90 days to restore.**
- A study permit helps a minor stay in school past majority, get a co-op permit and access some services.

### Graduate students
- **Doctoral fast track: about 2 weeks.** Doctoral program, outside Canada, online, complete application, biometrics within 2 weeks. Accompanying family included if filed together. Not for master's, undergraduate or graduate diploma programs, or complex files.
- Master's and doctoral at public DLIs: no PAL/TAL since 2026-01-01.
- **After graduation:** PGWP, or:

| Route | Key requirements |
|---|---|
| Post-doctoral fellow | Doctorate or dissertation submitted; job in a related field; paid; selected on academic merit; employer offer + $230 |
| Researcher | Listed federal sponsors, or research chair with government funding; employer offer |
| Research award recipient | Direct recipient; credible academic employer, not commercial; employer offer + $230 |
| Short-term researcher | **No work permit**: public degree-granting or affiliated institution; 120 consecutive days or less; once in 12 months; visitor record marked "authorized to work" |
- No LMIA for these. Work permit routes from outside Canada may get 2-week processing.

### Francophone Minority Communities Student Pilot (pages 2026-09-04, 06-22)
- Citizens of listed OIF countries in Africa, the Middle East and the Americas (33, including Morocco, Senegal, Cameroon, DRC, Haiti, Lebanon, Egypt); LOA from a participating DLI stating FMCSP; **NCLC 5** in all skills; funds; live outside Canada when applying. Fee $150. No PAL/TAL.
- **Participating DLIs (page 2026-10-16; list may grow):** Université de Saint-Boniface; Collège de l'Île; Université Saint Paul; University of Regina; Collège Éducacentre; Université Sainte-Anne; University of Ottawa; Université Laurentienne; Université de l'Ontario français; York University (Glendon); Université de Hearst; University of Alberta; CCNB; Collège La Cité; Collège Boréal; Université de Moncton; Collège Mathieu. Check the earliest eligible semester.
- **Living funds, single applicant (applied on or after 2026-09-01), by size of the main campus city:** Group A (500,000+) $23,448; B (100,000 to 499,999) $20,195; C (30,000 to 99,999) $20,069; D (under 30,000) $18,365; E (rural) $16,142. Lower than the regular requirement outside Group A.
- **PR path: "Opening winter 2027"** (not open yet). In Canada outside Quebec, with an FMCSP permit and valid status; degree or diploma from a participating DLI, at least 2 years full-time, over 50% in French.

### Indigenous border mobility (pages 2026-04-27)
- Member of a US Federally Recognized Tribe or citizen of the Manitoba Métis Federation; born in or citizen of the US; anchor family member in Canada who is a citizen, PR or registered under the Indian Act.
- Study or work; **most fees waived**; no PAL/TAL or proof of funds for study; no medical; open work permit up to 3 years. Not at the port of entry.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026-04-01: post-secondary co-op work permit ended; secondary students still need one (H)
- 2026: 60-day processing for students changing post-secondary DLI; 2-week doctoral processing (H)
- 2026-09-04: FMCSP eligibility page updated; PR path listed as opening winter 2027 (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- A permit extension doesn't renew the visitor visa.
- Finishing early shortens the permit to 90 days. Plan PGWP timing around it.
- Changing schools without extending first is non-compliance.
- Check that the work condition is printed on the permit before the student applies for a SIN.
- Unaccompanied children under 17 need a notarized custodian form from both sides. Start early.
- Never describe the FMCSP PR path as open.

## Common refusal reasons
- See `study-permits.md`. Also: LOA not validated by the DLI; non-compliance with enrolment conditions; change of school without extension
