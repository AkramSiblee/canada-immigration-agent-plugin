# LMIA work permits (TFWP)

## Must cover
- High-wage / low-wage / GTS / agricultural; refusal to process list; caps; fees; transition plans
- TFWP agricultural stream, SAWP, seasonal rules (gap-pass-01)
- LMIA process steps, advertising, transition plans (gap-pass-01)

## Current rules

- At or above the threshold: high-wage stream. Below: low-wage stream.

### Low-wage stream
- **Cap 10%** of the TFW workforce at a work location. **20%** for NAICS 23 (construction), 311 (food manufacturing), 622 (hospitals), 623 (nursing/residential care) and certain in-home caregiver NOCs. Under 10 employees: 1 worker at 10% or 2 at 20%.
- No cap for: primary agriculture; PR-support-only positions; positions of 120 days or less; seasonal up to 270 days (once a year per location).
- **Refusal to process**: low-wage LMIAs in **CMAs with unemployment of 6% or more** (since 2024-09-26). Exempt: primary agriculture, NAICS 23/311/622/623, caregivers, PR-only, 120 days or less. **Current list for applications 2026-10-09 to 2027-01-07** (next update 2027-01-08). Examples at 6% or more: Oshawa 9.8, London 9.1, Moncton 8.1, Kitchener-Cambridge-Waterloo 7.6, Edmonton 7.6, Vancouver 7.0. Full table (41 CMAs, three quarters) stored in `templates/lmia/cma-unemployment.csv` (read 2026-11-10): also at 6% or more: Toronto 7.5, Ottawa-Gatineau 7.9, Montréal 7.2, Calgary 6.4 (Winnipeg 5.9 is under). Check the ESDC page for the next quarter.
- **From 2026-11-06:** early childhood educators exempt from the 6% CMA rule. **Low-wage LMIA duration up to 2 years** (was 1) for applications on or after 2026-11-06 (ESDC news release).
- Recruitment: Job Bank + youth effort + 2 more methods each targeting a different underrepresented group. **8 consecutive weeks** within the 3 months before applying. Invite Job Match candidates rated 2+ stars within the first 30 days.

### High-wage stream
- **Transition plan** mandatory (exemptions: caregivers, SAWP/agricultural, certain Quebec occupations, limited-duration or unique-skill roles, PR-only).
- Recruitment: Job Bank + 2 more methods (1 national). **4 consecutive weeks** within the 3 months before applying. Invite Job Match candidates rated 4+ stars within 30 days.

### Global Talent Stream
- Category A (designated referral partner) / Category B (Global Talent Occupations List). LMIA in **10 business days** and work permit in **2 weeks** (80% of the time). Up to 3 yrs.
- **Routing (requirements page, read 2026-11-09):** an occupation on the list is processed as Category B even with a referral. Category B needs no referral; list includes 21211 (NOC example title: machine learning engineer), 21231, 21232, wage = prevailing. Category A: $38.46/h and $80,000 for the first 2 positions a calendar year; $72.11/h and $150,000 for more; referral form per worker; partner list updated 2026-09-15.
- No minimum recruitment; efforts must be described. LMBP: A = job creation; B = skills and training; plus at least 2 complementary benefits; reviewed annually. Independent contractors not allowed (2026-09-18). BC, MB, SK, NS: provincial employer registration first. Quebec: joint with MIFI.
- 2-week work permit: online from outside Canada, complete application, positive GTS LMIA, biometrics within 2 weeks of applying (GSS pages 2025-09-10). LMIA-exempt TEER 0-1 also eligible; IEC not.

### Agricultural
- Primary agriculture NOCs 80020, 80021, 82030, 82031, 84120, 85100, 85101, 85103.
- **SAWP**: Mexico and participating Caribbean countries; national commodities list; up to 8 months a season.
- **Agricultural stream**: any country, commodities list. Non-commodity production uses the high/low-wage streams.

### Forms (Service Canada catalogue, read 2026-11-10) (H)
- LMIA applications are filed in **LMIA Online** (Job Bank for employers account); the old paper application forms are no longer in the catalogue.
- Employment agreements: **EMP5711** (high-wage), **EMP5712** (low-wage), **EMP5713** (Global Talent). Third-party representative: **EMP5575** (Schedule A).

### Fees and obligations
- **$1,000 per position** (non-refundable; caregiver/on-farm exemptions). Not recoverable from the worker.
- Employment agreement signed by first day of work; prevailing wage reviewed annually; records kept 6 years.
- Work permit fee $155 (IRCC).

### Processing and changes (read 2026-11-09) (H)
- Average LMIA processing, September 2026 (page 2026-10-07): high-wage 98 business days, low-wage 82, GTS 9, PR stream 79. Updated monthly. Positive LMIA valid up to 6 months.
- **Modifications (page 2026-01-20):** no report: minor duty change with same NOC; raise up to the highest of 2%, prevailing wage or CPI (2.1% for 2026). Report: admin errors, employment breakdown, move within same economic region. **Material:** NOC change, employer or affiliated site, any wage cut, larger raise, hours, benefits, stream change, transition plan failure, education requirement. ESDC won't pre-approve; voluntary disclosure recommended.
- Non-compliance: warning, up to $100,000 per violation and $1M a year, bans, publication. Food services are a high-risk review sector (ESDC 2026-07-09).
- Chefs (NOC 62200): Job Bank provincial medians $19.00-$24.00 (wages updated 2025-11-19), far below high-wage thresholds, so restaurant chef LMIAs are usually low-wage and subject to the CMA refusal list.

## Consultant pitfalls
- Check the wage **against the threshold in force on the LMIA receipt date**.
- Advertising must be within the **3 months before** submission and still running or recently run. Count weeks exactly.
- A CMA list change every quarter can switch a file to "refused to process". File before the update if the rate is rising.
- The **Quebec**, Montreal and Laval low-wage restrictions differ (see 03-quebec).
- Charging recruitment or LMIA fees to the worker brings AMPs up to $1M/yr and bans.
- LMIA ≠ work permit. IRCC still assesses the worker's qualifications, genuineness and admissibility.

## Common refusal reasons
- ESDC: insufficient recruitment, wage below prevailing, cap exceeded, CMA refusal list, business not genuine
- IRCC: worker lacks experience/language for the job; dual intent concerns; LMIA conditions not met
