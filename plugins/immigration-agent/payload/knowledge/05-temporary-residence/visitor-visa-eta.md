# Visitor visa (TRV), eTA, visitor record

## Must cover
- Requirements, visitor record, refusals

## Current rules
**Related files:** forms and documents `visitor-visa-documents.md`; who needs a visa `visa-requirements-by-nationality.md`; each country's local form, processing time, centres and medical list `visitor-country-reference.md`; transit and purposes `transit-and-visit-purposes.md`; refusals `12-decision-trees/visitor-refusal.md`, `13-case-law/visitor-visa-refusals.md`.
- **TRV**: $100 (family of 5+ max $500) + biometrics $85. Valid up to **10 yrs** or to passport/biometrics expiry. Default stay **up to 6 months** per entry (the officer may set another period).
- **eTA**: $7. For visa-exempt nationals **flying** to or transiting through a Canadian airport. Not for land or sea (except SPM vessels, see below). Citizens and PRs cannot use an eTA.
- **eTA for some visa-required nationals (air only)**: must have held a Canadian visa in the last 10 yrs **or** hold a valid US non-immigrant visa. Listed countries (IRCC page 2026-10-30): Antigua and Barbuda, Argentina, Brazil, Costa Rica, Mexico, Morocco, Panama, Philippines, St Kitts and Nevis, St Lucia, St Vincent, Seychelles, Thailand, Trinidad and Tobago, Uruguay.
  - **Indonesia and Malaysia added 2026-05-26** (SOR/2026-87; IRCC visitor-visa page; IRCC entry requirements page 2026-07-31). **Conflict:** the IRCC eTA-eligibility list page omitted them when read 2026-11-07. The regulation is in force, so treat them as eligible, but check the eTA tool.
- **St Pierre and Miquelon vessels**: visa-exempt travellers need an eTA from 2026-06-05 (SOR/2026-101).
- **Visitor record** (extend stay): $100. "A visitor record is not a visa". Apply before status expires (maintained status applies).
- **Super Visa** holders: 5 yrs per entry (see 04-family/super-visa.md).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026-05-26: Indonesia and Malaysia partial visa exemption (eTA, air) (H)
- 2026-06-05: eTA for marine arrivals from SPM (H)
- 2026-05-27: Ebola temporary measures in force to 2026-11-27 (DRC, Uganda, South Sudan; by last country of residence) (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- The conditional eTA (visa-required countries) works **only for air travel**. Driving from the US needs a TRV.
- Dual citizens of Canada can't use a foreign passport + eTA. They need a Canadian passport.
- Visitors can't work or study (except courses of 6 months or less, and the 2026 worker-study policy doesn't apply to visitors).
- A visitor record doesn't allow re-entry. Leaving Canada needs a valid TRV for return.

## Common refusal reasons
- R179(b): not satisfied the applicant will leave (ties, travel history, purpose, finances, family in Canada); insufficient funds; prior overstay/non-compliance; misrepresentation

## Re-verify before advising
- eTA eligibility page vs SOR/2026-87 (Indonesia/Malaysia)
- Visa-exempt country list (IRCC "check visa" tool)
