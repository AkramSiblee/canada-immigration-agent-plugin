# International Experience Canada (IEC)

## Must cover
- Quotas by country, categories, C20/C21
- Current-season quotas and pool status (gap-pass-01)

## Current rules
- **2026 season: CLOSED.** "The pools for the 2026 season are closed." ITA holders can still submit. Others are removed and must recreate profiles for **2027** (opening date not announced, 2026-11-07).
- About 40 partner countries/territories.

| Category | Permit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Working Holiday | Open WP, usually up to 12 months (varies by country) | Age 18-30 or 18-35 by country |
| Young Professionals (C21) | Employer-specific; signed job offer | Employer Portal offer + $230 compliance fee |
| International Co-op (C20) | Employer-specific; internship tied to studies | Student or recent grad |
- Common requirements: CAN$2,500 funds; health insurance for the whole stay; return ticket or funds; no accompanying dependants; police certificate at work permit stage.
- Fees: **IEC participation $184.75** (since 2025-12-01) + $100 OWP holder fee (Working Holiday) + biometrics $85.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-12-01: IEC fee $179.75 rose to $184.75 (H)
- 2026 season closed (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- ITA acceptance and work permit application deadlines are short (typically 10 + 20 days) (M). Have the police certificate ready early.
- Insurance must cover the **full stay**. The port-of-entry officer can shorten the permit to the insurance end date.
- Country-specific age caps and participation limits (once or twice). Check the client's country page.
- Recognized Organizations route exists for non-partner countries (M).

## Common refusal reasons
- Insurance shortfall; police certificate missing; age over limit at submission; prior participation used up

## Re-verify before advising
- 2027 season opening and country quotas
