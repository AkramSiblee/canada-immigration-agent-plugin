# Employer compliance and inspections (TFWP and IMP)

## Must cover
- Who inspects, triggers, lookback, employer conditions (IRPR 209.2 IMP / 209.3 TFWP), justifications, procedure, AMPs and bans (IRPR Schedule 2), publication, worker permit revocation

## Current rules
### Who inspects (IRCC bulletin, page 2026-09-18) (H)
- **IMP (LMIA-exempt) employers: IRCC** staff. Referral to IRCC's Risk and Integrity Authority Branch, Compliance Inspections and Investigations Division; on-site visits by Immigration Operations.
- **TFWP (LMIA) employers: ESDC / Service Canada.**
- Covers employers whose offers led to permits issued **on or after 2013-12-31**.

### Triggers (IRPR 209.5 / 209.6)
1. Reason to suspect non-compliance (complaints, ESDC/CBSA, unions, NGOs, provinces, media, voluntary disclosure)
2. Past non-compliance
3. **Random selection** (ESDC publishes an Algorithmic Impact Assessment)
4. Emergencies Act / Quarantine Act order
5. Communicable disease at the workplace
- ESDC 2025-26 focus: high-risk sectors; food services named a high-risk review sector (ESDC 2026-07-09; see work-permits-lmia.md).

### Lookback and records
- Inspection can start from the worker's first day of employment **up to 6 years** after.
- Employer must **keep all records 6 years** from the first day of the employment period on the permit.
- On-site, announced or unannounced; warrantless except private dwellings (ESDC page 2026-05-12). Interviews with consent; photos/audio/video with written consent. Third parties (banks, payroll, accountants, recruiters) can be asked for documents.
- **Refusing to cooperate is itself a Type C violation** (R209.4(1)).

### IMP employer conditions (IRPR 209.2)
1. Remain **actively engaged in the business** in the offer
2. Give the worker the **rights information** (both official languages) on or before day 1
3. Comply with federal/provincial **employment and recruitment laws**
4. **Same occupation**; wages and working conditions **substantially the same as, but not less favourable than**, the offer
5. Reasonable efforts for a workplace **free of abuse** (physical, sexual, psychological, financial) and reprisal
6. Reasonable efforts to give **access to health care** if injured or ill at work
7. **No charging or recovering** the compliance fee or recruitment fees (also ensure recruiters don't)
8. Offer information accurate; **prove it for 6 years**; keep documents
- Employment agreement (R209.11(1)(e)) in the worker's chosen official language, signed by both, given to the worker (attested since May 2024).
- Duty changes need a **new offer and new permit first**.

### TFWP employer conditions (IRPR 209.3; ESDC page 2026-05-12)
- **29 conditions**, incl. all of the above plus: meet LMIA decision letter and annex terms; LMIA commitments (jobs, hiring, training, transition plan, skills transfer); accurate LMIA information; adequate housing (SAWP); live-in caregiver extras.
- Wage increases up to inflation may be acceptable. Report changes or errors to the **Employer Contact Centre 1-800-367-5693**.

### Justifications (R209.2(3), (3.1), (4); R209.4(2))
- Change in law; change in collective agreement; economic conditions affecting all employees equally; good-faith interpretation error **with compensation**; unintentional accounting/administrative error **with compensation**; similar circumstances; force majeure. Compensation must be shown **before** the preliminary finding. (List from bulletin summary portion: M on wording.)

### Procedure
- Inspection letter (trigger, documents, deadline) -> initial finding, chance to justify -> **Notice of Preliminary Finding (NOPF)**: violations, points, proposed sanction; **30 days to respond** (extension case by case) -> **Notice of Final Determination (NOFD)**.
- Extrinsic evidence must be disclosed in the NOPF.
- **Voluntary disclosure:** IMM 5964. If accepted, **4 points subtracted** (floor 0).

### Penalties: points, AMPs, bans (IRPR Schedule 2, laws-lois page 2026-11-02; IRCC consequences page 2024-07-19) (H)
- Violation types: **A** = records accuracy/retention; **B** = employment law, occupation/wages/conditions, rights information; **C** = abuse, health care, fee recovery, not actively engaged, failure to cooperate.
- Points = compliance history (1st violation: A/B 1, C 2; 2nd: A 2, B 2, C 3; 3rd+: A 2, B 3, C 4; **no expiry**) + severity modifiers (benefit 0-6, abuse 0-10, economic harm 0-6, failure to remediate 0-3, failure to prevent recurrence 0-3).
- **Under 2 points: warning letter** (not published).
- Small business = 100 or fewer employees **or** $5M or less gross revenue; large = more than 100 employees **and** more than $5M.

**Ban (period of ineligibility)**
| Points | Type A | Type B | Type C |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-5 | none | none | none |
| 6 | none | none | 1 yr |
| 7 | none | 1 yr | 2 yrs |
| 8 | 1 yr | 2 yrs | 5 yrs |
| 9-10 | 2 yrs | 5 yrs | 10 yrs |
| 11-12 | 5 yrs | 10 yrs | 10 yrs |

| 15+ | permanent | permanent | permanent |
- AMPs are per violation; **total cap $1M per NOFD** (12-month look-back). Bans are not cumulative; the longest applies. Violations before 2015-12-01: flat 2-year ban.
- Ban applies to **both TFWP and IMP**. Pending LMIAs refused, fees not refunded; previous LMIAs can be suspended or revoked.
- Name, address, violations and penalty **published** on the IRCC non-compliant employers list (live page; check the page itself). AMP without ban: employer stays eligible unless the AMP is unpaid.
- Worker impact: work permits of workers at a non-compliant employer **may be revoked** under Ministerial Instructions, depending on severity (bulletin; 2024-07-26 update). Vulnerable workers: see open-work-permits.md (VWOWP).

### Enforcement figures (secondary; ESDC release 2026-07-09 not read) (M)
- TFWP 2025-26: **1,488 inspections**, about 12% non-compliant, **AMPs over $10.2M** (2024-25: $4.5M), **30 employers banned**.
- IMP 2024-25 (secondary): 4,328 inspections, 55 penalties. Don't mix the two programs.

## Consultant pitfalls
- Most common real-world breach: **wage or duty drift** (raise withheld, new title, different worksite, reduced hours). Any change to occupation needs a new offer and permit before the change.
- **Recruitment fee recovery** (directly or through a recruiter) is Type C: high points fast. Ask how the worker was recruited.
- Keep a **6-year compliance binder** per worker: offer/LMIA, signed agreement, payroll, timesheets, rights-information proof, duty descriptions.
- Respond to the NOPF within 30 days with compensation proof; compensation shown after the preliminary finding may not count.
- Consider **voluntary disclosure (IMM 5964)** before an inspection: -4 points.
- An employer ban kills pending LMIAs and IMP offers: run a compliance check at intake for employer clients (see work-permit-process-and-status.md, non-compliant employers list).
- Representing the employer and the worker in the same inspection can be a conflict (see 10-consultant-practice/advertising-and-conflicts.md).
- Penalties are reviewable only by Federal Court JR: refer to a lawyer.
