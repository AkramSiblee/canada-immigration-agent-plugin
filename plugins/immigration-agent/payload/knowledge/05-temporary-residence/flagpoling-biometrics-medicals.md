# Flagpoling, biometrics, medicals, police certificates

## Must cover
- Rules, validity, fees
- Medical exam rules, panel physicians, validity (gap-pass-01)
- Police certificate requirements by country (gap-pass-01)
- Biometrics exemptions and 10-year validity

## Current rules
### Flagpoling
- **Ended 2024-12-23 at 23:59 ET**: "Work and study permits will no longer be provided to flagpolers at a port of entry."
- Exceptions: US citizens and LPRs; certain FTA professionals/technicians (US, Mexico, Chile, Panama, Peru, Colombia, South Korea) and some spouses; international truck drivers; people with a pre-booked CBSA appointment.
- In-Canada applicants must apply online.

### Biometrics
- Fee $85 individual / $170 family / $255 group of 3+ performing artists.
- **Valid 10 years**: no visa or permit beyond 10 yrs from the last biometrics (IRCC; IRPR s.183(4)(c.1)).
- **Required** for TR (except US nationals), PR, refugee/asylum; also extensions unless visa-exempt.
- **Exempt**: citizens; existing PRs; visa-exempt eTA applicants; visa-exempt visitor-record extensions; **children under 14**; **over 79** (not for asylum claimants); heads of state; diplomatic/official visas; US visa holders in transit; anyone with biometrics in a pending PR application.

### Immigration medical exam (IME)
- **Panel physicians only**. IRCC decides, not the doctor. Results valid **12 months**.
- TR stay of 6 months or less: generally none, unless health/childcare/school work or agricultural work after 6+ months in listed countries.
- Over 6 months: required if lived in or travelled to listed countries for 6+ consecutive months in the year before, if working in public health, or for a super visa. List by country: `visitor-country-reference.md`.
- **Upfront medical** possible for visit/work/study.
- In-Canada exemption public policy until **2029-10-05**: prior low/no-risk IME within 5 yrs.

### Police certificates
- PR, citizenship, IEC (and others if a record exists). Applicant and family members **18+**.
- Current country: issued at most **6 months** before submission. Other countries: issued **after the last 6+ consecutive months** lived there since age 18. Not for time under 18 or time in Canada.
- Express Entry: submit within the e-APR (60 days).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2024-12-23: flagpoling ended for work/study permits (H)
- 2026-07-09: biometrics page restates 10-yr limit (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Don't advise "drive to the border for a same-day permit". Only the exemption list qualifies.
- Biometrics expiry caps permit length. Re-enrol before a long permit or PR.
- IME expiry (12 months) sets the PR visa validity. Time the landing accordingly.
- Police certificates for countries with long processing (e.g. India PCC, US FBI) take time. Order them at intake.

## Common refusal reasons
- Missing police certificate or IME (incomplete/refusal); medical inadmissibility (see 08-inadmissibility)

## Re-verify before advising
- Biometrics fee changes; IME listed-countries list
