# LMIA-exempt work permits (International Mobility Program)

## Must cover
- IMP, CUSMA, ICT, C10/C11, reciprocal, Francophone Mobility, employer compliance fee
- CUSMA professionals list and ICT detail (gap-pass-01)

## Current rules
### Employer steps (employer-specific IMP)
- Submit an **offer of employment in the Employer Portal** (the worker gets a 7-digit offer number). Pay the **$230 employer compliance fee** (entertainers group $690). Then the worker applies ($155).
- Fee-exempt: R204(a) non-trade agreements (Fulbright etc.), R205(b) cultural reciprocal, R205(c)(i) research, R205(d) charitable/religious.
- **No work permits at the port of entry for most people already in Canada** (flagpoling ended 2024-12-23; see flagpoling-biometrics-medicals.md).

**Where to apply, who needs no permit, extensions, changing or losing a job, restoration and worker rights: see `work-permit-process-and-status.md`.**

### Key codes
| Code | Category | Key rules |
|---|---|---|
| **T23 (admin T36)** | CUSMA professional (R204(a)) | US/Mexican citizen; one of the **63** Appendix 2 professions; qualified; pre-arranged employment; **no self-employment**. Up to 3 yrs, unlimited 3-yr extensions. No wage refusal on wage alone |
| **T37 / T38 (formerly T24)** | CUSMA ICT (R204(a)) | US or Mexican citizen; executive or senior manager (T37) or specialized knowledge (T38); qualifying relationship; **1 yr continuous full-time** with the foreign enterprise in the last 3 yrs. T37: 3 yrs + 2-yr renewals, **7 yrs total**. T38: 3 yrs + 2-yr renewals, **5 yrs total**. Limit is cumulative across ICT categories. New office: 1 yr, renewals 6 months |
| **C12 (admin C61/C62/C63)** | Intra-company transferee (R205(a)) | MNC with a qualifying relationship; Canadian entity doing business; **1 yr continuous full-time** employment abroad in the last 3 yrs; position abroad kept. C62 executive/manager: 3 yrs + 2-yr renewals, **max 7 yrs**. C63 specialized knowledge (advanced proprietary knowledge + advanced expertise): **max 5 yrs**. C61 start-up: 1 yr |
| **C10** | Significant benefit (R205(a)) | Social, cultural or economic benefit with detailed evidence; "not for convenience"; copying IRCC text is not enough; no CAQ needed in Quebec |
| C11 | Entrepreneur / self-employed, temporary (R205(a)) | Business owner of 50%+ seeking temporary residence only. Temporary or seasonal work with plans to leave; significant benefit; **support funds equal to LICO for 18 months** (or the stay if shorter), separate from business funds. PR intent goes under C60 or A77 instead. Self-employment doesn't count for CEC |
| **C16** | Francophone Mobility (outside Quebec) | **NCLC 5+** French (from 2023-06-15); **any TEER** except primary-agriculture TEER 4-5; Employer Portal offer + $230 |
| C20/C21 | IEC Young Professionals / Co-op | see iec.md |
| R205(b) | Reciprocal employment | e.g. cultural exchanges; fee-exempt |

### Academics (read 2026-11-09) (H)
- **No blanket exemption for lecturers.** ESDC runs an academic LMIA stream (recruitment about 1 month on the recruitment page; 4 consecutive weeks within 3 months on the apply page, 2026-10-05; NOC 41200) for academics not covered by another exemption.
- **C22 (R205(b), page 2026-07-24):** visiting professor: up to 2 academic years, post-secondary, retains position abroad; job-for-job reciprocity not required. Guest lecturer: series of lectures, not a complete course, under 1 term. Offer of employment required; only the employer can correct it. The compliance fee exemption page lists only cultural agreements under R205(b), so treat C22 as paying $230 unless confirmed otherwise.
- Codes page: CUSMA professionals T23 before 2022-12-15, T36 after; Chile professionals T23 then F22; C44 post-doc; C52 award recipients; C31 research. ESDC's exemption page is dated 2017-10-16 and still names NAFTA.

### ICT detail (page 2025-01-06, read 2026-11-09) (H)
- 1 year must be continuous, full-time, in a similar position with the foreign enterprise; part-time can't be aggregated; employment may be by payroll or direct exclusive contract. Position abroad must remain available. Canadian enterprise must have a reasonable need for the managerial role. C63 wage floor is the regional prevailing wage. C63 holders can't perform manager duties. Dual intent assessed under R200(1)(b) and A22(2). The page labels managerial capacity "TEER 1" although NOC managers are TEER 0; assess by duties.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026-03-13: certain Quebec extension workers LMIA-exempt (H)
- 2026-10-20: C10 guidance page updated (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- **ICT specialized knowledge** is scrutinized heavily for TEER 3-5 roles. Document the proprietary knowledge and salary.
- CUSMA professionals: the job must match the **Appendix 2 profession and its credential**, e.g. "Management Consultant" requires a degree or experience.
- C10 is discretionary. Build a strong evidence package and avoid templated letters.
- C16 needs a French test (TEF/TCF) proving NCLC 5. It is good for TEER 4-5 roles in francophone communities.
- The employer must keep compliance (wages, duties as offered). Inspections and AMPs apply to IMP too.

## Common refusal reasons
- Qualifying relationship or 1-yr employment not proven (ICT); profession mismatch (CUSMA); benefit not shown (C10); French below NCLC 5 (C16)

## Re-verify before advising
- CUSMA ICT (T24), C11 page
- C61 extension inconsistency on the ICT page
