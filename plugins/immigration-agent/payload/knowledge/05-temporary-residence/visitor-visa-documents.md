# Visitor visa: forms and documents

## Must cover
- Forms, passport, photo, funds, invitation letter, translations, online vs paper

## Current rules
**Order of authority for any file:** (1) the personalized checklist in the portal, (2) the local visa office form for the place of application (`visitor-country-reference.md`), (3) the general IRCC list below.

### How to apply
- **Online is mandatory** (MI62). Paper only if unable to apply online (for example, disability), or travelling on a document issued to a refugee, stateless person or non-citizen.
- Accounts: IRCC secure account, IRCC portal, or Authorized Paid Representatives Portal.
- Fees are paid at the end: visa $100 (5 or more family members $500), biometrics $85 ($170 family). See `11-forms-fees-processing/fee-schedule.md`.

### Forms
| Form | When |
|---|---|
| IMM 5257 Application for Visitor Visa | Every applicant (online form in the portal) |
| IMM 5257 SCH1 Schedule 1 | Most local forms: if any background answer is yes |
| IMM 5645 or IMM 5707 Family Information | Each applicant 18+. Use the one the local form or portal names |
| IMM 5476 Use of a Representative | If using a representative |
| IMM 5475 Authority to Release Information | Only if file information may go to someone else |
| IMM 5409 Common-law declaration | If common-law |
| IMM 5484 Document Checklist (May 2026) | Paper only. Not submitted online |
| IMM 0149, 0150, 5546 | Some local forms: government, police or military service |

### Family Information form
- **All children must be listed**, even if PR or citizens of Canada, married, adopted, adopted by others, step-children, or in another person's custody.
- IMM 5645 also covers parents and brothers and sisters, including half and step. "Deceased" goes under present address, with city and date.
- Non-disclosure risks IRPA s.40 here and IRPR 117(9)(d) in any later sponsorship.

### Passport
- Digital colour copy of the bio page and **every page with stamps, visas or markings**.
- Should be valid at least **6 months from planned travel** (renew first). Some local forms set their own rule.
- At least one blank page other than the last page.
- **Not accepted:** any passport issued by Somalia; non-machine-readable passports from the Czech Republic; temporary passports from South Africa; provisional passports from Venezuela.
- **Venezuela, expired passport:** issued before 2019-06-07 and expired under 5 years: valid for 5 more years, add 5 years to the expiry date on the form, and include the required letter. Issued on or after that date and expired: not usable.
- After approval, **submit the same passport** given in the application. A newer one may delay processing and does not mean longer validity.
- A multiple-entry visa in a full or complete passport can stay valid if the holder carries both that passport and a newer valid one (PDI 2026-06-05). A visa never runs past the passport it was issued against, so check the actual foil date.

### Photo
- If biometrics are given at collection, **no other photo is needed**.
- Otherwise: 35 mm x 45 mm minimum; head 31 to 36 mm; taken in the last 6 months; plain white or light background; neutral expression, mouth closed.

### Purpose of visit (portal options)
Tourist; business; visiting a sponsoring spouse or partner; family visit (citizen, person registered under the Indian Act, PR, temporary resident); compassionate reasons; marine crew; none of these. See `transit-and-visit-purposes.md`.

### Supporting documents (general list)
| Document | Status | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Letter of invitation | Must include (family or tourist route) | See below |
| Proof of relationship | Must include (family route) | Marriage certificate, IMM 5409, birth certificate, or official document naming the parent |
| Travel history | Recommended | Previous passports and visas used in the last 10 years; entry and exit stamps; expired or valid visas |
| Itinerary | Recommended (required by some local forms) | Flight details, accommodation, event registration |
| Bank statements | Recommended (required by local forms) | Bank name and contact; name and address on statement; **at least 6 months** with balances. Local forms vary |
| Immigration status abroad | Must include if resident outside country of citizenship | Proof of legal status there |
| Employer letter | Recommended | Letterhead, dated within 3 months, job, full name, date of birth, start date, duties, salary, manager's contact, employer details, signature |
| Minor's travel authorization | If applicable | Signed by both parents or guardians; custody documents if relevant |

### Country rule example: Bangladesh (IMM 5890 E 05-2026, Singapore visa office, read 2026-11-10) (H)
- **Residents of Bangladesh must add IMM 5707** (Family Information Form), fully completed, dated and signed, as well as the usual forms.
- Must include: birth or baptismal certificate, marriage certificate if applicable; itinerary (provisional flight and hotel); proof of funds with **bank statements for the past 4 months** plus income proof (employment letter, pay slips, pension, business registration, investments).
- If someone else pays: their signed letter, passport or photo ID copy and a bank certificate, dated within 3 months of applying; say how they are related.
- Visiting family: letter of invitation stating purpose and length of visit, proof of relationship, and the inviter's citizenship or status document (PR card both sides).
- Employed: original signed employer letter granting leave (name, position, salary, date of hire, employer contact). Self-employed: business registration showing shares, licence and financial statements.
- The 4-month bank period is shorter than the general 6 months: give 6 where practical (see conflicts below).

### Letter of invitation
- **About the visitor:** full name, date of birth, address and phone, relationship, purpose, length of stay, where they will stay and how they will pay, date of departure.
- **About the host:** full name, date of birth, Canadian address and phone, job title, citizen or PR status, photocopy of proof of status, names and dates of birth of spouse and dependants.
- The host writes it. It "doesn't guarantee" a visa. Some visa offices may require it notarized (PDI).
- Super Visa letters also need the promise of financial support and family size calculation.

### Funds
- Enough to maintain the visitor and family in Canada and return home.
- Evidence can be the visitor's savings and employment, or a confirmable statement of support from family or friends.
- **Host's income documents can't be made mandatory.** A visa will not be refused just because the host's personal information is missing (PDI). Host documents still help when the host pays.

### Translation and copies
- Documents not in English or French need a translation **plus an affidavit from the translator**, unless the translator is certified. Include a copy of the original.
- Translators certified in Canada need no affidavit. Outside Canada, the affidavit is sworn before a notary.
- **Not allowed to translate:** the applicant; parent, spouse, child, sibling, grandparent, aunt, uncle, niece, nephew or first cousin.

### After filing
- Biometrics: book as soon as the instruction letter arrives; the guide allows 30 days.
- IRCC may ask for an interview, more information, a medical exam or a police certificate. An incomplete application may be refused.

## Consultant pitfalls
- A general list alone misses local requirements. Always pull the local form first.
- Family form conflict: the apply page names IMM 5645; the 2020 PDI and most local forms name IMM 5707; Guide 5256 says use the one listed for the country. Follow the local form and portal.
- Letter of invitation conflict: the apply page says it must be included, while the PDI says an applicant "may be asked". Include it.
- Bank period conflict: 6 months (general) vs 3, 4, 6 or 12 months (local). Give the longer period where practical.
- Immediate family cannot translate. This is a frequent informal practice.

## Common refusal reasons
- Incomplete package; missing bank history; inconsistent family information; document authenticity concerns (see `12-decision-trees/visitor-refusal.md`)
