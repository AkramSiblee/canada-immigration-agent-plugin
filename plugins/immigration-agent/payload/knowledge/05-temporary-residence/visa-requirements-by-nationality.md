# Who needs a visa or eTA, by nationality

## Must cover
- Visa-required, eTA-required, conditional eTA, no-document categories, medical list

## Current rules
Copied from the IRCC list (2026-07-31). Always confirm with the IRCC "find out what you need" tool for the exact passport and route.

### Visa required (any method of travel)
Afghanistan, Albania, Algeria, Angola, Antigua and Barbuda*, Argentina*, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Bahrain, Bangladesh, Belarus, Belize, Benin, Bhutan, Bolivia, Bosnia-Herzegovina, Botswana, Brazil*, Burkina Faso, Burundi, Cambodia, Cameroon, Cabo Verde, Central African Republic, Chad, China (PRC), Colombia, Comoros, Congo (Democratic Republic), Congo (Republic), Costa Rica*, Cuba, Djibouti, Dominica, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Egypt, El Salvador, Equatorial Guinea, Eritrea, Eswatini, Ethiopia, Fiji, Gabon, Gambia, Georgia, Ghana, Grenada, Guatemala, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Guyana, Haiti, Honduras, India, Indonesia*, Iran, Iraq, Israel (travel document in lieu of national passport only), Ivory Coast, Jamaica, Jordan, Kazakhstan, Kenya, Kiribati, North Korea, Kosovo, Kuwait, Kyrgyzstan, Laos, Lebanon, Lesotho, Liberia, Libya, Macao SAR, Madagascar, Malawi, Malaysia*, Maldives, Mali, Marshall Islands, Mauritania, Mauritius, Mexico*, Micronesia, Moldova, Mongolia, Montenegro, Morocco*, Mozambique, Myanmar, Namibia, Nauru, Nepal, Nicaragua, Niger, Nigeria, North Macedonia, Oman, Pakistan, Palau, Palestine, Panama*, Paraguay, Peru, Philippines*, Romania (non-electronic passport only), Russia, Rwanda, Sao Tomé e Principe, Saudi Arabia, Senegal, Serbia, Seychelles*, Sierra Leone, Somalia, South Africa, South Sudan, Sri Lanka, St. Kitts and Nevis*, St. Lucia*, St. Vincent and the Grenadines*, Sudan, Suriname, Syria, Taiwan (passport without personal ID number), Tajikistan, Tanzania, Thailand*, Timor-Leste, Togo, Tonga, Trinidad and Tobago*, Tunisia, Türkiye, Turkmenistan, Tuvalu, Uganda, Ukraine, Uruguay*, Uzbekistan, Vanuatu, Venezuela, Vietnam, Yemen, Zambia, Zimbabwe.

Stateless people and holders of a refugee travel document also need a visa.

**\*Conditional eTA (air only):** must hold a Canadian visa from the last 10 years or a valid US non-immigrant visa (see `visitor-visa-eta.md`). Arriving by car, bus, train or boat, including a cruise ship, needs a visa.

### eTA required (air only)
Andorra, Australia, Austria, Bahamas, Barbados, Belgium, Brunei, Bulgaria, Chile, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hong Kong SAR (HK SAR passport), Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Israel (national passport), Italy, Japan, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Monaco, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Papua New Guinea, Poland, Portugal, Qatar, Republic of Korea, Romania (electronic passport only), Samoa, San Marino, Singapore, Slovakia, Slovenia, Solomon Islands, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Taiwan (ordinary passport with personal ID number), United Arab Emirates, Vatican City State.

British: British citizen; British national overseas; British overseas citizen re-admissible to the UK; British overseas territory citizen (Anguilla, Bermuda, British Virgin Islands, Cayman Islands, Falkland Islands, Gibraltar, Montserrat, Pitcairn, Saint Helena, Turks and Caicos); British subject with right of abode in the UK.

By land or sea: no eTA needed, except by sea from Saint-Pierre-et-Miquelon (non-cruise), which needs one.

### No visa and no eTA
- US nationals and US lawful permanent residents (US PRs flying need passport plus green card or equivalent)
- Valid temporary residents returning after visiting only the US or Saint-Pierre-et-Miquelon, within the period authorized (R190(3)(f); a cruise entering international waters doesn't count)
- French citizens living in and flying directly from Saint-Pierre-et-Miquelon
- Passengers on refuelling stops bound for or from the US with proper documents, or on unscheduled stops
- Transit Without Visa or China Transit programs (see `transit-and-visit-purposes.md`)
- Accredited diplomats with Chief of Protocol acceptance; designated visiting forces on duty; certain air crew, aviation inspectors and accident investigators

### Canadian citizens and PRs
- Citizens, including most dual citizens, need a Canadian passport to fly. PRs need a PR card or travel document on commercial carriers.

### Legal basis (IRPR s.190, modified 2026-11-02)
- Citizens of Schedule 1.1 countries; British categories; US nationals and US PRs (s.190(1))
- Document-based: Holy See; national Israeli passport; HK SAR passport; British National (Overseas) born, naturalized or registered in Hong Kong; British Subject with right of abode; Taiwan ordinary passport with ID number (s.190(2)); Romanian e-passport (s.190(2.1))
- Purpose-based: crew, refuelling, unscheduled stops, MOU transit, visiting forces, re-entry (s.190(3))

### Medical exam country list
Each country's value is in the "Medical list" column of `visitor-country-reference.md`. Applies only to stays over 6 months with 6+ consecutive months in a listed country in the year before entry.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026-05-26: Indonesia and Malaysia added to conditional eTA (SOR/2026-87) (H)
- 2026-06-05: eTA for marine arrivals from Saint-Pierre-et-Miquelon (H)
- 2025-11-03: medical list changes (see `visitor-country-reference.md`) (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Check the **passport held**, not the place of birth or residence.
- UAE citizens are eTA. Residents of the UAE who are other nationals usually need a visa.
- Israel, Romania, Taiwan and Hong Kong depend on the passport type.

## Common refusal reasons
- n/a. Wrong document type leads to denied boarding or return of the application.
