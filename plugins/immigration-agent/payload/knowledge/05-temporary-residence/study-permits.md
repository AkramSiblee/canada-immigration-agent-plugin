# Study permits

## Must cover
- PAL/TAL, DLI, LOA, funds, SDS ended, GIC, s.216, CAQ
- Cap, allocations, exemptions, spousal OWP rules, refusals
- Proof of funds table and GIC amount (gap-pass-01)
- 2027 provincial allocations (gap-pass-01)

## Current rules
**Applying, arrival, conditions, on-campus and co-op work, changing schools, extensions, travel, minors, exemptions, graduate and Francophone routes: see `study-permit-status-and-compliance.md`.**

### Requirements
- DLI acceptance. **The LOA is verified with the school**; no response by the deadline means the application is returned with a refund.
- PAL/TAL (or CAQ in Quebec); proof of funds; temporary intent (IRPR s.216); admissibility; medical and biometrics as needed.
- Fee $150 + biometrics $85.

### PAL/TAL
- Usually required. **Exempt:**
  - public DLI master's/doctoral degree programs (from 2026-01-01)
  - preschool, primary and secondary
  - designated military colleges; some Quebec vocational programs; FMCSP schools
  - GAC scholarship holders; no-tuition exchange students
- **Minors with a study permit parent (IRCC minors page, read 2026-11-09):** coming from abroad for 6+ months: study permit required before entry, no LOA. Already in Canada with a parent holding a study or work permit: not required for K-12, recommended.
- 2026 PAL/TAL valid to **2026-12-31** (cap-year bound).
- Family (read 2026-11-09): a PAL is required for each study permit applicant who needs one, even when applying together. Exemption evidence goes in the Client information field. Applications missing a required PAL can be returned. Quebec needs a CAQ. Public DLIs offering exempt graduate programs are listed on IRCC's DLI page. Accompanying minors in K-12 are exempt as primary/secondary students; applying from abroad for 6+ months, they need a study permit, no LOA.
- PAL/TAL on a change (PAL/TAL PDI 2026-02-06): new DLI always needs a new permit and PAL (since 2025-01-22); same DLI and same level group (e.g. college to bachelor's, master's to doctorate) needs none; same DLI but a different level group needs one when an extension or new permit is filed; public master's/PhD exempt from 2026-01-01. Detail: study-permit-status-and-compliance.md.
- Reuse (IRCC PAL page 2026-09-02, read 2026-11-17) (H): same PAL only if still valid and the earlier application was withdrawn before decision or not accepted for processing. **Not reusable after an approval or refusal.** Must be valid on the day IRCC receives the application (not at program start); can't be sent after submission.
- **Joint programs** (IRCC program delivery instructions, modified 2026-02-06) (H): one PAL/TAL from the PT of the credentialling DLI; single LOA; one study permit for the credentialling DLI.
- **Students never apply to the province directly anywhere.** The DLI requests the letter (usually after offer acceptance and a deposit); the PT issues it against the DLI's allocation.

### Medical exam (students) (IRCC TR medical page, modified 2026-08-04; country list modified 2026-11-16; read 2026-11-17) (H)
- **Stay 6 months or less:** generally none, unless the study involves a job where public health must be protected (e.g. medical students admitted to university).
- **Stay over 6 months:** needed if (a) lived in or travelled to a listed country/territory **6+ consecutive months in the year before arrival**, or (b) will work (including placements) in **health care, child care, or primary/secondary school settings**.
- **Upfront:** panel physician before applying; upload the confirmation document. **Instructed:** apply first, IRCC sends instructions; nothing to send in advance.
- Results valid **12 months**; arrival after expiry (shown on letter of introduction) may need a new exam.
- Country list (2026-11-16): Philippines yes (Manila medical office), India yes (New Delhi), Vietnam yes (Manila), Ghana yes (London), Kenya yes (London), **Iran no** (residence-based only; Iran residents still need one if they lived 6+ months in a listed country).

### Quebec: CAQ then federal permit (quebec.ca, updated 2026-11-10; read 2026-11-17) (H)
**Detail and cap tracking: `knowledge/03-quebec/caq-csq-and-students.md`. Don't duplicate here.**
1. Admission letter from a Quebec institution (check the institution's remaining **CAQ cap**: periods 2025-12-17 to 2026-12-16 and 2026-12-17 to 2027-12-16).
2. Create **Arrima** account (names exactly as passport). Complete the MII temporary selection (studies) form in one sitting (no save).
3. Pay **$135** (2026; indexed each Jan 1; non-refundable) on Arrima **within 1 hour** or it isn't submitted.
4. File number within 48 h; upload document list **within 15 days**. **Only the applicant can upload on Arrima, not the representative.**
5. Decision: **25 business days** (80%) after complete file.
6. Attestation of issuance of CAQ in the online file = PAL equivalent for IRCC; must contain the cap line ("...has a place in Quebec's share... or is exempt from it").
7. Federal study permit with CAQ attestation. Study permit is capped at CAQ validity; extend later with a new CAQ.
- **Funds proof location:** residents of Austria, Canada, US, France, Greenland, Hong Kong, Réunion, Monaco, Mexico, Saint-Pierre-et-Miquelon file proof on Arrima; everyone else proves funds to the federal visa office, assessed on Quebec's criteria.
- **Quebec basic-needs amounts, applications from 2026-01-01** (quebec.ca costs page, updated 2026-01-01), living costs only; tuition and round-trip travel extra:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Per year | $24,617 | $34,814 | $42,638 | $49,234 | $55,045 | $60,299 | $5,254 |
- CAQ reuse: same valid CAQ reusable unless changing level; school change only if CAQ issued before 2024-12-06 at the same level, or minor in K-12 with CAQ not tied to a school (IRCC PAL page 2026-09-02). Quebec vocational DVS/AVS/STC: no PAL, CAQ still needed. CEGEP programs: CAQ attestation needed.

### Work while studying
- Off-campus: **24 h/week** in regular terms (a permit showing 20 h is read as 24); unlimited during scheduled breaks. Requires a program of at least 6 months leading to a credential, plus a SIN. Remote work for a foreign employer doesn't count.
  - Scheduled break (Help Centre 503, 2026-11-03, read 2026-11-09): set by the school (Help Centre 503 says at least 7 consecutive days; the off-campus PDI 2025-04-23 doesn't state a minimum: M); statutory holidays alone don't qualify; first 150 days only of back-to-back breaks over 150 days; **180 days per calendar year maximum** of unlimited off-campus work. On-campus has no hour limit. Required placements have no weekly limit and are separate from the 24.
- **Co-op: from 2026-04-01, post-secondary students no longer need a co-op work permit** for required placements (IRCC notice 2026-04-09). **Secondary students still do.**
- Authorized leave is capped at 150 days. No work during leave.

### Spouse of student (OWP), from 2025-01-21
- Student must be full time at a PGWP-eligible DLI, **not in the last term**, living in Canada, and in a master's of 16+ months, a doctoral program, a listed professional degree (MD; LLB/JD/BCL; DDS/DMD; OD; pharmacy; DVM; nursing; BEd; BEng/BE/BASc) or a listed program (FMCSP; certain provincial health programs; Quebec international nurse project). College diplomas excluded.
- OWP issued no later than the student's permit. Dependent children aren't eligible for an OWP. Full rules and what happens if the student's permit ends early: study-permit-status-and-compliance.md (spouse page 2026-02-03; C42 PDI 2026-08-24).

### SDS
- Student Direct Stream and Nigeria Student Express **ended 2024-11-08**, 2:00 p.m. ET (IRCC notice) (H). Later applications use the regular stream, which accepts GICs as proof of funds.
- **No replacement (checked 2026-11-09).** Only new-applicant study permit fast track: doctoral, about 2 weeks (page 2026-08-28). FMCSP page shows "No processing times available" (2026-06-22), so don't describe it as faster. Regular outside-Canada times change weekly; snapshot 2026-11-04: India 8, Nigeria 11, Pakistan 5, China 12 weeks.

### Local study instructions: Bangladesh (IMM 5850, 02-2026, Singapore office; read 2026-11-08) (H)
- Study plan (one page, set questions); notarized diplomas and transcripts; birth certificate if a minor; police certificate for each country lived in 6+ months since 18.
- Funds: bank history of at least 4 months, written source of funds, parents' employer letters or 2 years' income tax; business owners' registration, tax and audited statements; other sponsors must explain why. IELTS Academic highly recommended (within a year).
- **Nigeria (Lagos IMM 5832, 02-2026, read 2026-11-09):** passport 6+ months, previous passports; WAEC/SSCE/NECO, post-secondary records, NYSC certificate if applicable, street addresses of all schools; certified bank statements for past 6 months ending in or one month before submission, MyBankStatement ticket optional; reliable source of funds for the full program; non-immediate sponsors explain support. Processing from Nigeria: 11 weeks (IRCC data 2026-11-04).
- Other countries: each has its own form on the IRCC "How to apply" list. Processing from Bangladesh: 8 weeks (IRCC data 2026-11-04).

- **Iran sanctions (GAC academic sanctions guidance, modified 2026-03-19) (H):** tuition/fund transfers may be blocked where Iranian banks are listed; DLIs must screen payments and students for links to listed persons/entities; SEM (Iran) Regulations (SOR/2010-165) bar some "technical assistance", so sensitive fields (nuclear, advanced weapons, cyber) carry higher risk. ### Finding a school (DLI list, IRCC 2026-10-23)
- A DLI is approved by a province or territory. **All primary and secondary schools are DLIs** and aren't on the list.
- Enter the DLI number under "Details of intended study" on the application.
- The list shows which schools offer **PGWP-eligible programs** and which graduate programs need no PAL/TAL.
- Post-secondary DLIs must verify LOAs and report enrolment. Non-compliance can lead to suspension from accepting international students.
- Schools use the LOA Portal and DLI Portal (IRCC page for schools, 2025-12-18).

## Consultant pitfalls
- Check PGWP eligibility **before** choosing the program (field of study, DLI PGWP status, P3 colleges, distance share).
- PAL/TAL expires 2026-12-31. A 2026 PAL can't be used for a 2027 application.
- Funds must be genuine, sourced and seasoned. Large unexplained deposits lead to refusal and misrepresentation risk.
- A school change without a new PAL/TAL is non-compliance and puts future applications at risk.
- Working over 24 h in term breaches conditions and causes problems for PGWP/PR.

## Common refusal reasons
- s.216(1)(b): not satisfied the applicant will leave (weak ties, purpose of visit, travel history)
- Insufficient or unexplained funds; study plan not logical (career regression, unrelated program)
- LOA not verified; PAL/TAL missing or invalid
