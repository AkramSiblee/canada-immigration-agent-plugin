# Study permits

## Must cover
- PAL/TAL, DLI, LOA, funds, SDS ended, GIC, s.216, CAQ
- Cap, allocations, exemptions, spousal OWP rules, refusals
- Proof of funds table and GIC amount (gap-pass-01)
- 2027 provincial allocations (gap-pass-01)

## Current rules
**Applying, arrival, conditions, on-campus and co-op work, changing schools, extensions, travel, minors, exemptions, graduate and Francophone routes: see `study-permit-status-and-compliance.md`.**

### Requirements
- DLI acceptance. **The LOA is verified with the school**; no response by the deadline means the application is returned with a refund.
- PAL/TAL (or CAQ in Quebec); proof of funds; temporary intent (IRPR s.216); admissibility; medical and biometrics as needed.
- Fee $150 + biometrics $85.

### PAL/TAL
- Usually required. **Exempt:**
  - public DLI master's/doctoral degree programs (from 2026-01-01)
  - preschool, primary and secondary
  - designated military colleges; some Quebec vocational programs; FMCSP schools
  - GAC scholarship holders; no-tuition exchange students
- **Minors with a study permit parent (IRCC minors page, read 2026-11-09):** coming from abroad for 6+ months: study permit required before entry, no LOA. Already in Canada with a parent holding a study or work permit: not required for K-12, recommended. Study-permit spouse OWP: master's 16+ months, doctoral, listed professional degrees or listed programs only; college diplomas excluded.
- 2026 PAL/TAL valid to **2026-12-31** (cap-year bound).
- Family (read 2026-11-09): a PAL is required for each study permit applicant who needs one, even when applying together. Exemption evidence goes in the Client information field. Applications missing a required PAL can be returned. Quebec needs a CAQ. Public DLIs offering exempt graduate programs are listed on IRCC's DLI page. Accompanying minors in K-12 are exempt as primary/secondary students; applying from abroad for 6+ months, they need a study permit, no LOA.
- **Changing school** needs a new PAL/TAL (since 2025-01-22). Changing level usually also needs one, except moving to a public graduate degree.

### 2026 cap (notice 2025-11-25)
- Issuance target **408,000**: 155,000 new arrivals + 253,000 extensions (7% below 2025's 437,000).
- PAL/TAL-required issuance 180,000, distributed by population (ON 70,074; QC 39,474; BC 24,786; ...). **309,670 application spaces** (adjusted for approval rates). Nunavut gets 0.
- Levels 2027-2029: student arrivals target **150,000**/yr. **2027 cap/allocations not yet published** (2026-11-07).

### Work while studying
- Off-campus: **24 h/week** in regular terms (a permit showing 20 h is read as 24); unlimited during scheduled breaks. Requires a program of at least 6 months leading to a credential, plus a SIN. Remote work for a foreign employer doesn't count.
  - Scheduled break (Help Centre 503, 2026-11-03, read 2026-11-09): set by the school, at least 7 consecutive days; statutory holidays alone don't qualify; first 150 days only of back-to-back breaks over 150 days; **180 days per calendar year maximum** of unlimited off-campus work. On-campus has no hour limit. Required placements have no weekly limit and are separate from the 24.
- **Co-op: from 2026-04-01, post-secondary students no longer need a co-op work permit** for required placements (IRCC notice 2026-04-09). **Secondary students still do.**
- Authorized leave is capped at 150 days. No work during leave.

### Spouse of student (OWP), from 2025-01-21
- Eligible only if the student is in:
  - a master's of 16+ months, or a doctoral program
  - a listed professional degree (MD, JD/LLB, DDS, PharmD, DVM, BScN, BEd, BEng, etc.)
  - an eligible program (FMCSP; certain QC, ON, MB, BC health programs)
- OWP valid with the study permit. Dependent children aren't eligible for an OWP.

### SDS
- Student Direct Stream and Nigeria Student Express **ended 2024-11-08**, 2:00 p.m. ET (IRCC notice) (H). Later applications use the regular stream, which accepts GICs as proof of funds.
- **No replacement (checked 2026-11-09).** Only new-applicant study permit fast track: doctoral, about 2 weeks (page 2026-08-28). FMCSP page shows "No processing times available" (2026-06-22), so don't describe it as faster. Regular outside-Canada times change weekly; snapshot 2026-11-04: India 8, Nigeria 11, Pakistan 5, China 12 weeks.

### Local study instructions: Bangladesh (IMM 5850, 02-2026, Singapore office; read 2026-11-08) (H)
- Study plan (one page, set questions); notarized diplomas and transcripts; birth certificate if a minor; police certificate for each country lived in 6+ months since 18.
- Funds: bank history of at least 4 months, written source of funds, parents' employer letters or 2 years' income tax; business owners' registration, tax and audited statements; other sponsors must explain why. IELTS Academic highly recommended (within a year).
- **Nigeria (Lagos IMM 5832, 02-2026, read 2026-11-09):** passport 6+ months, previous passports; WAEC/SSCE/NECO, post-secondary records, NYSC certificate if applicable, street addresses of all schools; certified bank statements for past 6 months ending in or one month before submission, MyBankStatement ticket optional; reliable source of funds for the full program; non-immediate sponsors explain support. Processing from Nigeria: 11 weeks (IRCC data 2026-11-04).
- Other countries: each has its own form on the IRCC "How to apply" list. Processing from Bangladesh: 8 weeks (IRCC data 2026-11-04).

### University of Alberta (school pages via search extract, 2026-11-08) (M)
- DLI O19257171832. $5,000 deposit to accept the offer and receive the PAL; PAL usually within 10 business days once allocations are available; delivered through MyCreds. New PAL needed for errors or after a refusal.
- Tuition sample, Fall 2026: Arts $39,232.80 a year, Engineering $49,952.40. New students from Fall 2027: 5.5% increase in most programs.

### Finding a school (DLI list, IRCC 2026-10-23)
- A DLI is approved by a province or territory. **All primary and secondary schools are DLIs** and aren't on the list.
- Enter the DLI number under "Details of intended study" on the application.
- The list shows which schools offer **PGWP-eligible programs** and which graduate programs need no PAL/TAL.
- Post-secondary DLIs must verify LOAs and report enrolment. Non-compliance can lead to suspension from accepting international students.
- Schools use the LOA Portal and DLI Portal (IRCC page for schools, 2025-12-18).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-01-21: spouse OWP limited to master's 16+ months, PhD, professional degrees (H)
- 2025-01-22: new PAL/TAL required to change school (H)
- 2026-01-01: public master's/PhD exempt from PAL/TAL (H) - confirmed
- 2026-04-01: co-op work permit no longer needed for post-secondary students (H)
- 2026-09-01: proof-of-funds increase to $23,448 (single) (H)
- 2026-09-09: work permit holders may study up to 6 months without a study permit (to 2027-12-31) (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Check PGWP eligibility **before** choosing the program (field of study, DLI PGWP status, P3 colleges, distance share).
- PAL/TAL expires 2026-12-31. A 2026 PAL can't be used for a 2027 application.
- Funds must be genuine, sourced and seasoned. Large unexplained deposits lead to refusal and misrepresentation risk.
- A school change without a new PAL/TAL is non-compliance and puts future applications at risk.
- Working over 24 h in term breaches conditions and causes problems for PGWP/PR.

## Common refusal reasons
- s.216(1)(b): not satisfied the applicant will leave (weak ties, purpose of visit, travel history)
- Insufficient or unexplained funds; study plan not logical (career regression, unrelated program)
- LOA not verified; PAL/TAL missing or invalid
