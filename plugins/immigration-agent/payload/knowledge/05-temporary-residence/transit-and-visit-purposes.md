# Transit, visa types and visit purposes

## Must cover
- Transit visas and programs, single vs multiple entry, length of stay, visit purposes

## Current rules
### Transit
| Situation (visa-required national) | Document |
|---|---|
| International flight stops in Canada, or connecting between 2 international flights, 48 hours or less, no valid visitor visa, not eTA-eligible | **Transit visa** (free; 1 or 2 entries; apply online) |
| Transit over 48 hours, or planning to visit Canada even briefly | Visitor visa |
| Crossing by bus, car, train, boat or cruise ship | Visitor visa |
| eTA-required national flying | eTA |
| US citizen or US PR | None |
- Transit visa, mandatory documents: travel itinerary, national identity document, passport. Optional: IMM 0707 consent, employer letter, valid visa for the final destination, proof of funds. Some local forms ask for more (ticket, destination visa, invitation or letter of intent).
- **Transit Without Visa (page 2026-10-08):** Indonesia, Philippines, Thailand, and Taiwan (passport without personal ID number), flying to or from the US. China uses the China Transit Program below.
  - **To the US:** valid US visa; valid passport; confirmed ticket leaving Canada within 24 hours; participating airline; specific airports; stay in the international transit area.
  - **From the US:** documents for the destination; direct non-stop flight to Canada; ticket leaving within 24 hours; entered the US with a valid visa, no overstay, no removal order; Toronto Pearson Terminal 1 only, Vancouver, Calgary or Winnipeg.
  - Airlines: Air Canada, Air Canada Rouge, Air China, Cathay Pacific, China Airlines, China Eastern, China Southern, Hainan, Jazz, Philippine Airlines, WestJet, Xiamen.
  - **Fails if** the transit area closes during the connection (often overnight), the layover passes 24 hours, or the connection is missed. Then report to CBSA. The airline checks eligibility before boarding.

### China Transit Program (PDI 2019-11-22)
- PRC passport (not HK or Macao) with a valid US visa, and travelling between China and the US.
- Air Canada through Montréal, or an approved carrier through Calgary, Toronto Pearson (Terminal 1 only), Vancouver or Winnipeg.
- Confirmed onward flight within 24 hours, no overnight stay; no removal order; no US overstay; last embarkation at a listed city; approved airline.
- Fails any condition: transit visa.

### Single or multiple entry
- Officer's discretion. Multiple-entry maximum: **10 years or expiry of the passport or biometrics, whichever comes first**. Shorter validity is allowed.
- Factors (PDI 2026-06-05): purpose (one-time vs regular family visits); funds and whether the host is established; health conditions and insurance; ties; travel history and **past compliance**; earlier refusals.
- For a parent making regular visits, address these directly in the submission.

### Length of stay
- **Visa validity is not length of stay.** It is the date by which the visa must be used to seek entry.
- The border officer sets the stay. Usually 6 months. No stamp: 6 months from entry, or until passport or biometrics expire if earlier. Super Visa: 5 years per entry.
- PDI: officers should treat a 6-month stay as very likely, even when a shorter visit is requested.

### Visit purposes
| Purpose | Key points |
|---|---|
| Family visit | Citizen, Indian Act, PR or temporary resident family member. Invitation letter and proof of relationship |
| Children or grandchildren, over 6 months | Portal directs to Super Visa (`04-family/super-visa.md`) |
| Spouse or partner sponsoring for PR | After AOR, the visa gets faster processing automatically. No request needed. All requirements still apply |
| Business | Under 6 months, no entry to the labour market, main business and income outside Canada. Include any event code |
| Compassionate | Portal has a separate route. Documents vary, so use the portal checklist. Officer weighs whether single entry suits |
| Marine crew | Seaman's book, signed invitation from ship or port agent, recruiter letter, employment contract |
| Tourist | Purpose, itinerary, funds and ties |

### Business visitors (page 2026-08-28)
- **Qualifies if:** stay under 6 months; no entry to the Canadian labour market; main place of business and source of income and profits outside Canada; supporting documents; meets basic entry requirements. Otherwise a work permit may be needed.
- **Activities:** buying goods or services for a foreign business or government; taking orders; meetings, conferences, conventions, trade fairs; after-sales service under a warranty or sales agreement; training by a Canadian parent company; training employees of a Canadian branch of a foreign company; training by a Canadian seller of equipment or services. CUSMA adds other activities for US and Mexican nationals.
- **There is no separate business visa.** Use the visitor visa or eTA. Include the **event code** if the event is registered with IRCC.
- **Carry at the border:** passport valid for the entire stay; visa or the same passport used for the eTA; US PRs: green card and passport; letters of support from the parent company; letter of invitation from the Canadian host or CBSA letter of recognition; warranty, service agreements or contracts if relevant; 24-hour contact for the host; proof of funds.

### Canadian businesses and event organizers (pages 2023-09-14)
- Do due diligence on the foreign partner. Give each visitor **2 original invitation letters** (one for the application, one for the border) and a 24-hour contact.
- Register international events with IRCC's Special Events Unit for support. Report equipment and goods for the event to CBSA.
- Invitees should apply early and before booking flights. An eTA usually takes minutes, but some take days.
- IRCC says it issues multiple-entry visas whenever possible for repeat business visits.
- **After a refusal:** the person can reapply with new information, and there is no minimum waiting period.
- **Business invitation letter must include:**
  - **Invitee:** full name; date of birth if known; company and position; work and home address and phone; relationship and business history; whether known personally or related; purpose and any longer project; length of visit; expenses the company covers; departure date if known.
  - **Person inviting:** full name, title, Canadian address and phones, work email.
  - **Company:** full name, headquarters address, all facilities to be visited, website, date of registration or incorporation, type of business.
  - Some visa offices may require notarization.

### Workers and students: new visitor visa from inside Canada (page 2026-08-17)
- **Eligible only if:** in Canada; valid study or work permit; want to leave and return soon; and the current visa is expired, about to expire, or was single-entry.
- **A permit is not a visa.** Extending a permit doesn't issue a new visa. It's a separate application.
- Apply online in the IRCC secure account, at least **2 months before leaving**. Select "Study" or "Work", residence "Canada", then "Temporary Resident Visa". The form is IMM 5257 even though its title says outside Canada.
- Can apply as soon as the permit extension is approved, with proof such as a screenshot of the approval.
- **Leaving before it's issued:** possible biometrics, medical exam, longer processing, delayed return. For exceptional departures, tell IRCC through the web form first, or the passport may be returned without a visa.
- Already left Canada: use the outside-Canada process.

### At the border (CBSA pages 2026-08-18)
- A border officer decides entry and length of stay.
- **Declare CAN$10,000 or more.** Undeclared currency or monetary instruments can be seized.
- Advance Declaration in ArriveCAN can be used before flying in. Officers can examine digital devices. Bring identification for any children travelling.

### Giving birth in Canada (PDI 2019-11-21)
- Intent to give birth is not, alone, a ground for refusal. Assessment stays on funds, intent to leave and admissibility.
- Intentional concealment may lead to s.40 examination.

## Consultant pitfalls
- A transit visa cannot be used to visit, even for a day.
- Business guests doing hands-on work may need a work permit. Check the activity list and the labour-market test first.
- Students and workers: warn clients that a permit extension doesn't renew the visa.
- Don't tell clients a long visa means a long stay. Each stay is set at entry.
- Biometrics or passport expiring soon shortens the visa.
- Re-entry after a US-only trip may need no new visa (R190(3)(f)), but a cruise entering international waters doesn't qualify.
