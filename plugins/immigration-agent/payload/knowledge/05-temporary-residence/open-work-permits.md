# Open work permits

## Must cover
- Spousal, PGWP, BOWP, vulnerable worker, asylum claimant

## Current rules
Fee: $155 + **$100 open work permit holder fee** (except fee-exempt categories).

| OWP | Who | Key conditions |
|---|---|---|
| **Spouse of worker** (since 2025-01-21) | Spouse/partner of a principal in **TEER 0-1** or **select TEER 2-3** | Principal's permit valid for **at least 16 months** after the spouse applies (exceptions: certain Quebec health recruitment from 2026-05-25; SIP BC spouses from 2026-03-23). **Dependent children no longer eligible.** TEER 4-5 principals: no family OWP |
| **Spouse of student** (since 2025-01-21) | Student in a master's of 16+ months, PhD, listed professional degree, or eligible program | Valid with the study permit (see study-permits.md) |
| **Sponsored spouse in Canada** | Spouse/partner being sponsored, living with sponsor, with AOR; accompanying dependent child | No POE applications (see 04-family/spousal-common-law-conjugal.md) |
| **PGWP** | Graduates | see pgwp.md |
| **BOWP** (bridging) | Workers in Canada with a pending PR application (EE, PNP, etc.) whose permit is expiring | EE/PNP-EE: in Canada, outside Quebec for EE; valid permit, maintained status or restorable; **principal applicant only**; complete application past completeness check; AOR letter. PNP: no employment restriction in nomination; non-EE PNP needs eligibility assessment passed. Other programs have own sections. No "expires within X months" condition on the page (IRCC 2026-10-16, read 2026-11-09) (H) |
| **Vulnerable worker** | Workers on an employer-specific permit **abused or at risk of abuse** in their job | In Canada; valid employer-specific permit or extension filed in time. **No fee** (apply page 2026-07-14) (H). Abuse includes a third party charging job fees (eligibility page 2025-12-19) |
| **Asylum claimant** | Claim found **eligible** and awaiting IRB; can't meet basic needs without work; IME passed | **No fee**. Family only if they also claimed. Under SOR/2026-218 (2026-11-03), work permits once eligible for referral |
| **Quebec PSTQ candidates and spouses** | Public policy signed 2026-06-05; **expires 2026-12-31**. Worker in Quebec invited under PSTQ who filed a DSP: employer-specific permit, same employer. **Spouse or partner:** open work permit if included in the DSP as accompanying, with valid status, restoration, or status expired under 90 days | Dependent children not eligible. Apply online. Fees $155 + $100 OWP fee |

### Worker-spouse OWP mechanics (read 2026-11-09) (H)
- Occupation is tested **at the time the spouse submits**; the 16 months runs from IRCC's receipt and is measured on the principal's permit or authorization, not the contract. Filing together removes the proof of length, not the requirement (apply page 2026-05-28).
- 6-month test instead: principal's permit was issued or approved because of an eligible economic PR application (family must be listed as dependants). IRCC's "on a pathway" section is written for TEER 4-5 workers, so don't apply it to TEER 0-1 without checking.
- Extensions in Canada: current OWP shorter than principal's permit, applied before expiry, never past the principal's permit or passport. The page doesn't say what happens to an issued OWP if the principal changes occupation.

### BOWP notes (read 2026-11-09) (H)
- No job, employer, offer or LMIA is required (bridging page 2026-10-16). Maintained status keeps the prior conditions, so an employer-specific holder can't move to a new employer until the BOWP issues; PPCHANGEWORK2020 only applies to a new employer-specific application.
- Conflict: Help Centre 676 (2026-11-03) says the permit must expire in 4 months or less; the bridging page has no such condition. Official program page used; note both.
- PR effect: report job or job offer changes (EE after-apply page 2026-10-16). FSW relying on a valid offer needs funds if the offer is lost; FST needs a certificate of qualification (job offer page 2026-11-03). AOR alone doesn't prove completeness; the biometrics letter issues if the application is complete.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-01-21: worker-spouse OWP limited to TEER 0-1 / select TEER 2-3 with 16+ months remaining; dependent children excluded; student-spouse OWP limited (H)
- 2026-03-23 / 2026-05-25: exceptions to the 16-month rule (SIP BC; Quebec health) (H)
- 2026-11-03: asylum work permit rules under SOR/2026-218 (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- The 16-month remaining-validity test is measured **at the spouse's application date**. Time the application.
- "Select TEER 2-3" is a list. Check the occupation against IRCC's list, not just the TEER.
- Children of workers need their own study permit or visitor status. They have no OWP.
- BOWP requires the PR application to have passed completeness (AOR/ITA conditions). Check before the permit expires and use maintained status.
- Vulnerable worker OWP: document the abuse. It's a protective permit, but credibility matters.

## Common refusal reasons
- Principal's occupation not eligible or validity under 16 months; relationship not genuine; principal's permit expired; missing holder fee

## Re-verify before advising
- Select TEER 2-3 occupation list
- BOWP eligibility page; vulnerable worker eligibility page; Quebec 2026 public policy
