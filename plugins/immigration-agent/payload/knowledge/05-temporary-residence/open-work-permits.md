# Open work permits

## Must cover
- Spousal, PGWP, BOWP, vulnerable worker, asylum claimant

## Current rules
Fee: $155 + **$100 open work permit holder fee** (except fee-exempt categories).

| OWP | Who | Key conditions |
|---|---|---|
| **Spouse of worker** (since 2025-01-21) | Spouse/partner of a principal in **TEER 0-1** or **select TEER 2-3** | Principal's permit valid for **at least 16 months** after the spouse applies (exceptions: certain Quebec health recruitment from 2026-05-25; SIP BC spouses from 2026-03-23). **Dependent children not eligible under this measure** (other routes below). TEER 4-5 principals: no family OWP |
| **Spouse of student** (since 2025-01-21) | Student full time at a PGWP-eligible DLI, not in the last term, in a master's of 16+ months, PhD, listed professional degree, or eligible program (full list: study-permit-status-and-compliance.md) | Valid with the study permit (see study-permits.md) |
| **Sponsored spouse in Canada** | Spouse/partner being sponsored, living with sponsor, with AOR; accompanying dependent child | No POE applications (see 04-family/spousal-common-law-conjugal.md) |
| **PGWP** | Graduates | see pgwp.md |
| **BOWP** (bridging) | Workers in Canada with a pending PR application (EE, PNP, etc.) whose permit is expiring | EE/PNP-EE: in Canada, outside Quebec for EE; valid permit, maintained status or restorable; **principal applicant only**; complete application past completeness check; AOR letter. PNP: no employment restriction in nomination; non-EE PNP needs eligibility assessment passed. Other programs have own sections. No "expires within X months" condition on the page (IRCC 2026-10-16, read 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-17) (H). Full rules: "BOWP (A75) detail" below |
| **Vulnerable worker** | Workers on an employer-specific permit **abused or at risk of abuse** in their job | In Canada; valid employer-specific permit or extension filed in time. **No fee** (apply page 2026-07-14) (H). Abuse includes a third party charging job fees (eligibility page 2025-12-19) |
| **Asylum claimant** | Claim found **eligible** and awaiting IRB; can't meet basic needs without work; IME passed | **No fee**. Family only if they also claimed. Under SOR/2026-218 (2026-11-03), work permits once eligible for referral |
| **Quebec PSTQ candidates and spouses** | Public policy signed 2026-06-05; **expires 2026-12-31**. Worker in Quebec invited under PSTQ who filed a DSP: employer-specific permit, same employer. **Spouse or partner:** open work permit if included in the DSP as accompanying, with valid status, restoration, or status expired under 90 days | Dependent children not eligible. Apply online. Fees $155 + $100 OWP fee |

### Eligible TEER 2 and 3 occupations for the worker-spouse OWP (IRCC page 2026-06-12, read 2026-11-16) (H)
Machine-read by the agent (quote.py, intake_form.py): keep one occupation per line as `- NOCCODE Title`.
Principal in TEER 0 or 1: any occupation qualifies. TEER 2 or 3: only these. TEER 4 or 5: no spouse OWP under this measure (the economic-PR and PR-pathway routes on the same page have their own 6-month test).
Not eligible whatever the occupation: principal with a refugee claim referred to the IRB, an unenforceable removal order, a study permit or working as a student without a work permit, a pending PGWP, or holding a spousal OWP. Dependent children are not eligible under this measure (they are under the economic-PR, PR-pathway and SIP BC routes).
Exceptions without the 16-month test: SIP BC (job title SIPSPOUSEBC, since 2026-03-23, any TEER) and Quebec-recruited health professionals NOC 31301, 32103, 32120 (CONJOINTSANTEQC, since 2026-05-25). The page doesn't mention exemption codes C41 or C46: family OWPs on a PR pathway need the principal's economic PR application or an eligible PR-pathway stream.

### Worker-spouse OWP mechanics (read 2026-11-09) (H)
- Occupation is tested **at the time the spouse submits**; the 16 months runs from IRCC's receipt and is measured on the principal's permit or authorization, not the contract. Filing together removes the proof of length, not the requirement (apply page 2026-05-28).
- 6-month test instead: principal's permit was issued or approved because of an eligible economic PR application (family must be listed as dependants). IRCC's "on a pathway" section is written for TEER 4-5 workers, so don't apply it to TEER 0-1 without checking.
- Extensions in Canada: current OWP shorter than principal's permit, applied before expiry, never past the principal's permit or passport. The page doesn't say what happens to an issued OWP if the principal changes occupation.

### Worker-spouse OWP: principal changes, renewals, BOWP (C41 PDI 2026-08-11, read 2026-11-17) (H unless marked)
- **Test date:** principal must meet everything "at the time of decision"; 16 months counted from receipt of the spouse's application. Principal must be employed in the eligible TEER from receipt until decision (OWP/R186 principals: employer letter with NOC and duties).
- **Principal types that don't count:** FTA spousal OWPs, C17 (RNIP/RCIP/FCIP), co-op C32/C33, family permits C41/C42/C46-C49, C91, R206 S61/S62; work under R186(f), (v), (w). Principal on a family OWP stays the "spouse": an LMIA worker can't quit and take a C41 off the spouse's C41.
- **Principal on maintained status (R186(u)) when the spouse files:** application held until the principal's extension is decided.
- **Principal moves to PGWP:** no spouse OWP while principal works under R186(w); principal's PGWP must be issued before the spousal decision and principal employed in eligible TEER; priority request before PGWP approval = refusal. PGWP holders' spouses = C41 (family-members PDI 2026-08-11).
- **Principal changes job/employer/NOC after the spouse's OWP issued:** no PDI section. R209: a work permit becomes invalid only when it expires or is cancelled under R209.01 (administrative error), R209.02 (PR or death) or R243.2 (SOR/2025-11; Justice Laws current to 2026-10-29). So the issued OWP is not automatically invalidated by a job change (inference, M). Family-members PDI: "If a principal's work permit is revoked, the spousal work permit is also revoked"; family must be linked in GCMS "to ensure the ability to revoke" (C41/C46 PDI). Change of job matters at the next renewal.
- **Principal refused/loses status:** no express rule for an issued spouse permit (unknown). Family-group filings: principal assessed first; principal refused = family refused "for not meeting the requirements of this category" (family-members PDI 2026-08-11, re-read 2026-11-20). Client process page (2026-05-28, read 2026-11-20): filed together, family applications "will also be refused"; at a POE, those "submitted after yours will also be refused"; filed separately, "If your application is refused, your family members can't apply for a work permit." OWP holder fee ($100) refunded on refusal; processing fee not (H).
- **Renewal (standard):** spouse must still meet C41 at the renewal: principal holds or is approved for a permit with 16+ months from the spouse's filing, eligible TEER. Validity never past principal's authorized stay or spouse's passport.
- **Renewal (facilitation measure, since 2025-01-21):** in-Canada family whose C41/C46 or C47/C48 issued before 2025-01-21 may renew under old rules under R201(1) only to align with the principal's "existing" permit with 6+ months left. If the principal got any new permit since (even same job/employer), new rules apply (exception: earlier permit shortened by passport). PDI example: principal moved to a lower-TEER job after 2025-01-21, spouse not eligible for renewal.
- **C41/C46 PDI (2026-08-11; re-read 2026-11-20, unchanged):** still lists a 6-month test ("the authorization to work in Canada is for a period of 6 months or longer" after the family member's receipt date) and TEER 0-3; scope stated as applications "received by IRCC on or before January 21, 2025", renewals received on or after that date aligned with the principal's existing authorization, and family "transitioning to permanent residence". Covers pre-2025-01-21 cases, renewals and family of workers "transitioning to PR" (permit tied to a PR pathway, PR not yet filed: CSQ holders, nominees, AIP, start-up, Yukon), renewable under R201(1) regardless of filing date (M: page mixes old and new rules; read with the C41 PDI).
- **Spouse of a BOWP holder:** C49 (not C41). Principal holds a permit issued because of an economic PR application (BOWP A75 counts), PR still in process, principal authorized 6+ months after the family member's receipt date, family listed as accompanying dependants; dependent children included; renewal under R201(1) while C49 is still met; refusal ground R200(1)(c)(ii) (C49 PDI 2025-02-03; Refusals/Renewals added per update 2026-08-11). BOWP PDI: "genuine accompanying spouses, common-law partners and dependent children are eligible" under C49 (PDI 2025-07-30). Effect on a C49 permit if PR is refused/withdrawn: not stated (unknown).

### BOWP notes (read 2026-11-09) (H)
- No job, employer, offer or LMIA is required (bridging page 2026-10-16). Maintained status keeps the prior conditions, so an employer-specific holder can't move to a new employer until the BOWP issues; PPCHANGEWORK2020 only applies to a new employer-specific application.
- Conflict: Help Centre 676 (2026-11-03) says the permit must expire in 4 months or less; the bridging page has no such condition. Official program page used; note both. Resolved 2026-11-17: see below.

### BOWP (A75) detail (read 2026-11-17) (H)
- **Pages:** client page moved to `.../work-canada/pr-work-permits/bridging.html` (modified 2026-10-16); old `.../extend-permit/bridging-open-work-permit.html` reported 404; don't cite it. PDI R205(a) A75 (page details 2025-07-30).
- **All classes (PDI):** in Canada after entry as a temporary resident (IRPA s.22); at submission: valid status + valid work permit, OR maintained status with R186(u) work authorization after a R201(1) renewal, OR eligible for restoration with work authorization; **principal applicant** on the PR application. PDI 2022 update: authorized to work "at the time of submission only".
- **EE classes (FSW, CEC, FST) and EE-PNP:** e-APR passed the R10 completeness check (GCMS note "R10 OK"); intend to live outside Quebec; upload AOR. Incomplete e-APR: cancelled, BOWP refused, OWP holder fee refunded, processing fee not.
- **Non-EE PNP:** positive eligibility assessment (GCMS "Eligibility: Passed"), not just AOR; AOR + nomination letter. **PNP (both):** nomination with no employment restriction; restricted nomination = no A75. Work location restricted to the nominating province. Client page: PNP applicant must be in Canada with valid TR status when IRCC decides the BOWP.
- **Quebec skilled workers (QSWC) and Quebec investors:** CSQ valid when the PR application was filed; passed R10 completeness; live/intend to live in Quebec; letter with "E" file number; work restricted to Quebec. Validity 24 months or passport; extensions usually 12 months.
- **Caregivers:** HCCP/HSW pilots (2019-2024): approval in principle + 6 months qualifying experience (since 2024-06-16); codes CARE4411/CARE4412. Caring for Children / High Medical Needs: APR before 2019-06-18 + approval in principle. **Home Care Worker pilots opened 2025: no BOWP** (client page 2026-10-16).
- **Agri-Food Pilot** (closed 2025-05-14; pending files only): approval in principle; apply online.
- **AIP/RCIP/business/other classes:** not listed among A75 classes on either page (AIP has its own permit route; see C41/C46 "transitioning to PR" list). Treat as not BOWP-eligible unless IRCC lists them (H for absence on both pages).
- **Timing:** no "expires within X months" condition in the PDI or client page. Validity: 24 months from issue (PDI).
- **Family:** spouse/partner and dependent children of an A75 holder: C49 OWP (R205(c)(ii)). See worker-spouse section above.

### Q676 vs program page: resolution (2026-11-17) (H)
- Help Centre Q676 (modified 2026-11-03), answer to "Can I renew my post-graduation work permit?": may apply for a BOWP if waiting on an EE PR decision, "your work permit expires in 4 months or less", valid status, in Canada.
- Client page (2026-10-16) and A75 PDI (2025-07-30): no expiry window; only status at submission + stage of the PR application.
- **Governs: the PDI** (officers' instructions), consistent with the client page. The 4-month window was removed in 2021 (secondary sources; PDI 2021 update page exists, text not re-read) (M for the date). Q676 is outdated Help Centre text. Practice: file once eligible; no need to wait until 4 months before expiry.
- **Re-checked 2026-11-20:** Q676 still dated 2026-11-03 and still says "4 months or less". Not corrected yet (H for the text).
- PR effect: report job or job offer changes (EE after-apply page 2026-10-16). FSW relying on a valid offer needs funds if the offer is lost; FST needs a certificate of qualification (job offer page 2026-11-03). AOR alone doesn't prove completeness; the biometrics letter issues if the application is complete.

## Consultant pitfalls
- The 16-month remaining-validity test is measured **at the spouse's application date**. Time the application.
- "Select TEER 2-3" is a list. Check the occupation against IRCC's list, not just the TEER.
- Children of workers need their own study permit or visitor status. They have no OWP.
- BOWP requires the PR application to have passed the R10 completeness check (a profile or ITA is not enough; upload the AOR, which alone doesn't prove completeness). Check before the permit expires and use maintained status.
- Vulnerable worker OWP: document the abuse. It's a protective permit, but credibility matters.

## Common refusal reasons
- Principal's occupation not eligible or validity under 16 months; relationship not genuine; principal's permit expired; missing holder fee
