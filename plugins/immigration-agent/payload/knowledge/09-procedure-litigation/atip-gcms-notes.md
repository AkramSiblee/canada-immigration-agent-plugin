# ATIP and GCMS notes

## Must cover
- How to request, fees, reading notes, timelines
- Officer note abbreviations and reading guide (gap-pass-01)

## Current rules
| | Privacy Act request | Access to Information Act request |
|---|---|---|
| Who | The individual (any location) about their own info | Citizens, PRs, or individuals/corporations **in Canada** |
| Fee | **None** | **$5** (paid online in ATIP Online Request) |
| Typical use | Client's own GCMS notes | Representative or third party in Canada, with consent |
- Submit through IRCC **ATIP Online Request**. Statutory response time is **30 days** (extendable) (M).
- **Consent (IMM 5744)**: needed for representatives and for each person **16+** on the file. Valid **1 year**. **Original blue-ink signature**, no e-signatures (per IRCC guidance). A written consent letter with the required elements is also accepted.
- For CBSA records (POE examinations, removals), make a separate request to **CBSA ATIP** (M).

### Reading GCMS notes (M)
- Look for: the officer's **decision reasons** ("Notes" tab), risk indicators, the **eligibility vs admissibility** stages, verification results, requests for information, and dates.
- Common abbreviations: PA (principal applicant), AOR, FN, R179 (TRV refusal grounds), A40 (misrepresentation), BOC, PFL, DM (decision maker), ITA, e-APR, CSQ.
- Use the notes for: refusal analysis, PFL responses, reapplication strategy, and JR (lawyer).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- None found (pages dated 2018-2022) (M)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Requesting under the Access Act without being in Canada is refused. Use a Privacy Act request by the client, or a requester in Canada with IMM 5744.
- Don't wait for GCMS notes if a JR deadline (15/60 days) is running. Refer to a lawyer immediately.

## Common refusal reasons
- Missing consent; unsigned/e-signed consent; requester not eligible
