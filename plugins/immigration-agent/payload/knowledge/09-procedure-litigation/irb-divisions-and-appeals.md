# IRB divisions and appeals

## Must cover
- Deadlines, representation by RCIC-IRB, evidence rules

## Current rules
| Division | Hears | Key deadlines |
|---|---|---|
| **RPD** | Refugee claims | C-12: claim completion 60 days (+30); hearing scheduling no longer time-limited by regulation (SOR/2026-218) |
| **RAD** | Appeals of RPD decisions | **File 15 days / perfect 30 days** after receiving written reasons (IRPR s.159.91(1)); RAD may extend for fairness and natural justice (s.159.91(2)) (H) |
| **ID** | Admissibility hearings, detention reviews | Detention reviews at 48 h, 7 days, then every 30 days (IRPA s.57) (M) |
| **IAD** | Sponsorship, removal order, residency obligation, Minister's appeals | IAD Rules 2022 r.16: **sponsorship 30 days**; **removal order 30 days**; **residency obligation 60 days** (from receipt of decision); Minister 30 days |
- IAD: notice of appeal plus the decision (r.13); language choice (r.18). The Division can extend or shorten time (r.4). IAD stays (IRPA s.68). De novo hearings with H&C jurisdiction (s.67), subject to s.64-65.
- **Representation**: paid representatives must be lawyers, notaries (QC) or **CICC licensees with the RCIC-IRB class** (IRPA s.91) (see 10-consultant-practice).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026-11-03: SOR/2026-218 removed regulated RPD hearing time limits; SOR/2026-219 RPD Rules amended (H), in force with C-12 s.43(5):
  - No separate Basis of Claim Form. Basis of claim information goes in the single application to the Minister. Extensions come from the Minister, not the RPD.
  - **Personal documents: no later than 30 days after referral.** Later documents need an application. Country condition documents: up to 10 days before the hearing.
  - Changes to basis of claim information: without delay, and no later than 5 days after the notice to appear.
  - Abandonment before referral: hearing or written submissions. Fax is no longer accepted.
  - Former r.34(3)(a) continues for claims referred before the Rules came into force.

## Consultant pitfalls
- **Calendar IAD deadlines from the receipt date.** 30 days for sponsorship and removal, 60 days for residency obligation.
- RCIC (non-IRB class) **cannot** appear at the IRB. Refer or co-counsel.
- Disclosure deadlines before hearings are strict. Late evidence may be excluded.
