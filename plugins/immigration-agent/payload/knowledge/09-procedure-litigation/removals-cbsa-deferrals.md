# Removals, CBSA deferrals, stays

## Must cover
- Order types, bars, deferral requests, stay motions (lawyer)

## Current rules
### Removal orders (IRPR s.223)
| Order | Return rule |
|---|---|
| **Departure** | Leave within **30 days** of the order becoming enforceable and confirm departure; otherwise it becomes deportation (s.224) |
| **Exclusion** | ARC needed for **1 yr**; **5 yrs** for misrepresentation (s.225) |
| **Deportation** | ARC needed **permanently** (s.226) |
- Order by ground: IRPR s.228-229 (e.g. criminality: deportation; medical/financial/misrep: exclusion).
- In force (IRPA s.49): on the day made if no appeal; after the appeal period; or on final determination. **Refugee claimants: conditional orders** (e.g. 7 days after an ineligibility notice; 15 days after RPD rejection if no appeal).
- **Enforceable: must leave immediately** (s.48(2)).

### Statutory stays (IRPA s.50)
- Contrary to a judicial decision; during a prison sentence; IAD/court stay; s.114(1)(b) (PRRA); Minister's stay. Plus regulatory PRRA stays (IRPR s.232) and the RAD stay where applicable.

### CBSA deferral request
- A request to the removals officer to defer removal (short-term reasons: pending H&C filed in time, medical, school year, BIOC). Officer discretion is **narrow**. A refusal can be challenged with a **Federal Court stay motion (lawyer)** (M).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026-04-01: removal cost repayment indexed (H)
- 2026-11-03: C-12 ineligible claimants get conditional removal orders enforceable after 7 days (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Advise voluntary compliance (departure order within 30 days) to avoid a deportation order and the permanent ARC requirement.
- Stay motions are urgent and lawyer-only. Refer the same day a removal date is given.
- Keep proof of departure (CBSA confirmation) for future ARC/admissibility.

## Common refusal reasons
- Deferral refused: reasons not short-term or not compelling
