## Current rules
**Confidence M: official IRB lists read 2026-11-08. Individual IAD and RAD decisions are on CanLII, which blocks automated checks, so no 2024-2026 digest yet.**
- **Binding vs persuasive:** IRB **jurisprudential guides** (designated by the Chairperson) should be followed unless distinguished. **Persuasive decisions** may be adopted. Other decisions don't bind. Check the IRB list of jurisprudential guides and persuasive decisions (irb-cisr.gc.ca).
- **IAD spousal appeals:** de novo; credibility of both spouses at hearing; ADR available for some sponsorship appeals (M).
- **IAD H&C (s.67(1)(c)):** *Ribic* factors for removal appeals (seriousness of offence, rehabilitation, establishment, family, hardship, BIOC); *Chieu* for foreign hardship (M).
- **RAD:** correctness review of RPD findings, with deference on credibility where the RPD had a meaningful advantage (*Huruglica*, 2016 FCA 93) (M).
- **RPD under C-12:** RPD Rules amended by SOR/2026-219, in force 2026-11-03 (see `09-procedure-litigation/irb-divisions-and-appeals.md`). Watch for new practice notices.

### Pass 2: IRB categories and examples (IRB pages via search; snapshot ages vary, M)
- **Jurisprudential guides** (IRB list read 2026-11-08, H): chosen by the Chairperson under IRPA s.159(1)(h); members are expected to follow them or explain why not. **Current:** TC1-05038 (July 2022: gang-targeting, s.97(1)(b) generalized risk); MB8-00025 (Nov 2020: Article 1E, risk in country of residence); TB7-01837 (May 2017: Ahmadis in Pakistan); TB4-05778 (June 2016: North Korean citizens and South Korea). **Revoked:** TB7-19851 (April 2020; now Reasons of Interest), TB6-11632 (June 2019), MB6-01059/60 (Nov 2018), TA2-14980 (Costa Rica, 2003).
- **Persuasive decisions**: identified by a division head; members encouraged to rely on them. Examples: **TC0-09011** (RAD, Cuba, Aug 2023): new evidence on Article 1E; **TB7-16268** (Georgia): similar BOC narratives.
- **Reasons of interest**: lower tier. Revoked guides moved here: **VB9-01721** (Mexico) and **TB7-19851** (Nigeria IFA).
- The list changes often. Check https://www.irb-cisr.gc.ca/en/legal-policy/policies/Pages/jurisprudential-guides.aspx and https://www.irb-cisr.gc.ca/en/decisions/Pages/index.aspx before citing.
