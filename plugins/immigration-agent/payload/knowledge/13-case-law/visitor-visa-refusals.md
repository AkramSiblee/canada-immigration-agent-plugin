# Federal Court: visitor visa refusals

## Must cover
- Family-visit and dual-intent decisions, with outcomes and themes

## Current rules
Citations and outcomes are confirmed on the **official Federal Court site** (2026-11-08). Summaries are short; read the full decision before citing. **Federal Court work goes to a licensed lawyer.**

### Set aside
| Case | Facts | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *Baghdadi*, **2026 FC 1338** (2026-10-30) | Visit to brother-in-law; Iran; redetermined refusal | Officer ignored evidence of parents in Iran and other ties. Unclear why ties were found insufficient. Counsel's explanation at hearing couldn't fill the gap |
| *Rehman*, **2026 FC 805** (2026-06-16) | Pakistani in Saudi Arabia; visa to write a trade exam for PR | Dual intent (A22(2)) not properly assessed. Living apart from family was assessed incompletely |
| *Kumar*, **2026 FC 333** (2026-03-12) | Parents visiting 2 children in Canada; third refusal | Family ties must be weighed against evidence of motivation to return (elderly parents, business, past compliance). Generic reasons. Returned on an expedited basis |
| *Noori*, **2026 FC 95** (2026-01-21) | Family visiting daughter for 2 weeks | Reasoning on finances, family ties and status lacked justification, intelligibility and transparency |
| *Rafiq*, **2025 FC 160** (2025-01-28) | Misrepresentation; PFL response raised innocent mistake | Officer didn't engage with the response |
| *Kashefi*, **2024 FC 856** (2024-06-06) | Couple visiting children | Only an equivocal line that incentives "may outweigh" ties. No mention of the facts listed |
| *Akinremi*, **2024 FC 723** (2024-05-10) | Nigerian parents visiting daughter | Found no significant ties abroad although the Family Information forms showed them |
| *Tavakoli Dinani*, **2012 FC 1063** | Retired Iranian couple | Redetermination ordered. Discusses policy favouring visas for parents |

### Upheld
| Case | Facts | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *Mawassi*, **2025 CF 1885** (2025-11-26, French) | Lebanese family; school enrolment for a citizen child and leaving conflict | Reasonable. Court won't reweigh the evidence |
| *Akhtar*, **2022 FC 595** (2022-04-25) | Parents in UAE helping daughter with childcare | Genuine assessment of finances, status and ties. No breach of procedural fairness |
| *Rahim*, **2022 FC 486** (2022-04-07) | Biological parents and aunt of children adopted in Canada | Finding that the strongest ties were in Canada was reasonable |
| *Wang*, **2010 FC 201** (2010-02-23) | Mother visiting only son | Weak ties finding reasonable and defensible on the facts |

### Themes
- **Onus on the applicant** to show they will leave (*Rahim*, citing *Khan*, 2001 FCA 345).
- **Family in Canada is not enough to refuse.** Officers must weigh it against ties and motivation to return (*Kumar*; see *Gholami*, 2024 FC 201, cited there).
- **Reasons must engage with the record.** Boilerplate fails (*Kashefi*, *Noori*, *Baghdadi*).
- **Facts on the Family Information form count** (*Akinremi*). Complete forms help.
- **Low procedural fairness duty** on sufficiency of evidence (*Akhtar*). Applicants get one chance, so file everything upfront.
- **Dual intent is legitimate** but doesn't remove the duty to leave (*Rehman*; A22(2)).
- Standard of review: reasonableness (*Vavilov*).
- Courts remit for redetermination; a win does not issue the visa.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026: repeated set-asides for generic family-ties reasoning (*Noori*, *Kumar*, *Rehman*, *Baghdadi*) (H)
