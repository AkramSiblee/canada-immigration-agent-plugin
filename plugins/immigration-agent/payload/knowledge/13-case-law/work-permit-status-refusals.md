# Federal Court: work permit, restoration, spouse OWP and status refusals (2024-2026)

## Must cover
- Restoration (R182), BOWP, spouse/partner OWP (worker spouse), PGWP transition (cross-ref), maintained status (R183(5)) and in-Canada extensions, TRP as fallback

## How these were verified (read first)
- On 2026-11-17 the official Federal Court site and CanLII returned HTTP 403 to automated reading. Decisions were **not opened**.
- Each row was matched across at least two secondary summaries (mainly dadkhah.ca case-law posts, which link the official item; amcaim.ca case summaries and monthly case-law libraries; canadianimmigration.net applicant case summaries). Official **item numbers** come from those links.
- Rows marked **(CanLII)** have no confirmed official item number. The CanLII link was built from the citation or taken from a summary and was not opened.
- Holdings are **M** unless marked otherwise. Read the full decision before citing it to a client, an officer or the Court.
- **Federal Court work goes to a licensed lawyer** (see Escalation).
- Rules (90 days, fees, maintained status mechanics) live in `knowledge/05-temporary-residence/trp-restoration-maintained-status.md`. This file is the case law only.

## Current rules

### (a) Restoration (R182): 90 days, same status, no discretion
| Case | Result | Holding | Practice lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Dhir*, **2026 FC 903** (2026-07-03, IMM-156-25, Gleeson J.) | Upheld | 90-day period is mandatory and "not discretionary". A first refusal letter saying the applicants were "eligible to restore" created no legitimate expectation that could extend it; second application was late | Diarize day 90 from the loss of status yourself. Never rely on refusal-letter wording |
| *Singh*, **2026 FC 828** (2026-06-18, IMM-2649-25) | Upheld | No discretion to extend. Each application stands alone: an earlier timely but refused restoration doesn't make a later late one valid | One shot inside 90 days. File the strongest complete package first time |
| *Egwuatu*, **2025 FC 1950** (2025-12-10, IMM-11126-24) | Upheld | Minor's third restoration filed late because of a passport renewal delay. No power to waive; refusal reasonable. TRP (IRPA s.24) noted as the alternative | Passport or document delays don't stop the clock. Move to TRP or departure planning |
| *Kohli*, **2026 FC 381** (2026, Sadrehashemi J.) (CanLII) | Upheld | Former student whose employer-specific WP application was refused couldn't restore directly as a worker. Restoration is only to the status held immediately before; apply for the new permit after. No fairness letter owed | Restore the **last-held** status, then switch. See also *Kaur* 2026 FC 173 (worker can't restore as student), in the student file |
| *Peligrino*, **2026 FC 341** (2026-03-13, IMM-2716-25, Southcott J.) | Set aside | Caregiver whose WP expired asked for restoration plus a new LMIA permit within 90 days. Officer refused to process under Ministerial Instructions and ignored the restoration request | When restoration is requested, the officer must deal with it. Ask for it expressly on the form and in the cover letter |
| *Tanisha*, **2025 FC 1828** (2025-11-14, IMM-10138-24) | Set aside | Former counsel failed to file the restoration within 90 days and said he had. Incompetence caused a miscarriage of justice: procedural fairness breach | Rare exception. Requires the incompetence-of-counsel protocol (notice to former rep) and proof of prejudice. Lawyer only |
| *Singh*, **2026 FC 352** (2026-03-16, IMM-4100-25) | Upheld | Rep didn't pay a fee on an earlier application. Incompetence claim failed: no proof the outcome would differ; having to leave and reapply from abroad is not enough prejudice | Contrast with *Tanisha*. Blaming the rep rarely wins |
| *Chamah*, **2026 FC 449** (2026, IMM no. not confirmed) | Set aside | Student restoration plus extension refused over a 2020 study gap that IRCC had already accepted in an earlier extension. "Not the brevity of the reasons but rather their silence" | Point the officer to prior approvals on the same issue. Put them in the record |
| *Taduvayai*, **2025 FC 1173** (2025-07-02, IMM-5485-24, Thorne J.) | Upheld | Expired-PGWP holder sought OWP plus restoration but didn't say she was applying under the temporary public policy, which required applicants to say so | Name the public policy expressly and follow its instructions to the letter |

### (b) TRP when restoration is out of reach
| Case | Result | Holding | Practice lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Howlader*, **2025 FC 274** (2025-02-12, IMM-8941-23, Battista J.) | Set aside | Officer required "unique" or "compelling" reasons; s.24(1) isn't limited to exceptional cases. Immigration history also misapprehended | **Conflict:** *Jasmeet Kaur* 2026 FC 1095 (student file) upheld a "compelling reason" standard for TRP after PGWP ineligibility. Argue need plus low risk, and address both lines |
| *Singh*, **2025 FC 1520** (2025-09-12, IMM-15600-23) | Upheld | Out-of-status worker (stopped studying, worked without authorization). TRP and WP refused because he could apply from India; he showed no difficulty doing so | Show why leaving and reapplying is impractical or harmful, with evidence |
| *Yarimdag*, **2025 FC 1158** (2025-06-27, IMM-2811-24) (CanLII) | Set aside | Officer faulted efforts to maintain status when the relevant conduct was her efforts to restore after a consultant's misconduct | Document the rep misconduct and the client's prompt corrective steps |
| *Ocran*, **2025 FC 517** | Set aside | See student file: TRP refusal ignored the 6-month unauthorized-work bar vs PGWP timing clash | Cross-ref `study-permit-pgwp-refusals.md` |

### (c) BOWP refusals
- **No verifiable 2024-2026 Federal Court decision found** on a refused bridging open work permit (AOR, expiring permit or eligibility). Don't cite one until found.
- Nearest analogue: *Ihenyen*, **2026 FC 1143** (2026-09-10, IMM-24575-24) | Upheld | TR to PR pathway required valid temporary status both at receipt and at approval; the work permit expired before the decision and status wasn't maintained. Requirement "clear, mandatory, and unambiguous" | Lesson for BOWP/PR clients: keep status alive through to the PR decision (extension or BOWP before expiry). A pending PR application alone is not status

### (d) Spouse/partner OWP (worker principal)
Student-principal cases (*Zarate*, *Adiele*) are in `study-permit-pgwp-refusals.md` (f).

| Case | Result | Holding | Practice lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Khanam*, **2026 FC 883** (2026, McDonald J.) (CanLII) | Upheld | Principal a food services supervisor. Online application received after the **2025-01-21 05:00 UTC** cutoff per GCMS; a fee receipt dated 2025-01-20 didn't prove filing. New eligibility rules properly applied | Filing date = IRCC receipt, not payment. Keep the submission confirmation. **(L: one firm's summaries only)** |
| *Singh*, **2026 FC 544** (2026-04-23, IMM-19561-24, Gascon J.) | Set aside | Wife held LMIA-based permit in a qualifying TEER. Refusal for missing bank statements showing salary deposits ignored employer letter, pay stubs, T4 and NOA: "defies logic". Redetermination within 30 days | File the full employment set; bank deposits help but aren't mandatory |
| *Kaur*, **2026 FC 1123** (2026-09-02, IMM-3291-25) | Set aside | Spouse WP and children's study permits refused for "insufficient" proof of the worker's qualifying employment. Officer ignored WP, LMIA, contract, pay stubs, bank statements | Same: officers must engage with substantial TEER evidence |
| *Salkhan*, **2025 FC 1746** (2025-10-30, IMM-23240-24) | Upheld | Employment letter and pay stubs not in the record; applicant couldn't show they were sent | Keep upload proof. Check the submitted PDF before sending |
| *Khanhosseinpoor*, **2025 FC 1497** (2025-09-10, IMM-13355-24) | Upheld | No marriage certificate in the record: relationship not proven. "A bare assertion ... that the document was sent will not suffice" | Prove the relationship with documents every time |
| *Majid*, **2026 FC 871** (2026-06-29, IMM-5779-25) | Upheld | Illegible pay stubs and employment letters; officer needn't ask for better copies | Legibility is the applicant's job |
| *Beygmohammadi*, **2026 FC 712** (2026-06-02, IMM-7115-25) | Upheld | Key proof of spouse's Canadian employment not uploaded; no duty to flag missing documents | Checklist every upload |
| *Baring*, **2025 FC 1668** (2025-10-09, IMM-13243-24) | Upheld | Principal on a PGWP. Funds not shown to be readily available (assets certificate mixed in another person's assets); officer may go "beyond simply accepting financial documents at face value". No fairness letter for insufficiency | Spouse OWP from abroad still needs R200(1)(b) temporary-intent and funds evidence |
| *Noori*, **2026 FC 773** (2026-06-10, IMM-3753-25) | Upheld | Family of five (spouse OWP plus TRVs): officer may measure family funds against LICO and weigh family ties | Size funds to the whole family |
| *Shahzad*, **2025 FC 1999** (2025-12-18, IMM-16187-24) (CanLII) | Upheld | OWP to join wife: insufficient incentive to leave Kuwait given strong family ties in Canada | Address ties to country of residence, not only citizenship |

### (f) Maintained status (R183(5)) and in-Canada extensions
| Case | Result | Holding | Practice lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Bolaji*, **2026 FC 59** (2026-01-15, IMM-1897-25) | Upheld | LMIA WP extension (Direct Support Professional, NOC 42201) refused: education and experience didn't meet NOC requirements; earlier Canadian work was under a permit coded for another occupation. Applicant responsible for knowing and correcting permit errors | Check every permit for errors on receipt; fix through IRCC before relying on the work |
| *Ihenyen*, **2026 FC 1143** | Upheld | See (c): status must last to the decision | Track expiry against pending PR and WP files |
| *Kaur*, **2026 FC 173** | Upheld | Student file: maintained status after a PGWP; can't restore as a student | Cross-ref |

- **No verifiable 2024-2026 decision squarely on R183(5)** (e.g., whether a knowingly non-qualifying extension creates maintained status or R186(u) work authorization). Secondary commentary (settler.ca) says *Kaur* 2026 FC 173 did not decide it. Treat as open.

### Themes
- **R182 is strict liability on time:** *Dhir*, *Singh* 828, *Egwuatu*. Only route past day 90 is TRP, or rarely incompetence of counsel (*Tanisha*).
- **Restore the last status first** (*Kohli*, *Kaur* 173).
- **Onus and record:** missing, unproven or illegible uploads lose (*Salkhan*, *Khanhosseinpoor*, *Majid*, *Beygmohammadi*). Officers who ignore filed employment proof lose (*Singh* 544, *Kaur* 1123).
- **TRP standard is contested** (*Howlader* vs *Jasmeet Kaur*).
- A win sends the file to a new officer; it doesn't grant status.

## Escalation
- **JR = refer to a licensed lawyer.** RCICs can't appear in Federal Court. Rep-incompetence allegations (*Tanisha*) need the Court's protocol and are lawyer-only.
- Leave required. Application for leave and JR: **15 days** (in-Canada decision: restoration, extension, in-Canada spouse OWP, TRP) or **60 days** (decision outside Canada, e.g. spouse OWP from abroad) from notification (IRPA s.72(2)(b)). (H)
- **JR doesn't restore status or authorize work.** The R182 90-day clock keeps running during JR. Plan restoration/TRP/departure in parallel with the lawyer.
- Order GCMS notes at once (see `knowledge/09-procedure-litigation/atip-gcms-notes.md`).

## Reapply vs restore vs TRP vs JR
| Situation | First choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Extension/OWP refused, within 90 days of losing status, evidence gap | **Restore** (same last-held status) + new permit application, complete package | Only in-Canada route; one shot (*Singh* 828) |
| Refused for missing/illegible/unproven document | **Reapply/restore** with the document | JR record can't be supplemented; court sides with officer (*Salkhan*, *Majid*) |
| Officer ignored evidence on file, no reasons, ignored a restoration request | **JR (lawyer)**, plus restoration filed in parallel if within 90 days | *Singh* 544, *Kaur* 1123, *Peligrino*, *Chamah* |
| Past day 90 | **TRP** (s.24) with need-and-risk evidence, or leave and apply from abroad | No discretion on restoration (*Dhir*, *Egwuatu*); show why leaving is impractical (*Singh* 1520) |
| Lost status because of the rep | Lawyer: JR on incompetence (high bar) and/or TRP citing the misconduct | *Tanisha*, *Yarimdag* vs *Singh* 352 |
| Outside Canada, spouse OWP refused on funds/ties | **Reapply** with stronger evidence | 60-day JR rarely beats a reasoned insufficiency finding (*Baring*, *Noori*) |

- Don't reapply and JR the same decision without the lawyer's advice; a new decision can moot the JR.
- Check `knowledge/14-policy-tracker/` before relying on any public policy or spouse-OWP category.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-2026: consistent line that the R182 90-day limit can't be extended (*Egwuatu*, *Singh* 828, *Dhir*) (M)
- 2026: worker-spouse OWP refusals set aside where filed employment proof was ignored (*Singh* 544, *Kaur* 1123) (M)
- 2026: first reported decision applying the 2025-01-21 spouse-OWP cutoff (*Khanam*) (L)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Never promise restoration after day 90; never treat a refusal letter's "eligible to restore" as a deadline extension (*Dhir*).
- Flag misrepresentation (IRPA s.40) risk on employment letters and funds documents; undisclosed prior refusals sink OWPs.
- Ghost-consultant and rep-failure files: document the timeline; refer incompetence claims to a lawyer.
