# Medical, security, financial and other grounds

## Must cover
- Excessive demand, ss.34/35/37, ministerial relief
- Excessive demand threshold for current year (gap-pass-01)

## Current rules
### Medical (s.38)
- Inadmissible if the condition is likely to be (a) a danger to **public health**, (b) a danger to **public safety**, or (c) **excessive demand** on health or social services.
- **2026 excessive demand threshold: $144,390 over 5 years ($28,878/yr).**
- **Exempt from excessive demand** (s.38(2)): sponsored **spouses, partners and dependent children**; Convention refugees and protected persons; their family members. Never exempt from public health or safety.
- **PFL first**; **90 days** to respond. The officer must consider the **mitigation plan** and the ability and intent to pay (IRPR s.20).

### Security, rights, organized crime
- **s.34**: espionage, subversion, terrorism, danger to security, violence, membership.
- **s.35**: war crimes/crimes against humanity; senior officials of designated regimes; s.35.1 sanctions.
- **s.37**: organized criminality, people smuggling, trafficking, laundering.
- **Ministerial relief (s.42.1)** for ss.34, 35(1)(b), 37: "not contrary to the national interest" (national security and public safety only).
- No IAD appeal for these grounds (s.64).

### Financial (s.39)
- Unable or unwilling to support self and dependants without social assistance. Protected persons exempt (IRPR s.21).

### Non-compliance (s.41) and family (s.42)
- s.41: any contravention (FN), or s.27(2)/s.28 breaches (PR).
- s.42: inadmissible family member. For TRs, only if the family member is inadmissible under ss.34, 35, 35.1, 37.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026: excessive demand threshold updated to $144,390/5 yrs (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Excessive demand: build a **costed mitigation plan** (private insurance, declaration of ability, community supports). Social services that can't be waived (e.g. special education) need specific planning.
- Security and s.35 cases are complex. Refer to counsel; expect long security screening.
- Undisclosed military or police service can trigger s.34/35 concerns. Disclose and explain fully.

## Common refusal reasons
- Mitigation plan inadequate; condition costs over threshold; membership in an organization (s.34(1)(f)) found on low evidentiary threshold ("reasonable grounds to believe", s.33)

## Re-verify before advising
- Excessive demand threshold (updated annually)
