# Criminality, rehabilitation, ARC

## Must cover
- s.36, equivalency, deemed/individual rehabilitation, record suspension, TRP, ARC
- Criminal equivalency method and worked examples - lawyer referral flag (gap-pass-01)

## Current rules
### IRPA s.36
| Ground | Test |
|---|---|
| **Serious criminality** (PR + FN), s.36(1)(a) | Canadian conviction with **max 10+ yrs**, or **more than 6 months imposed** |
| s.36(1)(b)/(c) | Foreign conviction or act that would carry a max of 10+ yrs in Canada |
| **Criminality** (FN only), s.36(2)(a) | Canadian indictable conviction, or **2 offences** not from a single occurrence |
| s.36(2)(b)-(d) | Foreign equivalents; offence committed on entry |
- **Hybrid offences are deemed indictable** (s.36(3)(a)), even if prosecuted summarily.
- Not inadmissible: a record suspension granted and not revoked; acquittal (s.36(3)(b)); contraventions; youth offences (s.36(3)(e)).
- **Impaired driving**: maximum raised to **10 yrs** (Criminal Code, 2018-12-18), so it is now **serious criminality** (M: not re-read this pass).

### Equivalency (foreign offences)
- Compare the foreign offence's **essential elements** with the Canadian offence in force at the time of the **offence** (or most favourable). Then use the Canadian maximum to classify. **Criminal equivalency: refer to a lawyer** (CLAUDE.md rule 4).

### Rehabilitation
- **Deemed** (IRPR s.18(2)), foreign convictions only:
  - (a) **one** offence with Canadian max **under 10 yrs**: **10 yrs** after the sentence was completed, and no other convictions (Canadian indictable; summary in 10 yrs; other foreign)
  - (b) two or more foreign **summary** offences: **5 yrs** after sentences
  - (c) one act not convicted: 10 yrs
  - Not available for serious criminality.
- **Individual** (s.36(3)(c); IRPR s.17): **5 yrs** after the end of the sentence (incl. probation). Application to IRCC. Fee **$246.25** (criminality) / **$1,231** (serious criminality) since 2025-12-01. Processing can exceed 1 yr.
- **Record suspension** (Canada): ends inadmissibility. **Foreign pardons**: case-by-case (visa office assesses).
- **TRP** (IRPA s.24): interim entry where rehabilitation is not yet available (see 05-temporary-residence/trp-restoration-maintained-status.md).

### ARC (IRPR ss.224-226)
- **Departure order**: no ARC if the person leaves within **30 days** of the order becoming enforceable; otherwise it becomes a deportation order.
- **Exclusion order**: ARC needed for **1 yr** (s.225(1)-(2)); **5 yrs** if for misrepresentation (s.225(3)).
- **Deportation order**: ARC needed **permanently** (s.226(1)).
- Fee **$492.50** (since 2025-12-01). Repay removal costs if CBSA paid (escorted air $13,098.96; other $3,905.28).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-12-01: ARC $492.50; rehabilitation $246.25 / $1,231 (H)
- 2026-04-01: removal cost repayment indexed (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Ask about **all** charges, convictions, discharges, DUIs and police cautions. Non-disclosure is misrepresentation (s.40) plus an AMP.
- US DUI convictions are a common serious criminality issue. Check dates for deemed rehab (not available) vs individual rehab.
- Count rehabilitation periods from **sentence completion** (incl. fines paid, probation ended, licence suspension).
- All criminal equivalency questions, and serious criminality with appeal implications: **lawyer referral**.

## Common refusal reasons
- Rehabilitation not demonstrated (insufficient evidence of reform, recent offences); equivalency determined unfavourably; undisclosed records
