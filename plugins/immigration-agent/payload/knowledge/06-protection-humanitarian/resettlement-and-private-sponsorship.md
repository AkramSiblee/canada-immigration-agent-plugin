# Resettlement and private sponsorship

## Must cover
- GAR, BVOR, SAH, Group of Five intake status
- Intake caps and status for 2026-2027 (gap-pass-01)

## Current rules
| Program | Status (2026-11-07) |
|---|---|
| Government-Assisted Refugees (GAR) | Active (UNHCR/referral only; no direct application) |
| Sponsorship Agreement Holders (SAH) | Sponsoring continues within allocations. **New SAH applications closed; "There won't be a 2026 application intake."** |
| **Groups of Five** | **Paused until 2026-12-31** (since 2024-11-29) |
| **Community Sponsors** | **Paused until 2026-12-31** |
| BVOR | Active at token level: **2026 target 50** (Levels 2026-2028) (H) |
| Economic Mobility Pathways Pilot (EMPP) | **Closed** (refugees page) |
- 2026 SAH allocation caps: not published on pages read.
- **Public policy for refugees who lost their SAH sponsor** (in effect 2026-05-07; **expires 24 months later**, may be revoked). Principal applicant whose sponsor's agreement was cancelled, who received a PFL asking them to find a new sponsor, within the PFL period. Waives the refugee status document so a Group of Five or Community Sponsor can sponsor them (IRPR 153(1)(b)). All other requirements apply. Note the G5 and CS pause above: confirm how IRCC is accepting these files.

### Levels 2027 (each of 2027-2029)
- GAR **11,750** (10,000-16,000; 8,500-14,500 in 2028-29)
- PSR **17,550** (14,500-20,500)
- Protected persons in Canada 20,000. Total refugees 49,300.

### Who can be resettled (IRCC 2025-06-26)
- **Must be referred** by UNHCR, a designated referral organization or a private sponsor. No direct application.
- **Convention refugee abroad class** or **Country of asylum class** (seriously affected by civil war or armed conflict, or denied basic human rights on an ongoing basis).

### Programs
| Program | Key rules |
|---|---|
| **GAR** | Referral only; support up to 1 year or until self-supporting. Human rights defenders stream: up to 250 people a year, referral only |
| **BVOR** (open to Groups of Five and Community Sponsors during the pause) | Refugee already approved. Sponsor gives 6 months' financial and 12 months' settlement support; Canada gives the other 6 months' financial |
| **Group of Five** (new applications paused) | Each member citizen or PR, 18+, living in the community; settlement plan; funds for usually 1 year; refugee status document needed for sponsor-referred cases. The 160-space Sudan initiative is closed |
| **Community Sponsor** (new applications paused) | Organization, association or corporation in the community; need not be incorporated; usually 1 year; co-sponsors possible |

### Money
- **Resettlement Assistance Program:** for resettled refugees (mainly GARs); arrival, temporary housing, household items, income support up to 1 year. **Not for people who claimed inside Canada.**
- **Immigration loans:** for refugees selected for resettlement; travel and some settlement costs. **Repayment starts after 12 months in Canada.** Can ask IRCC to review if unable to pay.
- SOGIESC refugees are generally referred by UNHCR, Rainbow Railroad or others, or privately sponsored (IRCC 2025-03-27).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-11-21: G5/CS pause extended to 2026-12-31 (H)
- 2026: no new SAH intake; EMPP closed (H)
- 2026-05-07: subsequent public policy on refugees previously sponsored by an SAH (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Don't prepare G5/CS sponsorships for filing before IRCC announces the post-2026 status.
- PSR files are slow and allocation-limited. Advise sponsors on the multi-year timeline.

## Common refusal reasons
- Refugee not meeting Convention/Country of Asylum class; sponsor ineligible or settlement plan inadequate

## Re-verify before advising
- G5/CS pause after 2026-12-31; 2027 SAH allocations; BVOR status
