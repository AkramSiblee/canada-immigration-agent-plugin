# STCA and PRRA

## Must cover
- Exceptions, 14-day clock, PRRA access and bars

## Current rules
### STCA (IRPR ss.159.1-159.7; US = designated country)
- Applies at land ports of entry, and between ports where the claim is made **under 14 days** after entry (s.159.4(1.1); Additional Protocol, SOR/2023-58). Airports and harbour ports are not covered (s.159.4(1)(b)-(c)).
- After 14+ days between ports, the claim is ineligible under s.101(1)(b.2) (C-12).
- **Exceptions (s.159.5):**
  - (a) family member in Canada who is a citizen
  - (b) family member who is a PR, protected person, or has a stayed removal order
  - (c) adult family member with a referred claim
  - (d) adult family member holding a work or study permit
  - (e) unaccompanied minors
  - (f) holders of a Canadian visa, TRP or travel document
  - (g) visa-exempt in Canada but would need a visa for the US
  - (h) re-entries after US refusal without adjudication
- s.159.6: death penalty exception.

### PRRA
- **Only if CBSA says the person is eligible.**
- **Barred:** STCA-ineligible claimants; Convention refugees of another country they can return to; protected persons; extradition subjects.
- **12-month bar** after the last negative decision (RPD/RAD, PRRA, abandonment/withdrawal, FC refusal). Country exemptions apply only to negative decisions within listed windows. **All listed windows have expired as of 2026-11-07**, incl. Iran (to 2025-12-19), Uganda (to 2026-06-19) and Israel/West Bank/Gaza (to 2026-09-29).
- **C-12-ineligible claimants** (1-yr, 14-day bars) are **not** expressly excluded from PRRA. Eligibility is confirmed at the CBSA removal interview.
- Deadline: **15 days** (in person) / **22 days** (by mail). No fee stated.
- No removal while PRRA is pending in time, until refused, withdrawn or abandoned (IRCC).

### PRRA: steps and hearings (IRCC pages 2026-09-23, 2026-04-14, 2025-09-05)
- CBSA gives the form and guide. **Form IMM 5508**; each family member in Canada 18+ applying completes their own. Identity and relationship documents, with translations stamped by a certified translator. Untranslated documents are not considered.
- **After an earlier refused claim or PRRA:** only new evidence (arose after the rejection, inaccessible, or not reasonably available then). Identify and explain it clearly.
- **Considered:** persecution, torture, risk to life or of cruel and unusual treatment. **If inadmissible on serious grounds:** no persecution assessment, and only torture and risk to life or treatment.
- **Hearing** may be held if credibility is key to the decision, or the person's only ineligibility is having claimed in a country with an information-sharing arrangement. **All remote on Microsoft Teams.** Paid representative must be a registered consultant, a lawyer in good standing, or a Quebec notary. Observers on request. No witnesses unless the officer needs them. Interpreter by form.
- **Leaving Canada during the process:** application abandoned and rejected.
- **Work:** first PRRA, valid permit continues to its expiry or decision. Others: check IRCC page.
- **Decision given in person by CBSA.** Accepted: usually protected person, then apply for PR. Serious inadmissibility: may stay until circumstances change, with no protected person status.
- **Rejected:** must leave. Federal Court review does not stop removal without a court stay (lawyer). IFHP continues until departure.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-12-19 / 2026-06-19 / 2026-09-29: PRRA bar exemption windows for Iran, Uganda, Israel/Palestine ended (H)
- 2026-11-03: C-12 bars feed many claimants into PRRA-only (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- The PRRA deadline is short and strict. Prepare risk evidence in advance for any ineligible claimant.
- PRRA for C-12 ineligibles: watch for any regulatory limit on "restricted PRRA" scope (s.112(3) and s.113 types) (M).
- Stays of removal at Federal Court go to a lawyer.

## Common refusal reasons
- Insufficient new evidence of risk; generalized risk; state protection/IFA available
