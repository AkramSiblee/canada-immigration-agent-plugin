# Victims of family violence, human trafficking, and abused workers

Three separate, fee-exempt tools. Pick by **who is abusing** and **what status the client holds**.

| Situation | Tool | Processed by |
|---|---|---|
| Abused by spouse/partner; status or PR depends on staying in the relationship | **Family violence TRP (FMV-TRP)** + fee-exempt OWP/study permit | IRCC Vulnerable Persons Unit (VPU) |
| Trafficked (labour or sexual exploitation) | **Victims of trafficking TRP (VTIP-TRP)** + fee-exempt OWP/study permit | VPU |
| On an employer-specific work permit, abused at work | **Open work permit for vulnerable workers (OWP-V)**, IRPR 207.1 | IRCC (online) |
| PR route lost because the relationship ended | **H&C** (`humanitarian-compassionate.md`) | IRCC |
| Fear of return to home country | Refugee claim (`refugee-claims-inland.md`) | IRB |

## 1. Family violence TRP (PDI page 2025-04-04) (H)
- In force since 2019-07-26 (IRCC news 2019-05).
- **Who:** in Canada; experiencing abuse from a **spouse or common-law partner**; status or PR **contingent on the relationship** (mainly family-class or spouse-in-Canada class applicants; also those without status because the abuser did not renew/sponsor). Dependent children in Canada without status are also eligible.
- **Not for** people who already have status through another program ("the officer should not issue a TRP"). Abuse by an employer or a spouse of a worker is not this tool - see OWP-V or a regular spousal OWP.
- **Abuse** = physical, sexual, psychological or financial abuse, or neglect.
- **Evidence (examples):** police reports, restraining/peace bond orders, shelter or counsellor letters, medical records, photos of injuries, evidence of withheld passport/documents, client's own statement.
- **Validity:** officer "may issue a TRP for a minimum of 12 months". Subsequent TRPs possible.
- **Fees exempt:** TRP (any length), work/study permit, biometrics (code B12); open work permit privilege fee not charged.
- **Work/study:** a TRP valid 180+ days -> eligible for an open work permit and study permit; fee-exempt permits "should be offered".
- **Health:** **IFHP** for the TRP's duration if no provincial/private cover (basic, supplemental incl. mental health counselling, prescription drugs).
- **Processing:** priority, by the **Vulnerable Persons Unit** (not CPC-Edmonton). Mail: IRCC Vulnerable Persons Unit, 5343 Dundas Street West, Suite 105, Toronto ON M9B 6K5. Representatives: mark "FV" (Guide 5554).
- **To PR:** typical routes are H&C, the permit-holder class (IRPR 64-65; continuous TRP holding for a set period - check timing before advising), or an economic class once working (M).

## 2. Victims of human trafficking TRP (PDI page 2025-04-04) (H)
- **Test:** recruitment, transport or transfer was "fraudulent, deceptive or coerced", plus restriction of freedom or coercion into exploitation (labour, sexual).
- **No law-enforcement report needed:** "The absence of a law enforcement endorsement or report is not a valid reason to refuse." Client need not testify.
- **Initial TRP:** up to 12 months after a preliminary assessment; at least 12 months after an in-depth assessment.
- **Subsequent TRP:** up to **3 years**.
- **Fees exempt** (public policy): initial and subsequent TRPs, OWP, study permit, biometrics.
- **Work/study:** TRP valid 180+ days -> OWP and study permit.
- **IFHP** for the TRP duration if not otherwise covered.
- **Dependants** in Canada without status: TRP, fee-exempt OWP and study permit, IFHP.
- Clients may self-identify to IRCC. Processed as a priority by the VPU.
- Outside Canada: not available; refer to support NGOs and the Canadian Human Trafficking Hotline 1-833-900-1010 (M).

## 3. Open work permit for vulnerable workers (OWP-V) (H)
- **Who:** holds a **valid employer-specific work permit** (or applied to renew/maintained status) and is being abused or at risk of abuse **in relation to the job** in Canada. Includes TFWP and IMP employer-specific permits.
- **Abuse** (defined in regulations): physical, sexual, psychological, financial, and reprisals.
- **Evidence:** at minimum a description of the abuse/risk; can add sworn statement, complaint to ESDC/provincial employment standards/police, support-organization letter, medical records.
- **Fees:** none (processing and OWP holder fee).
- **Length:** typically **1 year**; generally non-renewable. Use the year to find a new employer-specific permit or PR route.
- **Family members in Canada** can apply for permits once the principal is issued an OWP-V.
- Apply online (IRCC account); IRCC may interview. The employer is not told by IRCC that the worker applied (M).
- Also report to ESDC (TFWP tip line) -> may trigger an employer inspection (`05-temporary-residence/employer-compliance-inspections.md`).

## Consultant pitfalls
- Safety first: give shelter and police contacts before immigration strategy. Never contact the abuser.
- Spousal sponsorship withdrawn by an abusive sponsor -> do not wait; TRP plus H&C, or keep the spouse-in-Canada file if the sponsor has not withdrawn.
- Abuse by a spouse who is a **work permit holder** (client is on a spousal OWP): FMV-TRP is not designed for this; consider maintaining own status, a new permit, OWP-V only if employer-specific, H&C. Check for any new public policy before advising (M).
- Do not draft the client's narrative/declaration for them; help them organise their own account and evidence.
- Flag misrepresentation risk if earlier applications hid the relationship breakdown (A40).
