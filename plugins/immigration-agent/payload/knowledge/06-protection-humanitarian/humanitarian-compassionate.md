# Humanitarian and compassionate (IRPA s.25)

## Must cover
- A25 factors, Kanthasamy, bars, evidence, forms, stages, removal, work permit, Quebec, family members, 25.1/25.2

## Legal framework (IRPA, last amended 2026-03-26; read 2026-11-19) (H)
- **s.25(1):** in Canada the Minister *must* examine a request; outside Canada (PR visa applicant) the Minister *may*. Grants PR or an exemption from any IRPA criterion/obligation if justified by H&C considerations, taking into account the **best interests of a child directly affected**.
- **Cannot overcome** inadmissibility under **s.34 (security), 35 (human/international rights violations), 35.1 (sanctions), 37 (organized criminality)**. Applies to s.25 and s.25.1. Other inadmissibility (criminality, health, financial, misrepresentation) can be exempted, "only in exceptional circumstances" (Guide 5291).
- **s.25(1.1):** the Minister is seized only once fees are paid.
- **s.25(1.2) bars - the Minister may not examine the request if:**
  - (a) an H&C request is already pending
  - (b) a refugee claim is pending before the RPD or RAD
  - (b.1) a claim was found ineligible and a protection application (PRRA) is pending
  - (c) **less than 12 months** since the RPD rejection/withdrawal or the last of the RPD/RAD/Federal Court decisions. Guide 5291: claims withdrawn **before** the IRB hearing are not caught.
- **s.25(1.21) exceptions to the 12-month bar:** risk to life from the country's inability to provide adequate health/medical care; or adverse effect on the best interests of a child directly affected ("sufficient credible, and objective evidence" - Guide 5291).
- **Designated foreign nationals (s.25(1.01)-(1.03)):** no request for 5 years (from final determination or designation); a pending request is suspended if designated after applying.
- **s.25(1.3):** s.96/97 risk factors are **not** assessed, but the Minister **must** consider hardship elements (the same facts can be argued as hardship).
- **s.25.1:** Minister's own initiative (same s.34/35/35.1/37 carve-out; fee can be waived).
- **s.25.2:** public policies for groups (special measures). No s.34-37 carve-out in the text; conditions may be imposed (e.g. undertakings, third-party eligibility). See `special-measures-and-public-policies.md`.

## Kanthasamy v Canada, 2015 SCC 61 (H)
- Global assessment: do the circumstances "excite in a reasonable and fair-minded person a desire to relieve the misfortunes of another".
- "Unusual and undeserved or disproportionate hardship" is descriptive, not a threshold test.
- BIOC must be "well identified and defined" and examined "with a great deal of attention".

## Who applies on Guide 5291 (in-Canada H&C) (Guide 5291, modified 2026-10-22) (H)
- Foreign nationals living in Canada who need an exemption to apply for PR from within Canada and are **not eligible under another in-Canada class**.
- **Do not use** if eligible as: spouse/common-law in Canada class (use IMM 5289), economic class, protected person, TRP-holder class.
- **Cannot apply:** citizens/PRs; anyone with an H&C application undecided; anyone with an outstanding refugee claim; DFN within 5 years.
- Outside Canada: no stand-alone H&C form. Ask for H&C in a written submission inside a PR application (e.g. family class, excluded-relative cases) (M).

## Forms and submission (Guide 5291) (H)
- **PR Online Application Portal** (main method). Paper/braille/large print only as an accommodation. Incomplete applications are returned.
- Principal applicant and each family member 18+: **IMM 0008**, **IMM 5669** (Schedule A), **IMM 5406**.
- PDF uploads (no wet signature): **IMM 5280** (checklist), **IMM 5283** (Supplementary Information - H&C).
- If applicable (printed, hand-signed): IMM 5409 (common-law), IMM 5604 (minors). Optional: IMM 5475, **IMM 5476** (representative).
- **State the specific exemption(s) requested** and the reasons. IRCC only considers the exemptions asked for.
- Biometrics required. Police certificates for each country (other than Canada) with 6+ consecutive months after age 18. IME after IRCC asks, not before.
- Translations: English/French + translator affidavit unless a Canadian certified translator; never by the applicant or family.
- Updates (marriage, birth, convictions, new employer): notify immediately via the IRCC Web form. Decisions are made on the file as it stands.

## Fees (fee list; since 2026-04-30) (H)
- Principal $660 + RPRF $600 = **$1,260**; spouse/partner $1,260; dependent child $180 (no RPRF).
- RPRF loans possible; processing fee not. Pay online.

## Process: two stages (H unless marked)
1. **Stage 1 - approval in principle (AIP):** officer decides whether H&C grounds justify the exemption(s) requested.
2. **Stage 2:** admissibility (medical, criminality, security) and any remaining eligibility; then PR.
- **Removal:** applying does **not** stop removal ("You must leave on the specified removal date" - Guide 5291). No stay before stage 1 (2020 PACP briefing).
- **After AIP:** IRPR **s.233** stays the removal order (incl. family members) where the Minister grants an H&C exemption, until the PR decision. IRCC treats a stage 1 approval as triggering the regulatory stay (PACP 2020) (M for practice; text H).
- **Work permit:** IRPR **s.207(d)** - open work permit for a PR applicant granted an s.25(1)/25.1(1)/25.2(1) exemption; **s.207(e)** family members. Apply in Canada (R199(f)); a s.207 permit does not by itself confer temporary resident status (R202) (H). IRCC usually invites the AIP client to apply (M).
- **Quebec:** after AIP, file goes to MIFI. MIFI emails the CSQ invitation, form and document list. CSQ valid 24 months, or until IRCC decides if PR is in process.
- **Family:** spouse/partner and dependent children **in Canada** processed concurrently. Family **abroad** are not included but **must be declared and examined**. An unexamined family member can never be sponsored later (R117(9)(d)).
- **Sponsorship undertakings:** no longer assessed as a requirement in in-Canada H&C; an existing undertaking may be a factor.
- No appeal. Refusal -> Federal Court JR (15 days in Canada; lawyer).

## Factors IRCC lists (Guide 5291) and evidence
- **Establishment:** time in Canada, work, tax filing, savings, property, studies, volunteering, community ties; and whether inability to leave caused the establishment.
- **Family ties in Canada** and effect of separation.
- **BIOC:** age, establishment, country conditions, medical needs, education, gender. Address each affected child (incl. Canadian-born children and children abroad).
- **Hardship in home country:** health care access, family violence, discrimination, country conditions; whether it is countrywide; whether help from authorities/NGOs was sought (explain if not).
- **Health** and medical care availability.
- Negative factors: immigration history (overstay, unauthorized work), misrepresentation, criminality.
- Cost and inconvenience of applying abroad are "not sufficient factors" on their own.
- Evidence: NOAs, T4s, pay stubs, leases, school records, medical reports with treatment availability, country reports, specific (not form) support letters, Canadian-child evidence.

## Consultant pitfalls
- Long, low-volume route. Pair with status maintenance (visitor record, work permit) where possible.
- Do not file while a refugee claim is pending (barred). Withdrawal **before** the IRB hearing avoids the 12-month bar.
- C-12-ineligible claimants: H&C possible once no claim/PRRA is pending; check (b.1) and the 12-month bar.
- If another class fits (spouse in Canada, economic), use it - IRCC can return the application.
- Name each exemption sought (e.g. "A11 visa requirement", "R72(1)(a) in-Canada", inadmissibility under A36(1)/A40).
- Removal date pending -> H&C does not stop it. Refer for a CBSA deferral request and, if needed, a Federal Court stay (lawyer). See `09-procedure-litigation/removals-cbsa-deferrals.md`.
- Never write or rewrite the client's personal narrative for them; draft submissions from their own evidence (CLAUDE.md rule 7 spirit).

## Common refusal reasons
- Establishment "normal" for the time spent; BIOC not developed or generic; hardship speculative or not personal; risk recycled without hardship framing; negative immigration history; misrepresentation; specific exemption not requested.
