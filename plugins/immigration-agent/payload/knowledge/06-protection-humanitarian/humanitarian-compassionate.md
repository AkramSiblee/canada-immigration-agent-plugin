# Humanitarian and compassionate (IRPA s.25)

## Must cover
- A25 factors, Kanthasamy, bars, evidence

## Current rules
- Exemption from any IRPA requirement on H&C grounds. The Minister is seized only once fees are paid (s.25(1.1)).
- **Factors (IRCC):** establishment in Canada; family ties; **best interests of any child** directly affected; hardship if refused. Risk factors under ss.96-97 are **not** assessed (s.25(1.3)), but the underlying facts can show hardship.
- **Kanthasamy v Canada, 2015 SCC 61:** a global assessment of whether the circumstances "excite in a reasonable and fair-minded person a desire to relieve the misfortunes of another". "Unusual and undeserved or disproportionate hardship" is descriptive, not a threshold. BIOC must be "well identified and defined".
- **Bars:**
  - **12 months** after a negative IRB decision (incl. abandonment/withdrawal). Exceptions: a child under 18 adversely affected; life-threatening medical condition untreatable in home country.
  - **No H&C while a refugee claim is pending.** Withdraw before the IRB hearing.
  - Designated foreign nationals: 5-yr bar.
- Applying does **not** stop removal. No appeal; JR only (lawyer).
- **Fees:** $660 + RPRF $600 = **$1,260** principal; spouse $1,260; child $180 (since 2026-04-30; fee list).
- **Levels:** "H&C and other" 5,000/yr 2027-2029 (4,500-8,000). Includes public policies and the permit holder class.
- **2027 measure:** in-Canada H&C applicants **approved in principle** are in the humanitarian cohort (up to 100,000, outside Levels).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026-04-30: H&C fee $635 rose to $660 (H)
- 2026-11-05: H&C AIP cases in 2027 time-limited measure (H)
- H&C admissions target 5,000/yr (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- H&C is a long, low-volume route (5,000/yr). Set expectations and pair it with status maintenance.
- C-12-ineligible claimants: H&C is possible (no pending claim), but the 12-month bar may apply if any IRB decision exists.
- Undeclared family (117(9)(d)) cases: H&C is the main remedy now that the public policy has ended (2026-09-10).
- Evidence: tax returns, employment, community letters, school records, medical reports, country evidence on hardship. Avoid generic letters.

## Common refusal reasons
- Establishment "normal" for time spent; BIOC not developed; hardship speculative; negative immigration history (overstay, misrepresentation) weighed against
