# Special measures and public policies

## Must cover
- Ukraine, Gaza, Sudan, Hong Kong, Afghan: status and end dates

## Current rules
| Situation | Status (2026-11-07) | Key date |
|---|---|---|
| **Ukraine** | CUAET ended 2023-07-15. OWP extensions for public-policy OWP holders: **apply by 2027-03-31**. PR family pathway **closed 2024-10-22** (processing continues; in 2027 humanitarian cohort). Measures changed 2026-04-01 | 2027-03-31 |
| **Gaza / Palestine** | Gaza family special measures **closed** to new applicants. TR measures for arrivals on or before 2027-09-30 (public policy 2026-08-24) | 2027-09-30 |
| **Sudan** | Family PR pathway **closed**. Work permit renewals for prior public-policy holders (updated 2026-10-28). 23,170 PR approvals (to 2026-08-31) | — |
| **Haiti** | Work permit renewal measure **extended to 2027-12-30** (updated 2026-10-28) | 2027-12-30 |
| **Iran** | From 2026-03-01: measures only for **work permit holders** (permit issued by 2025-02-28); apply by **2027-03-31**. Visitors, students and TRP holders excluded | 2027-03-31 |
| **Afghanistan** | **Not accepting new applications**; existing files prioritized | — |
| **Hong Kong** | PR pathways **closed 2026-08-31**. OWP policy remains to May 2029 | 2029-05 |
| **Ebola** (DRC, South Sudan, Uganda) | In force **2026-05-27 to 23:59:59 ET 2026-11-27**. Immigration documents **suspended** for foreign nationals whose **last country of residence** (not citizenship) is an affected country; applications not finalized; travel restricted. Affected people may request an exemption. Page updated 2026-09-28 | **2026-11-27** |

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026-03-01: Iran measures narrowed to work permit holders (H)
- 2026-04-01: Ukraine measures changed (H)
- 2026-08-31: Hong Kong PR pathways closed (H)
- 2026-10-28: Haiti extended to 2027-12-30; Sudan update (H; baseline "Oct 27" is the notice date)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Most crisis PR pathways are closed. Only renewals or extensions remain. Check eligibility dates (permit issue dates, arrival dates).
- The Iran measure requires the work permit to have been issued by **2025-02-28**.
- Remaining Ukraine and Hong Kong PR applications are in the 2027 humanitarian cohort. No new filing is needed.

## Common refusal reasons
- Outside eligibility window; prior status not under the qualifying public policy

## Re-verify before advising
- Public policy end dates (Ebola 2026-11-27; Ukraine/Iran 2027-03-31; Haiti 2027-12-30)
- After 2026-11-27: check whether the Ebola measures were extended or ended
