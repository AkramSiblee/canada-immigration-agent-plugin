# Inland and port-of-entry claims (after Bill C-12)

## Must cover
- Bill C-12 bars (1-year, 14-day), Nov 3, 2026 regulations, 60-day deadline, transition

## Current rules
### New ineligibility grounds (IRPA s.101, amended S.C. 2026 c.4)
- **s.101(1)(b.1), one-year bar:** the claimant entered after 2020-06-24 and claimed **more than 1 year after entry**. With re-entries, the clock runs from the first entry (s.101(1.1)). Applies to claims **started after 2025-06-02** (IRCC) (the retroactive reference date).
- **s.101(1)(b.2), 14-day bar:** entry at a land border **between ports**, claim made after the 14-day limit in IRPR s.159.4(1.1). Within 14 days, the STCA applies (s.101(1)(e)), unless an exception applies.
- **Exempt from the new rules:** **unaccompanied minors**, and claims started within 1 yr of first entry (IRCC). STCA exceptions in IRPR s.159.5-159.6 (see stca-prra.md).

### Process (from 2026-11-03; SOR/2026-218)
- **Online claim in the IRCC Portal** (POE claims continue via the CBSA-linked option). The claim becomes official once presented in person to an officer.
- **Complete the claim within 60 days** of the Notice of Application Due Date; one 30-day extension may be requested in the portal before the due date. Otherwise referred to the IRB for **pre-referral abandonment**.
- Minister has up to **365 days** of due diligence before IRB referral (SOR/2026-218, H). Designated representatives for minors and incapable persons. DCO regime repealed.
- Work permit once the claim is eligible for referral (see 05-temporary-residence/open-work-permits.md).

### If ineligible (IRCC)
- STCA-ineligible: returned to the US immediately.
- Others: decision letter + **conditional removal order** + RPID. The removal order becomes enforceable after **7 days** unless stayed. **CBSA says at the removal interview whether a PRRA is available.** The C-12 bars don't by themselves exclude PRRA.
- IFHP health coverage with RPID. Existing work permit valid to expiry; a new one can be requested with RPID. Study permits cancelled once the order is enforceable (new permit possible if PRRA-eligible and applied within 15 days).

### Transition
- Already-submitted claims are processed "as usual". Portal claims started but not submitted before 2026-11-03 must answer new questions.

### Interim Federal Health Program (IRCC page 2026-05-01)
- Limited, temporary coverage until provincial or territorial insurance applies. Coverage depends on the eligibility group. **Use IFHP-registered providers only** (Medavie Blue Cross).
- **Basic coverage: free** (hospital, doctors, nurses, lab, ambulance, pre and postnatal).
- **Prescriptions: $4 co-payment** each.
- **Supplemental: 30% co-payment** (urgent dental, vision, mental health counselling, physiotherapy, assistive devices, home care).
- One immigration medical exam for certain groups. Pre-departure medical services for resettled refugees.
- See also `refugee-legal-tests-and-hearings.md` for the legal tests and hearing preparation.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026-03-26: Bill C-12 Royal Assent (H) - confirmed
- 2026-11-03: regulations in force. 1-year bar and 14-day between-ports bar, retroactive to claims after 2025-06-02/03; 60-day completion deadline (H) - confirmed
- Refugee admissions target 49,300/yr (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- **Retroactive bar and refugee matters: refer to counsel.** Retroactivity challenges, Charter issues and stays are lawyer territory (CLAUDE.md rule 4). RCICs may assist with forms within scope.
- Calculate the **first entry date** carefully. Prior visits count (s.101(1.1)).
- Missing the 60-day completion leads to abandonment proceedings. Calendar it at once.
- Ineligible claimants: advise on PRRA timing (15 days in person / 22 by mail) as soon as CBSA notifies.
- Consider H&C only after checking bars (see humanitarian-compassionate.md).
- Negative RPD decision: RAD appeal 15 days to file / 30 days to perfect from written reasons (IRPR s.159.91; see 09-procedure-litigation/irb-divisions-and-appeals.md) (H). Some claimants have no RAD access (IRPA s.110(2)).

## Common refusal reasons
- Ineligibility (1-yr, 14-day, STCA, prior claim); abandonment (incomplete claim)

## Re-verify before advising
- IRB practice notice on transition (irb-cisr.gc.ca)
- Exact retroactive start (IRCC "after June 2, 2025" vs 2025-06-03)
- SOR/2026-218 time limits (60/30/365)
