### Key 2026 Ministerial Instructions and public policies
- **MI 2026-1 (Express Entry amendment)**, in force 2026-11-03: adds points for high-wage Canadian experience and job offers, removes LMIA-linked job-offer provisions. IRCC maintains the high-wage occupation list on its website (3-year wage data).
- **LMIA exemption for Express Entry job offers** (FSW/FST), a temporary public policy in force 2026-11-03 until revoked.
- **Study without a study permit (2026-2)**, 2026-09-09: work permit holders can study up to 6 months (or until permit expiry) without a study permit, until 2027-12-31. Full-time study still needs a permit.
- **Haiti and Sudan OWP renewals**, 2026-10-28. **Palestinian measures**, 2026-08-24: covers arrivals on or before 2027-09-30.
- **Ukraine OWP extensions**, 2026-04-01; Ukrainian family reunification temporary residence access, 2026-01-16.
- **Iranian nationals temporary measures**, revised 2026-03-04.
- **Manitoba workforce transition bridge** (prospective PNP applicants), 2026-08-05.
- **Quebec PR candidates and spouses work permits** (work-permits-qc-pr-2026-2), 2026-06-05.
- **Francophone Minority Communities Student Pilot**, 2026 updates 2026-06-25.
- **OWP for claimants ineligible for IRB referral**, 2026-03-26 (Bill C-12 context).
- **Unaccompanied minors ineligible-claim exemption** policy (2026-05-19): revoked 2026-11-03.
- **Natural disasters and emergency services personnel** policies, 2026-2028 (2026-04-01).
- **PRRA changes:** Iran (2025-12-19), Uganda (2026-06-19), Israel and Palestine (2026-09-29).
- **Ebola temporary measures** (DRC, South Sudan, Uganda): in force 2026-05-27 to 23:59:59 ET 2026-11-27. Immigration documents suspended and applications not finalized for foreign nationals whose **last country of residence** (not citizenship) is an affected country; exemption requests possible. Re-check on 2026-11-28 (H; see 06-protection-humanitarian/special-measures-and-public-policies.md).

- 2025-12-31: SUV closed to new PR applications except holders of a valid 2025 commitment certificate (H)
- 2026-06-30: SUV final deadline for 2025-commitment holders; SUV fully closed (H)
- 2026-01-01: MI89 - no new PGP applications in 2026; up to 10,000 from the 2025 intake processed (H)
- 2026-07-15: PGP pause confirmed "until further notice"; about 15,000 PGP admissions planned in 2026 (H)
- 2026-08-31: Hong Kong PR pathways closed (H)
- 2026-09-09: work permit holders may study up to 6 months without a study permit, until 2027-12-31 (H)

- 2026-11-03: MI 2026-1 Express Entry rescoring; LMIA exemption public policy (H)
- **Correction to baseline:** the PGP pause started with MI89 for the 2026 calendar year (effective 2026-01-01). The 2026-07-15 notice extends it "until further notice"; it did not create it.

## Consultant pitfalls
- "Until further notice" means no date. Never quote a reopening date for PGP, Self-Employed or Home Care without a new MI or notice.
- The PGP admissions target (15,000 in 2026) is processing of existing inventory, not an intake opening.
- SUV is fully closed: the 2025-commitment exception ended 2026-06-30. No new SUV PR applications are possible; only files received by that date are processed.
- The Groups of Five pause ends 2026-12-31. Check in January 2027 whether it was extended before filing.
- Hong Kong PR closed, but the Hong Kong OWP continues to May 2029. Don't confuse the two.
- The 6-month study exemption is part-time or short only and ends at permit expiry. It does not create PGWP eligibility.
- Super Visa remains open despite the PGP pause.

## Common refusal reasons
- Applications filed under a closed or paused program are returned or rejected as "not received for processing" (MI), with the fee refunded. That is not a merits refusal.
