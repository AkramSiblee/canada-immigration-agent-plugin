# Announced for 2027

## Must cover
- Pending measures, consultations, expected changes

## Current rules

### 1. Time-limited TR-to-PR measure, 2027 (up to 200,000)
- **Outside the Levels targets**, one year (2027). Not a new pathway; no recurring increase. (Measures page)
- **No separate application.** "Applicants do not need to apply separately or take any additional steps."
- **Workers cohort, up to 100,000**: workers in communities outside Quebec, plus their families. Two groups:
  - people waiting on PR under closed programs (Start-Up Visa, Self-Employed, Agri-Food Pilot, home care workers; the backgrounder also names AIP)
  - people eligible for the PNP. Delivered through **increased PNP allocations**; provinces identify the workers.
- **Humanitarian cohort, up to 100,000**:
  - spouses, partners and children of Canadians and PRs already applied (target: spousal processing "closer to 12 months" outside Quebec)
  - remaining Hong Kong PR applications (pathways closed 2026-08-31)
  - remaining Ukraine family reunification applications (closed 2024-10-22)
  - in-Canada H&C applicants approved in principle
- Detailed criteria: none published beyond the above (2026-11-07).

### 2. 2027-2029 Levels Plan (tabled 2026-11-05)
| Category | 2027 target (range) |
|---|---|
| Total PR | 380,000 (352,500-422,000), same for 2028 and 2029 |
| Economic | 244,700 (230,000-270,000) |

| - Federal Business | 1,500 (500-5,000); 2028: 2,725; 2029: 1,000 |
| - Federal economic pilots | 7,900 (5,000-15,450) |
| - Rural Community Immigration | 5,500 (4,000-6,500); 2028: 6,000; 2029: 6,500 |
| - Atlantic Immigration Program | 6,000 (5,000-8,500) |
| - PNP | 95,000 (83,000-106,000) |
| - Quebec | TBD (Quebec's plan due fall 2026; about 28,800 implied by subtraction, not stated) |
| Family | 81,000: spouses, partners, children 66,000; PGP 15,000 (12,000-18,000) |
| Refugees and protected persons | 49,300: protected persons in Canada 20,000; GAR 11,750; PSR 17,550 |
| H&C and other | 5,000 (4,500-8,000) |

- 2028-2029 temporary figures are "notional," to be confirmed by Nov 1 each year. Goal: PR admissions under 1% of the population beyond 2027.

### 3. 2026 one-time initiatives ending 2027
- In-Canada Workers Initiative: 33,000 work permit holders to PR, ends 2027.
- Protected persons initiative: up to 115,000 over two years, ends 2027.

### 4. Other announced, not yet in force
- **Federal entrepreneur pilot**: promised "in 2026" (notice 2025-12-19); not launched as of 2026-11-07.
- **TFWP Low-Wage Stream reforms, proposed for 2027-03-01**, contingent on provinces opting in:
  - CMAs with unemployment of 6% or more: refusal to process continues, except priority sectors
  - CMAs over 250,000 with unemployment under 6%, rural parts of large CMAs, and CMAs of 100,000-250,000: targeted access to COPS occupations plus provincial additions
  - rural communities: full access
  - cap of 15% of workforce on request of a province (now 10%); priority sectors stay at 20%
- **Rural "workforce retention" time-limited measure**: announced as separate from the levels plan; no details.
- **Rural Community Immigration class**: allocations from 2027-01-01; pilot MIs end 2027-06-30.
- **CICC public register** (SOR/2026-68 s.22) in force 2027-04-16.
- **Groups of Five / community sponsor pause** expires 2026-12-31. Watch for an extension.
- **Ontario Workforce Priority Phase 2**: timing unconfirmed (see 02-pnp/ontario.md).
- **PGP**: no reopening announced; 15,000 admissions is inventory processing.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)

- Planned federal entrepreneur pilot (H) - still not launched

## Consultant pitfalls
- Don't tell clients to "apply for the 200,000 measure". There is no application. Keep existing applications complete and current. For the workers cohort, provinces select through PNP allocations.
- Quebec residents are mostly excluded from the workers cohort ("outside Quebec").
- The PNP allocation boost under the workers cohort is extra to the 95,000 target. Watch for provincial notices.
- TFWP March 2027 reforms are proposals contingent on provincial opt-in. Don't advise on them as law.
