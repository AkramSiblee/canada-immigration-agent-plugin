# Announced for 2027

## Must cover

## Current rules

### 1. Time-limited TR-to-PR measure, 2027 (up to 200,000)
- **Outside the Levels targets**, one year (2027). Not a new pathway; no recurring increase. (Measures page)
- **No separate application.** "Applicants do not need to apply separately or take any additional steps."
- **Workers cohort, up to 100,000**: workers in communities outside Quebec, plus their families. Two groups:
  - people waiting on PR under select federal programs: **Atlantic Immigration Program** (open), Start-Up Visa, Self-Employed, Agri-Food Pilot, home care workers (closed). AIP is named in both the 2026-11-05 backgrounder and the 2026-11-12 release (re-read 2026-11-17, H)
  - people eligible for the PNP. Delivered through **increased PNP allocations**; provinces identify the workers.
  - 2026-11-12 release: "up to a combined total of 100,000 workers and their families to permanent residence in 2027"; space is optional; provinces/territories "will be able to choose how much of this additional space they use"; allocations to be used by **2027-03-31**; does not reopen closed pathways or create new programs; "Applicants do not need to do anything or contact IRCC". No per-province figures published. (H)
- **Humanitarian cohort, up to 100,000** (per-group figures: IRCC release 2026-11-10, re-read 2026-11-17, H; the 2026-11-05 backgrounder gives only the 100,000 total):

| Group | 2027 admissions, "up to" | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Hong Kong PR pathways (remaining applications) | **32,000** | closed 2026-08-31 |
| Ukraine Family Reunification Pathway (remaining) | **18,800** | closed 2024-10-22 |
| H&C, people "already found eligible" for PR | **12,600** | release wording; "approved in principle" is the stage-1 term, not IRCC's phrase here |
| Spouses, common-law partners, dependent children of citizens/PRs **living in Canada** | **45,000** | spousal processing target "closer to 12 months" outside Quebec (backgrounder) |

- Humanitarian cohort notes:
  - **Sum = 108,400**, above the 100,000 cohort cap. Figures are "up to" maximums; IRCC gives no reconciliation (H for each figure; the gap is arithmetic).
  - **No client application step:** "Applicants do not need to do anything or contact IRCC" (2026-11-10 release).
- Detailed criteria: none published beyond the above (2026-11-17).

### 3. Budget 2025 one-time initiatives (2026-2027)
- Supplementary levels page (2026-11-05, read 2026-11-17): IRCC will "continue delivering existing one-time initiatives announced in Budget 2025" and "accelerate the transition of up to 115,000 Protected Persons in Canada (PPiC) and their in-Canada dependants and 33,000 work permit holders to permanent residence in 2026 and 2027" (H).
- **In-Canada Workers Initiative** (up to 33,000): IRCC release 2026-05-04: at least 20,000 in 2026, remainder 2027; applicants who already applied under PNP, AIP, community pilots, caregiver pilots or Agri-Food Pilot and lived in smaller communities for 2+ years; no action needed. 3,600 admitted 2026-01-01 to 2026-02-28 (H). URL: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/news/2026/04/filling-labour-gaps-in-smaller-communities-by-accelerating-permanent-residence-for-33000-workers.html
- **Protected persons in Canada** (up to 115,000 plus in-Canada dependants, 2026-2027): source is the supplementary levels page only; no per-year split or separate release found (H for the figure).
- Distinct from the 2027 200,000 time-limited measure (section 1).

## Consultant pitfalls
- Don't tell clients to "apply for the 200,000 measure". There is no application. Keep existing applications complete and current. For the workers cohort, provinces select through PNP allocations.
- Quebec residents are mostly excluded from the workers cohort ("outside Quebec").
- The PNP allocation boost under the workers cohort is extra to the 95,000 target. Watch for provincial notices.
- TFWP March 2027 reforms are proposals contingent on provincial opt-in. Don't advise on them as law.
- SUV/Self-Employed waiting applicants: don't promise PR via the workers cohort. It is province-chosen, optional, one-time and framed for workers already in Canada. No province has published criteria. Never tell a client to submit a fresh SUV or Self-Employed application, and never market the entrepreneur pilot as open.
- Students: don't quote "150,000" as the 2027 study cap. It is the Levels arrivals target; the cap and provincial PAL numbers are not published. Don't tell 2027 applicants a field of study stays PGWP-eligible after 2026; the freeze is for 2026 only.
- Quebec business clients: programs open today, but a PQ moratorium on economic PR from abroad is proposed. Flag the risk before the client pays investment or fees.
