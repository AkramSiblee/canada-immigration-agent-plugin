- Values are rounded to multiples of 5 and approximate.
- FSW + CEC (Sep 2025-Aug 2026): about 76% decided within the 6-month standard; about 50% in 5 months or less.
- Spouses, partners, children: decided in about 15 months (Sep 2025-Aug 2026). The 2027 humanitarian cohort aims for about 12 months outside Quebec.
- PGP inventory (Jan 31, 2026, CIMM briefing): about 48,400 outside Quebec and 12,100 Quebec (H).
- **Note:** IRCC changed format. The page now shows "not yet finalized" and the share "waiting for space under yearly targets", not the old backlog-vs-service-standard split. The old 80% target is not stated on the current page.

## Consultant pitfalls
- "Waiting for space under yearly targets" means a complete file may sit idle until a Levels space opens, especially Family (42%) and Protection (43%). Set client expectations; don't quote service standards as promises.
- Don't compare figures across the two formats. The old backlog % and the new "waiting for space" % measure different things.
