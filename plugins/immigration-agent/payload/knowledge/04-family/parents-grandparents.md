# Parents and Grandparents Program (PGP)

## Must cover
- Pause since 2026-01-01 (MI89), confirmed until further notice 2026-07-15; inventory; MNI+30%; undertaking length
- Reopening signals; admissions target vs intake

## Current rules
- **PAUSED.** MI89: no new PGP applications **from 2026-01-01** "until further instructions". The 2026-07-15 notice adds no new interest-to-sponsor forms and no invitations "until further notice". Super Visa is unaffected.
- MI89 allowed up to **10,000** applications from the 2025 intake to be processed in 2026.
- **Admissions target**: 15,000 per year 2027-2029 (12,000-18,000). This is **processing of existing inventory**, not new intake.
- Inventory: about 48,400 outside Quebec and 12,100 in Quebec (2026-01-31, CIMM briefing) (H).

### Rules (for existing files and any future intake)
- Sponsor income: **MNI + 30% for each of the 3 tax years** before applying (s.133(1)(j)(i)(B)). Quebec has its own test.
- IRCC's latest published table is **for the 2025 intake** (tax years 2022 to 2024; amounts already include the 30%). Read 2026-11-10 (H). No table for a later intake has been published, since intake is paused:

| Family size | 2024 | 2023 | 2022 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | $47,549 | $44,530 | $43,082 |
| 3 | $58,456 | $54,743 | $52,965 |
| 4 | $70,972 | $66,466 | $64,306 |
| 5 | $80,496 | $75,384 | $72,935 |
| 6 | $90,784 | $85,020 | $82,259 |
| 7 | $101,075 | $94,658 | $91,582 |
| each extra | $10,291 | $9,636 | $9,324 |
- Income is the Notice of Assessment total (line 15000); a co-signing spouse or partner's income is added. Family size is counted year by year. Form: IMM 5768 (07-2024).
- Not counted (IMM 5748 (07-2025)): Employment Insurance other than special benefits (2023 and 2024 tax years), provincial training allowances, provincial social assistance, federal resettlement assistance, and the guaranteed income supplement. IMM 5748 is required when the sponsor or co-signer doesn't consent to IRCC getting their tax information from CRA (IMM 5768 questions 8 and 16) (H).
- Undertaking: **20 years** (s.132(1)(b)(iv)). Quebec: 10 years.
- Fee: $1,260 per parent/grandparent ($90 + $570 + $600 RPRF); spouse $1,260; dependent child $180.
- IRCC estimate for files already in process: outside Quebec about 28 months; Quebec about 63 months (2026-10-09) (H).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026-01-01: MI89. No new PGP applications in 2026 (H)
- 2026-07-15: pause confirmed "until further notice" (H)
- 2026-11-05: 15,000/yr admissions target 2027-2029 (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Don't take PGP retainers for a "next intake". There is no date. Offer the Super Visa (see super-visa.md) instead.
- Clients with files already in process: keep CRA proof for all 3 years ready, plus any update requests.
- The 20-year undertaking survives the sponsor's change of circumstances. Explain the repayment liability for social assistance.

## Common refusal reasons
- Income shortfall in any of the 3 years; sponsor bars (s.133); applicant inadmissible

## Re-verify before advising
- PGP reopening date (15,000/yr admissions target is not an intake announcement)
- New MI for 2027
