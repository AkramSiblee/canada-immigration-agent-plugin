# Dependent children and adoption

## Must cover
- Age rules, custody, adoption routes, s.5.1 citizenship

## Current rules
### Dependent child (IRPR s.2) (H)
- A biological child (not adopted by someone other than the parent's spouse/partner) or an adopted child, **and**:
  - **under 22** and not a spouse or common-law partner, **or**
  - **22+**, depended substantially on the parent's financial support since before 22, **and** unable to be self-supporting because of a physical or mental condition
- **Lock-in**: age is locked on the date the application is received. Other requirements (no spouse, dependency) must continue until PR.
  - Exceptions in IRPR s.25.1: provincial nominees lock on the application for nomination to the province (s.25.1(4)); Quebec economic candidates on the application for selection (s.25.1(3)).
- Undertaking: under 22, 10 yrs or to age 25; 22+, 3 yrs.

- **Full-time study is not a dependency route** under the current s.2 definition. IMM 5533 (09-2022 version retrieved) requires a covering page titled "Proof of Dependency for Child 22 or Older". A dependent child of the sponsor is exempt from excessive demand (IRPA 38(2)(a); IRPR 24(1) removes the exemption if not dependent) (read 2026-11-09) (H).

### Custody and consent
- If the other parent is not accompanying: proof of sole custody **or** the non-accompanying parent's consent. The non-accompanying child must still be **examined** (117(9)(d)) (H/M).

### Adoption routes (H for IRPR, M for practice)
- **Adopted abroad, under 18** (s.117(2)-(3)): best interests, genuine parent-child relationship, not for status (s.4(2)), valid under the law of the adoption place, Hague Convention compliance where applicable, provincial approval.
- **Intended adoption in Canada** (s.117(1)(g)): orphan/abandoned child under 18 to be adopted in Canada; province confirms no objection.
- **Adopted 18+**: only if the parent-child relationship existed before 18 (s.117(4)) (M).
- **Citizenship route** (Citizenship Act **s.5.1**, read 2026-11-08) (H):
  - **Minor at adoption (s.5.1(1)):** best interests; genuine parent-child relationship; valid under the laws of the place of adoption and the adopting citizen's country of residence; did not circumvent international adoption rules; not primarily for status.
  - **Adult at adoption (s.5.1(2)):** genuine parent-child relationship before 18 and at adoption, plus the same legal and no-status-purpose tests.
  - **Quebec adoptions (s.5.1(3)):** Quebec authority's written opinion, and not primarily for status.
  - **First-generation limit (s.5.1(4), Bill C-3):** adoptions on or after the 2025 Act's coming into force are barred where the only citizen adoptive parent (or both) was born abroad and citizen by descent or similar grounds, **unless** that parent was physically present in Canada 1,095 days before the adoption. Multigenerational cases go to a lawyer.

### Fees
- Family class dependent child $180 ($90 + $90; no RPRF). Economic dependent child $270.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026-04-30: family-class child fee $175 to $180 ($90 + $90); economic child $260 to $270 (H)
- Bill C-3 (2025-12-15) affects citizenship by descent for children born abroad (see 07-citizenship-pr-status/citizenship-proof-bill-c3.md) (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- A child who marries or enters a common-law relationship before PR loses dependant status.
- For dependants 22+, get medical evidence of the condition **and** proof of continuous financial dependence since before 22.
- Child of a principal applicant is still examined even if not accompanying. Never omit them (117(9)(d)).
- Choose PR vs s.5.1 citizenship for adoptions. A s.5.1 grant needs a citizen adoptive parent at the time of adoption. Check s.5.1(4) if that parent was born abroad.

## Common refusal reasons
- Age or dependency not proven; custody/consent missing; adoption not genuine or not Hague-compliant; DNA mismatch for biological claims
