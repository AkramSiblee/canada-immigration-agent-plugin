# Family appeals at the IAD

## Must cover
- Deadlines, availability, s.64 limits, strategy

## Current rules
### Right of appeal (IRPA s.63(1))
- A sponsor who filed a family class sponsorship can appeal a refusal of the PR visa to the **Immigration Appeal Division**.
- **Not available for the in-Canada spouse class** (only JR).

### No appeal (IRPA s.64)
- s.64(1): inadmissibility on **security, human or international rights violations, sanctions, serious criminality or organized criminality**.
- s.64(2): serious criminality here means a crime **punished in Canada by at least 6 months' imprisonment**, or s.36(1)(b)/(c) offences.
- s.64(3): **misrepresentation** refusals: no appeal **unless the applicant is the sponsor's spouse, common-law partner or child**.
- s.65: H&C relief only if the applicant is a member of the family class and the sponsor is eligible. So no H&C relief for 117(9)(d) excluded persons or ineligible sponsors.

### Grounds and outcome (s.66-67)
- Allowed if wrong in law or fact, or natural justice breached, or H&C (best interests of a child directly affected).
- IAD can allow the appeal, dismiss it, or refer the case back.

### Deadline
- **30 days** after the appellant receives the officer's decision (IAD Rules 2022, SOR/2022-277 r.16(a)) (H).

### Representation
- RCICs can appear at the IAD only with the **RCIC-IRB** licence class (see 10-consultant-practice/licensing-cpd-insurance.md).

### Bad faith appeals (read 2026-11-09)
- IAD notice of appeal form (August 2026): sponsor only; notice and documents must be **received within 30 days** of receiving the decision (H). IRB bad faith page (2023-03-22): officer's notes come in the appeal record; testimony by Teams, phone or in person; an allowed appeal returns the file to IRCC (H).
- Repeat appeals (IRB paper, 2008): no new evidence, may be dismissed as abuse of process; with new evidence, res judicata unless special circumstances. Verify current authority (M).
- s.65: a s.4 finding means the person isn't a family class spouse, so H&C relief generally isn't available unless genuineness is established. A child is strong evidence but doesn't answer the primary-purpose prong by itself.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- IRPA last amended 2026-03-26 (C-12). No change to s.63-67 found (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- For s.4 bad-faith refusals, the IAD hears the case **de novo**. New evidence and testimony are allowed. Prepare both spouses.
- For 117(9)(d) refusals, the IAD has no H&C jurisdiction (s.65). Consider an A25 H&C application or JR (lawyer).
- Reapplying vs appealing: a new application may be faster, but res judicata/abuse of process risk exists after an IAD dismissal (M).
- Watch for IAD ADR (alternative dispute resolution) for genuineness cases (M).
