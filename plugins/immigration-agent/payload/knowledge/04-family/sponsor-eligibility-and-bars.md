# Sponsor eligibility and bars

## Must cover
- IRPR ss.130/133 bars, undertakings, IRPR 117(9)(d)

## Current rules
### Sponsor must (s.130, s.133(1))
- Be **18+** (s.130(1)(a)); be a citizen, PR or registered Indian; live in Canada
- Intend to fulfil the undertaking (s.133(1)(b))
- Meet minimum income where required (s.133(1)(j)): PGP = MNI **+30% for 3 tax years**. Spouse/partner/child: no income test (s.133(4)) unless the dependent child has their own child.

### Bars (s.130(3), s.133(1))
| Bar | Provision | Exception |
|---|---|---|
| Sponsored as spouse/partner and PR/citizen **under 5 years** (can't sponsor a new spouse/partner) | s.130(3) | 5 years elapsed |
| Previous spouse undertaking still running (3 yrs) | s.117(9)(b); IRCC | Undertaking ended |
| **Removal order** | s.133(1)(c) | — |
| **Detained** in penitentiary/jail/prison | s.133(1)(d) | — |
| Convicted (Canada) of sexual offence, indictable violent offence (max at least 10 yrs), or offence causing bodily harm to a relative/partner | s.133(1)(e) | Record suspension, acquittal, or **5 yrs after sentence completed** (s.133(2)) |
| Same convictions outside Canada | s.133(1)(f) | Acquittal, or 5 yrs and rehabilitated (s.133(3)) |
| **Default** on undertaking or court-ordered support | s.133(1)(g) | Repaid (s.137(c)) |
| Default on immigration loan / performance bond | s.133(1)(h); IRCC | Repaid |
| **Undischarged bankrupt** | s.133(1)(i) | Discharge |
| **Social assistance** other than disability | s.133(1)(k) | — |

### Excluded relationships (s.117(9))
- (a) sponsor under 18 at time of... (age-related exclusions)
- **(b)** sponsor has an existing spouse undertaking not yet ended
- **(c)(i)** sponsor or spouse was married to another person at the time of marriage
- **(c)(ii)** sponsor and spouse lived separate for at least 1 yr and either is in a common-law/conjugal relationship with another person
- **(c.1)** marriage where a party was not physically present (proxy/phone/online), unless CAF service
- **(d)** foreign national was a **non-accompanying family member not examined** when the sponsor immigrated. **Permanent bar**; s.117(10)-(11) carve-outs are narrow.

### Bad faith (s.4, s.4.1)
- s.4(1): not a spouse/partner if the relationship was entered primarily for status **or** is not genuine (disjunctive since 2010).
- s.4.1: a new relationship with the same person after a dissolution done for status.

### Undertaking length (s.132(1))
- Spouse/partner: **3 yrs** from PR
- Dependent child under 22: **10 yrs or to age 25**, whichever first
- Dependent child 22+: 3 yrs
- Parents/grandparents: **20 yrs**
- Other relatives: 10 yrs
- Quebec: MIFI sets its own lengths.

### Co-signer and undertaking notes (read 2026-11-09) (H)
- No co-signer when sponsoring a spouse, common-law or conjugal partner (IMM 1344 instructions, page 2026-11-03). Income test applies only if the spouse's dependent child has their own dependent child (s.133(4)). Income under s.134: last NOA, or Canadian income in the prior 12 months; a co-signer's income counts only where one is allowed.
- Undertaking isn't cancelled by divorce, separation, breakdown, citizenship or a move (Guide 5289).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- **2026-09-10: 117(9)(d) undeclared-family public policy ENDED.** It does not apply to applications submitted after 2026-09-10 (IRCC who-you-can-sponsor page, 2026-09-11) (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- **Always ask: did the sponsor declare every family member when they immigrated?** If not, 117(9)(d) applies. The only routes are H&C (A25) or JR (lawyer). The public policy relief has ended.
- Check the sponsor's own immigration history: sponsored as spouse less than 5 yrs ago? Prior undertaking active? Prior default?
- Social assistance includes provincial welfare. Disability benefits are fine. Check the date of the last payment.
- An undertaking survives divorce, breakdown and citizenship. The sponsor remains liable to repay social assistance.
- Criminal bar: count 5 yrs from the **end of sentence**, not the conviction date.

## Common refusal reasons
- Sponsor ineligible (bars above); 117(9)(d); 117(9)(c.1) proxy marriage; s.4 bad faith
