# Super Visa (parents and grandparents)

## Must cover
- Requirements, insurance, LICO, validity, refusals

## Current rules
**Open** (not affected by the PGP pause).
- Multiple entry, valid up to **10 years**. Stay **5 years per entry** (applied/entered on or after 2023-06-22, even without a passport stamp). Dependants cannot be included.
- Fee **$100** + biometrics $85.

### Host (child or grandchild)
- Citizen, PR or registered Indian; 18+; lives in Canada; signed letter of invitation; meets the minimum necessary income.

### Income (table updated 2025-07-29)
| Persons | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | each extra |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum | $30,526 | $38,002 | $46,720 | $56,724 | $64,336 | $72,560 | $80,784 | $8,224 |
- Family size counts the host's household **plus** the visiting parents/grandparents.
- **From 2026-03-31 (notice 2026-03-20)**, applies to new and in-process files:
  - Option 1: host (+ co-signer) meets the minimum in **either of the 2 tax years** before applying (previously only the last year)
  - Option 2: host income at least **75%** of the minimum in the prior year; the **visiting parent's or grandparent's own income** can top up the rest
- Proof: CRA Notice of Assessment (preferred). If unavailable: T4 or T1 for the last tax year, 12 months of pay stubs, an employer letter, or bank statements. The visitor's own income (option 2) is proved with pay stubs, an employer letter, bank statements or pension statements. Wording confirmed on IRCC's host financial support page 2026-11-10 (H).
- Family size also counts earlier super visa guests under a letter of invitation still applicable (signed by the host or their spouse) and persons the host or co-signer sponsored whose undertaking is still in effect.

### Insurance (IRCC super visa documents page, read 2026-11-10)
- Canadian insurer, **or** a foreign insurer authorized by OSFI (on the federally regulated list).
- Valid at least **1 year from entry**, for each entry; at least **$100,000** emergency coverage; covers health care, hospitalization and repatriation.
- Paid in full **or by instalments with a deposit**. Quotes are not accepted.
- The policy must name the insurer (underwriter). A foreign insurer's policy must state it was issued or made while doing insurance business in Canada. OSFI's list doesn't include foreign insurers unless registered as branches or subsidiaries in Canada. Brokers and claims administrators aren't insurers (H).
- Letter of invitation: written and signed by the host; promise of financial support for the stay; the list and number of people counted for family size, with each person's name and date of birth; co-signer only the host's spouse or common-law partner (H).

### Applicant
- Outside Canada when applying; immigration medical exam; admissible; genuine temporary intent.

### Pre-existing conditions (read 2026-11-09) (H for rules; M for application)
- IRCC's super visa pages don't address pre-existing conditions; they list only insurer, $100,000, 1 year, health care, hospitalization, repatriation and payment. A policy excluding a condition may meet the list but leaves cost exposure; assess case by case. Income table still shows 'Updated July 29, 2025' on the page read today. MI 2026 requires no A38(1) inadmissibility; parents aren't exempt from excessive demand. Brokers and claims administrators aren't insurers. Applicants apply individually; a spouse or partner is the only co-signer.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026-03-31: income assessment widened (2-year look-back; 75% plus parent income) (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Super Visa is the main alternative while PGP is paused. Use it for clients waiting on PGP.
- A foreign insurer must be OSFI-authorized. Many cheap foreign policies fail.
- Count household size correctly (spouse, children, plus visitors). Undercounting is a common refusal.
- Immigration medical before applying (upfront) avoids delay.

## Common refusal reasons
- Income below the threshold for the correct family size; insurance non-compliant; ties to home country / dual intent concerns; medical inadmissibility

## Re-verify before advising
- Income table (updated yearly, usually July/August)
- Extend-stay rules for super visa holders
