| Skilled military recruits | **New** | CAF job offer; 10-yr foreign service record; 2-yr post-secondary |
- Renewed categories: experience requirement raised from **6 months to 12 months** (backgrounder).
- Healthcare page wording (IRCC 2026-10-16, read 2026-11-09): 12 months full-time or equivalent in the past 3 years, need not be continuous, **in a single listed occupation, no matter the primary occupation**, in Canada or abroad. Months across different NOCs don't combine.
- **STEM list (IRCC page 2026-10-16, read 2026-11-09) does not include 21231 or 21232:** only 20011, 21220, 21300, 21301, 21310, 21321, 21331, 22300, 22301, 22310, 63100. 21231 and 21232 sit in the 1.5x high-wage level (job offer +70). Education list includes 41220, 41221.
- **Agriculture and agri-food**: not in the 2026 list. Last agri-food round in the feed was 2024-02-16 (#285). Treat as dropped.
- No per-category targets published. All ITAs fall within the Levels Plan; extra categories don't add ITAs.

## Consultant pitfalls
- FSW-only candidates outside Canada with no French or PNP have had no realistic route in 2025-2026. CEC, French and PNP dominated. Advise PNP or French as levers.
- French is the lowest-cutoff large category (382-435). NCLC 7 in all four abilities is often the best single investment.
- The category experience must be in **one** listed occupation. Physicians, senior managers and researchers need it in Canada.
- Don't quote third-party draw trackers. They disagree with IRCC; use the JSON feed.
- STEM and education are renewed but undrawn in 2026. Don't promise category draws there.
- Chefs (62200) and cooks (63200) are in no category. Butchers (63201) are the only food trade in Trades. Neither is on the high-wage list, and 62200 is not on the select TEER 2-3 list for spouse OWPs. Both remain FST-eligible NOC groups (IRCC pages read 2026-11-08) (H).

## Common refusal reasons
- Invited under a category without meeting it (experience not in the listed NOC or under 12 months), which leads to refusal at e-APR.
