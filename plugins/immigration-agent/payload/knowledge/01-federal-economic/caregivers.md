# Caregivers (home child care, home support, nurses in the home)

## Must cover
- PR pilot status, pending files, work permit route, LMIA rules for families, PR alternatives, Quebec

## PR programs - status (H)
| Program | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Home Care Worker Immigration Pilots** (Child Care; Home Support) - streams "Workers in Canada" and "Applicants not working in Canada" | **Closed - intake paused 2025-12-19 "until further notice"**; will not reopen in March 2026 as planned | Applications received are processed "in line with the Immigration Levels Plan" |
| Home Child Care Provider / Home Support Worker pilots (2019) | Ended 2024-06-17 | Applications received by then still processed |
| Caring for Children / High Medical Needs | Closed (2019) | Legacy files only |
| Live-in Caregiver Program | Closed, except those with an LCP work permit based on an LMIA filed before Dec 2014 | Legacy |
- No reopening announced (pages to 2026-04-23; no new notice found 2026-11-20). Never quote a reopening date (`14-policy-tracker/ministerial-instructions-and-program-delivery.md`).
- Pending pilot applicants are in the **2027 In-Canada workers cohort** where eligible (`14-policy-tracker/announced-for-2027.md`).
- Interim OWP for pilot applicants (approval in principle, codes CARE4411/4412 etc.): see `05-temporary-residence/open-work-permits.md`.

## Work permit route (IRCC page 2026-04-14) (H)
- Covers NOC **44100** (home child care provider) and **44101** (home support worker); other caregiver occupations follow the normal LMIA process. IRCC says these get "faster processing".
- **From outside Canada: only if the job is in Quebec.** From inside Canada: holder of a valid study/work permit (or authorized to work without one), or a Quebec job with in-Canada eligibility. **Not** at a port of entry; **not** as a visitor (except the Quebec route).
- Employer needs a **positive LMIA** first.

## LMIA for in-home caregivers (ESDC, pages 2026-06-02 / 09-18 / 11-10) (H)
- NOCs: 44100, 44101; also 31301 (RN/RPN) and 32101 (LPN) for high medical needs.
- **LMIA fee $1,000 waived** for: care of a person with **high medical needs** (medical certificate); **child care for a child under 13 where family gross income is $150,000 or less**.
- **Wage:** at least the Job Bank prevailing (median) wage; Quebec uses Québec emploi data; review annually (fall 2026 update 2026-11-18).
- **Live-in cannot be required.** If live-in: private lockable room, no room-and-board charge. ESDC may refuse to process LMIAs for **live-in-only** positions (Ministerial Instructions).
- Employer pays: transportation (low-wage), private health insurance until provincial cover, workplace safety insurance, LMIA fee; **no recruitment fees** (negative LMIA); signed contract by day one; no independent-contractor classification. Up to **2 employers may share one LMIA**.
- Duration: low-wage max 1 year (high-wage up to 3).
- Language requirement: English or French only, unless another language is essential.
- Quebec: MII wage tables and contract (French); CAQ/temporary selection (`03-quebec/temporary-workers-quebec.md`).

## PR alternatives while the pilots are closed (M unless noted)
- **Express Entry: not available** for 44100/44101 - both are **TEER 4**; CEC/FSW need TEER 0-3 (H). FST does not cover them.
- **PNP:** Alberta and the regional community streams **exclude 44100 and 44101** (H, `02-pnp/alberta.md`, `regional-community-streams.md`). Manitoba and Nova Scotia exclude live-in caregivers in Canada. Check each province's in-demand/TEER 4-5 lists (`02-pnp/comparison-table.md`).
- **Rural / Francophone community programs:** several communities list **44101** (e.g. North Bay, Sudbury, Timmins, Thunder Bay, Steinbach, Moose Jaw, West Kootenay) (H, `rural-franco-communities.md`). Rural pilot closes 2027-01-01; permanent Rural Community Immigration Program from 2027-01-01.
- **AIP** with a designated Atlantic employer (TEER 4 allowed with conditions) (`pilots-and-community-programs.md`).
- Nurses (31301/32101): Express Entry healthcare category and provincial health streams.
- Spouse/partner sponsorship or H&C where facts fit.

## Consultant pitfalls
- Families: no fee if the $150,000 child-care or medical-needs test is met - get the income proof and medical certificate up front.
- A visitor already in Canada cannot get a caregiver work permit inside Canada (except Quebec route) - do not advise "come as a visitor and switch".
- Any recruiter charging the worker = ghost/unauthorized practice and TFWP breach; flag it (CLAUDE.md rule 5).
- Do not promise a pilot reopening; build a TEER 4 PR plan via PNP/community programs.

## Re-verify before advising
- Any IRCC notice on reopening or replacing the Home Care Worker pilots.
- Live-in-only refusal Ministerial Instruction scope.
- Provincial lists that include 44100/44101.
