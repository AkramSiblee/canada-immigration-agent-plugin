# Medical doctors: immigration routes

## Must cover
- Express Entry physician category, PNP reserved spaces, other routes, licensing

## Current rules
**First steps for every route:** foreign credentials assessed, and a licence from the provincial or territorial regulatory authority. Both take time.

### With Canadian experience (page 2026-10-19)
| Route | Requirements |
|---|---|
| **Express Entry: physician category** (new in 2026) | Eligible for an Express Entry program; **at least 1 year full-time as a doctor in Canada in the last 3 years**. Can also be invited in general or program rounds |
| **PNP** | Job, job offer or letter of support as a doctor. Provinces have **up to 5,000 federal spaces reserved** for doctors, in addition to regular allocations |
- Eligible Express Entry occupations (announcement 2025-12-08): general practitioners and family physicians, specialists in surgery, specialists in clinical and laboratory medicine.
- **Nominated doctors get a work permit processed in 14 days**, so they can work while waiting for PR.
- Family: spouse or partner, dependent child, dependent grandchild.

### Without Canadian experience, or experience over 3 years old (page 2025-12-23)
| Route | Key condition |
|---|---|
| PNP | Job offer or letter of support; reserved spaces apply |
| Express Entry | Eligible for a program; no job offer yet |
| Atlantic Immigration Program | Work or post-secondary study in Atlantic Canada; designated employer offer |
| Rural Community Immigration | 1 year as a doctor in the last 3 years, or eligible graduate; designated employer offer |
| Francophone Community Immigration | Same, plus French |

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-12-08: physician Express Entry category and 5,000 reserved PNP spaces announced (H)
- 2026: category is in use (IRCC page marks it "New in 2026") (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Confirm licensing is realistic before advising a route. Licensing is provincial and outside IRCC.
- Each province sets its own doctor stream. Check `02-pnp/` and the provincial site.
- A letter of support can replace a job offer on the PNP route only.
- Don't quote remaining PNP spaces. No running count was found (2026-11-08).
- RCI recommendations under the pilot end 2026-12-31. See `pilots-and-community-programs.md`.

## Common refusal reasons
- Licence or credential evidence missing; Canadian experience outside the 3-year window

## Re-verify before advising
- Physician category round history (`category-draws-history.md`)
- Reserved spaces by province
