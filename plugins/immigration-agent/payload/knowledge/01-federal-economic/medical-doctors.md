# Medical doctors: immigration routes

## Must cover
- Express Entry physician category, PNP reserved spaces, other routes, licensing

## Current rules
**First steps for every route:** foreign credentials assessed, and a licence from the provincial or territorial regulatory authority. Both take time.

### With Canadian experience (page 2026-10-19)
| Route | Requirements |
|---|---|
| **Express Entry: physician category** (new in 2026) | Eligible for an Express Entry program; **12 months full-time (or equal part-time) as a doctor in Canada in the last 3 years; need not be continuous** (category page 2026-10-16). NOCs 31100-31102 are all at the 2x high-wage level (offer = 100 CRS); publicly funded physicians' offers may be non-continuous or under 1 year, and their self-employed Canadian experience counts (MI 2026-1 ss.15.1(6)(b), 15.2(2)(a); crs-scoring.md). Can also be invited in general or program rounds |
| **PNP** | Job, job offer or letter of support as a doctor. Provinces have **up to 5,000 federal spaces reserved** for doctors, in addition to regular allocations |
- Eligible Express Entry occupations (announcement 2025-12-08): general practitioners and family physicians, specialists in surgery, specialists in clinical and laboratory medicine.
- **Nominated doctors get a work permit processed in 14 days**, so they can work while waiting for PR.
- Family: spouse or partner, dependent child, dependent grandchild.

### How provinces use the reserved PNP spaces (re-checked 2026-11-20)
- **Federal terms (IRCC release 2025-12-08) (H):** up to **5,000** federal admission spaces for provinces and territories to nominate **licensed doctors with job offers**, on top of regular PNP allocations; NOCs 31100, 31101, 31102. IRCC's page adds letters of support. Quebec has no PNP.
- **No federal per-province split or running count is published** (searched 2026-11-20). Secondary sources say it is unclear which allocation the spaces come from.
- **Conflict (M):** Alberta's official AAIP processing page describes a national pool of **up to 10,000** extra spaces for **practice-ready physicians or Francophones** (2026). CIC News reports the same as 5,000 physicians + 5,000 Francophones. Not contradicted by IRCC, but the 10,000 combined figure is not on canada.ca. Quote "up to 5,000 for doctors" (IRCC) and note the provincial wording.
- **Province use found (H unless marked):**
  - **Alberta:** nominations under the federal spaces are counted **outside** its 6,603 allocation: **physicians 50 issued**, Francophones 12 (AAIP processing page 2026-11-04; see `02-pnp/alberta.md`). Physicians also fit the AAIP Dedicated Health Care pathway.
  - **Ontario:** Workforce Priority stream has a **self-employed physician** pathway (no job offer; OHIP billing number; CPSO good standing; EE option) since 2026-06-25 (`02-pnp/ontario.md`). Whether those nominations draw on the federal spaces is not stated.
  - **Nova Scotia:** Physicians tab (NSHA/IWK opportunity, Return for Service Agreement, NSNP Letter of Interest) (`02-pnp/nova-scotia.md`).
  - **Manitoba:** physician-specific rules in some community streams (see `02-pnp/manitoba.md`).
  - Other provinces: no public statement found on using the federal physician spaces (searched 2026-11-20). OPEN.

### Without Canadian experience, or experience over 3 years old (page 2025-12-23)
| Route | Key condition |
|---|---|
| PNP | Job offer or letter of support; reserved spaces apply |
| Express Entry | Eligible for a program; no job offer yet |
| Atlantic Immigration Program | 1 year (1,560 h in the last 5 yrs; IRCC table: offer TEER 0 = experience TEER 0-4, offer 1 = 1-4, offer 2 = 2-4, offer 3 = 3-4, offer 4 = 4), not limited to Atlantic Canada (Canadian work needs a valid permit), or exemption as a recent Atlantic post-secondary graduate (2+ yr credential, under 2 yrs ago, full time, 16 months in the province); designated employer offer (IRCC AIP work-experience page, modified 2026-05-28, read 2026-11-20, H) |
| Rural Community Immigration | 1 year as a doctor in the last 3 years, or eligible graduate; designated employer offer |
| Francophone Community Immigration | Same, plus French |

## Consultant pitfalls
- Confirm licensing is realistic before advising a route. Licensing is provincial and outside IRCC.
- Each province sets its own doctor stream. Check `02-pnp/` and the provincial site.
- A letter of support can replace a job offer on the PNP route only.
- Don't quote remaining PNP spaces. No running count was found (re-checked 2026-11-20); only Alberta publishes its own physician nominations (50).
- RCI recommendations under the pilot end 2026-12-31. See `pilots-and-community-programs.md`.

## Common refusal reasons
- Licence or credential evidence missing; Canadian experience outside the 3-year window

- New physician rounds (rounds JSON)
- Reserved spaces by province (no federal split published)
