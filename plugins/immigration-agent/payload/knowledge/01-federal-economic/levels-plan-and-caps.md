# Levels Plan and caps

## Must cover
- PR targets by category and year
- Temporary resident arrival targets; IMP/TFWP split
- Study permit cap, provincial allocations, PAL/TAL exemptions
- 2027 in-Canada TR-to-PR measure (up to 200,000) criteria; 2026-2027 one-time initiatives

## Current rules
**Full 2027-2029 table: see 14-policy-tracker/announced-for-2027.md.** Key figures:
- **2027-2029: PR 380,000/yr** (352,500-422,000). Economic 244,700; Family 81,000; Refugees 49,300; H&C/other 5,000.
- Economic detail 2027: Federal High Skilled 100,000; PNP 95,000; AIP 6,000; RCI 5,500; pilots 7,900; Business 1,500; Quebec TBD.
- **Temporary arrivals: 370,000/yr**: workers 220,000 (**IMP 170,000 / TFWP 50,000**), students 150,000. 2028-29 are notional.
- **Time-limited 2027 measure:** up to 200,000 in-Canada TR-to-PR (100k workers via PNP + 100k humanitarian), **outside Levels**, no application.
- One-time 2026-2027 initiatives: 33,000 work permit holders (In-Canada Workers Initiative) and up to 115,000 protected persons. Both end 2027.
- Goal: PR admissions under 1% of population after 2027; reduce the temporary population (5% of population target, secondary) (M).
- **Study cap 2026**: 408,000 issuance target; 309,670 application spaces; public master's/PhD PAL-exempt (see 05-temporary-residence/study-permits.md). 2027 cap not yet published.

### 2026 targets (2026-2028 plan, official)
- **2026:** PR **380,000** (350,000-420,000). Economic 239,800 (Federal High Skilled 109,000; PNP **91,500** (82,000-105,000); AIP 4,000; pilots 8,175; Business 500; Quebec TBD). Family 84,000 (spouses/partners/children 69,000; PGP 15,000). Refugees 49,300 (protected persons 20,000; GAR 13,250; **BVOR 50**; PSR 16,000). H&C 1,100 + other 5,800 = 6,900. Francophone outside Quebec 9% (30,267). **Temporary arrivals 2026: 385,000** (workers 230,000: IMP 170,000 / TFWP 60,000; students 155,000) (H).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026-01-01: public master's/PhD exempt from PAL/TAL (H)
- 2026-11-05: 2027-2029 Levels Plan tabled (H) - confirmed
- One-time 2027 measure: up to 200,000 in-Canada TR-to-PR (100k workers via PNP, 100k humanitarian), outside Levels, no application (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- The IMP target (170,000) is far larger than the TFWP (50,000). Most work permits will be LMIA-exempt.
- Targets are not guarantees for individual files. Category queues ("waiting for space") matter more (see 14-policy-tracker/backlogs-and-inventory.md).
