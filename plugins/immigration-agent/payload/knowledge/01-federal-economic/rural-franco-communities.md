## Current rules
**Basis: each community's official page, read 2026-11-08. Intakes and caps change often, so re-read the community page before advising. Items marked (M) came from a summarized part of a long page.**

### Rural Community Immigration: how it works
- Employer-driven. Candidates generally do not apply to the community; a **designated employer** submits the recommendation application.
- Federal: job offer from designated employer; 1,560 h in past 3 yrs; CLB 6 (TEER 0-1), 5 (TEER 2-3), 4 (TEER 4-5); education or ECA; funds.
- PR forms: portal IMM 0008, 5669, 5406, 5562; upload IMM 0246 (checklist), IMM 0247 (offer of employment), IMM 0248 (Schedule 1), IMM 0249 (recommendation); IMM 5409 and 5604 if applicable (IRCC page 2026-04-23).
- Each community sets 5-6 priority sectors and 25 NOCs a year, and adds its own limits. Recommendation certificates are valid **6 months** (Timmins, Sault Ste. Marie, Peace Liard pages).
- Common employer tests: 2 yrs operating under same management in boundary; 75% of work in boundary; IRCC Employer Onboarding Training and Building Welcoming Workplaces.
- **Commonly excluded:** gas stations, convenience stores, fast food, staffing agencies, consulates, businesses owned by the candidate or an immigration representative.

### Francophone Community Immigration pilot: federal rules (IRCC)
- Status open (page 2026-04-30). Designated employer job offer; community recommendation.
- 1,560 h in past 3 yrs. TEER map: offer 0-1 takes experience 0-3; 2 takes 1-4; 3-4 takes 2-4; 5 same NOC.
- **NCLC 5 in all 4 abilities**; TEF Canada or TCF Canada only; under 2 yrs old.
- Education: Canadian credential or ECA under 5 yrs old.
- Funds: 1 $10,507; 2 $13,080; 3 $16,080; 4 $19,524; 5 $22,143; 6 $24,975; 7 $27,806; each extra $2,831 (table 2025-07-29). Not needed if working in Canada on a valid permit.
- Optional 2-yr employer-specific work permit. Spouse may get an open work permit limited to the community.

## Consultant pitfalls
- A TEER or NOC on IRCC's federal rules means nothing locally. The community's list decides, and it changes during the year.
- Most communities have little or no 2026 capacity left by November. Plan for 2027 lists, which are not yet published.
- Food service is often limited: check dine-in versus fast food, per-employer caps, and whether the NOC is full (Moose Jaw).
- Local wage floors exceed Job Bank in BC communities.
- Maintained status is treated differently by community (Thunder Bay refuses it; Sudbury needs extra documents; North Bay holds the file).
- Fees to candidates for job offers are prohibited; several communities post fraud notices. Flag IRPA s.40 and s.91 risk.

## Common refusal reasons
- Job outside community boundary; NOC not on the current list; employer not designated; certificate expired; low score
