# Pilots and community programs

- Home Care Worker pilots (paused); Agri-Food Pilot; EMPP
- RCIP, FCIP, Atlantic Immigration Program
- Municipal Nominee Program (never launched); Francophone Minority Communities Student Pilot

### AIP and Rural eligibility (IRCC pages read 2026-11-08) (H)
| | AIP | Rural Community Immigration |
|---|---|---|
| Experience | 1,560 h in past 5 yrs, paid, not self-employed. Offer TEER 0: exp 0-4; TEER 1: 1-4; TEER 2: 2-4; TEER 3: 3-4; TEER 4: 4 | 1,560 h in past 3 yrs. Offer TEER 0-1: exp 0-3; TEER 2: 1-4; TEER 3-4: 2-4; TEER 5: same NOC |
| Language | CLB 5 (TEER 0-3); CLB 4 (TEER 4) | CLB 6 (TEER 0-1); CLB 5 (TEER 2-3); CLB 4 (TEER 4-5) |
| Education | TEER 0-1: 1-yr post-secondary; TEER 2-4: high school; ECA for foreign credential, under 5 yrs old | Canadian credential or foreign equivalent |
| Funds, family of 5 | $8,042 (table 2025-07-29); not needed if working in Canada on a valid permit | $22,143 (table 2025-07-29) |
| Work permit | 2 yrs, employer-specific; job offer + provincial referral letter | 2 yrs, employer-specific; offer + community recommendation |
- **AIP job offer (IRCC page 2026-09-22, read 2026-11-09):** full-time 30+ h, non-seasonal; TEER 0-3 at least 1 year from PR; **TEER 4 permanent, no end date**; not from a company majority-owned by applicant or spouse. Settlement plan required from an approved provider, individualized per accompanying family member. IRCC estimate about 26 months, about 11,500 waiting (2026-10-09).
- **Nova Scotia AIP:** designation then endorsement through online EOI; endorsement needs failed local hiring, full-time non-seasonal co-signed offer, co-signed settlement plan. NOC 62020 paused.
- AIP forms: IMM 0155 checklist; portal IMM 0008, 5669, 5406, 5562; upload IMM 5501, IMM 0157. AIP applicants may be abroad.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-05-14: Agri-Food Pilot ended (H)
- 2025-12-19: Home Care Worker pilots intake paused (H)
- 2026-10-22: Rural Community Immigration class (SOR/2026-217) (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Caregivers: no open PR pathway. Use an LMIA-based work permit, then CEC/PNP, or wait for the 2027 cohort if already filed.
- The RCI transition: watch for the 2027 community list and EDO recommendations under the new class.
- The employer's designation status (AIP, RCI, FCI) must be valid at recommendation and endorsement.

## Common refusal reasons
- Employer not designated; recommendation/endorsement expired; job offer NOC ineligible for community priorities

## Re-verify before advising
- RCI permanent class implementation details (2027); FMCSP page; EMPP status
