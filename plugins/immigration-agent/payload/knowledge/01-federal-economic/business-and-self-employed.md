# Federal business programs

## Must cover
- Start-Up Visa closure and inventory handling
- Self-Employed Persons pause; planned federal entrepreneur pilot
- Provincial entrepreneur streams cross-reference
- Provincial entrepreneur streams by province (gap-pass-01)

## Current rules
| Program | Status (2026-11-07) |
|---|---|
| **Start-Up Visa (PR)** | **CLOSED** (final deadline 2026-06-30 passed; IRCC's page label reads "Paused"). Only applicants with a valid **2025 commitment certificate** who applied **by 2026-06-30**; "closed to all other applications". Designated organization path paused |
| SUV work permit | Closed to new applicants (since 2025-12-19; extensions only for those in Canada) |
| **Self-Employed Persons** | **Paused** (cultural/athletic experience; significant contribution) |
| **Federal entrepreneur pilot** | Announced 2025-12-19 ("more information in 2026"); **not launched** |
| Quebec business programs | Separate (MIFI); CSQ needed before the federal stage (IRCC page 2026-06-15, which gives no intake status). Status not confirmed, so never present as open. Official figures only: 2026 fees investor $18,241, entrepreneur or self-employed $1,272; French required since 2024-01-01; 2026 plan target about 500 (the plan shows both 450-550 and 500-600) (M) |
- **Levels: Federal Business** 1,500 (2027), 2,725 (2028), 1,000 (2029).
- **2027 workers cohort**: people waiting on SUV or Self-Employed PR decisions can be identified by provinces through extra PNP allocations (no new application).
- Fees (for existing files): business principal $1,895 + RPRF $600.

### Provincial entrepreneur streams (see 02-pnp)
- BC Base/Regional/Strategic Projects
- AB Rural, Graduate, Farm, Foreign Graduate
- SK Entrepreneur/Farm **permanently closed 2025-03-27**
- MB Business Investor
- NS Entrepreneur ($2,000 fee)
- NB Business Immigration
- PEI Business Impact (0 ITAs 2026)
- NL International (Graduate) Entrepreneur
- YT: no business stream listed for 2026; NWT Business
- ON none

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-12-19: SUV work permit closed; Self-Employed pause extended (H)
- 2025-12-31 / 2026-06-30: SUV closed except 2025-commitment holders applying by 2026-06-30 (H)
- 2026-11-05: SUV/SE inventory in 2027 workers cohort (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- No federal entrepreneur route is open now. Use provincial entrepreneur streams or a C11 work permit (temporary) with a PNP plan.
- SUV applicants already in process: keep work permits valid (extensions only) and watch for provincial outreach under the 2027 cohort.

## Common refusal reasons
- SUV: commitment not genuine, or business not viable; SE: experience not "relevant" at world-class/national level

## Re-verify before advising
- Federal entrepreneur pilot launch
