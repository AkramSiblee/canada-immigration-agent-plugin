## Current rules
| Program | Status (rechecked 2026-11-17) |
|---|---|
| **Start-Up Visa (PR)** | **CLOSED** (final deadline 2026-06-30 passed; IRCC's page label reads "Paused", page 2026-07-21). Only applicants with a valid **2025 commitment certificate** who applied **by 2026-06-30**; "closed to all other applications". Designated organization path paused |
| SUV work permit | Closed to new applicants (since 2025-12-19; extensions only for those in Canada while PR is processed). IRCC prioritizes PR files of people in Canada on an SUV work permit (notice 2025-12-19) |
| **Self-Employed Persons** | **Paused** "until further notice" (page 2026-03-11; pause extended 2025-12-19) |
| **Federal entrepreneur pilot** | Announced 2025-12-19 ("details in 2026"); Budget 2026 refers to a start-up pilot. **Not launched**: no canada.ca launch notice, criteria or intake found as of 2026-11-17 (H for "not launched" on canada.ca; the name "High Impact Start-up Pilot" is from secondary sources only, M) |
| Quebec business programs | **Open** (watch: PQ government proposes a moratorium on economic immigration from abroad, not adopted, M) (quebec.ca pages 2026-11-09/10): Investor, Entrepreneur (3 streams) and Self-Employed accept applications at any time, no cap. French oral 7. Detail: 03-quebec/business-immigration.md (H) |
- **Levels: Federal Business** 1,500 (2027), 2,725 (2028), 1,000 (2029).
- **2027 workers cohort** (measures page 2026-11-05; news release 2026-11-12) (H): time-limited, up to **100,000** extra PR spaces in 2027, **outside the Levels targets**, for workers already in communities **outside Quebec** and their families. Provinces may pick workers **already waiting for a PR decision** under the Atlantic Immigration Program (open) or closed programs (SUV, Self-Employed, Agri-Food Pilot, home care workers), **or** PNP-eligible workers. Delivered through **optional extra PNP allocations** that provinces/territories use as they choose, "intended to be used by **2027-03-31**". "Applicants do not need to apply separately or take any additional steps." Not a new pathway. The supplementary levels page itself does not describe the mechanism.
- Fees (for existing files): business principal $1,895 + RPRF $600.

## Consultant pitfalls
- No federal entrepreneur route is open now. Use **provincial entrepreneur streams** (or Quebec business programs).
- **CORRECTION (2026-11-17): do not pair a C11 work permit with a PNP plan.** C11 is for **temporary intent only** (no dual intent); IRCC's C11 instructions redirect PR-intent applicants to **C60** (provincial business candidates, Quebec CSQ entrepreneurs/self-employed) or A77 (SUV). Declaring PR plans on a C11 file invites an R200(1)(b) refusal; hiding them is a misrepresentation risk (A40). Pre-nomination provincial entrepreneurs use **C60** with a provincial support letter (max 2 yrs outside Quebec); after nomination, **T13 (R204(c))**. Detail: 05-temporary-residence/work-permits-lmia-exempt.md.
- Owner-operator LMIA is another temporary route for a controlling owner: see 05-temporary-residence/work-permits-lmia.md.
- SUV applicants already in process: keep work permits valid (extensions only) and watch for provincial outreach under the 2027 cohort.
