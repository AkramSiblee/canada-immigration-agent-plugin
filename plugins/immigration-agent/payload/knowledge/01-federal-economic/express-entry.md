# Express Entry: FSW, CEC, FST

## Must cover
- Eligibility per program (IRPR s.75, 87.1, 87.2); Francophone Mobility is a work permit (C16)
- Profile validity, ITA, 60-day e-APR, claims true on ITA date
- ECA issuers, language tests (IELTS, CELPIP, PTE Core, TEF, TCF), validity
- NOC 2021 TEER matching; work experience hour counting; proof of funds table
- Common refusals; misrepresentation and undeclared family traps

## Current rules

### Program eligibility (IRCC pages, 2026-10-16 / 2026-11-03)
| | FSW (IRPR s.75) | CEC (s.87.1) | FST (s.87.2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Work experience | 1 yr continuous full-time or 1,560 h in last **10 yrs**, TEER 0-3, primary occupation | 1 yr (1,560 h) **in Canada** in last **3 yrs**, TEER 0-3 | 2 yrs (3,120 h) in a skilled trade in last **5 yrs** |
| Language | CLB 7 all four abilities | CLB/NCLC 7 (TEER 0-1); CLB/NCLC 5 (TEER 2-3) | CLB 5 speaking/listening; CLB 4 reading/writing |
| Education | Required; ECA for foreign credential (less than 5 yrs old) | Not required | Not required |
| Other | 67/100 selection grid; proof of funds unless valid job offer + work authorization | No proof of funds | Job offer (1 yr full-time) **or** certificate of qualification; proof of funds unless job offer + authorization |
- Student work and self-employment don't count for CEC or FST (IRCC pages).
- FSW grid: language 28, education 25, experience 15, age 12, arranged employment 10, adaptability 10. **From 2026-11-03 arranged employment needs no LMIA.**
- Francophone Mobility is a work permit (IMP C16), not an EE program.

### Expired profile and re-entry (IRCC pages read 2026-11-09)
- Profile lasts 12 months, then is removed. A new one can be created; no waiting period stated. Information isn't saved (Help Centre 1164). Don't create a new profile before expiry without withdrawing the old one. New profile number and Job Seeker code. A draft stays open 60 days.
- Must update for: new career, job offer gained or lost, new language test, marriage or divorce, new child. Ineligibility triggers: work outside the 3-year window, expired language or ECA, insufficient funds, changed primary occupation (create-profile page 2026-10-16).
- Dependent child details are requested only at invitation (same page), yet family size drives proof of funds. A profile alone isn't "applied before" on the e-APR.
- Program order when eligible for more than one: CEC, FSW, FST. Candidates can't choose.

### After the ITA: police, medical, passport (IRCC pages read 2026-11-09)
- **60 days only**; no published extension. Decline = back to pool; lapse = profile removed. Incomplete application: IRCC notifies and refunds processing fee and RPRF if paid.
- **Police (page 2026-10-16):** each person 18+, every country of 6+ months in a row in the last 10 years; none for Canada or before 18. Current country: issued within 6 months before submission. Other countries: issued after the last 6-month stay; expired ones accepted if so. Colour scans of originals only; certified copies cause rejection. Request-letter countries: upload IRCC's statement in the police field. **Not in time:** letter of explanation + proof of best effort in the country field; officer may reject as incomplete.
- **Medical (page 2026-08-04):** upfront exam after ITA and before applying; upload information printout sheet or IMM 1017B; all family members, including non-accompanying; one appointment only. IME within 5 years while living in Canada may be reused. Results valid 12 months. Unable to complete: letter of explanation.
- **Passport (page 2026-10-16):** expiring within 6 months of application: "should" renew and update the application after.
- **Minimum at submission:** processing and biometrics fees; RPRF can follow. Couple: $2,150 minimum, $3,350 in full (fee list checked 2026-11-09).

### Educational Credential Assessment (IRCC page 2026-10-16; read 2026-11-09)
- Must be under 5 years old at profile and at application; must be for immigration purposes; enter result and reference number in the profile. Each credential needs its own ECA to claim two or more credentials.
- **Designated:** WES, IQAS, ICAS, ICES (BCIT), CES (U of T); professional bodies MCC, PEBC, CACB where the primary occupation requires. IRCC doesn't rank them.
- **CES stopped accepting new applications 2026-10-22** (U of T notice), though IRCC's page still lists it. Paid applications continue.
- **Times (provider pages, change often):** IQAS about 15 business days, rush 5 to 7 ($260 + $25 + courier; rush +$250; daily cap; not guaranteed for institution verification); WES about 2 weeks verification + 1 to 2 weeks evaluation (about C$264); ICAS average 20 weeks (about $231, M).
- **Effect:** an equivalency assessment is conclusive evidence of equivalency (IRPR s.75(8)); FSW selection points use the submitted assessments giving the highest points (s.78(2)). No IRCC guidance found on conflicting ECAs for CRS.
- ITA: 60 days. If a recalculated score falls below the round's lowest score, decline (returns to the pool). If the invitation lapses, the profile is removed.

### Federal Skilled Trades detail (IRCC page 2026-10-16; LMIA policy 2026-11-03; read 2026-11-09)
- **All experience in the same NOC**, in Major Groups 72 (excluding 726), 73, 82, 83, 92, 93 (excluding 932), Minor Group 6320 or Unit Group 62200. **Must have been qualified to practise where the work was done.**
- **Certificate of qualification** must be from a Canadian provincial, territorial or federal authority, after assessment and exam. A foreign licence doesn't qualify. **IRCC doesn't require a Red Seal.** Certificate + language CRS: 25, or 50 with CLB 7 in all four.
- **Job offer:** full-time, at least 1 year in total, up to 2 employers, existing or new. **No LMIA** for ITAs on or after 2026-11-03 (public policy until revoked). Earns CRS only if the NOC is high-wage.
- **Electricians:** 72200 and 72201 are in the Trades category; 72202 isn't. 72201 and 72202 are on the high-wage list; 72200 isn't. Electrician is a compulsory trade in Ontario (registered training agreement, provisional certificate or certificate of qualification required); requirements vary by province.

### Language tests (valid less than 2 yrs at profile creation AND at e-APR submission)
- English: CELPIP-General, IELTS General Training (no One Skill Retake), PTE Core
- French: TEF Canada, TCF Canada
- LINC not accepted for CEC.

### Proof of funds (CAD, table "Updated July 7, 2025")
| Family size | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | each extra |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Funds | 15,263 | 19,001 | 23,360 | 28,362 | 32,168 | 36,280 | 40,392 | 4,112 |
- Not required for CEC, or for FSW/FST with a valid job offer and current work authorization.
- **No 2026 update is posted** as of 2026-11-07, although IRCC says it updates the table yearly.

### Process
- Profile, then ranking by CRS (see crs-scoring.md), then rounds (general, program-specific, PNP, category: see category-draws-history.md), then ITA, then e-APR within 60 days (M: long-standing rule, not re-read this pass).
- Claims must be true on the ITA date and at e-APR. Profile valid 12 months (M).
- Tie-break: profile submission date and time.
- **Levels 2027-2029:** "Federal High Skilled" (FSW + FST + CEC) target 100,000/yr (87,000-123,000).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-03-25: arranged-employment CRS points repealed (MI s.8(1)(d)(ii) repealed) (H)
- 2026-02-18: 2026 categories announced; renewed categories need 12 months of experience (was 6) (H)
- 2026-10-16: MI 2026-1 signed. Candidates had to update their profiles **before Nov 3** to claim job-offer points (H)
- 2026-10-28: last round before rescoring. Rounds paused for up to 2 weeks during recalculation; **no round as of 2026-11-07** (H)
- 2026-11-03: labour market integration factor in force; qualifying job offers need **no LMIA** (temporary public policy, until revoked) (H)
- Federal High Skilled admissions target 100,000/yr in 2027-2029 plan (H)
- 2026-11-07: still no round after #453 (IRCC JSON checked) (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- **Job offers now count only for high-wage NOCs** (1.3x/1.5x/2x lists). A TEER 0-3 offer that is not high-wage earns 0 CRS points, though it still may satisfy the FSW grid or FST requirements.
- The high-wage level is locked at the ITA date. If the list changes after ITA, the invited level stays.
- The LMIA exemption is a public policy "until revoked". Check before each ITA.
- Embassy, high commission and consulate offers never qualify. FST offers can come from up to 2 employers; other offers from 1 employer only.
- Work hours: count only paid hours; cap at 30 h/week for full-time equivalency (M).
- Language results expire. A test valid at profile creation but expired at e-APR leads to refusal.
- Proof of funds: IRCC page (2026-10-16, read 2026-11-09) says funds must be available when applying and when the PR visa is issued. Bank letter shows debts and, per account, number, opening date, balance and 6-month average. Can't be borrowed from another person; no real property equity. IRPR 76(1)(b): transferable, available, unencumbered by debts or other obligations.
  - **No published minimum holding period, and no published rule on parent joint accounts, gifts or fixed deposits** (only spouse joint/sole accounts addressed). Program instructions not located. Gift, parent-account and fixed-deposit treatment is applied judgment (Conf. M).
- **Undeclared family (IRPR 117(9)(d))**: every dependant must be declared and examined, even non-accompanying ones. Otherwise they are permanently barred from family sponsorship.
- **Misrepresentation (IRPA s.40)**: inflated duties or hours, or fake offers, bring a 5-year bar **plus an AMP since 2026-10-23** (SOR/2026-220, baseline $15,000).

## Common refusal reasons
- Work experience reference letters lack duties, hours or salary, or the duties don't match the NOC lead statement
- Claimed points not proven: ECA, language, CWE, job offer genuineness (s.15.2(4) of the MI)
- Insufficient or unexplained proof of funds
- Expired test or passport; missing police certificates or medicals
- Undeclared family member or prior refusal; misrepresentation

## Re-verify before advising
