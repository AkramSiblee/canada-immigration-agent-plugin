# CRS scoring (in force 2026-11-03)

## Must cover
- Full factor breakdown single vs spouse
- Labour Market Integration Factor (Nov 3, 2026): 50/70/100 job offer, 20/40/70 experience, no LMIA
- High-wage occupation list (count, tiers) - verify on IRCC page
- History: job-offer points removed Mar 25, 2025; PNP +600; French and post-secondary bonuses

## Current rules
**Maximum 1,270** (MI s.8(2)) = core human capital 570 single / 530 with spouse + spouse factors 40 + skill transferability 100 + additional 600. (Core includes the new labour market integration factor.)

_(**Every age 17 to 45+ confirmed** against the IRCC CRS criteria page (modified 2026-11-03) on 2026-11-20. Conf. H.)_

**Education** (max 140 / 150): secondary 28/30; 1-yr post-secondary 84/90; 2-yr 91/98; bachelor's or 3+ yr 112/120; two or more credentials, one 3+ yr, 119/128; master's or entry-to-practice professional degree 126/135; doctorate 140/150.
- **Foreign secondary (high school) diploma needs an ECA to score.** MI s.11(1)(a)(ii) gives 30 points (single) for "a secondary school credential", and s.11(3) requires either a Canadian educational credential or an equivalency assessment from an organization designated under IRPR s.75(4). ECA must be under 5 years old (MI ss.3(3)(b), 11(5)). IRCC's ECA page: the report must show the foreign credential equals "a completed Canadian secondary school (high school) or post-secondary credential"; if a post-secondary credential is not equivalent, "you could get your secondary credential assessed" (page modified 2026-10-16). No ECA = 0 education points (H). The CRS criteria page itself doesn't mention ECAs.

**First official language**, per ability (max 32/34 each; 128/136 total): CLB 4-5: 6/6; CLB 6: 8/9; CLB 7: 16/17; CLB 8: 22/23; CLB 9: 29/31; CLB 10+: 32/34.

**Second official language**, per ability (max 22 / 24 total): CLB 5-6: 1; CLB 7-8: 3; CLB 9+: 6.

**Labour market integration** (new; max 140 / 150): award the greater of (i) or (ii). **Never both.**
- (i) Canadian work experience (s.15.1)

| CWE | 1 yr | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| With spouse | 35 | 46 | 56 | 63 | 70 |
| Single | 40 | 53 | 64 | 72 | 80 |

  plus high-wage CWE (at least 1 yr): **70** (2x level), **40** (1.5x, or combination with 2x), **20** (1.3x, or any combination)
- (ii) Qualifying high-wage job offer (s.15.2): **100** (2x), **70** (1.5x), **50** (1.3x), 0 if not high-wage
- Caps: 150 single, 140 with spouse (s.15(2))

### B. Spouse factors (max 40)
- Only when the spouse/partner is accompanying and is not a Canadian citizen or PR; otherwise use the single grid. A foreign credential needs an ECA (as for the principal).
- Education (max 10): secondary 2; 1-yr 6; 2-yr 7; bachelor's 8; two+ credentials 9; master's/professional 10; doctorate 10
- First official language per ability (max 20): CLB 5-6: 1; CLB 7-8: 3; CLB 9+: 5
- Canadian work experience (max 10): 1 yr 5; 2 yrs 7; 3 yrs 8; 4 yrs 9; 5+ yrs 10

### C. Skill transferability (max 100; same for single and spouse)

| Combination | Lower tier | Upper tier |
|---|---|---|
| Education + language | CLB 7+ (any under 9): 13 (1-yr+) / 25 (two+ credentials, master's, PhD) | CLB 9+ all four: 25 / 50 |
| Education + CWE | 1 yr CWE: 13 / 25 | 2+ yrs: 25 / 50 |
| Foreign experience + language | CLB 7+ (any under 9): 13 (1-2 yrs) / 25 (3+ yrs) | CLB 9+: 25 / 50 |
| Foreign experience + CWE | 1 yr CWE: 13 / 25 | 2+ yrs CWE: 25 / 50 |
| Certificate of qualification + language | CLB 5+ (any under 7): 25 | CLB 7+ all four: 50 |
- Each pair (education, foreign experience) is capped at 50; certificate of qualification is capped at 50; total capped at 100.

### D. Additional points (max 600)
- Provincial or territorial nomination: **600**
- French: NCLC 7+ all four abilities, plus CLB 4 or lower in English (or no test): 25; NCLC 7+ plus CLB 5+ in all four English abilities: 50
  - **Separate from French-proficiency rounds**, which need NCLC 7 in all four and give a lower cut-off (382 to 435 in 2026, old CRS). The same test opens both. Enter TEF Canada scores from the "équivalence ancien score" column, not the score out of 699 (IRCC language test page, 2026-10-16). NCLC 7 on that column: speaking 310, listening 249, reading 207, writing 310.
- Post-secondary education in Canada: 1-2 yr credential 15; 3+ yr credential 30
- Sibling in Canada (18+, citizen or PR): 15
- Job offer: no longer an additional factor (moved into core as of 2026-11-03)

### What work experience counts (MI ss.15.1, 25, read 2026-11-09)
- **Different NOCs add together** if each is TEER 0-3; each five-digit NOC must be specified; lead statement and main duties, including all essential ones, must be met.
- Last 10 years. Full-time is 30 h/week; part-time by equivalent. Excess hours or simultaneous full-time jobs count as one period (s.15.1(5)).
- **Canadian:** excludes periods of full-time study, self-employment (except publicly funded physicians) and unauthorized work; temporary resident status required (s.15.1(6)).
- **Foreign:** paid wages or commission, outside Canada, TEER 0-3 (s.25).

### High-wage occupation list (IRCC page, effective 2026-11-03)
- High-wage = NOC whose median wage is at least **1.3x the median wage** of the whole Canadian workforce. Levels: **2x**, **1.5x** (under 2x), **1.3x** (under 1.5x).
- Data: Job Bank, Labour Force Survey and Census over 3 years (2023-2025). Each NOC gets the highest level met in any of those years.
- **186 NOCs**: 31 at 2x, 89 at 1.5x, 66 at 1.3x (our count of every row on the official list, stored in templates/ee/high-wage.csv; IRCC states no total) (H).
- No dollar thresholds published. Update frequency not stated.
- The level is locked at the ITA date.

### High-wage list (stored)
- File: `templates/ee/high-wage.csv` (columns `noc,title,level,page_modified,read_date`; NOC as 5 digits with leading zeros; level is `2x`, `1.5x` or `1.3x`).
- Copied from the IRCC high-wage page, Date modified **2026-10-16**, read 2026-11-17. List effective 2026-11-03, based on 2023-2025 data.
- Counts: **31 at 2x, 89 at 1.5x, 66 at 1.3x = 186 NOCs**. Matches the "about 186 (31/89/66)" figure above. IRCC states no total; counts are ours.
- Level meaning: the **occupation's national median wage** relative to the median wage of all occupations in Canada (2x = at least 2 times; 1.5x = at least 1.5 but under 2 times; 1.3x = at least 1.3 but under 1.5 times). It is **not** the client's own pay.
- Look up with `python3 scripts/crs.py --noc NOC`.
- Re-check the page's Date modified before relying on the CSV. If it is later than 2026-10-16, re-read the page and rebuild the file.

### Job offer and high-wage experience points (MI 2026-1, from 2026-11-03)
_Read in full 2026-11-17: MI 2026-1 (page 2026-11-03), CRS criteria page (2026-11-03), high-wage list page (2026-10-16), job offer page (2026-11-03), LMIA-exemption public policy (2026-11-03). Conf. H unless marked._

**Legal basis.** MI 2026-1 signed 2026-10-15 under IRPA s.10.3(1)(h), in force 2026-11-03. Adds "labour market integration" as a core factor (MI s.8(1)(a)(iv)). Deletes "qualifying offer of arranged employment" and adds "qualifying offer of employment" (s.15.2(2)). Repeals MI s.29 and ss.33-34. **No transitional or grandfathering clause.**

**Point values** (s.15; greater of s.15.1 or s.15.2, never both; cap 150 single / 140 with spouse)

| Route | 2x level | 1.5x level | 1.3x level | Not high-wage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qualifying job offer (s.15.2) | 100 | 70 | 50 | 0 |
| High-wage CWE bonus, 12+ months (s.15.1(8)), on top of CWE duration points | 70 | 40 | 20 | 0 |

- CWE duration points (s.15.1): single 40/53/64/72/80; with spouse 35/46/56/63/70 for 1 to 5+ yrs (table above).
- Combinations (s.15.1(8)): 70 only if 12+ months at the 2x level. 40 if 12+ months at 1.5x "or a combination of that level and" 2x. 20 if 12+ months at 1.3x or any combination of the three levels.
- Simultaneous jobs: one period for duration points (s.15.1(5)). "Despite subsection (5)", they count as **distinct periods** when deciding the high-wage level (s.15.1(9)).
- Self-employment excluded (s.15.1(6)(b)), except publicly funded physicians (NOC 31100-31102). Full-time study and unauthorized work are excluded. Temporary resident status is required. CRS page: remote work for a Canadian employer counts only if the person was physically in Canada.

**Wage thresholds: which median?**
- **National, by NOC.** A NOC is high-wage if the median wage of everyone in Canada in that NOC is at least 1.3x the median wage of the whole Canadian workforce. MI s.1 and the high-wage page. **Not provincial**, and **not the ESDC LMIA provincial median wage table**.
- Tiers: 2x or more; 1.5x to under 2x; 1.3x to under 1.5x. Each NOC sits at the highest tier it reached in any of the 3 data years (2023-2025: Job Bank, Labour Force Survey, Census).
- **Not the client's own salary.** The high-wage page says it "is **not** determined based on a person's specific job position or salary". A low offered wage in a 2x NOC still earns 100. A high wage in a non-listed NOC earns 0. _Wording trap: the CRS criteria page table says "Job in a high-wage occupation paying 2x the median wage". Read it as the NOC's median (MI s.1 definition governs)._
- IRCC publishes no dollar figure for the national median or the multiples (M: unknown).
- List: IRCC prepares and publishes it and updates it "from time to time" (MI s.1). No cadence is stated. The tier in effect **on the ITA date** applies, even if the NOC later moves or drops (s.15.1, s.15.2; high-wage page). About 186 NOCs (31 / 89 / 66); count not stated by IRCC (M).

**Which offers qualify** (s.15.2(2)-(5))
- Full-time (30+ h/wk, s.15.2(3)), "continuous duration of at least one year" from the day the **PR visa is issued**, in a high-wage NOC, and one of these:
  (a) NOC 31100, 31101 or 31102 for publicly funded medical services;
  (b) TEER 0-3, from **one employer**, paid by wages or commission;
  (c) **skilled trade**, from **up to two employers**.
- Excluded employers: embassies, high commissions and consulates in Canada, and employers under IRPR s.200(3)(h)(ii)-(iii). These are employers made ineligible under s.209.95(1)(b) (non-compliance list), and employers in default on an administrative monetary penalty. Justice Laws, read 2026-11-17.
- **Genuineness** (s.15.2(4)): an officer must find the offer genuine. Factors: the employer is actively engaged in the business, the offer fits the employer's reasonable needs, the employer can reasonably fulfil the terms, the candidate is qualified and likely to accept, and the employer's past compliance with federal/provincial employment and recruitment laws.
- A revoked offer stops being qualifying (s.15.2(5)). Update the profile; claiming it after revocation is an IRPA s.40 risk.
- An existing or new position, with an existing or new employer (job offer page). Physician public policy (ITAs from 2023-04-25): the offer may be non-continuous or under 1 year and still earn points (job offer page).

**Evidence (no LMIA)**
- **No LMIA and no LMIA-exemption code needed** for any Express Entry job offer (CRS page and job offer page, 2026-11-03). Holding an LMIA gives **no extra points or priority**.
- Offer letter: recent, in writing, signed by the employer. It sets out pay and deductions, duties, and conditions such as hours. Profile entries for new offers: start date, employer name and address, NOC (job offer page). IRCC publishes no fuller checklist. Good practice (M, derived from s.15.2(4)): show 30+ h/wk, a permanent or at least 1-year continuous term from PR visa issuance, the NOC code and duties matching the lead statement, the employer's business details, and the signatory's contact information.
- **Profile workaround** (system not updated): Work history, positive LMIA "Yes", number 00000, issued January 1, 2026, expiry January 1, 2029.

**Transition (candidates with LMIA-based offers)**
- No transitional provision. Arranged-employment CRS points (50/200) were already repealed on 2025-03-25, so before 2026-11-03 an LMIA only mattered for FSW/FST eligibility, not CRS. All profiles were rescored automatically from 2026-11-03 (up to 2 weeks; rounds paused).
- An existing LMIA-backed offer earns points only if the NOC is on the high-wage list on the ITA date. Otherwise it earns 0, even though the offer is still valid for FSW/FST.
- LMIA exemption public policy for FSW and FST eligibility: ITAs **on or after 2026-11-03**. ITAs issued before that date fall under the old rules (eAPR filed under the old LMIA conditions; conf. M on how pending files are treated: not addressed by IRCC).

**When is an LMIA no longer needed for Express Entry, and when is one still needed?**
| Purpose | LMIA needed? | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| CRS job offer points (s.15.2) | **No** | MI 2026-1; CRS page |
| FSW arranged employment (10 grid points; proof of funds exemption) for ITAs from 2026-11-03 | **No**: IRPR s.82(2)(a)-(d) LMIA limbs and s.83(1)(e) exempted. The offer must still be from an active employer, fit the employer's needs, be fulfillable, be in an occupation the applicant has experience or education in, and the employer must have no prior non-compliance | Public policy (IRPA s.25.2) "until revoked" |
| FST job offer for ITAs from 2026-11-03 | **No**: IRPR s.87.2(3)(d) LMIA limbs exempted; same conditions | Same policy |
| PNP employer streams | Provincial rules: some PNP streams require a job offer for eligibility (job offer page). Check each stream | Province |
| **Work permit to actually start work** before PR | **Yes**, unless an LMIA exemption applies (IRPR s.204-205, e.g. CUSMA, ICT, C10, PGWP, spousal OWP). The policy exempts selection rules only and says nothing on work authorization. **Derived (H on logic, policy silent)** | IRPR s.200, 203 |
| FSW/FST proof-of-funds exemption | Needs a valid offer **and** current authorization to work in Canada | IRPR s.76(2)(b)/(c) logic; express-entry.md |
| ITA issued before 2026-11-03 | The old LMIA/exempt-offer rules apply (M) | Policy scope |

## Consultant pitfalls
- Labour market integration is either CWE (plus high-wage bonus) or a job offer, **not both**. Model both and take the higher.
- Worked example (single, 3 yrs CWE in a 2x NOC): CWE 64 + high-wage 70 = 134 (within the 150 cap), versus a 2x job offer at 100. CWE wins. (Derived from the grid.)
- Spousal maximums drop (140, not 150). Check whether declaring the spouse as non-accompanying improves the score. It does not avoid the 117(9)(d) examination duty.
- Skill transferability still uses Canadian work experience. Don't drop CWE from the profile when claiming a job offer.
- French bonus requires NCLC 7 in **all four** abilities.
- **Choosing the principal applicant:** only the PA must meet FSW, CEC or FST. French bonus (MI s.32) and French rounds use the candidate's own results; a spouse's NCLC 9 earns at most 20 as a partner. Model both ways. As PA, an unassessed foreign degree costs education and transferability points and closes FSW. The PA's Canadian work drops to max 10 if they become the partner.
- IRCC's 2026 consultation report records support for removing the spousal grid. Proposal only (read 2026-11-09).
- A PNP nomination (600) still dominates. Combine with PNP strategy (02-pnp).

## Common refusal reasons
- Points claimed not substantiated at e-APR (job offer not genuine or not high-wage NOC; CWE hours short; ECA level lower than claimed). Score falls below the round cut-off, leading to refusal.
