# CRS scoring (in force 2026-11-03)

## Must cover
- Full factor breakdown single vs spouse
- Labour Market Integration Factor (Nov 3, 2026): 50/70/100 job offer, 20/40/70 experience, no LMIA
- High-wage occupation list (count, tiers) - verify on IRCC page
- History: job-offer points removed Mar 25, 2025; PNP +600; French and post-secondary bonuses

## Current rules
**Maximum 1,270** (MI s.8(2)) = core human capital 570 single / 530 with spouse + spouse factors 40 + skill transferability 100 + additional 600. (Core includes the new labour market integration factor.)

**Labour market integration** (new; max 140 / 150): award the greater of (i) or (ii). **Never both.**
- (i) Canadian work experience (s.15.1)

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

  plus high-wage CWE (at least 1 yr): **70** (2x level), **40** (1.5x, or combination with 2x), **20** (1.3x, or any combination)
- (ii) Qualifying high-wage job offer (s.15.2): **100** (2x), **70** (1.5x), **50** (1.3x), 0 if not high-wage
- Caps: 150 single, 140 with spouse (s.15(2))

### B. Spouse factors (max 40)
- Education (max 10): secondary 2; 1-yr 6; 2-yr 7; bachelor's 8; two+ credentials 9; master's/professional 10; doctorate 10
- First official language per ability (max 20): CLB 5-6: 1; CLB 7-8: 3; CLB 9+: 5
- Canadian work experience (max 10): 1 yr 5; 2 yrs 7; 3 yrs 8; 4 yrs 9; 5+ yrs 10

### C. Skill transferability (max 100; same for single and spouse)

| Combination | Lower tier | Upper tier |
|---|---|---|
| Education + language | CLB 7+ (any under 9): 13 (1-yr+) / 25 (two+ credentials, master's, PhD) | CLB 9+ all four: 25 / 50 |
| Education + CWE | 1 yr CWE: 13 / 25 | 2+ yrs: 25 / 50 |
| Foreign experience + language | CLB 7+ (any under 9): 13 (1-2 yrs) / 25 (3+ yrs) | CLB 9+: 25 / 50 |
| Foreign experience + CWE | 1 yr CWE: 13 / 25 | 2+ yrs CWE: 25 / 50 |
| Certificate of qualification + language | CLB 5+ (any under 7): 25 | CLB 7+ all four: 50 |
- Each pair (education, foreign experience) is capped at 50; certificate of qualification is capped at 50; total capped at 100.

### D. Additional points (max 600)
- Provincial or territorial nomination: **600**
- French: NCLC 7+ all four abilities, plus CLB 4 or lower in English (or no test): 25; NCLC 7+ plus CLB 5+ in all four English abilities: 50
  - **Separate from French-proficiency rounds**, which need NCLC 7 in all four and give a lower cut-off (382 to 435 in 2026, old CRS). The same test opens both. Enter TEF Canada scores from the "équivalence ancien score" column, not the score out of 699 (IRCC language test page, 2026-10-16). NCLC 7 on that column: speaking 310, listening 249, reading 207, writing 310.
- Post-secondary education in Canada: 1-2 yr credential 15; 3+ yr credential 30
- Sibling in Canada (18+, citizen or PR): 15
- Job offer: no longer an additional factor (moved into core as of 2026-11-03)

### What work experience counts (MI ss.15.1, 25, read 2026-11-09)
- **Different NOCs add together** if each is TEER 0-3; each five-digit NOC must be specified; lead statement and main duties, including all essential ones, must be met.
- Last 10 years. Full-time is 30 h/week; part-time by equivalent. Excess hours or simultaneous full-time jobs count as one period (s.15.1(5)).
- **Canadian:** excludes periods of full-time study, self-employment (except publicly funded physicians) and unauthorized work; temporary resident status required (s.15.1(6)).
- **Foreign:** paid wages or commission, outside Canada, TEER 0-3 (s.25).

### High-wage occupation list (IRCC page, effective 2026-11-03)
- High-wage = NOC whose median wage is at least **1.3x the median wage** of the whole Canadian workforce. Levels: **2x**, **1.5x** (under 2x), **1.3x** (under 1.5x).
- Data: Job Bank, Labour Force Survey and Census over 3 years (2023-2025). Each NOC gets the highest level met in any of those years.
- About **186 NOCs**: about 31 at 2x, 89 at 1.5x, 66 at 1.3x (counted from the list; IRCC states no total). Matches baseline secondary figures. Conf. M on exact count (reverify-list item 2).
- No dollar thresholds published. Update frequency not stated.
- The level is locked at the ITA date.

### Qualifying job offer (s.15.2)
- Full-time (at least 30 h/wk), continuous, at least 1 yr from PR visa issuance, in a high-wage NOC, and:
  (a) NOC 31100/31101/31102 for publicly funded medical services, or
  (b) TEER 0-3 from 1 employer, paid wages or commission, or
  (c) skilled trade from up to 2 employers
- Embassies, high commissions and consulates are excluded. The officer must find the offer genuine. A revoked offer stops counting.
- **No LMIA required** (temporary public policy from 2026-11-03).
- High-wage status goes by the NOC's median wage, **not the person's own salary** (IRCC high-wage page).
- **Profile workaround (IRCC job offer page 2026-11-03, read 2026-11-09):** system not yet updated. Answer "Yes" to positive LMIA; LMIA number 00000; issuance January 1, 2026; expiry January 1, 2029. An LMIA gives no extra points or priority.
- Public policy covers FSW and FST eligibility for ITAs on or after 2026-11-03; employer must have no previous non-compliance with employment or recruitment laws. Does not authorize work: a work permit still needs an LMIA or exemption.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-03-25: arranged-employment additional points (50/200) repealed (H)
- 2026-11-03: Labour Market Integration factor up to 150 (140 with spouse); job offer 50/70/100 by wage tier, no LMIA; high-wage CWE 20/40/70 (H) - **confirmed**
- 2026-11-03: old core "Canadian work experience" factor folded into labour market integration (H)
- Profiles recalculated automatically; rounds paused up to 2 weeks (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Labour market integration is either CWE (plus high-wage bonus) or a job offer, **not both**. Model both and take the higher.
- Worked example (single, 3 yrs CWE in a 2x NOC): CWE 64 + high-wage 70 = 134 (within the 150 cap), versus a 2x job offer at 100. CWE wins. (Derived from the grid.)
- Spousal maximums drop (140, not 150). Check whether declaring the spouse as non-accompanying improves the score. It does not avoid the 117(9)(d) examination duty.
- Skill transferability still uses Canadian work experience. Don't drop CWE from the profile when claiming a job offer.
- French bonus requires NCLC 7 in **all four** abilities.
- **Choosing the principal applicant:** only the PA must meet FSW, CEC or FST. French bonus (MI s.32) and French rounds use the candidate's own results; a spouse's NCLC 9 earns at most 20 as a partner. Model both ways. As PA, an unassessed foreign degree costs education and transferability points and closes FSW. The PA's Canadian work drops to max 10 if they become the partner.
- IRCC's 2026 consultation report records support for removing the spousal grid. Proposal only (read 2026-11-09).
- A PNP nomination (600) still dominates. Combine with PNP strategy (02-pnp).

## Common refusal reasons
- Points claimed not substantiated at e-APR (job offer not genuine or not high-wage NOC; CWE hours short; ECA level lower than claimed). Score falls below the round cut-off, leading to refusal.

## Re-verify before advising
- High-wage list changes (no stated cadence); exact count per tier
- Intermediate age points (17-19, 30-44)
- LMIA exemption public policy still in force
