# Nunavut

## Must cover
- No PNP; options for clients

## Current rules
- **No PNP.** IRCC: "Quebec and Nunavut don't have programs." (PNP page, 2026-09-14) (H)
- Options for clients in or going to Nunavut:
  - **Express Entry** (FSW, CEC, FST). A job offer in a high-wage NOC now earns CRS points without an LMIA (since 2026-11-03).
  - **Work permits**: LMIA (employer in Nunavut) or IMP. Then CEC after 1 yr of skilled work.
  - **Family sponsorship**; **H&C**; refugee routes.
  - Another province's PNP if the client will actually live there. Intent to reside is assessed by that province (SOR/2026-63). Misrepresentation risk if not genuine.
- The Nunavut government immigration page (https://www.gov.nu.ca/en/immigration/immigrate-nunavut) could not be read (403).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- None.

## Consultant pitfalls
- Never suggest a PNP nomination elsewhere while the client intends to settle in Nunavut. That is an intent-to-reside misrepresentation risk (IRPA s.40).
