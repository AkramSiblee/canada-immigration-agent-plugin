# Yukon (YNP)

## Must cover
- Skilled Worker, Critical Impact, Express Entry (no Business stream in 2026); EOI windows; priorities

## Current rules
_Official YNP page read via browser 2026-11-07 (page updated 2026-11-02)._

### Streams (3 only)
- **Skilled Worker**: TEER 0-3, Yukon job offer.
- **Critical Impact Worker**: TEER 4-5, Yukon job offer. Secondary sources: 6 months Yukon experience, CLB 4 (M).
- **Yukon Express Entry**: candidates in the IRCC EE pool.
- **No Business stream in 2026.** Francophones are a selection priority, not a separate stream.

### Process (employer-driven)
- The **employer** submits the EOI and application (1 per worker) and attends an info session after invitation.
- **Tripartite Agreement** (Yukon, nominee, employer); compliance checks at any time.
- Nominee must **apply to IRCC within 6 months** of approval.
- No consultant is required (Yukon states this).

### 2026 allocation and priorities
- **282 nominations** (IRCC maximum). Intake #1 used 75% of it. Unused nominations must be used by 2026-12-31; no carry-forward. EOIs don't carry over between intakes.
- **Priorities:** regulated health care professionals; rural Yukon employers; foreign nationals with at least 1 yr living and working in Yukon, Yukon University graduates, Francophones, or holders of a 2024/2025 Temporary Measure Letter of Support.
- No published numerical EOI scoring.

### 2026 dates (both intakes CLOSED)
- EOI #1 Jan 19-30; ITAs Feb 9-13; applications due Mar 4.
- EOI #2 Jul 6-17; ITAs Aug 3-7; applications due Aug 26.

### Fees
- Not published on the yukon.ca pages read (2026-11-07). Ask YNP: yukon.nominee@yukon.ca, 867-667-5131.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026: intake-window model with 2 EOI windows; allocation 282; no Business stream (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- No open Yukon intake until the 2027 windows are announced. Don't promise a Yukon nomination for late-2026 files.
- Job offer and Yukon employer participation are mandatory. The employer drives the process.

## Common refusal reasons
- Employer not meeting recruitment/wage requirements; worker not meeting stream language/experience

## Re-verify before advising
- 2027 intake dates and allocation (official yukon.ca)
- Fees
