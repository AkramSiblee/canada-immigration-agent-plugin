# New Brunswick (NBPNP)

## Must cover
- Skilled Worker, NB Express Entry, Strategic Initiative; NB Experience limits
- AIP designation pause; allocation; fees

## Current rules
Official site: welcomenb.ca now redirects to gnb.ca.

### Streams
| Stream | Pathways |
|---|---|
| NB Skilled Worker | NB Experience; NB Graduates; NB Priorities |
| NB Express Entry | Employment in New Brunswick |
| NB Strategic Initiative | Francophone Workers in NB; NB Francophone Priorities |
| NB Business Immigration | Single stream (see Business streams). Entrepreneurial Stream closed/replaced; Post-Graduate Entrepreneurial not listed |
| Pilots | NB Critical Worker Pilot; NB Private Career College Graduate Pilot (extended to end of 2027) |

### Restrictions
- **From 2026-02-03:** no EOIs considered (SW, EE) from candidates in **accommodation and food services (NAICS 72)**. Certain NOCs are excluded regardless of sector, e.g. 14400, 60020, 62010, 64100, 65100, 94142, 95106, 95107.
- **From 2026-05-04:** NB Experience invitations limited to **health care, education, construction** (limited allocation remaining).
- **AIP:** employer designation intake **paused for the rest of 2026** (from 2026-09-14). From 2026-10-14, AIP endorsement is open to all occupations except NOCs 14400, 60030, 62020, 94142; TEER 5 ineligible.

### EOI
- Valid **365 days**. Not first-come, first-served. Selection by labour need and allocation.

## Business streams

### Status (as of 2026-11-17)
- **NB Business Immigration Stream (NBBIS):** listed as current and active on BIS-PAGE. EOI system; invitation not guaranteed. No intake pause notice found on important-notices (read 2026-11-17) (H).
- **2026 EOI selections:** **none published.** The invitation-rounds page lists AIP, SW, EE and SI only (read 2026-11-17). Volume/frequency of business invitations is **unknown** (H that none are published).
- **Entrepreneurial Stream:** **closed**. Replaced by NBBIS; its forms (NB-013ES, NB-030ES, NB-032ES, NB-033ES refund) are for existing clients only (BIS-PAGE) (H).
- **Post-Graduate Entrepreneurial Stream:** **not listed** among current streams on the official NB PNP page (read 2026-11-17). Treat as not available; secondary sites still describe it and are outdated. No official closure notice found (M).
- **NAICS 72 restriction (2026-02-03)** applies to **SW and EE only** per notice text; it does **not** name NBBIS (H). Restaurants/hotels are not on the NBBIS ineligible list, except small inns/boutique hotels and B&Bs (see below). ImmigrationNB can still limit business types for labour-market/allocation reasons (BIS-PAGE).

### Eligibility (BIS-PAGE + BIS-GUIDE, H)
- Age 19-59 (locked at application after ITA).
- Language: **CLB 4** in all four skills; test < 2 years old; no combining tests.
- Education: Canadian high school diploma or foreign equivalent; foreign credential needs ECA < 5 years old.
- Experience: 2 years in last 5 as owner (51%+, managing daily operations, supervising 2+ employees) or senior manager of a for-profit business.
- Personal net worth: **$500,000** ($300,000 agriculture/farming). Verified by a designated third party within 90 days of ITA; report valid 12 months. Gifts/inheritances/donations < 6 months before application not counted. **No separate liquid-asset minimum in the guide** (secondary sites claim $300,000 liquid; not official).
- Designated verifiers (important-notices, undated, read 2026-11-17): MDD Forensic Accountants **no longer accepting new NBBIS clients**; use Doane Grant Thornton LLP or MNP LLP.
- Investment: **$150,000** eligible investment (pre-tax). Excludes principal residence, personal vehicles. Caps: building/land and leasehold improvements up to 25% each; inventory up to 50%; professional services lower of 4% or $10,000; vehicle lower of 5% or $15,000; first 6 months of operating costs count.
- Ownership: **at least 33.3% equity** with control (BIS-GUIDE). Partnerships only with a PR/citizen per guide.
- Jobs: **at least 1 full-time job** (30+ h/week) for a Canadian citizen/PR living in NB; not the applicant, spouse, dependants or other family.
- Points: **minimum 65/100**.
- Exploratory visit: 5 full business days in NB (excludes travel days, weekends, holidays) within 12 months before application, with Exploratory Visit Report with EOI. **Mandatory for agriculture**; otherwise optional but worth 5 points.
- Residence during the Business Establishment Period (BEP): live in NB 75% of BEP, within 100 km of the business (10 km for farms).
- Not eligible: refused for misrepresentation in any program in past 5 years (important-information page).

### Points grid (BIS-GUIDE, H)
- Age: 19-49 = 10; 50-59 = 5.
- First official language: CLB 7+ = 5/skill (max 20); CLB 5-6 = 4/skill; CLB 4 = 3/skill. Second official language CLB 4+ all skills = 5.
- Education: master's/PhD 20; 3+ yr degree 16; 2+ yr diploma 13; high school 10.
- Ownership (51%+, 2+ employees): 10 of last 10 yrs = 15; 6-9 of 10 = 12; 2 of last 5 = 8. Senior management: 9/7/5.
- Business plan / economic benefit (max 25): exploratory visit 5; 5 yrs same-sector experience; located > 20 km from Fredericton, Saint John, Moncton; investment > $500,000 = 3, $150,000-499,999 = 2; laws/accreditation addressed 3; detailed market research 5.
- Adaptability (max 5): spouse studied 1+ yr in NB, worked 6+ months in NB, or CLB/NCLC 4.
- Passive shareholders get no points.

### Purchase of existing business (BIS-GUIDE, H)
- Same owner (PR/citizen) operated it continuously 3 years; fair market value; net profit 2 of last 3 years (audited statements + CRA assessments); keep existing staff on similar terms; no receivership/bankruptcy in 3 years; goodwill max 10% of net book value.

### Ineligible businesses (BIS-GUIDE, non-exhaustive, H)
- Adult services; B&Bs; coin-operated; consultancies; online language/education; e-commerce/online-only; domain names; newcomer settlement/temporary facilities; co-ops; not-for-profits; brokerages; financial services (credit unions, payday loans, cheque cashing, pawn); home-based; landlord/rental management; property investment (narrow construction exception); small inns/boutique hotels < 5 units or < $100,000 revenue; unlicensed professional practices; passive investment; run remotely from outside NB; seasonal (except eligible tourism/agriculture); controlled substances/illegal goods; adult residential/long-term care; hobby farms; anything bringing ImmigrationNB into disrepute.

### Process and deadlines (BIS-PAGE + BIS-GUIDE, H)
1. EOI (free; valid 12 months) -> ITA.
2. Complete application within **90 calendar days** of ITA, with checklist NBBIS-001, business plan NBBIS-BP, NBBIS-002 net worth, NBBIS-003 source of funds, NB-005. Pay **$2,000 non-refundable** fee via INB portal (no extra fee for family). Interview possible.
3. Approval -> **work permit support letter** (discretionary). Sign and return **Business Performance Agreement (BPA) within 60 days** of the support letter.
4. Apply to IRCC for work permit. **Guide and page do not name the IRCC code.** IRCC's instructions (C60 page, 2026-04-16) put provincial business candidates seeking PR under **C60** (R205(a)), then T13 after nomination (05-temporary-residence/work-permits-lmia-exempt.md).
5. NBBIS-004 arrival report within **1 month** of arrival; optional NBBIS-007 business plan change within **3 months**; NBBIS-005 business opening within **9 months**.
6. BEP = 2 years from arrival. BPA requires operating **1 year within the BEP**; nomination request (NBBIS-006) may be made after **6 consecutive months** of operation with BPA conditions and investment met. Read both together (see conflicts).
7. NBBIS-006 to bis-vie@gnb.ca with: registration, permits/licences, bank statements, invoices proving eligible investment, CPA-prepared financial statements, business tax assessments, payroll records. Site visit (may be unannounced) and interview. Then nomination certificate -> PR application.
- **Deposit:** **none** in the current guide or page. The $100,000 good-faith deposit (refund form NB-033ES) belongs to the closed Entrepreneurial Stream. Secondary sites still cite $100,000 for NBBIS; not official (H on official silence).
- Non-compliance (late arrival report, not opening in 9 months, not operating 6 consecutive months, BPA breach) is reported to IRCC.

### Refusal and review (BIS-GUIDE, H)
- Grounds: ineligibility, missing documents, unsatisfactory net worth evidence, unapproved verifier, misrepresentation, missed interview, BPA not returned in time.
- **No appeal process**; fee not refunded. Misrepresentation: 5-year ban and report to IRCC. Withdrawal allowed (NB-011) unless misrepresentation suspected. Only judicial review at Federal Court remains (lawyer escalation).

### Business stream pitfalls
- Using outdated secondary figures (CLB 5, 2-year post-secondary, $250,000 investment, $100,000 deposit, $300,000 liquid). Official: CLB 4, high school, $150,000, no deposit.
- MDD no longer taking new clients; choosing an unapproved verifier is a refusal ground.
- Missing the 90-day application or 60-day BPA deadline.
- Partnership with another foreign national, or passive ownership: guide requires active management and partnership only with PR/citizen.
- Job filled by a family member does not count.
- Excluded business types (B&B, consultancy, e-commerce, home-based, rental). Non-agriculture clients skipping the exploratory visit lose 5 points.
- Living outside NB or > 100 km away during the BEP.
- Source of funds narrative (NBBIS-003) gaps: flag IRPA s.40 misrepresentation risk.

## Consultant pitfalls
- Clients in restaurants or hotels (NAICS 72) currently have no NB PNP route through SW/EE. Consider AIP with an already-designated employer.
- Check the excluded NOC list even outside NAICS 72.
- New AIP employer designations are paused. Only already-designated employers can endorse in 2026.
- Strategic Initiative is the Francophone route (NCLC-based). It has the highest 2026 volume.

## Common refusal reasons
- Sector/NOC excluded; insufficient NB connection; job offer issues; language
