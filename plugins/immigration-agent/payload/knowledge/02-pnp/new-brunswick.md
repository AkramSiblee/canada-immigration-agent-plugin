# New Brunswick (NBPNP)

## Must cover
- Skilled Worker, NB Express Entry, Strategic Initiative; NB Experience limits
- AIP designation pause; allocation; fees

## Current rules
Official site: welcomenb.ca now redirects to gnb.ca.

### Streams
| Stream | Pathways |
|---|---|
| NB Skilled Worker | NB Experience; NB Graduates; NB Priorities |
| NB Express Entry | Employment in New Brunswick |
| NB Strategic Initiative | Francophone Workers in NB; NB Francophone Priorities |
| NB Business Immigration | — |
| Pilots | NB Critical Worker Pilot; NB Private Career College Graduate Pilot (extended to end of 2027) |

### Restrictions
- **From 2026-02-03:** no EOIs considered (SW, EE) from candidates in **accommodation and food services (NAICS 72)**. Certain NOCs are excluded regardless of sector, e.g. 14400, 60020, 62010, 64100, 65100, 94142, 95106, 95107.
- **From 2026-05-04:** NB Experience invitations limited to **health care, education, construction** (limited allocation remaining).
- **AIP:** employer designation intake **paused for the rest of 2026** (from 2026-09-14). From 2026-10-14, AIP endorsement is open to all occupations except NOCs 14400, 60030, 62020, 94142; TEER 5 ineligible.

### EOI
- Valid **365 days**. Not first-come, first-served. Selection by labour need and allocation.

- 2026-10-14: AIP endorsement restriction loosened (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Clients in restaurants or hotels (NAICS 72) currently have no NB PNP route through SW/EE. Consider AIP with an already-designated employer.
- Check the excluded NOC list even outside NAICS 72.
- New AIP employer designations are paused. Only already-designated employers can endorse in 2026.
- Strategic Initiative is the Francophone route (NCLC-based). It has the highest 2026 volume.

## Common refusal reasons
- Sector/NOC excluded; insufficient NB connection; job offer issues; language
