# Ontario (OINP)

## Must cover
- Ontario Workforce Priority stream, pathways, EOI, phase 2 plans
- Closed legacy streams (do not file), allocation, fees

## Current rules
**One stream only: Ontario Workforce Priority ("OWP" in this file only; elsewhere OWP = open work permit).** "All other streams are now closed." O. Reg. 422/17 amendments in force 2026-06-25; announced 2026-06-26; **EOI portal opened 2026-08-04**.

### OWP pathways
| Pathway | Key requirements |
|---|---|
| TEER 0-3 job offer | Full-time permanent Ontario job offer (any TEER 0-3 NOC). Experience: 6 months consecutive full-time in the offered position in the last 12 months (3 months for recent Ontario grads), OR 2 yrs cumulative in last 5 yrs in the same NOC, OR required licence. CLB 6 (CLB 5 for listed trades). Post-secondary of at least 1 yr (secondary for listed trades) |
| TEER 4-5 | 9 months cumulative in last 2 yrs; CLB 4; Canadian secondary diploma |
| Self-employed physicians | No job offer; OHIP billing number; CPSO member in good standing |
- **Express Entry option:** TEER 0-3 job-offer and physician applicants.

### EOI scoring (examples from stream page)
- TEER 0/1 = 9; TEER 2/3 = 6
- wage $40+/h = 15
- Ontario experience with the job-offer employer over 24 months = 18
- CRA earnings $80k+ = 10 (updated 2026-10-05)
- CLB 9+ = 15
- master's = 8
- regional: Northern Ontario 15, Toronto 0
- The full grid is on the stream page. **OWP draw results and cut-offs: none published yet (2026-11-07).**

### Employer requirements (stream page 2026-10-20 and employer guide, read 2026-11-09) (H)
- **Active business at least 3 years**; premises in Ontario; no outstanding ESA or OHSA orders.
- Revenue: GTA $1,000,000; listed census divisions (Ottawa, Waterloo, Hamilton, Simcoe, Middlesex, Niagara, Essex, Wellington, Greater Sudbury, Frontenac, Brant, Peterborough, Hastings, Thunder Bay) $500,000, both in the most recently completed fiscal year; elsewhere $250,000 in the last two completed fiscal years.
- Full-time citizen or PR staff at the location: 5 GTA, 3 elsewhere.
- Wage: regional median; low wage level for a recent Ontario graduate in TEER 0-3. Bonuses, commissions and non-financial pay excluded.
- Recent Ontario graduate: eligible credential within the last 3 years, 3 consecutive months in the position in the last 12.
- Employer submits job offer first; EOI within 30 days of that.

### Timelines
- EOI valid 12 months
- After invitation: employer portion within **14 days**; applicant application and fee within **17 days**
- Nomination certificate valid **6 months**; PR application to IRCC within that time
- EE route: accept within 30 days, then e-APR within 60 days of ITA

### Fees
- OWP application: **$1,500** (job or practice outside GTA) / **$2,000** (inside GTA: Toronto, Durham, Halton, Peel, York). EOI free.

### Allocation
- **2026: 14,119 nominations** (2026-02-06).

### Closed legacy streams (do NOT file; EOI closed 2026-06-26)
- Employer Job Offer: Foreign Worker / In-Demand Skills / International Student
- Master's Graduate; PhD Graduate
- Human Capital Priorities; French-Speaking Skilled Worker; Skilled Trades (EE)
- Last Employer Job Offer draw: 2026-04-30 (GTA, 1,063 invitations)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Clients with old-stream advice (Master's/PhD without a job offer, HCP via EE NOI) **have no OINP route now**. Re-assess: they need a TEER 0-3 job offer, or must use federal routes.
- The 6-month-in-position rule is measured in the **offered position**, within the last 12 months. Check the client's current role matches.
- GTA fee depends on job location, not the applicant's residence.
- The 14/17-day post-invitation deadlines are short. Prepare employer documents before the EOI.
- The TEER 4-5 pathway needs a **Canadian** secondary diploma, which excludes many foreign-educated workers.

## Common refusal reasons
- Job offer not genuine / employer criteria not met; experience not in the offered NOC; language below CLB; misrepresented EOI points (points must be provable at application)
