### Alberta: Rural Renewal
- 2-step: community endorsement, then AAIP EOI and invitation. Do not apply without an **Endorsement of Candidate letter**, valid 1 year from issue and required at submission and assessment.
- Community tests: population under 100,000, outside Calgary and Edmonton CMAs; minimum 150 points; designation 3 yrs, extendable to 5. Each community gets a yearly allocation.
- Candidates: temporary workers in Canada with a valid permit, or people outside Canada. Must intend to live and work in the community.
- Job offer: full-time 30+ h, established business in the community. Excludes part-time, casual, seasonal, contractors, owners, temp agencies, home-based, remote.
- Experience: 12 months full-time in last 18 months. TEER map: offer 0 takes 0-1; 1 takes 0-2; 2 and 3 take 1-4; 4 takes 2-5; 5 takes 5, same NOC.
- Occupations: TEER 0-5 if working in Alberta on a valid permit; **TEER 0-3 only if outside Alberta or outside Canada**.
- Excluded NOCs: 00010, 60040*, 41100, 51111, 51122, 42200*, 53121, 53122, 53124, 53200, 33100*, 44100, 44101, 64321, 55109, 65229, 85101.
- Language: CLB 5 (TEER 0-3), CLB 4 (TEER 4-5), under 2 yrs old. Education: high school with ECA.

### Alberta: Rural Entrepreneur
- Currently accepting EOIs. Requires a **Community Support Letter** and an exploratory visit.
- 3 yrs as active owner/manager or 4 yrs as senior manager in last 10; high school with ECA; CLB 4; net worth $300,000; investment $100,000; 51% ownership (100% for succession); 1 full-time job for a citizen or PR, not a relative (not for succession). Grid maximum 175.
- Ineligible: passive investment, rentals, home-based, B&B, seasonal or project businesses, payday loans.

**Participating communities (57):** Brooks, Camrose, Cold Lake, Grande Prairie, Lacombe, Wetaskiwin (cities); Camrose, Wetaskiwin, Flagstaff, Foothills, Lacombe, Lac La Biche, Red Deer counties; Fort McMurray Wood Buffalo; Montana First Nation; MDs of Peace 135, Smoky River 130, Spirit River 133; Special Areas Board; towns of Blackfalds, Bonnyville, Bow Island, Coronation, Claresholm, Falher, Fort Macleod, Fox Creek, Grimshaw, High Prairie, Innisfail, McLennan, Mundare, Olds, Oyen, Peace River, Penhold, Ponoka, Provost, Sedgewick, Slave Lake, Stettler, Sundre, Sylvan Lake, Two Hills, Viking, Wainwright, Westlock, Whitecourt; villages of Acme, Berwyn, Chauvin, Clive, Consort, Donnelly, Forestburg, Girouxville, Vilna. Newest: Fort Macleod 2026-03-10, Foothills 2026-05-13. Named contacts are on the Alberta page.

### Alberta: Tourism and Hospitality
- **Only for workers already living and working in Alberta** on an LMIA-based permit. 6 consecutive months (780 h) with the same employer; approved sector employer; CLB 4; high school with ECA.
- Includes 62200 Chefs, 63200 Cooks, 60030, 60031, 62020, 64300, 64301, 64314, 64320, 64322, 54100, 65200, 65201, 65210, 65310, 65311, 65312, 65320.

### Ontario: REDI
- OINP reserves nomination spaces for partner communities. **No separate application**; applicants meet OINP rules.
- 2026-27 partners: Greater Sudbury, Kenora District, Nipissing District, Rainy River District, Renfrew County, Thunder Bay. New round announced 2026-10-14, runs to end of 2027.
- Neither REDI page, nor the OINP Workforce Priority and 2026 updates pages, says how REDI fits the new stream. The last REDI invitations found (47, February 2026) were under the old streams, now closed. Confirm with OINP before advising. Earlier partners (Lanark, Leeds and Grenville, Sarnia-Lambton) are not on the 2026-27 list.

### BC: Regional Immigration Collaboration
- Supports the **Entrepreneur Immigration Regional stream** outside Metro Vancouver. Each community sets priority sectors. Not a worker route.
- Communities: Campbell River, Comox, Mount Waddington, Powell River; Mackenzie, Quesnel; Bulkley-Nechako; Castlegar and Central Kootenay (Areas I, J), Columbia Valley and East Kootenay, Kimberley, Nelson and Central Kootenay (Areas E, F), Rossland, Trail; Dawson Creek, Fort St. John; Penticton, Salmon Arm, Vernon.
- Campbell River and Comox paused intake until 2026-09-30 and 2026-08-31; current status unconfirmed.
- **Eligibility (WelcomeBC page; guide effective 2024-05-27):** personal net worth at least **$300,000**; personal investment at least **$100,000**; minimum CLB 4; **community referral** after an exploratory visit; invitation needed. Fees: registration $300, application $3,500. At least 1 new full-time job (guide, M: search extract).

### New Brunswick: Critical Workers Pilot
- 5-year employer-driven pilot, **not open to broader participation or direct applications**. Employers: Cooke Aquaculture, J.D. Irving, Groupe Savoie, Groupe Westco, Imperial Manufacturing, McCain Foods. Application within 45 days of ITA; certificate valid 6 months.

### Yukon
- Community Program pilot (up to 3 part-time jobs; Dawson, Carmacks, Carcross, Haines Junction, Watson Lake, Whitehorse) **ended 2025-06-30**.

## Consultant pitfalls
- Alberta Rural Renewal limits applicants abroad to TEER 0-3.
- Alberta Tourism and Hospitality looks open to hospitality workers abroad but is not.
- A community letter is never a nomination.
- BC regional is for entrepreneurs only.

## Common refusal reasons
- Endorsement expired; job outside the designated community; excluded NOC; experience outside the 18-month window

## Re-verify before advising
- Alberta community designations past their 3-year term; per-community allocations
