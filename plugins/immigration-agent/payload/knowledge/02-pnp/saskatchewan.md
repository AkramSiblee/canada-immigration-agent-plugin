# Saskatchewan (SINP)

## Must cover
- Experience and job-offer categories; ISW EOI status; priority sectors and caps
- Intake windows, fees, entrepreneur/farm closures

## Current rules
_Official SINP pages read via browser 2026-11-07 (FAQ updated 2025-12-19)._

### Programs
- **International Skilled Worker (ISW)**: Employment Offer; Occupation In-Demand; Saskatchewan Express Entry; Health Talent Pathway; Agriculture Talent Pathway; Innovation and Tech Talent Pathway; International Healthcare Worker EOI Pool
- **Saskatchewan Experience** (in-Canada; job-offer based; includes Student category for Saskatchewan DLI graduates who resided in Saskatchewan during studies)
- **Entrepreneur and Farm Owner/Operator: PERMANENTLY CLOSED since 2025-03-27.** No new EOIs or invitations; existing files processed.
- **ISW EOI:** "no scheduled EOI draws at this time". Practical routes are job-offer and Saskatchewan Experience categories.
- EOI selection results PDF (product 102708, read 2026-11-09) shows latest selection **2024-09-12** (OID, lowest 88, 32 invited; 11101 among selected NOCs). May 2024 excluded list doesn't name 21231, 21232 or 11101; it names 11100. Processing goal 16 weeks; Q3 2026 published only job offer, tech, agriculture, health and SK Experience categories (2 weeks). One EOI only. Misrepresentation: 2-year SINP ban. **Consultants must also be on Saskatchewan's licensed list** (EOI page).

### 2026 allocation and sectors
- **4,761 nominations** (initial). No federal temporary-resident quota for 2026; IRCC may add nominations.
- At least **50% (2,381) priority sectors**: Healthcare, Agriculture, Skilled Trades, Mining, Manufacturing, Energy, Technology. Includes **750 reserved for Saskatchewan post-secondary graduates** working in priority sectors.
- At most **25% (1,190) capped sectors**: Accommodation and Food Services 15% (714), Trucking 5% (238), Retail 5% (238). NAICS 72, 44, 45, 48, 49 per the employer's COR. Mid-year reassessment; unused capped space may be reallocated.

### Intake
- **Capped sectors:** **6 windows** (Jan, Mar, May, Jul, Sep, Nov 2026). Applicants may apply only in the **last 6 months of work-permit validity**.
- **Priority and other sectors:** continuous intake; can apply from overseas; no 6-month rule.

### Employer Position Assessment (EPA)
- The EPA **replaced the Job Approval Form**. Submit through OASIS.
- Candidate validates within **10 days** and applies within **60 days** of the conditional EPA. Capped sectors: up to 3 candidates per EPA; others up to 10. One-time 30-day extension (request 15 days before expiry).
- Employer must report employment changes for a nominee within **15 days**.

### Work-permit eligibility
- **Spousal OWP holders**: only Agriculture, Health, Innovation/Tech talent pathways and ISW Employment Offer.
- **Vulnerable OWP holders**: all sub-categories.
- **PGWP holders**: only Student, Health, Agriculture, Innovation/Tech pathways and ISW Employment Offer. PGWP holders who studied outside Saskatchewan are excluded from Saskatchewan Experience pathways.
- Study permit holders: Student category only (Saskatchewan post-secondary completed + priority-sector job offer).

### Fees and timelines
- **$500** non-refundable processing fee (official ISW page). The reported $350 figure is not supported.
- Nominee applies to IRCC within **6 months**. Nomination extension requests within 90 days of expiry. Job loss: 45 days to find a new EPA (+ two 45-day extensions).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-03-27: Entrepreneur and Farm Owner/Operator permanently closed (H)
- 2025: federal allocation cut; SINP moved to sector-priority, in-Canada focus (H)
- 2026: allocation 4,761; EPA replaced the Job Approval Form; capped sectors limited to 6 intake windows (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Don't file ISW EOIs expecting a draw. Use a job offer (EPA) or the Saskatchewan Experience categories.
- Capped sectors (accommodation/food, trucking, retail): apply only in a window **and** in the last 6 months of the work permit. Prepare files before the window opens.
- Check the employer's NAICS code. Misstating it to avoid caps is a program-integrity issue (misrepresentation).
- Spousal OWP and out-of-province PGWP holders face pathway restrictions. Check eligibility before the EPA.
- The candidate's 10-day EPA validation is easy to miss. Missing it means a new EPA (next window for capped sectors).

## Common refusal reasons
- Employer not registered or EPA not approved; experience not in the same NOC; licensing missing for regulated jobs; language below the stream minimum; work-permit type ineligible for the pathway
