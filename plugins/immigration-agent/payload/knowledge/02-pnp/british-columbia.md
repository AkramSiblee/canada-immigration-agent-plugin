# British Columbia (BC PNP)

## Must cover
- Care / Build / Innovate / Entrepreneur streams after Apr 23, 2026 overhaul
- SIRS scoring, wage-based draws, regional rules, fees

## Current rules

### Structure after the 2026-04-23 overhaul
- Draw priorities: **Care** (health, childcare, veterinary), **Build** (construction), **Innovate** (points draws and "High Economic Impact" wage draws).
- At least **35% of nominations** go to candidates working outside Metro Vancouver.
- **Skills Immigration streams:**
  - Skilled Worker: TEER 0-3, full-time indeterminate job offer, 2 yrs FTE experience, CLB 4
  - Health Authority: health-authority job offer
  - Either can be paired with **Express Entry BC**.
- **Entrepreneur Immigration:** Base, Regional, Strategic Projects.
- **Closed:** Entry Level and Semi-Skilled (last ITAs 2024-12-10). No new student streams. Tech draws ended 2024-12-03.

### Employer and change rules (Program Guide effective 2026-06-10, Post-Nomination Guide; read 2026-11-09) (H)
- No separate employer registration: employer support needed before registering; Employer Declaration Form. Operated in B.C. at least 1 year; 5 indeterminate FTEs in Metro Vancouver, 3 elsewhere (payroll, working in B.C., no contractors); fixed place of business; business structure; municipal licence; compliance (fines 2 yrs, immigration-related 5 yrs). Recruitment evidence unless already working full-time for the supporting employer in the occupation on a valid permit.
- Changes: notify within **15 calendar days** after applying (employer, job title and duties, lower salary, hours under 30). Employer notifies within 30 days (demotion, termination, layoff, leave, delayed start, closure, ownership). After nomination: change of employment request in BCPNP Online. Nomination valid **180 days**. Work permit support letter allows LMIA-free application.
- **Conflict:** the employers page still mentions "BC PNP Tech occupations" for non-permanent offers, and the guide refers to priority technology occupations for existing nominees; Tech draws ended 2024-12-03 and the selection list names no tech NOCs.

### Registration and ITA
- Registration (SIRS) scored on human capital and economic factors; active **12 months**.
- After ITA: **30 calendar days** to submit a complete application.
- Pool size 2026-11-03: 7,235 registrations.

### Fees
- Registration free. **Application $1,750** (was $1,475; from 2026-01-22). Request for review $500.

### Processing (80% estimate)
- Application about 3 months; post-nomination request about 1 month; review about 6 months.

### Allocation
- **2026: 6,254** (after +1,000 federal top-up, 2026-08-18). BC plans to use the full allocation by 2026-12-31.

### Time-limited
- Temporary Rural/Remote Health Support: registration closed 2026-10-07 (up to 250 candidates).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026-01-22: application fee $1,475 to $1,750 (H)
- 2026-04-23: BC PNP overhaul (Care/Build/Innovate priorities; 35% outside Metro Vancouver) (H) - confirmed
- 2026-08-18: +1,000 allocation, total 6,254 (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Wage-based High Economic Impact draws use **hourly and annual** thresholds. Check the offer meets both as stated in the draw notice.
- The CLB 4 minimum is low, but scoring rewards higher language. Retest strategically.
- Job offers must be full-time **indeterminate**. Fixed-term contracts fail.
- Regional (non-Metro Vancouver) jobs get preference. Check the employer location.
- No student streams. Graduates need a job offer under Skilled Worker.

## Common refusal reasons
- Employer ineligible or job offer not genuine; wage below prevailing; experience not in the same/related NOC; registration claims not supported
