# British Columbia (BC PNP)

## Must cover
- Care / Build / Innovate / Entrepreneur streams after Apr 23, 2026 overhaul
- SIRS scoring, wage-based draws, regional rules, fees

## Current rules

### Structure after the 2026-04-23 overhaul
- Draw priorities: **Care** (health, childcare, veterinary), **Build** (construction), **Innovate** (points draws and "High Economic Impact" wage draws).
- At least **35% of nominations** go to candidates working outside Metro Vancouver.
- **Skills Immigration streams:**
  - Skilled Worker: TEER 0-3, full-time indeterminate job offer, 2 yrs FTE experience, CLB 4
  - Health Authority: health-authority job offer
  - Either can be paired with **Express Entry BC**.
- **Entrepreneur Immigration:** Base, Regional, Strategic Projects.
- **Closed:** Entry Level and Semi-Skilled (last ITAs 2024-12-10). No new student streams. Tech draws ended 2024-12-03.

### Employer and change rules (Program Guide effective 2026-06-10, Post-Nomination Guide; read 2026-11-09) (H)
- No separate employer registration: employer support needed before registering; Employer Declaration Form. Operated in B.C. at least 1 year; 5 indeterminate FTEs in Metro Vancouver, 3 elsewhere (payroll, working in B.C., no contractors); fixed place of business; business structure; municipal licence; compliance (fines 2 yrs, immigration-related 5 yrs). Recruitment evidence unless already working full-time for the supporting employer in the occupation on a valid permit.
- Changes: notify within **15 calendar days** after applying (employer, job title and duties, lower salary, hours under 30). Employer notifies within 30 days (demotion, termination, layoff, leave, delayed start, closure, ownership). After nomination: change of employment request in BCPNP Online. Nomination valid **180 days**. Work permit support letter allows LMIA-free application.
- **Conflict:** the employers page still mentions "BC PNP Tech occupations" for non-permanent offers, and the guide refers to priority technology occupations for existing nominees; Tech draws ended 2024-12-03 and the selection list names no tech NOCs.

### Registration and ITA
- Registration (SIRS) scored on human capital and economic factors; active **12 months**.
- After ITA: **30 calendar days** to submit a complete application.
- Pool size 2026-11-03: 7,235 registrations.

### Base stream (Base-PG, eff. 2024-05-27) (H)
- **Net worth:** at least $600,000, including spouse and dependants. Must be lawfully obtained and verifiable. Verified after the ITA by a BC PNP-authorized accounting firm; the report is valid 12 months and can take up to 60 days.
- **Experience (last 10 yrs):** more than 36 months as an active owner-manager (10%+ ownership), OR more than 48 months as a senior manager (TEER 0/1, supervising 3+ FTE), OR 12+ months owner-manager plus 24+ months senior manager.
- **Education:** a post-secondary credential, OR 36 of the last 60 months as active owner-manager of a 100%-owned business (ownership can be shared within the family).
- **Language:** CLB 4 in all four skills (IELTS G 4.5/3.5/4.0/4.0 L/R/W/S; CELPIP 4). Proof required with the final report, or at registration if language points are claimed. Tests valid 24 months.
- **Business:** a new business, or purchase and improvement of an existing one. A purchased business must have had the same owner for at least 5 years. The full 6-digit NAICS code is set at registration and **cannot be changed afterwards**.
- **Ownership:** at least 33 1/3%, or less if the equity investment is at least $1M. Ownership points only above 50%.
- **Minimum investment:** $200,000 eligible personal investment from personal net worth, into one location, within 610 days of arrival on the work permit.
  - Purchase: business value counts up to $150,000, plus at least $50,000 for improvement or expansion.
  - Vehicle counts up to $15,000, and only if essential.
  - Operating expenses: up to 6 months for a new business; 3 months for a purchase or new franchise. Inventory: up to 3 months.
- **Jobs:** at least 1 new permanent FTE (30+ h/wk, 1,560 h/yr) for a citizen or PR within 420 days of arrival.
  - Contractors, remote staff and 10%+ shareholders do not count.
  - On a purchase, existing jobs must also be kept.
  - Jobs must be held continuously for 180 days before the final report.
- **Exploratory visit:** not required, but scored (up to 8 adaptability points for a visit to the regional district within the last year).
- **Co-registrant partner allowed.** Each must create 1 FTE. The pair's scores drop to the lower score. The second registration is due within 30 days of the first.
- **Residency:** live within 50 km of the business (no ferry over 30 min). Physically present in B.C. at least 75% of the work-permit period.

### Regional stream (Reg-PG, eff. 2024-05-27) (H)
- Made a permanent stream on 2024-05-27 (no longer a pilot).
- **Net worth:** at least $300,000.
- **Experience:** same three options as Base, but within the **last 5 years**.
- **Education:** same as Base. The credential must be "recognized in Canada".
- **Language:** CLB 4. A valid test result (under 24 months old) **must be uploaded at registration**, or the registration is disqualified.
- **Business:** **new business only**; a purchase disqualifies. It must be in a participating community and its 6-digit NAICS code must match a community priority.
- **Ownership:** at least 51%. No co-registrant partner allowed; a local partner is allowed.
- **Minimum investment:** $100,000 eligible personal investment within 610 days of arrival. Buying a business or business assets is ineligible.
- **Jobs:** at least 1 new FTE for a citizen or PR within 420 days. Must be held continuously for 180 days before the final report.
- **Exploratory visit: mandatory.**
  - Visit the community and meet its Designated Contact Person (DCP), then request a referral.
  - The referral is valid **90 days** and must be uploaded with the registration.
  - Communities may cap referrals and add their own conditions.
  - Neither BC PNP nor the community issues TRV support letters for the visit.
- **Residency:** live within the supporting community's boundaries. Physically present in B.C. at least 75% of the work-permit period.
- **Monitoring:** the DCP checks in 6-8 months after arrival and reports to the BC PNP.

### Strategic Projects ([EI-page], 2025-09-10) (H for listed facts; no guide published)
- **Who:** a well-established foreign corporation in good financial standing, opening a B.C. subsidiary or branch tied to its core business. It must show investment readiness and capacity to expand internationally.
- **Investment:** at least $500,000 equity directed to the B.C. operations.
- **Key staff:** up to 5, in permanent full-time roles, with the experience to actively manage the B.C. business.
- **Jobs:** at least 3 new FTE for citizens or PRs **per key staff member**.
- **No net worth, CLB, or personal ownership minimum** is published for key staff ("N/A" on the EI page).
- **Process:**
  1. Information meeting (request through the contact page). Executive visit encouraged.
  2. BC PNP may invite the company to register.
  3. ITA, then application within **90 days**. Key staff may be interviewed.
  4. Corporate Performance Agreement, signed by the company and each key staff member.
  5. One support letter per key staff member. Apply for the WP within 90 days; arrive within 6 months; arrival report within 2 months.
  6. Final report **12-24 months after arrival**, then Conditions of Nomination, then nomination of the key staff.
- No SP program guide is listed on the documents page (2026-06-25).

### Registration scoring grids (summary) (H)
- **Base** (200 points; minimum total **115**; business concept minimum **40**).
  - Self-declared 120: experience and ownership 24 (min 8), net worth 12 (min 5), total personal investment 20 (min 8), jobs 12 (min 4), development region 12, adaptability 40.
  - Adaptability 40: language 6, education 10, age 8, exploratory visit 8, Canadian work or study 8.
  - Region points: Mainland/SW 0; VI-Coast and Thompson-Okanagan 6; Cariboo 10; Northeast, Kootenay, Nechako and North Coast 12.
  - Business concept 80: commercial viability 30, transferability of skills 15, economic benefits 35 (key sector or significant economic benefit up to 23; jobs 6; TEER 0/1 jobs 6).
  - Key sectors: aerospace, agri-food, clean tech, digital media, forestry, ICT, life sciences, mining, natural gas.
- **Regional** (200 points; minimum total **105**; no business-concept minimum).
  - Self-declared 140: experience and ownership 24 (min 8), net worth 6 (min 2), investment 10 (min 6), jobs 15 (min 10), community population 6, B.C. region 12, adaptability 67.
  - Adaptability 67: language 23 (min 5), education 16, age 16, Canadian experience 8, family in the community 4.
  - Business concept 60: commercial viability 30, transferability 15, economic benefits 15.
- Qualified-pool registrations expire after **180 days**. ITA selection may also weigh region, community size, start-up vs purchase, and priority sectors (Base), or spread across communities and time in pool (Regional).
- **Actual 2026 cut-offs** were 115-121 (Base) and 105-129 (Regional), well above Regional's 105 floor. [ITA]

### Application, performance agreement, work permit
- **After the ITA:** **120 days** to submit the application (Base and Regional), including the net worth report and a full business plan. Strategic Projects: 90 days.
  - The plan must be materially consistent with the registration.
  - An interview in Vancouver is possible; interviews are in English, or with a certified court interpreter (form due 10 days before).
- **Deposit: none.** No good-faith deposit appears in the guides or on the EI page. (H)
- **Performance agreement:** signed before approval. It sets the business type and location, eligible investment, number and type of jobs, active management, residency and deadlines.
  - Terms cannot be varied. Contact the BC PNP if the plan is at risk.
- **Work permit support:** the BC PNP issues a letter of confirmation (WP support letter), valid **90 days**. At most 2 letters; an extension letter can be requested when under 180 days remain on the permit.
  - Must arrive in B.C. within **365 days** of the letter (Strategic Projects: 6 months).
  - The WP "date issued" is the Commencement Date for all milestones.
  - Arrival report due within 60 days. Spouse eligible for an OWP; children eligible for school study permits.
- **IRCC code:** provincial business candidates seeking PR are assessed under **R205(a) – C60**, LMIA-exempt. C11 now covers business owners seeking temporary residence only. Since 2024 the support letter addresses the significant-benefit test, so officers focus on R200 (intent, ability, departure). (IRCC update 2024-02-16) (M-H; see conflict)
- **Base check-in:** post-arrival progress check-in due by 365 days after WP issuance.

### Minimum operation and final report (nomination request)
- **Base:** final report due between **550 and 610 days** (about 18-20 months) after WP issuance. **Regional:** between **366 and 610 days** (about 12-20 months). **Strategic Projects:** 12-24 months after arrival.
- **One final report only.** You must be physically in Canada when it is filed and stay until the decision.
- **Contents:**
  - Evidence that every performance agreement term was met: investment amount, job creation (180 days continuous), active management, and residency.
  - CPA review-engagement financial statements from a conflict-free CPA. Regional requires one full year.
  - CLB 4 test result if not already filed.
  - Documents listed in the EI Post-Arrival Guide (not read; see Unknowns).
- BC PNP may request more information, visit the site, or interview.
- **Nomination steps:** sign the Conditions of Nomination, then receive the confirmation of nomination. It is valid **180 days** to apply for PR.
  - Conditions continue until PR: same business, location and ownership; no other employer; status maintained.
  - Report any absence from B.C. over 3 weeks and any material change.

### Refusal and review (Base-PG/Reg-PG 4.3) (H)
- **Reviewable:** EI registration disqualifications, stage-1 application refusals, and stage-2 nomination refusals.
- **Deadline:** **30 calendar days** from the decision if resident in Canada (anyone in Canada on a WP counts as resident); **60 days** if outside Canada. Late requests are not accepted.
- **How:** in writing via BCPNP Online, stating the grounds, with the **$500** fee.
- **Scope:** fairness and reasonableness only. No new evidence and no re-arguing. The outcome is final.
- **Not reviewable:** cancellation of a nomination (PIPA s.6). Escalate to a lawyer for judicial review options.
- **Misrepresentation:** refusal or cancellation, and BC may refuse new registrations or applications for up to **2 years**.

### Common pitfalls
- **Ineligible businesses (disqualify the registration):**
  - immigration-linked investment schemes, passive investments, redemption options (IRPR 87);
  - B&Bs, hobby farms, home-based businesses;
  - payday loans, cheque cashing, currency exchange, ATMs; pawnbrokers;
  - tanning salons; DVD rental; coin laundries; automated car washes; scrap metal;
  - used-goods sales without value-add; real estate, insurance or business brokerage; real estate development;
  - import/export trading without value-add; sex-industry businesses; anything that could bring BC into disrepute.
  - Seasonal businesses only with year-round presence. Regulated sectors need a regulatory plan.
- **Spending before the agreement:** anything spent before the ITA is ineligible. BC "strongly discourages" any spending or commitment before the performance agreement and work permit; it is at the applicant's own risk.
- **Ineligible investment items:** working capital and reserves; wages paid to self, family or co-owners; refundable deposits; real estate; R&D; immigration, net-worth-report and CPA fees; professional fees on a purchase.
- **Source of funds:**
  - The minimum investment must come from personal funds with documented provenance.
  - External financing is allowed only above the minimum, and it costs 2 risk points (Base).
  - Undeclared assets or unverifiable accumulation can mean refusal. Watch IRPA s.40 misrepresentation risk.
- **Material inconsistency:** an application or business plan that departs from the registration concept or NAICS code is a common refusal ground. Regional: the concept must also match the referral.
- **Undeclared paid help:** paid assistance must be disclosed, and only CICC licensees or lawyers may be paid. Failure can mean refusal. Watch for ghost-consultant risk.
- **Too little time in B.C.:** below 75% physical presence, or living outside 50 km (Base) or outside the community (Regional), defeats nomination.
- **Children's ages** lock in at registration submission.

## Consultant pitfalls
- Wage-based High Economic Impact draws use **hourly and annual** thresholds. Check the offer meets both as stated in the draw notice.
- The CLB 4 minimum is low, but scoring rewards higher language. Retest strategically.
- Job offers must be full-time **indeterminate**. Fixed-term contracts fail.
- Regional (non-Metro Vancouver) jobs get preference. Check the employer location.
- No student streams. Graduates need a job offer under Skilled Worker.

## Common refusal reasons
- Employer ineligible or job offer not genuine; wage below prevailing; experience not in the same/related NOC; registration claims not supported

### EI conflicts and unknowns (2026-11-17)
- **Experience wording.** The EI page (2025-09-10) gives Base experience as "3 years as owner-manager in last 10 years" and Regional as "in last 5 years". The guides (which prevail) also accept 48+ months as a senior manager, or a 12 + 24 month mix.
- **Regional business-model score.** Reg-PG 5.2(B)(a): the table gives Excellent = 12 points, but the text says 10. Treat 12 as the grid value.
- **Registration processing time.** The EI page says 6 weeks for all streams; Reg-PG says 4 weeks for Regional.
- **Work permit code.** The 2024 IRCC update and the C11 page route PR-seeking provincial business candidates to **C60**. The IRCC T13 federal-provincial page and the 2022 update still say **C11**. Use C60 and cite the BC support letter.
- **Unknowns:**
  - whether a final-report or nomination fee exists (none listed);
  - the Strategic Projects deadline to file the final report beyond "12-24 months";
  - 2025 EI draw history (archive not checked);
  - current status of the Comox and Campbell River sector pauses.
