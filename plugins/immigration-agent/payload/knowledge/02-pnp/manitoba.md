# Manitoba (MPNP)

## Must cover
- Skilled Worker in MB / Overseas / International Education / Business
- EOI, Letters of Advice to Apply, draw log, LAAs, fees
- Regional immigration initiatives, including Morden (MCDII)

## Current rules
**Official MPNP pages read via browser 2026-11-07 and 2026-11-08 (draws, monthly data, SWO eligibility, settlement funds, regional initiatives). Fee, IDOL and annual report facts are secondary or search extracts, as marked.**

### Streams
- Skilled Worker in Manitoba; Skilled Worker Overseas; International Education Stream; Business Investor Stream
- EOI ranked by points. Invitations are **Letters of Advice to Apply (LAAs)**. An LAA is an invitation, not a nomination.

### Federal public policy
- **Manitoba Workforce Transition Bridge** (IRCC public policy, read 2026-11-08) (H): **open work permit**, cohort up to 2,700. Needs a Manitoba support letter plus a current employer letter. Expires **2027-12-31**, revocable.
  - Who: (1) holds a valid work permit and has applied for a permit under R200 or R201 plus extension; (2) held a valid work permit on 2024-05-07, since expired, applying under R200 with extension or restoration; (3) authorized to work under R186(u) on 2024-05-07 after a renewal application, applying under R200.
  - Support letter: from Manitoba, stating the person was screened and placed in the MPNP EOI pool on or before **2025-12-31**, and itself issued on or before **2025-12-31**. New EOI candidates cannot qualify.
  - Date conflict: text "Dated at Ottawa, this 6th day of July, 2026"; IRCC index and page date 2026-08-05.

### Regional immigration initiatives (added 2026-11-08)
**Basis: MPNP regional page, SWO eligibility, settlement funds and each initiative's own page read directly in a browser on 2026-11-08 (H). IDOL and annual report facts come from search extracts (M).**
- **Six initiatives listed as active** on the MPNP regional page. Listing does not mean intake is open:

| Initiative | Intake on 2026-11-08 | Key rules |
|---|---|---|
| Morden (MCDII) | **Open**, no deadline | See below |
| Winkler-Stanley (WSEDC) | **Temporarily closed** | Age 21-45 (over 45 only with 60 MPNP points); CLB 4+ (higher by NOC); 2 yrs full-time in last 5; 1-yr post-secondary; funds 50% of LICO; up to 150 applications a year (2024-25); no exploratory visit if endorsed via its labour forecast; application valid 6 months; once a year; 3-4 months to answer |
| Immigrate Parkland | **Coming soon**: reopening fall 2026, focus on healthcare | No longer run by Parkland Chamber; immigrateparkland@gmail.com |
| Southwestern (SWMII) | Not stated (page 2026-08-18) | Brandon, Boissevain, Virden, Neepawa. Run by Economic Development Brandon under SWO. Job on priority list, full-time, permanent, at least Job Bank minimum (M). Also business investors, with exploratory visit |
| West Central (WCMII) | Application form live (2025 profile) | 8 communities: Russell-Binscarth, Riding Mountain West, Yellowhead, Rossburn, Roblin, Prairie View, Ellice-Archie, Gambler FN. NOC must be on its 2023 Labour Market Survey unless already working there. 2 yrs full-time in last 5; IELTS General; exploratory report required; no age bar but 21-45 scores maximum |
| Northwest (NWMII) | **Closed for maintenance**; next intake "soon" | The Pas, RM of Kelsey, Flin Flon, Swan River, Snow Lake. Sectors: healthcare; sales and service; manufacturing and utilities; transport; automotive repair; construction and processing; natural and applied sciences; education |

- **Temporary measure to 2027-12-31** (West Central page): designated initiatives may endorse workers already in their area, including those awaiting MPNP decisions, with a valid work permit and 12 months' continuous full-time local employment.
- **MPNP settlement funds (official, H):** at least **$10,000 plus $2,000 per accompanying family member**. Applicants with an EE profile and code must meet IRCC's table. Skilled workers employed in Manitoba still show funds, but current income is considered. Not borrowed. Needs bank letters with 6-month average balance and 3 months of statements. Local initiatives can set a different test (Winkler-Stanley: 50% of LICO).
- A regional initiative is a **strategic recruitment initiative**. An invitation under one is worth **500 EOI points** (EOI ranking) and **20 adaptability points** on the Skilled Worker Overseas (SWO) grid, and it supplies the Manitoba connection that SWO requires.
- **SWO (official, H):** connection required (close relative or friend in Manitoba; 6 months' Manitoba work or a completed Manitoba program; or an MPNP Invitation under a Strategic Recruitment Initiative, which applicants cannot self-select). Minimum **60 of 100 points**:
  - Language (max 25): first language CLB 8+ 20, 7 18, 6 16, 5 14, 4 12; second language CLB 5+ 5. TEER 4-5 must have at least CLB 4.
  - Age (max 10): 21-45 10; 46 8; 47 6; 48 4; 49 2; 50+ 0; 18 4; 19 6; 20 8.
  - Experience, last 5 yrs (max 15): 1 yr 8; 2 yrs 10; 3 yrs 12; 4+ yrs 15. Full-time jobs of 6 months or longer only.
  - Education (max 25): master's or doctorate 25; two 2-yr programs 23; one 2-yr+ program 20; one 1-yr program or trade certificate 14.
  - Adaptability (max 25): close relative 20; strategic invitation 20; 6 months' Manitoba work 12; 2-yr Manitoba program 12; 1-yr Manitoba program 10; friend or distant relative 10; plus regional settlement 5.
  - MPNP recruitment missions and exploratory visits generally need age 21-45, 1-yr post-secondary, 2 yrs full-time in last 5, no stronger connection elsewhere, and **CLB 5** in each category.
  - Not eligible: refugee claimants; live-in caregivers in Canada; TFWs in other provinces; spouses of citizens or PRs; refused by MPNP in last 6 months without addressing the reason; active application with another program (an EE profile does not count).
- MPNP FAQ points applicants with no Manitoba connection to regional initiatives. 2024 annual report: 134 skilled worker nominees named Morden as destination; SWO 1,869 nominations (19.6%), 90% approval.
- IDOL (read 2026-11-08): cooks (63200) minimum CLB 5, TEER 3. **Chefs (62200) do not appear on the list.** A 2024 draw (#228) named both 62200 and 63200, so check the current draw notice. Pathways may set higher minimums.

### Morden (MCDII), City of Morden Skilled Worker Program
- Formed 2012 (M). Runs through the MPNP Strategic Initiative Pathway. City selects **about 50 families a year, about 5% of applications**. Applicants who cannot qualify alone apply for a City **support letter**.
- **Status: open.** City FAQ: the program "continues to accept applications", no application deadline. **No cost to apply**; exploratory visit travel is at the applicant's expense.

| Requirement | Rule (City page, read directly 2026-11-08) |
|---|---|
| Age | Between 21 and 45 |
| Education | Completed post-secondary or training program of at least 1 year, with diploma, certificate or degree |
| Experience | Outside Canada: 2 yrs in the occupation anywhere in last 5 yrs. Working in Morden: 1 yr with the same Morden employer on a work permit |
| Occupation | On Morden's **priority list**, **or** working in Morden on a work permit, **or** job offer from a Morden business |
| Language | MPNP-approved test under 2 yrs old. CLB 7 each band (TEER 0-1); CLB 5 (TEER 2-3); CLB 4 (TEER 4-5). Test required even for native speakers |
| Funds | Settlement funds as MPNP requires |
| Exploratory visit | Required for all supported applicants. May be waived with: formal Morden job offer; strong family connection with labour market attachment to Morden; strong family-like connection with attachment in a priority occupation; practice-ready physicians and nurses |

**Current priority occupations (2026-11-08):**
- NOC 31102 Physicians: IELTS 7 each band; educated in and fully licensed in USA, UK, Ireland, Australia or New Zealand
- NOC 31301 Registered Nurses: IELTS 7 each band; preference for practice in those five countries
- French teachers: CLB 5 in both English and French

**Other occupations** may apply only if they meet all other requirements and are working for a Morden business as a TFW or hold a current Morden job offer; or hold a unique or specialized position bringing substantial benefit to Morden.

- Priority goes to applicants with no connection to other parts of Canada. A friend or family connection relied on must continue, or Morden reassesses its support.
- Supported applicants are expected to live and work in Morden.
- Process: determine eligibility; apply online (https://forms.mordenimmigration.ca/skilled-worker-application-form/); if chosen, support letter and/or exploratory visit invitation; MPNP invitation to EOI pool; apply online to MPNP after draw; nomination; federal PR application. Morden job offer or Morden workers: nomination and support letter can support a closed work permit.
- MPNP timing: full application within 60 days of LAA (2022 policy guidelines, L); PR application within 180 days of nomination (L).
- Contact: immigration@mymorden.ca, email only. Morden is not affiliated with any representative and gives them no preferential treatment.

**Conflicts recorded (not resolved):**
- Language floor: Morden and Winkler-Stanley allow CLB 4 for TEER 4-5. MPNP's SWO page requires CLB 4 for TEER 4-5 generally, and CLB 5 for its own recruitment missions and exploratory visits. West Central says the Strategic Initiative minimum is **CLB 5**. No MPNP page sets a floor for regional-initiative candidates, so treat CLB 5 as the safe floor.
- Express Entry profile: commercial guides disagree. City page treats it as optional ("whether or not you also have a federal express entry profile"). City page governs.
- Target occupations: commercial guides list welders, carpenters, plumbers/pipefitters and cooks (L, undated). **Outdated against the City's current list.** City page governs.

## Consultant pitfalls
- LAA cut-offs in Manitoba streams are high (816-846). Manitoba connection (study, work, family) is critical.
- An LAA has a short window (typically 60 days) to submit a full application. Have documents ready (M).
- Strategic recruitment invitations follow Manitoba missions or initiatives, not open EOI.
- A Morden support letter is community endorsement, not a nomination. MPNP still decides.
- Without a Morden job or job offer, Morden is currently open only to physicians, registered nurses and French teachers (plus rare unique or specialized positions). Trades, cooks and chefs need a Morden employer. Re-read the list before each use; it changes.
- Selection is about 5% of applications. Meeting the minimums is not selection.
- Heavily marketed by commercial sites. Screen for paid "support letters" and unlicensed agents (IRPA s.91).
- Claiming a Morden settlement intent the client does not hold is a misrepresentation risk (IRPA s.40).

## Common refusal reasons
- Insufficient Manitoba connection; settlement funds; intent to reside doubts (provincial decision since SOR/2026-63); language below the stream minimum
