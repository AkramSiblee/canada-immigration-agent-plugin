# Manitoba (MPNP)

## Must cover
- Skilled Worker in MB / Overseas / International Education / Business
- EOI, Letters of Advice to Apply, draw log, LAAs, fees
- Regional immigration initiatives, including Morden (MCDII)

## Current rules
**Official MPNP pages read via browser 2026-11-07 and 2026-11-08 (draws, monthly data, SWO eligibility, settlement funds, regional initiatives). Fee, IDOL and annual report facts are secondary or search extracts, as marked.**

### Streams
- Skilled Worker in Manitoba; Skilled Worker Overseas; International Education Stream; Business Investor Stream
- EOI ranked by points. Invitations are **Letters of Advice to Apply (LAAs)**. An LAA is an invitation, not a nomination.

### Federal public policy
- **Manitoba Workforce Transition Bridge** (IRCC public policy, read 2026-11-08) (H): **open work permit**, cohort up to 2,700. Needs a Manitoba support letter plus a current employer letter. Expires **2027-12-31**, revocable.
  - Who: (1) holds a valid work permit and has applied for a permit under R200 or R201 plus extension; (2) held a valid work permit on 2024-05-07, since expired, applying under R200 with extension or restoration; (3) authorized to work under R186(u) on 2024-05-07 after a renewal application, applying under R200.
  - Support letter: from Manitoba, stating the person was screened and placed in the MPNP EOI pool on or before **2025-12-31**, and itself issued on or before **2025-12-31**. New EOI candidates cannot qualify.
  - Date conflict: text "Dated at Ottawa, this 6th day of July, 2026"; IRCC index and page date 2026-08-05.

### Regional immigration initiatives (added 2026-11-08)
**Basis: MPNP regional page, SWO eligibility, settlement funds and each initiative's own page read directly in a browser on 2026-11-08 (H). IDOL and annual report facts come from search extracts (M).**
- **Six initiatives listed as active** on the MPNP regional page. Listing does not mean intake is open:

| Initiative | Intake on 2026-11-08 | Key rules |
|---|---|---|
| Morden (MCDII) | **Open**, no deadline | See below |
| Winkler-Stanley (WSEDC) | **Temporarily closed** | Age 21-45 (over 45 only with 60 MPNP points); CLB 4+ (higher by NOC); 2 yrs full-time in last 5; 1-yr post-secondary; funds 50% of LICO; up to 150 applications a year (2024-25); no exploratory visit if endorsed via its labour forecast; application valid 6 months; once a year; 3-4 months to answer |
| Immigrate Parkland | **Coming soon**: reopening fall 2026, focus on healthcare | No longer run by Parkland Chamber; immigrateparkland@gmail.com |
| Southwestern (SWMII) | Not stated (page 2026-08-18) | Brandon, Boissevain, Virden, Neepawa. Run by Economic Development Brandon under SWO. Job on priority list, full-time, permanent, at least Job Bank minimum (M). Also business investors, with exploratory visit |
| West Central (WCMII) | Application form live (2025 profile) | 8 communities: Russell-Binscarth, Riding Mountain West, Yellowhead, Rossburn, Roblin, Prairie View, Ellice-Archie, Gambler FN. NOC must be on its 2023 Labour Market Survey unless already working there. 2 yrs full-time in last 5; IELTS General; exploratory report required; no age bar but 21-45 scores maximum |
| Northwest (NWMII) | **Closed for maintenance**; next intake "soon" | The Pas, RM of Kelsey, Flin Flon, Swan River, Snow Lake. Sectors: healthcare; sales and service; manufacturing and utilities; transport; automotive repair; construction and processing; natural and applied sciences; education |

- **Temporary measure to 2027-12-31** (West Central page): designated initiatives may endorse workers already in their area, including those awaiting MPNP decisions, with a valid work permit and 12 months' continuous full-time local employment.
- **MPNP settlement funds (official, H):** at least **$10,000 plus $2,000 per accompanying family member**. Applicants with an EE profile and code must meet IRCC's table. Skilled workers employed in Manitoba still show funds, but current income is considered. Not borrowed. Needs bank letters with 6-month average balance and 3 months of statements. Local initiatives can set a different test (Winkler-Stanley: 50% of LICO).
- A regional initiative is a **strategic recruitment initiative**. An invitation under one is worth **500 EOI points** (EOI ranking) and **20 adaptability points** on the Skilled Worker Overseas (SWO) grid, and it supplies the Manitoba connection that SWO requires.
- **SWO (official, H):** connection required (close relative or friend in Manitoba; 6 months' Manitoba work or a completed Manitoba program; or an MPNP Invitation under a Strategic Recruitment Initiative, which applicants cannot self-select). Minimum **60 of 100 points**:
  - Language (max 25): first language CLB 8+ 20, 7 18, 6 16, 5 14, 4 12; second language CLB 5+ 5. TEER 4-5 must have at least CLB 4.
  - Age (max 10): 21-45 10; 46 8; 47 6; 48 4; 49 2; 50+ 0; 18 4; 19 6; 20 8.
  - Experience, last 5 yrs (max 15): 1 yr 8; 2 yrs 10; 3 yrs 12; 4+ yrs 15. Full-time jobs of 6 months or longer only.
  - Education (max 25): master's or doctorate 25; two 2-yr programs 23; one 2-yr+ program 20; one 1-yr program or trade certificate 14.
  - Adaptability (max 25): close relative 20; strategic invitation 20; 6 months' Manitoba work 12; 2-yr Manitoba program 12; 1-yr Manitoba program 10; friend or distant relative 10; plus regional settlement 5.
  - MPNP recruitment missions and exploratory visits generally need age 21-45, 1-yr post-secondary, 2 yrs full-time in last 5, no stronger connection elsewhere, and **CLB 5** in each category.
  - Not eligible: refugee claimants; live-in caregivers in Canada; TFWs in other provinces; spouses of citizens or PRs; refused by MPNP in last 6 months without addressing the reason; active application with another program (an EE profile does not count).
- MPNP FAQ points applicants with no Manitoba connection to regional initiatives. 2024 annual report: 134 skilled worker nominees named Morden as destination; SWO 1,869 nominations (19.6%), 90% approval.
- IDOL (read 2026-11-08): cooks (63200) minimum CLB 5, TEER 3. **Chefs (62200) do not appear on the list.** A 2024 draw (#228) named both 62200 and 63200, so check the current draw notice. Pathways may set higher minimums.

### Morden (MCDII), City of Morden Skilled Worker Program
- Formed 2012 (M). Runs through the MPNP Strategic Initiative Pathway. City selects **about 50 families a year, about 5% of applications**. Applicants who cannot qualify alone apply for a City **support letter**.
- **Status: open.** City FAQ: the program "continues to accept applications", no application deadline. **No cost to apply**; exploratory visit travel is at the applicant's expense.

| Requirement | Rule (City page, read directly 2026-11-08) |
|---|---|
| Age | Between 21 and 45 |
| Education | Completed post-secondary or training program of at least 1 year, with diploma, certificate or degree |
| Experience | Outside Canada: 2 yrs in the occupation anywhere in last 5 yrs. Working in Morden: 1 yr with the same Morden employer on a work permit |
| Occupation | On Morden's **priority list**, **or** working in Morden on a work permit, **or** job offer from a Morden business |
| Language | MPNP-approved test under 2 yrs old. CLB 7 each band (TEER 0-1); CLB 5 (TEER 2-3); CLB 4 (TEER 4-5). Test required even for native speakers |
| Funds | Settlement funds as MPNP requires |
| Exploratory visit | Required for all supported applicants. May be waived with: formal Morden job offer; strong family connection with labour market attachment to Morden; strong family-like connection with attachment in a priority occupation; practice-ready physicians and nurses |

**Current priority occupations (2026-11-08):**
- NOC 31102 Physicians: IELTS 7 each band; educated in and fully licensed in USA, UK, Ireland, Australia or New Zealand
- NOC 31301 Registered Nurses: IELTS 7 each band; preference for practice in those five countries
- French teachers: CLB 5 in both English and French

**Other occupations** may apply only if they meet all other requirements and are working for a Morden business as a TFW or hold a current Morden job offer; or hold a unique or specialized position bringing substantial benefit to Morden.

- Priority goes to applicants with no connection to other parts of Canada. A friend or family connection relied on must continue, or Morden reassesses its support.
- Supported applicants are expected to live and work in Morden.
- Process: determine eligibility; apply online (https://forms.mordenimmigration.ca/skilled-worker-application-form/); if chosen, support letter and/or exploratory visit invitation; MPNP invitation to EOI pool; apply online to MPNP after draw; nomination; federal PR application. Morden job offer or Morden workers: nomination and support letter can support a closed work permit.
- MPNP timing: full application within **60 days** of the LAA (LAA page); PR application to IRCC within **180 days** of nomination (How to Apply page) (H, read in browser 2026-11-20).
- Contact: immigration@mymorden.ca, email only. Morden is not affiliated with any representative and gives them no preferential treatment.

**Conflicts recorded (not resolved):**
- Language floor: Morden and Winkler-Stanley allow CLB 4 for TEER 4-5. MPNP's SWO page requires CLB 4 for TEER 4-5 generally, and CLB 5 for its own recruitment missions and exploratory visits. West Central says the Strategic Initiative minimum is **CLB 5**. No MPNP page sets a floor for regional-initiative candidates, so treat CLB 5 as the safe floor.
- Express Entry profile: commercial guides disagree. City page treats it as optional ("whether or not you also have a federal express entry profile"). City page governs.
- Target occupations: commercial guides list welders, carpenters, plumbers/pipefitters and cooks (L, undated). **Outdated against the City's current list.** City page governs.

### Fees and allocation
- MPNP application fee: **$500** non-refundable, paid when the full application is submitted after the LAA; Visa or Mastercard credit card only (Visa Debit fails); pay in Microsoft Edge (official LAA page https://immigratemanitoba.com/mpnp/apply/laa/, read 2026-11-20) (H). Applies to the Skilled Worker and International Education streams. EOI is free.
- **Business Investor Stream fee: $2,500 CAD, non-refundable**, paid by wire transfer when the full application is submitted after the LAA. Source: official BIS FAQ (https://immigratemanitoba.com/?p=15379) and EP process page (https://immigratemanitoba.com/?p=18197). Read via search extract 2026-11-17; direct fetch blocked (HTTP 403); page dates not visible (H). Federal PR and RPRF fees are extra. Ignore secondary guides that quote $500 for BIS.
- **2026 allocation: 8,000** = 6,239 (January) + 1,761 (August top-up) per official MPNP Monthly Data 2026 page (H). Secondary sources quoting 6,239 omit the top-up.

## Business Investor Stream
**Basis (2026-11-17):** every immigratemanitoba.com HTML page returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch, and the PDF guides downloaded but could not be text-extracted. Official facts below come from **search extracts of official pages and guides** (marked official-extract) and are cross-checked against secondary guides. **A browser read is needed before advising on any figure.**
- Official guides: EP Program Guide, July 2024 (https://immigratemanitoba.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/BIS-Entrepreneur-Stream-Guidelines-final-July-2024.pdf); FIP Guidelines Kit, file dated 07-2024 but cover June 2022 and footer 062023 (https://immigratemanitoba.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/BIS-FIP-Guidelines-Kit-072024.pdf); sample BPA, May 2022 (https://immigratemanitoba.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/bis-bpa-sample-may-2022.pdf); EP checklist, 12-2023 (https://immigratemanitoba.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/bis-enp-checklist-122023.pdf).
- Two pathways: **Entrepreneur Pathway (EP)** and **Farm Investor Pathway (FIP)**. Stream hub: https://immigratemanitoba.com/mpnp/bis/

### Status and 2026 selections
- **No BIS EOI draws.** Official EP page: "EOI draws for the Business Investor Stream are not currently being conducted" (official-extract, undated) (M).
- **Interim paper/email intake (EP):** email the EOI to **MPNP-BusinessStream@gov.mb.ca** with the Business Concept Form, Self-Assessment Form and MPNP Code of Conduct. This stays in place until MPNP Online is updated. MPNP "aims to review all EOIs within four weeks". Each EOI gets either an **LAA** or a **feedback letter**. Source: https://immigratemanitoba.com/mpnp/bis/ep/apply/ (read in browser 2026-11-20) (H). The page also confirms the $2,500 wire-transfer fee and minimums: 3 yrs full-time business owner (33%+) or senior manager in last 5 yrs, net worth $500,000, CLB/NCLC 5, high school.
  - Treat this as **case-by-case intake, not an open ranked draw**. Confirm by email before a client spends money.
  - Conflict: the July 2024 guide says documents go by mail or in person; the apply page says email. The newer web page governs, but confirm.
- **2026 output is minimal:** **5 BIS nominations** Jan-Sep 2026, against 4,584 Skilled Worker nominations (official Monthly Data 2026, H).
- No 2026 BIS draw appears on https://immigratemanitoba.com/draws/ (read 2026-11-07).
- Feedback letter: secondary sources say wait **at least 2 months** before resubmitting (L; not on the official EP page).
- **Refusals (official FAQ, Assessment and Procedural Fairness, read 2026-11-20) (H):** no appeal; no reconsideration after the decision date; reapply **6 months** after the refusal letter (or once the refusal reasons are addressed, per the Skilled Worker policy manual); **2 years** if refused for falsified documents, misrepresentation or concealment.

### Entrepreneur Pathway (EP)
Eligibility source: https://immigratemanitoba.com/mpnp/bis/ep/eligibility/ (official-extract, undated). Grid, timing and BPA details: July 2024 guide and secondary sources.

| Item | Rule | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| Net worth | **$500,000 CAD** minimum, verified by an MPNP-approved third-party supplier | M |
| Investment | **$250,000** in the Winnipeg Metropolitan Region (Manitoba Capital Region); **$150,000** outside it. Must be in an eligible business | M |
| Ownership | At least **33 1/3%** for owner points. A secondary source says $1,000,000+ equity can replace the 33 1/3%; not confirmed officially | M / L |
| Jobs | Create or maintain **at least 1 job** for a citizen or PR, excluding owners and their close relatives | M |
| Experience | **3 yrs full-time in last 5** as an active owner or senior manager of a successful business. Owners score more than managers | M |
| Language | **CLB/NCLC 5** minimum | M |
| Education | Canadian high-school equivalent (secondary) | L |
| Age | No maximum. Ranking points for about 25-49 (sources say 25-48 or 25-49) | L |
| Exploratory (research) visit | Not a minimum requirement on the EP apply page; worth **up to 20 of 150 points** on the official Self-Assessment Form | H |
| Deposit | **No deposit.** The old $100,000 MPNP-B good-faith deposit was replaced by the BPA at the 2017-2018 redesign. Secondary sources still quoting $75,000-$100,000 for EP are outdated or confused with FIP | M |

- **EOI points (official Self-Assessment Form, effective May 2019, posted 08-2024; read 2026-11-20) (H): 150 max.** Human capital: age 10 (36-45 = 10), education 10, business experience 30 (owner 11+ yrs = 30, 7-10 = 25, 3-6 = 20; senior manager 7+ = 10, 3-6 = 5), official language 25. Business: net worth 10, business investment 15, economic priorities 5, **business research visit 20**. Adaptability 25. Pass mark: not stated on the form (unknown). Older secondary grids are superseded. URL: https://immigratemanitoba.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/bis-enp-self-assessement-Aug-2024.pdf
- **Process (official guide + secondary, M):**
  1. Email the EOI.
  2. LAA, then the full application, **net worth verification report** and the **$2,500 fee within 120 days** of the LAA (official EP Program Guide, H); a new EOI is needed if the LAA expires. Late files may be refused.
  3. Approval letter, then sign the **Business Performance Agreement (BPA)** with Manitoba.
  4. MPNP issues a **work permit support letter** ("Entrepreneur Work Permit Support Letter" plus a candidate letter of confirmation, per secondary sources), valid about **3 months** (secondary, L). The temporary-resident applicant then applies for a work permit. This is not PR.
  5. Arrive and establish or buy the business within **24 months of arrival**.
  6. Operate the business for **at least 6 months** before the final report or nomination.
  7. Submit the nomination request (final report), then nomination, then the federal PR application.
- **Work permit code:** IRCC **C60**, the R205(a) administrative exemption for entrepreneurs supported by a province or territory, which replaced C11 for these cases from Dec 2022 (IRCC program delivery update 2022 and the C60 instructions; search extract) (M).
  - Formal nominees use **T13** (R204(c)) instead.
  - IRCC says the support letter **should not be the only evidence** for the permit.
  - Sources: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/operational-bulletins-manuals/updates/2022-self-employed-provincially-supported-entrepreneurs.html
- **Deadlines (sample BPA May 2022 + secondary, M/L):**
  - Report **in person** within **30 days** of the date of arrival on the work permit (EP apply page, H).
  - Progress reports: first within **6 months** of arrival; final no later than **20 months** after arrival, with the business operating at least 6 months before the final report (EP apply page, H).
  - Business established and operating within **24 months** of arrival (BPA) (M).
  - **Final report no later than 20 months** after arrival (EP apply page, H). This leaves time for the 6-month operation before the 24-month limit.
  - Operate the business **at least 6 months** before the final report (sample BPA) (M).
- **Nomination request contents (sample BPA + secondary, M/L):** final report; proof the investment was made (amount, location, eligible expenses); ownership documents; proof of job creation (payroll, ROEs or T4s for citizen or PR staff); proof of active day-to-day senior management from Manitoba; business financial statements and bank records; proof of residence in Manitoba. **Exact checklist not retrieved.**
- **BPA not met by the deadline:** the application can be refused and no nomination issues. The work permit is not converted to PR.
- **Nomination validity:** PR application within **180 days** of nomination (How to Apply page, H; stated for SW/IES - confirm for BIS).

### Farm Investor Pathway (FIP)

| Item | Rule | Conf. |
|---|---|---|

| Investment | **$300,000** in eligible tangible farm assets in **rural Manitoba**. Older guides say $150,000. No speculative or passive investment | M / L |

| Fee | $2,500 (BIS fee, official FAQ); not separately confirmed for FIP | M |

- FIP intake status: **not confirmed**. The "no BIS EOI draws" notice covers the stream. Do not present FIP as open without confirming by email.

### Refusals and review
- **No appeal.** BIS decisions are final (FIP kit, official-extract) (M).
- **Procedural fairness letter:** **30 days** from the letter date to respond. This is the only chance to address concerns. Source: https://immigratemanitoba.com/?p=15370 (official-extract) (M).
- **Request for review (RFR)** applies only to applications submitted **before 2015-04-30** (official-extract) (M). It is not available now.
- After a refusal, file a new EOI. Secondary sources say refusal in the last 12 months, or for misrepresentation in the last 24 months, bars reapplying (L).

### BIS pitfalls
- No draws; only a handful of nominations in 2026 (5 to September). Do not sell EP as a predictable route. Manage the client's expectations in writing.
- Net worth must be **verified by an MPNP-approved third party**, and funds legally obtained. Unverifiable source of funds is fatal. Flag misrepresentation risk (IRPA s.40).
- Mixing up EP and FIP: EP has **no deposit**, a BPA and a C60 work permit first. FIP has a **$75,000 deposit**, a Deposit Agreement and a direct PR nomination.
- Passive or speculative investment, or management without daily hands-on control from Manitoba, does not count.
- Job created for a relative or co-owner does not count.
- Missing the 120-day LAA window, the 24-month BPA window or the 6-month operating minimum leads to refusal.
- Support letter is not a work permit approval. IRCC assesses C60 independently and wants more than the letter.
- Ghost consultants and "guaranteed nomination" business-purchase packages: screen them (IRPA s.91). A business bought only for immigration fails the genuineness test.

## Consultant pitfalls
- LAA cut-offs in Manitoba streams are high (816-846). Manitoba connection (study, work, family) is critical.
- An LAA has a short window (typically 60 days) to submit a full application. Have documents ready (M).
- Strategic recruitment invitations follow Manitoba missions or initiatives, not open EOI.
- A Morden support letter is community endorsement, not a nomination. MPNP still decides.
- Without a Morden job or job offer, Morden is currently open only to physicians, registered nurses and French teachers (plus rare unique or specialized positions). Trades, cooks and chefs need a Morden employer. Re-read the list before each use; it changes.
- Selection is about 5% of applications. Meeting the minimums is not selection.
- Heavily marketed by commercial sites. Screen for paid "support letters" and unlicensed agents (IRPA s.91).
- Claiming a Morden settlement intent the client does not hold is a misrepresentation risk (IRPA s.40).

## Common refusal reasons
- Insufficient Manitoba connection; settlement funds; intent to reside doubts (provincial decision since SOR/2026-63); language below the stream minimum
