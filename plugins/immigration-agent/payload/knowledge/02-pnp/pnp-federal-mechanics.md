# PNP federal mechanics

## Must cover
- Base vs enhanced; +600 CRS; nomination-to-PR timelines
- SOR/2026-63: province sole responsibility for intent-to-reside and establishment
- 2026 allocations and mid-year top-ups; 2027 allocations when published

## Current rules

### Two routes
| | Base (non-EE; PR Portal required, paper only as an alternate format on request) | Enhanced (Express Entry-aligned) |
|---|---|---|
| How | Province nominates, then PR application to IRCC outside EE | Province nominates via EE profile (or candidate accepts in the EE portal), then +600 CRS, then ITA, then e-APR |
| Federal eligibility | PNP class (IRPR s.87) + admissibility | Must also meet FSW, CEC or FST |
| Speed | Slower (months to years) | EE service standard 6 months |
- IRCC pages no longer use the labels "base" or "enhanced". The terms are industry usage (M).
- **Dependent child age lock-in for provincial nominees is the date of the application for nomination to the province** (IRPR s.25.1(4), read 2026-11-09).
- A nomination doesn't authorize work. BOWP: EE-PNP needs AOR, completeness, principal applicant, no employment restriction; non-EE PNP also needs eligibility assessment passed (IRCC bridging page 2026-10-16).

### +600 and acceptance
- Nomination = **600 additional CRS points**, which is the maximum additional points. No stacking.
- Candidate has **30 calendar days** to accept or reject an EE nomination in the profile.
- After an ITA: **60 days** to submit the e-APR.
- **Linking steps (IRCC get-or-confirm page, read 2026-11-09):** profile details, Nomination and selection, "Yes", choose province; give the province the profile number and Job Seeker validation code; province confirms electronically; accept at top of profile details. Answer "Yes" only after the province has agreed to nominate.
- **PT portal guide (2024-10-01):** provinces send letters of interest to the candidate's account. Not available for nomination if profile expired, withdrawn or cancelled (including expired language or insufficient funds), or at stages such as another province's pending or accepted nomination or an outstanding ITA (M). Rejecting an ITA returns the candidate to Available.
- 2026 PNP rounds: 21, cut-offs 697-805, last 2026-10-26 (checked 2026-11-09).

### SOR/2026-63 (in force 2026-03-30)
- The province alone assesses **ability to become economically established** and **intent to reside**. IRCC no longer substitutes its own evaluation (no more R87(3) substitution for these points).
- IRCC still decides admissibility (medical, criminal, security, misrepresentation) and completeness.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026-03-30: SOR/2026-63, provinces solely decide intent-to-reside and economic establishment (H) - confirmed
- 2026-08: mid-year top-ups (BC +1,000; AB +200; MB +1,761) (H)
- 2026-11-05: PNP target 95,000/yr for 2027-2029; workers cohort via added PNP allocations (H)

### Applying to several provinces (read 2026-11-09)
- No federal bar found. **Manitoba:** not eligible with an active application in any other provincial or federal program (EE profile excepted); earlier applications elsewhere must be disclosed; connections elsewhere are EOI risk factors. **PEI:** may be ineligible with an application in process elsewhere. **Saskatchewan and Alberta:** one application within their own program; no published cross-province rule found.
- No published provincial data-sharing rule found. PT portal shows pending or accepted nominations from other provinces (guide 2024-10-01).

### Provincial refusal review routes (read 2026-11-09)
- AB reconsideration: 30 calendar days, no new evidence. BC review: $500, 30 days in Canada / 60 outside, no new evidence. SK second review: $250, 30 days, no new documents unless SINP's error; reapply only after reasons addressed. ON internal review: 30 calendar days through e-Filing Portal. MB: no appeal, decisions final; refused in last 6 months and unable to address reasons is not eligible. NS, NB, PEI, NL: not confirmed. Court route: lawyer to confirm court and deadline (not verified).

## Consultant pitfalls
- Base-route nominees who don't meet FSW/CEC/FST cannot use EE. Check federal program eligibility before choosing the EE pathway.
- Nomination certificates expire (e.g. OINP 6 months). File the PR application before expiry.
- Even after SOR/2026-63, misrepresentation in the provincial file can lead to a s.40 finding by IRCC.
- The 30-day acceptance window is strict. If it is missed, the nomination lapses.

## Common refusal reasons
- Inadmissibility; nomination withdrawn by province; EE program requirements not met at e-APR; expired nomination
