# Nova Scotia (NSNP)

## Must cover
- Four streams after Feb 18, 2026 consolidation; EOI; food/accommodation pause
- Allocation (conflicting figures), fees

## Current rules
Official site moved: novascotiaimmigration.com redirects to **liveinnovascotia.com** (checked 2026-11-16).

### Streams (consolidated 2026-02-18; portal closed Feb 13-18). Stream pages read 2026-11-16 (no page dates shown) (H)
1. **Skilled Worker** (liveinnovascotia.com/skilled-worker)
   - Full-time permanent job offer from a Nova Scotia employer; 1 year of experience related to the job. TEER 4-5: already 6 months with the employer.
   - Age 21-55. High school diploma plus the training, skills or accreditation the job needs.
   - Language: **CLB 5 for TEER 0-3** (English or French first language accepted as proof); **TEER 4-5: CLB 4 by test**, even for native speakers.
   - Settlement funds. PGWP holders in TEER 5 jobs not eligible.
   - Construction tab: NAICS 23 employer, listed NOCs (e.g. 72310, 72200); high school OR construction training; CLB 5 / 4 by test.
   - Physicians tab: NSHA or IWK opportunity, NOC 31100, 31101, 31102.
   - Occupations in Demand tab: **no occupations listed at this time**.
2. **Nova Scotia Graduate** (liveinnovascotia.com/nova-scotia-graduate)
   - **Open only to NOC 32102, 32124, 33102 and 42202**, with a full-time permanent NS job offer matching the field of study.
   - Age 19-55. Program at an eligible NS DLI completed within 3 years of applying: 30+ weeks (24 for 33102), at least 50% in NS; diploma (certificate, diploma or degree for 33102). NS regulatory certification if the job needs it.
   - CLB 5. Settlement funds. No work experience requirement listed.
3. **Nova Scotia: Express Entry** (liveinnovascotia.com/express-entry; the AIP page still calls it "NSEEE")
   - **At least 1 year of Nova Scotia work experience in TEER 0-3** (PGWP work only if the PGWP came from an NS institution). Express Entry profile required.
   - Age 21-55; Canadian high school credential or equivalent.
   - Language: **CLB 7 for TEER 0-1, CLB 5 for TEER 2-3**.
   - No job offer or settlement funds listed on the stream page. A banner says candidates with a **Notice of Interest** in their Express Entry account should see the Critical Vacancies page (not read): the stream page itself does not offer a Notice of Interest instead of the year of NS work.
   - Physicians tab: NSHA/IWK opportunity, 2-year Return for Service Agreement, NSNP Letter of Interest, EOI within 30 days.
4. **Entrepreneur** (liveinnovascotia.com/entrepreneur): by invitation only after an EOI
   - Age 21+; intends to live in NS and own and actively manage the business.
   - **Net worth $600,000 ($400,000 outside Halifax Regional Municipality)**; invest **$150,000 of own funds ($100,000 outside HRM)**.
   - 3 years owning and managing a business (1/3 ownership) or 5+ years senior management. CLB 5 in all four abilities.
   - International Graduate Entrepreneur: 1 year owning and managing a current NS business (33.33%); 2-year full-time in-person NS university or NSCC credential; valid PGWP; CLB 7.

### Pauses (official pages, read 2026-11-16) (H)
- **Accommodation and Food Services (NAICS 72): NSNP stopped taking new EOIs from this sector on 2024-04-17** (AIP, Skilled Worker and Express Entry pages). People in food and accommodation jobs with an employer **outside** NAICS 72 may still apply.
- **NOC 62020 (food service supervisors) paused** for AIP job offers and as work experience under the Express Entry stream (AIP page; the notice itself is undated).

### EOI
- Free EOI; selection by provincial priorities, **not first-come, first-served**.
- **7 calendar days** after selection to upload documents and pay.
- **EOI valid 12 months** (from 2026-05-01).
- Nominee must apply to IRCC within **12 months** of the nominee certificate.

### One-time 2026 selection expansion (2026-07-14)
- For EOIs submitted on or before 2026-06-30, from people in NS whose work permit expires in 2026 or earlier and who meet one of:
  - TEER 0-4 key-sector job
  - NS graduate, TEER 0-5
  - outside Halifax, TEER 0-5
  - sales/service TEER 0-2 earning $20+/h
  - any job earning $27+/h
- "Within available nomination spaces in 2026."

### Fees (from 2026-09-01, for EOIs selected on or after that date)
- Worker streams (Skilled Worker, NS Graduate, NS Express Entry): **$1,000**
- Entrepreneur: **$2,000**
- AIP: no provincial fee

### Allocation
- **No official 2026 figure.** The NSNP pages (2026-04-27 priorities; 2026-07-14 selection update) speak only of "limited federal immigration allocations" and "available nomination spaces in 2026", with no number (read 2026-11-16). ## Entrepreneur stream process
Source: **NSNP Entrepreneur Stream Application Guide, "September 2026"** (footer on every page; file path says 2026-02; stream page shows a guide change log dated 2026-07). Full 79-page PDF read 2026-11-17 (H). "p." = guide page. Stream page liveinnovascotia.com/entrepreneur read 2026-11-17.

### Status (read 2026-11-17)
- **Open by EOI and invitation only.** No pause or closure notice on the stream page.
- **2026 Entrepreneur draws: none published.** The EOI selection page (updated 2026-10-23) gives monthly totals for all streams together, with no split by stream. The stream page's invitation-history links point to 2024 files.
- **Two categories in one stream** (p.4, 6): (A) experienced business owner / senior manager; (B) Nova Scotia graduate entrepreneur (the web page calls it "International Graduate Entrepreneur"). The graduate route is still in the guide; it was not dropped in the 2026-02-18 consolidation.
- Guide warns the stream can close or be suspended, and an application filed before a closure may not be processed (p.17).

### Not eligible: don't submit an EOI if any apply (p.5)
- Misrepresentation finding on any immigration application in the last 5 years
- Living in a country without legal status there. In Canada: TR document must be valid on the EOI date; **maintained status not eligible** (p.12, 48)
- NSNP or AIP nomination/endorsement in the last 12 months
- In Canada under the Live-in Caregiver Program
- Applied for H&C or refugee status; refused refugee claimant; removal order (IRCC/CBSA)
- **Currently studying at a Canadian post-secondary institution**
- Scholarship with a return-home condition not yet met
- Business operated outside NS; or a licence/accreditation needed and not held
- Can't be used for settling in another province, family reunification, protection or H&C (p.5)

### Ineligible business types (p.5-6, 24-25)
- Property rental, investment or leasing; real estate, insurance or business brokerage
- Payday loans, cheque cashing, money changing, cash machines; pawn broker; credit union
- Home-based business (**exception:** may be accepted if "of great benefit" to NS, p.25)
- Co-operative; passive investment; pornography / sexually explicit images
- Joint venture with another nominee applicant; commission-only pay
- Professional services or self-employed operators needing licensing/accreditation
- Anything bringing NS into disrepute
- Real estate construction and development: **case by case**, must show compelling benefit (p.6)

### Eligibility: category A, owner / senior manager (p.6-7, 42)
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Age | 21+ |
| Net worth | **$600,000 in HRM / $400,000 outside HRM**; must show it was **legally earned**. Spouse's assets may count; must be movable to Canada quickly (p.32) |
| Investment | **$150,000 in HRM / $100,000 outside HRM**, own money |
| Ownership | **at least 1/3 of equity** |
| Experience | Within the **last 10 years**: **3+ years as a business owner**, or **more than 5 years as a senior business manager** (the guide table lists both without "or"; the web page says either one) |
| Senior manager proof | Reference letters must match the NOC 2021 **TEER 0, 1 or 2** lead statement and main duties (p.34) |
| Education | Equivalent of a **Canadian high school diploma** |
| Language | **CLB/NCLC 5** in all four skills, **by test even for native speakers**. Test no more than **2 years before EOI submission** (p.42). Minimums: IELTS G R4 W5 L5 S5; CELPIP 5 each; PTE Core R39 W42 L51 S51; TEF R151 W226 L181 S226; TCF R375 W6 L369 S6 |
| Business | For-profit; sells goods or services; fixed address (ITR s.400(2)); pays tax; benefits the NS economy; owner involved day to day |

### New business: hiring rule (p.10, 27)
- At least **1 permanent full-time employee**, or part-timers whose hours add up to **at least 1,560 hours**. Permanent = works all year, not seasonal.
- Employees must be **Canadian citizens or PRs** and **not related to you**. The guide **does not define "related"**: get the definition in writing from LSI before relying on any hire.
- Work must be directly related to the business and different from the owner's work.
- Wage similar to others in the same field and area (Job Bank).
- Hiring **confirmed by a Financial Statement Review Service Provider**.

### Buying a business (p.11, 35-36)
- Category A, 5 tests:
  - same owner ran it continuously for the 5 years before purchase
  - operating at purchase and not in receivership
  - applicant and partners **visited in person** and met the owner
  - **fair market value** agreed
  - existing staff kept on similar wages and conditions
- Category B, 3 tests: 5-year same owner; fair market value; staff on similar terms.
- Required: Exploratory Visit Report (flights, hotel receipts, meeting summaries, photos, emails); 5 years of seller financials; employee list; staff acceptance letters; proof of fair market value; preliminary purchase price and evaluation.
- If no business is chosen yet, say so on the EOI (p.50).

### Investment rules (p.25-28, 57)
- **Only money spent AFTER signing the Business Performance Agreement (BPA) counts.** Buying the business before the BPA means the purchase price does not count.
- Investment must be confirmed by a Financial Statement Review Service Provider.
- **Eligible:**
  - land, buildings, equipment, software
  - permits and licences, franchise fees, leasehold improvements
  - inventory, business supplies, business-related courses
  - prepaid insurance, marketing, start-up research
  - set-up professional fees (not immigration fees)
  - **one vehicle** (more for transportation, manufacturing or construction businesses, or above CRA limits)
  - office furniture and fixtures
- **Operating expenses** (rent, wages, utilities, advertising, accounting, insurance) also count: **6 months for a new business**, **3 months for a purchase**, **none for a franchise**.
- **Excluded:** immigration fees; principal residence; anything spent before the BPA; the owner's or family members' salaries.
- **Risk factors in the business plan review** (p.57): external financing, and investing **more than 50% of personal net worth**.
- Internal inconsistency: p.26 says the investment "must not include cash or loans", yet the same page asks for the loans and lines of credit that make up part of it. Ask LSI before relying on borrowed money.

### EOI points grid, category A (p.51-53)
| Factor | Points |
|---|---|
| Export business | 5 |
| Outside HRM | 10 |
| Buying an existing business | 10 |
| Personal investment $100k-$300k / over $300k | 10 / 15 |
| Net worth $400k-$999,999 / $1M-$1.5M / over $1.5M (per Net Worth Verification Report) | 5 / 7 / 10 |
| Senior management more than 5 yrs in last 10 | 20 |
| Business ownership 3-5 yrs / more than 5 yrs in last 10 | 20 / 35 |
| Own past NS work / spouse's past NS work | 10 / 5 |
| Education: high school 8; 2-yr NS program 10; 1-yr post-secondary 12; 2-yr 19; 3+ yr 21; two or more credentials (one 3-yr) 22; master's 23; degree for a licensed profession (NS licensing body) 23; PhD 25 | max 25 |
| Spouse: 2-yr NS program | 10 |
| First official language CLB 5 / 6 / 7+ | 20 / 24 / 28 |
| Second official language CLB 4+ | 7 |
| Spouse CLB 4+ | 5 |
| Age at EOI: 21-24: 5; "24-32": 7; 33-39: 10; 40-44: 7; 45-55: 5; 56+: 0 (bands overlap at 24 in the guide) | max 10 |
| Relative in NS (19+, citizen/PR, 1 yr in NS; parent, grandparent, sibling, aunt/uncle, niece/nephew) | 5 |
- Past NS work points: 3+ years in the last 10 at TEER 0-2 (spouse: 1 year full-time on a work permit) (p.38).
- Ranking: highest score first; LSI may create "priority sectors" and select by score within them (p.50).
- EOI kept 1 year. A new EOI may be filed if points rise (p.54).
- Must be completed in one sitting (p.50).

### Forms (p.21)
- **eNSNP 100** (online, novascotia.ca/ensnp)
- **NSNP 400** (business / senior management experience; category A)
- **NSNP 500** (business description; category B)
- **NSNP 50** (representative; a paid rep must file the whole EOI through their portal account; an unpaid rep's form goes with the EOI)
- **NSNP 60** (release of information only, no authority to act)
- Business Performance Agreement

### Category A: 7 steps and deadlines (p.49-61)
| Step | Deadline / rule |
|---|---|
| 1. EOI | Free; must be honest and complete |
| 2a. Pick a Net Worth Verifier from the LSI list and tell LSI | **20 calendar days** from the ITA |
| 2b. New business: eNSNP 100 + documents + **$2,000 fee** and proof of payment + Business Establishment Plan | **90 calendar days** from the ITA |
| 2b. Purchase: the same, plus Net Worth Verification Report and preliminary price/evaluation | **180 calendar days** from the ITA |
| 2c. Net Worth Verification Report (all files) | **180 calendar days** from the ITA (p.32); a mismatch with the EOI means the file is closed |
| 2d. Business plan review | LSI may use an outside assessor **at the applicant's cost**; the plan can't be changed without written permission |
| 3. Interview | Usually virtual (in person on request), usually within 30 days of contact; missing it closes the file |
| 3a. BPA | Sign, date, scan and return within **15 business days** |
| 3b. After the BPA | Register the company (NS Registry of Joint Stock Companies) and **sign up for the IRCC Employer Portal** |
| 4. Entrepreneur Approval Letter, then work permit | Apply to IRCC **within 60 calendar days** of the letter; include the letter; IRCC refusal may close the file |
| 5. Arrive in NS | Within **6 months of the IRCC port of entry introduction letter** (exception for circumstances beyond control) |
| 5a. Arrival meeting with LSI | Within **60 calendar days** of arrival; bring the Arrival Meeting Form |
| 5b. Start operating | "Should" start within **6 months** of arrival (delays understood) |
| 6. Nomination Request (in writing to the assigned officer) | After **at least 1 full year of operation**; applicant, **spouse and dependants living in NS**; legal status kept; BPA met; required funds moved to Canada; includes the **Review Engagement and Special Purpose Report** |
| 7. PR application to IRCC | Within **12 months** of the nomination letter; a one-time extension is possible, case by case |
| WP support letter | Ask LSI **at least 90 calendar days** before the work permit expires |
| Report PR | Tell LSI within **30 business days** of confirmation of PR |
- The Financial Statement Review Service Provider must come from the LSI list, and can't also have helped write the business plan (conflict of interest) (p.33).
- Every change must be reported (marital status, job duties, location, immigration status), including after nomination (p.13, 18).

### Category B: NS graduate / International Graduate Entrepreneur (p.8-9, 43-44, 62-69)
- Age 21+.
- **2+ years of full-time study** (15+ h/week) at a recognized NS university or NSCC, living in NS during studies. The web page adds "in-person".
- **Valid PGWP** when applying.
- Owned and operated the NS business for **1+ full year** on the PGWP, with **at least 1/3 equity**. Must actively manage it.
- Salary of at least **Statistics Canada LICO**; the business must earn enough to support the applicant.
- **CLB 7** by test (IELTS 6 each; CELPIP 7 each), test within 2 years of EOI.
- No net worth or investment minimum in the guide; no work permit step.
- 4 steps:
  1. EOI
  2. Application within **90 days** of the ITA (eNSNP 100, NSNP 500, $2,000). Also a net worth vendor choice within 20 days (p.66), even though category B has no net worth minimum: unexplained.
  3. Interview **and business visit**, usually within 15 business days of contact
  4. Nomination certificate, then PR within 12 months
- Separate points grid (p.63-65), e.g. outside HRM 5, purchase 5, CLB 7/8/9+ = 20/24/28, NS master's 22, NS PhD 25, age 21-39 = 10.
- A BPA is listed among the category B documents (p.8) but not in its steps.

### Refusal, no appeal, withdrawal (p.16-18, 56, 58, 60-61)
- **No appeal** of file closure, failed interview or refusal; **fees are not refunded**. The guide doesn't mention reconsideration either way: treat it as unavailable unless LSI confirms.
- **Misrepresentation** (false information or omissions, by the applicant, business partners, spouse, dependants or representative): file closed and a **5-year bar on any EOI or "any immigration stream"** (presumably NS streams; the guide doesn't say).
- **Nomination can be withdrawn before PR** if:
  - the BPA is not followed
  - the applicant or business no longer meets the stream rules
  - IRCC reports false or fraudulent information
  - IRCC finds the applicant or a family member inadmissible on health, safety or security grounds
  - IRCC reports an invalid passport
- Applicants may withdraw before nomination, but not "without penalty" if misrepresentation is known or suspected.

### Unknowns / not in the guide
- **Work permit exemption code** is not stated in the guide or on the stream page. IRCC's instructions (C60 page, 2026-04-16) put provincial business candidates seeking PR under **C60** (R205(a)), then T13 after nomination; not C11 (05-temporary-residence/work-permits-lmia-exempt.md).
- No **good-faith deposit**: the BPA is the compliance tool.
- **"Related" employee** is not defined.
- **Application caps and 2026 Entrepreneur invitations** are not published.
- Processing times: the guide says LSI "cannot tell you".
- Inconsistency: p.10 says "4 steps" but category A has 7 (p.49).

## Consultant pitfalls
- The 7-day post-selection deadline is very short. Have the full document set ready **before** the EOI.
- Fees are new from 2026-09-01. Older client quotes showing "no fee" are wrong.
- Food and accommodation: an employer in NAICS 72 can't support an NSNP EOI; NOC 62020 can't support an AIP offer or count as Express Entry stream experience. Check the employer's NAICS, not just the NOC.
- Nova Scotia Graduate is open to four NOCs only.
- The expanded 2026 selection covers EOIs filed by 2026-06-30 only.

## Common refusal reasons
- Documents not uploaded in 7 days; employer/job offer criteria; NOC outside stream; insufficient language/education
