# Nova Scotia (NSNP)

## Must cover
- Four streams after Feb 18, 2026 consolidation; EOI; food/accommodation pause
- Allocation (conflicting figures), fees

## Current rules
Official site moved: novascotiaimmigration.com redirects to **liveinnovascotia.com** (checked 2026-11-16).

### Streams (consolidated 2026-02-18; portal closed Feb 13-18). Stream pages read 2026-11-16 (no page dates shown) (H)
1. **Skilled Worker** (liveinnovascotia.com/skilled-worker)
   - Full-time permanent job offer from a Nova Scotia employer; 1 year of experience related to the job. TEER 4-5: already 6 months with the employer.
   - Age 21-55. High school diploma plus the training, skills or accreditation the job needs.
   - Language: **CLB 5 for TEER 0-3** (English or French first language accepted as proof); **TEER 4-5: CLB 4 by test**, even for native speakers.
   - Settlement funds. PGWP holders in TEER 5 jobs not eligible.
   - Construction tab: NAICS 23 employer, listed NOCs (e.g. 72310, 72200); high school OR construction training; CLB 5 / 4 by test.
   - Physicians tab: NSHA or IWK opportunity, NOC 31100, 31101, 31102.
   - Occupations in Demand tab: **no occupations listed at this time**.
2. **Nova Scotia Graduate** (liveinnovascotia.com/nova-scotia-graduate)
   - **Open only to NOC 32102, 32124, 33102 and 42202**, with a full-time permanent NS job offer matching the field of study.
   - Age 19-55. Program at an eligible NS DLI completed within 3 years of applying: 30+ weeks (24 for 33102), at least 50% in NS; diploma (certificate, diploma or degree for 33102). NS regulatory certification if the job needs it.
   - CLB 5. Settlement funds. No work experience requirement listed.
3. **Nova Scotia: Express Entry** (liveinnovascotia.com/express-entry; the AIP page still calls it "NSEEE")
   - **At least 1 year of Nova Scotia work experience in TEER 0-3** (PGWP work only if the PGWP came from an NS institution). Express Entry profile required.
   - Age 21-55; Canadian high school credential or equivalent.
   - Language: **CLB 7 for TEER 0-1, CLB 5 for TEER 2-3**.
   - No job offer or settlement funds listed on the stream page. A banner says candidates with a **Notice of Interest** in their Express Entry account should see the Critical Vacancies page (not read): the stream page itself does not offer a Notice of Interest instead of the year of NS work.
   - Physicians tab: NSHA/IWK opportunity, 2-year Return for Service Agreement, NSNP Letter of Interest, EOI within 30 days.
4. **Entrepreneur** (liveinnovascotia.com/entrepreneur): by invitation only after an EOI
   - Age 21+; intends to live in NS and own and actively manage the business.
   - **Net worth $600,000 ($400,000 outside Halifax Regional Municipality)**; invest **$150,000 of own funds ($100,000 outside HRM)**.
   - 3 years owning and managing a business (1/3 ownership) or 5+ years senior management. CLB 5 in all four abilities.
   - International Graduate Entrepreneur: 1 year owning and managing a current NS business (33.33%); 2-year full-time in-person NS university or NSCC credential; valid PGWP; CLB 7.

### Pauses (official pages, read 2026-11-16) (H)
- **Accommodation and Food Services (NAICS 72): NSNP stopped taking new EOIs from this sector on 2024-04-17** (AIP, Skilled Worker and Express Entry pages). People in food and accommodation jobs with an employer **outside** NAICS 72 may still apply.
- **NOC 62020 (food service supervisors) paused** for AIP job offers and as work experience under the Express Entry stream (AIP page; the notice itself is undated).

### EOI
- Free EOI; selection by provincial priorities, **not first-come, first-served**.
- **7 calendar days** after selection to upload documents and pay.
- **EOI valid 12 months** (from 2026-05-01).
- Nominee must apply to IRCC within **12 months** of the nominee certificate.

### One-time 2026 selection expansion (2026-07-14)
- For EOIs submitted on or before 2026-06-30, from people in NS whose work permit expires in 2026 or earlier and who meet one of:
  - TEER 0-4 key-sector job
  - NS graduate, TEER 0-5
  - outside Halifax, TEER 0-5
  - sales/service TEER 0-2 earning $20+/h
  - any job earning $27+/h
- "Within available nomination spaces in 2026."

### Fees (from 2026-09-01, for EOIs selected on or after that date)
- Worker streams (Skilled Worker, NS Graduate, NS Express Entry): **$1,000**
- Entrepreneur: **$2,000**
- AIP: no provincial fee

### Allocation
- **No official 2026 figure.** The NSNP pages (2026-04-27 priorities; 2026-07-14 selection update) speak only of "limited federal immigration allocations" and "available nomination spaces in 2026", with no number (read 2026-11-16). Secondary estimates conflict (comparison-table.md): don't quote one.

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2025-11-28: EOI process formalized (H)
- 2026-02-18: streams consolidated to four (H)
- 2026-05-01: 12-month EOI validity (H)
- 2026-07-14: one-time selection expansion for expiring work permits (H)
- 2026-09-01: first NSNP application fees ($1,000 / $2,000) (H)
- 2024-04-17: NAICS 72 EOI pause (still in place 2026-11-16) (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- The 7-day post-selection deadline is very short. Have the full document set ready **before** the EOI.
- Fees are new from 2026-09-01. Older client quotes showing "no fee" are wrong.
- Food and accommodation: an employer in NAICS 72 can't support an NSNP EOI; NOC 62020 can't support an AIP offer or count as Express Entry stream experience. Check the employer's NAICS, not just the NOC.
- Nova Scotia Graduate is open to four NOCs only.
- The expanded 2026 selection covers EOIs filed by 2026-06-30 only.

## Common refusal reasons
- Documents not uploaded in 7 days; employer/job offer criteria; NOC outside stream; insufficient language/education
