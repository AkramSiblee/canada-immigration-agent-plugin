# Nova Scotia (NSNP)

## Must cover
- Four streams after Feb 18, 2026 consolidation; EOI; food/accommodation pause
- Allocation (conflicting figures), fees

## Current rules
Official site moved: novascotiaimmigration.com now redirects to **liveinnovascotia.com**.

### Streams (consolidated 2026-02-18; portal closed Feb 13-18)
1. Skilled Worker
2. Nova Scotia Graduate
3. Nova Scotia: Express Entry
4. Entrepreneur

### EOI
- Free EOI; selection by provincial priorities, **not first-come, first-served**.
- **7 calendar days** after selection to upload documents and pay.
- **EOI valid 12 months** (from 2026-05-01).
- Nominee must apply to IRCC within **12 months** of the nominee certificate.

| 363 | 357 | 370 | 161 | 789 | 531 | 671 | 1,212 | 946 |
- Jan-Sep total: 5,400 (derived).

### One-time 2026 selection expansion (2026-07-14)
- For EOIs submitted on or before 2026-06-30, from people in NS whose work permit expires in 2026 or earlier and who meet one of:
  - TEER 0-4 key-sector job
  - NS graduate, TEER 0-5
  - outside Halifax, TEER 0-5
  - sales/service TEER 0-2 earning $20+/h
  - any job earning $27+/h
- "Within available nomination spaces in 2026."

### Fees (from 2026-09-01, for EOIs selected on or after that date)
- Worker streams (Skilled Worker, NS Graduate, NS Express Entry): **$1,000**
- Entrepreneur: **$2,000**
- AIP: no provincial fee

### Allocation
- 2026 figure not stated on official pages read. Baseline secondary figures conflict. **Unresolved.**

## Consultant pitfalls
- The 7-day post-selection deadline is very short. Have the full document set ready **before** the EOI.
- Fees are new from 2026-09-01. Older client quotes showing "no fee" are wrong.
- The food/accommodation pause wasn't found on current official pages. Don't cite it without checking (baseline item unconfirmed).
- The expanded 2026 selection covers EOIs filed by 2026-06-30 only.

## Common refusal reasons
- Documents not uploaded in 7 days; employer/job offer criteria; NOC outside stream; insufficient language/education

## Re-verify before advising
- Allocation (conflicting figures) - official figure not found
- Food/accommodation sector exclusion status
