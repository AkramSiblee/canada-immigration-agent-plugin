### Streams
- **Employer-Driven (EOI):** valid full-time permanent job offer from an NWT employer, where no Canadian or PR is available. The employer submits an EOI for a named worker, then the worker completes theirs. One active EOI per person. Points: human capital (language, age, experience, education), NWT labour-market connection (NWT employment and time employed, trades job offer, closed NWT work permit, small community work), adaptability (immediate family in NWT, NWT post-secondary). Invited applications are assessed in the order received.
  - **Skilled Worker:** TEER 0-3; 1 yr full-time in last 10 yrs in same or similar occupation; valid work permit; language tested at application.
  - **Entry Level/Semi-Skilled:** TEER 4-5; 12 months full-time in the same position with the employer; valid work permit; language tested.
  - **NWT Express Entry:** TEER 0-3; Express Entry profile and Job Seeker Validation Code; NWT job offer; valid work permit; 1 yr full-time in last 10 yrs; certification if required. Adds 600 CRS.
- **Francophone:** NWT job offer; TEER 0-5; bilingual French and English; language tested in both.
- **Business:** equity $200,000 (Yellowknife) or $100,000 (elsewhere); ownership 33.3% unless equity is $1,000,000+; net worth $500,000 (Yellowknife) or $250,000 (elsewhere); CLB 4 or NCLC equivalent (IELTS, CELPIP, TEF; under 2 yrs old); exploratory visit and interview; 2-year Business Performance Agreement.

### Business stream detail (re-verified 2026-11-17)
- **Status: open, first come, first served**; not part of the EOI system ("not oversubscribed") (GNWT release 2026-02-18; CIC News 2026-11-11 says no change) (H). Page claims "no wait-list".
- Figures on https://www.immigratenwt.ca/business-stream (undated; read 2026-11-17) (H):
  - Equity **$200,000** in Yellowknife / **$100,000** elsewhere (cut from $300,000 / $150,000 in the Nov 2022 guidelines).
  - Ownership **33.3%** unless equity is **$1,000,000+**.
  - Net worth **$500,000** Yellowknife / **$250,000** elsewhere, with proof.
  - **CLB 4 / NCLC 4** (IELTS, CELPIP, TEF; under 2 yrs).
  - No active application in another PNP or another NTNP stream; refugee claimants are ineligible; must intend to settle full-time.
- **Steps:** self-assessment -> **exploratory visit with face-to-face interview** (mandatory; **4 business days minimum** per secondary sources, L) -> pre-screen -> ITA -> application + business plan -> **2-yr Business Performance Agreement (BPA)** -> arrival and interim reports -> final report -> nomination (H, official page).
- **No appeal**; decisions are final; denial possible at any step (H, official page).
- **Business Stream Program Guidelines, effective 2026-01-01** (official PDF, read in full 2026-11-20: https://www.immigratenwt.ca/sites/immigratenwt/files/resources/ntnp_program_guidelines_-_business_stream.pdf) (H):
  - **Fee $2,800, non-refundable**, paid at Initial Registration in the NTNP online account (s.7.0, 8.1). Third-party financial verification is extra, paid to the provider.
  - **No deposit** of any kind appears in the 2026 guidelines.
  - Equity, net worth, 33.3% ownership (less if equity $1,000,000+, IRPR 87(6)(b)(ii)), CLB/NCLC 4 in all four skills in one sitting: as above.
  - Exploratory trip **minimum 4 full business days** in the proposed community (travel, interview days and holidays excluded); interview request at least 2 weeks ahead; pre-interview submission (with language results) 1 week before. **Representatives may not attend the interview.** No interviews from the 3rd week of December to the 2nd week of January.
  - ITA -> formal application within **6 months**; financial service provider contacted within 1 month of ITA.
  - BPA signed within **30 days**; then a support letter for a **2-year work permit**. Investment made before the BPA is signed **and** the work permit is issued does not count.
  - **Residency:** live within **100 km** of the business and in NWT **75%** of the time on the work permit.
  - Arrival Report within **60 days** of the work permit "date signed" (with a Canadian bank statement showing the full investment); invest within **12 months** of the Arrival Report; **interim reports every 6 months** with verifiable invoices; business operated **12+ months**; Final Report and eligibility for nomination in the **19th month** after the Commencement Date; exit interview.
  - **Jobs:** plan at least **1 full-time + 1 part-time** NWT job (Yellowknife) or **1 full-time or 2 part-time** (outside Yellowknife).
  - Buying a business: same owner for the previous **3 years**; jobs and wages not reduced; a new location of an existing business is not an expansion; cash/working capital not eligible.
  - Eligible investment: cash/working capital up to **$10,000** (new business only), start-up inventory, 4 months' operating costs (new) / 3 months (purchase), one vehicle up to **$25,000**, fair-market purchase price, renovations/leasehold improvements. Applicant and family wages are not eligible.
  - Ineligible: restaurants inside Yellowknife, seasonal (<6 months/yr), B&B, property management, real estate development, home-based, non-profits, payday/cheque cashing/pawn, businesses owned by a nominee in the last 5 yrs, IRPR 87(5)(b) schemes, redemption options.
  - Not eligible for Express Entry nomination. Denial is final, **no appeal**; may reapply **9 months** after denial.
- **Deposit conflict resolved:** secondary sources cite a **$75,000 good-faith deposit**; the official 2026 guidelines have no deposit. Do not quote the $75,000 figure (H for the official text).
- **Work permit:** PNP support letter -> **R205(a) C60** (IRCC update 2024-02-16) (H).
- Forms: NTNP-03, -04, -05, -09.

- **Economic Mobility Pathways Pilot:** federal EMPP is **closed** (IRCC refugees page).

### Employer-Driven guidelines (effective 2026-03-09; official PDF read in full 2026-11-20) (H)
- Source: https://www.immigratenwt.ca/sites/immigratenwt/files/resources/ntnp_program_guidelines_-_employer_driven_stream_0.pdf
- **Language (all four skills, one sitting; IELTS, CELPIP, PTE Core, TEF, TCF; under 2 yrs):** Skilled Worker TEER 0-1 **CLB 6**; NWT Express Entry TEER 0-1 **CLB 7**; Skilled Worker and NWT EE TEER 2-3 **CLB 5**; Entry Level/Semi-Skilled TEER 4-5 **CLB 4**.
- Employer: registered and operating full-time in NWT **1+ year**; WSCC good standing; Employment Standards Verification Form (valid 6 months). Wage at least the NWT Job Bank median (else Yukon, Alberta, Canada).
- Positive LMIA **or** advertising on 1 local + 1 national platform: **14 consecutive days** (TEER 0-3) / **30 consecutive days** (TEER 4-5); ads valid 18 months.
- ELSS: 12 months full-time in the same position with the employer immediately before applying; pay stubs for 12 months; settlement funds **$10,000 + $2,000** per accompanying dependant (Canadian bank, 3 months). Skilled: 1 yr in last 10 yrs, same or higher TEER; funds shown by the job offer.
- EOI: survey score emailed within 5 business days; EOI valid 1 year; ITA -> **30 calendar days** to apply; incomplete at pre-screen -> **2 business days** to fix; information requests 14 days.
- **Appeal:** employer may appeal a denial in writing within **15 calendar days** (no new documents; eligibility-criteria appeals not considered). Misrepresentation -> 1-year bar. MOU breach -> employer barred up to 3 years.
- Nomination valid **6 months**; one use only.

### Fees
- **Employer-Driven (Skilled Worker, NWT EE, ELSS): no NTNP application fee** in the 2026 guidelines (employer registration "free of charge"). The only fee named is the **$230 IRCC employer compliance fee** before the work permit (H). A secondary site lists a "$300 provincial fee" for Employer-Driven and Francophone: **not in the official guidelines; do not quote** (conflict noted).
- **Francophone: no NTNP fee** (registration "free of charge"; only the $230 IRCC employer compliance fee). Guidelines effective 2025-11-10, still the linked version (they still show 2025 intake windows; the 2026 first-come-first-served release governs). Language **NCLC/CLB 5 French + CLB 4 English**, all four skills; 1 yr in the occupation in last 10 yrs **or** 6 months in NWT; high school; settlement funds $10,000 + $2,000 per dependant; employer pays the worker's round-trip airfare if abroad; nominee may appeal within 15 days. Source: https://www.immigratenwt.ca/sites/immigratenwt/files/resources/2025_-_11_-_ntnp_program_guidelines_-_francophone_stream-_final.pdf (read 2026-11-20) (H).
- Business stream: **$2,800** non-refundable (2026 guidelines) (H).

## Business stream pitfalls
- Exploratory visit and interview are mandatory before ITA; budget travel.
- Money spent before BPA signing and work permit does not count toward equity.
- No appeal; decisions final.
- No deposit in the 2026 guidelines; residency 75% and within 100 km (H). Clients quoting a $75,000 deposit are using outdated secondary material.

## Consultant pitfalls
- The cap is small. Confirm the intake directly with NTNP: 1-855-440-5450, immigration@gov.nt.ca.
- Worker-side language levels: see the Employer-Driven guidelines section (CLB 4/5/6/7 by category).
- Employer-driven. A worker cannot apply without an NWT employer.

## Common refusal reasons
- Labour market test or recruitment not shown; job offer issues; stream-specific language
