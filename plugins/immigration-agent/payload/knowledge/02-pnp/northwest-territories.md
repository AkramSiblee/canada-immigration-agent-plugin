### Streams
- **Employer-Driven (EOI):** valid full-time permanent job offer from an NWT employer, where no Canadian or PR is available. The employer submits an EOI for a named worker, then the worker completes theirs. One active EOI per person. Points: human capital (language, age, experience, education), NWT labour-market connection (NWT employment and time employed, trades job offer, closed NWT work permit, small community work), adaptability (immediate family in NWT, NWT post-secondary). Invited applications are assessed in the order received.
  - **Skilled Worker:** TEER 0-3; 1 yr full-time in last 10 yrs in same or similar occupation; valid work permit; language tested at application.
  - **Entry Level/Semi-Skilled:** TEER 4-5; 12 months full-time in the same position with the employer; valid work permit; language tested.
  - **NWT Express Entry:** TEER 0-3; Express Entry profile and Job Seeker Validation Code; NWT job offer; valid work permit; 1 yr full-time in last 10 yrs; certification if required. Adds 600 CRS.
- **Francophone:** NWT job offer; TEER 0-5; bilingual French and English; language tested in both.
- **Business:** equity $200,000 (Yellowknife) or $100,000 (elsewhere); ownership 33.3% unless equity is $1,000,000+; net worth $500,000 (Yellowknife) or $250,000 (elsewhere); CLB 4 or NCLC equivalent (IELTS, CELPIP, TEF; under 2 yrs old); exploratory visit and interview; 2-year Business Performance Agreement.

### Business stream detail (re-verified 2026-11-17)
- **Status: open, first come, first served**; not part of the EOI system ("not oversubscribed") (GNWT release 2026-02-18; CIC News 2026-11-11 says no change) (H). Page claims "no wait-list".
- Figures on https://www.immigratenwt.ca/business-stream (undated; read 2026-11-17) (H):
  - Equity **$200,000** in Yellowknife / **$100,000** elsewhere (cut from $300,000 / $150,000 in the Nov 2022 guidelines).
  - Ownership **33.3%** unless equity is **$1,000,000+**.
  - Net worth **$500,000** Yellowknife / **$250,000** elsewhere, with proof.
  - **CLB 4 / NCLC 4** (IELTS, CELPIP, TEF; under 2 yrs).
  - No active application in another PNP or another NTNP stream; refugee claimants are ineligible; must intend to settle full-time.
- **Steps:** self-assessment -> **exploratory visit with face-to-face interview** (mandatory; **4 business days minimum** per secondary sources, L) -> pre-screen -> ITA -> application + business plan -> **2-yr Business Performance Agreement (BPA)** -> arrival and interim reports -> final report -> nomination (H, official page).
- **No appeal**; decisions are final; denial possible at any step (H, official page).
- From the Sept 2023 guidelines (official PDF, via search snippets; PDF not machine-readable here) (M):
  - **Fee $2,800**, non-refundable, paid through the NTNP online account.
  - Sign the BPA within 30 days. Investment made **before the BPA is signed and the work permit is issued does not count**.
  - 12 months from the Arrival Report to make the investment; **interim reports every 6 months** with verifiable invoices (BPA Appendix A).
  - Nomination: by **month 19 after the BPA Commencement Date**, the business must have operated **12+ months**. Final Report from month 18 (secondary).
  - Residency **75%** of the period in NWT (2022 guideline snippet, wording truncated) (L).
- **Deposit: unconfirmed.** Secondary sources cite a **$75,000 good-faith deposit**; not found on the official page or in the guideline snippets read. Do not quote it without checking the current guidelines (L, conflict).
- **Work permit:** PNP support letter -> **R205(a) C60** (IRCC update 2024-02-16) (H); about 2 yrs per BPA term (secondary). Arrive within 60 days of WP (secondary, L).
- Jobs: no fixed number; purchased businesses must not reduce staff (secondary) (L).
- Forms: NTNP-03, -04, -05, -09.

- **Economic Mobility Pathways Pilot:** federal EMPP is **closed** (IRCC refugees page).

### Fees
- Business stream: **$2,800** non-refundable (Sept 2023 guidelines, via search snippet) (M). Other streams: not stated on pages read.

## Business stream pitfalls
- Exploratory visit and interview are mandatory before ITA; budget travel.
- Money spent before BPA signing and work permit does not count toward equity.
- No appeal; decisions final.
- Deposit and residency % unconfirmed; check the current guideline PDF before quoting.

## Consultant pitfalls
- The cap is small. Confirm the intake directly with NTNP: 1-855-440-5450, immigration@gov.nt.ca.
- Worker-side language levels are not on the web pages; check the guideline PDFs.
- Employer-driven. A worker cannot apply without an NWT employer.

## Common refusal reasons
- Labour market test or recruitment not shown; job offer issues; stream-specific language

## Re-verify before advising
- Early 2027 draw date; 2027 allocation; application fees; CLB levels in guideline PDFs; 2026 nominations issued
