### Streams
- **Employer-Driven (EOI):** valid full-time permanent job offer from an NWT employer, where no Canadian or PR is available. The employer submits an EOI for a named worker, then the worker completes theirs. One active EOI per person. Points: human capital (language, age, experience, education), NWT labour-market connection (NWT employment and time employed, trades job offer, closed NWT work permit, small community work), adaptability (immediate family in NWT, NWT post-secondary). Invited applications are assessed in the order received.
  - **Skilled Worker:** TEER 0-3; 1 yr full-time in last 10 yrs in same or similar occupation; valid work permit; language tested at application.
  - **Entry Level/Semi-Skilled:** TEER 4-5; 12 months full-time in the same position with the employer; valid work permit; language tested.
  - **NWT Express Entry:** TEER 0-3; Express Entry profile and Job Seeker Validation Code; NWT job offer; valid work permit; 1 yr full-time in last 10 yrs; certification if required. Adds 600 CRS.
- **Francophone:** NWT job offer; TEER 0-5; bilingual French and English; language tested in both.
- **Business:** equity $200,000 (Yellowknife) or $100,000 (elsewhere); ownership 33.3% unless equity is $1,000,000+; net worth $500,000 (Yellowknife) or $250,000 (elsewhere); CLB 4 or NCLC equivalent (IELTS, CELPIP, TEF; under 2 yrs old); exploratory visit and interview; 2-year Business Performance Agreement.
- **Economic Mobility Pathways Pilot:** federal EMPP is **closed** (IRCC refugees page).

## Consultant pitfalls
- The cap is small. Confirm the intake directly with NTNP: 1-855-440-5450, immigration@gov.nt.ca.
- Worker-side language levels are not on the web pages; check the guideline PDFs.
- Employer-driven. A worker cannot apply without an NWT employer.

## Common refusal reasons
- Labour market test or recruitment not shown; job offer issues; stream-specific language
