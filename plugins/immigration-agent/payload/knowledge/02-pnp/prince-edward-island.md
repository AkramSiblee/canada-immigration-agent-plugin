# Prince Edward Island (PEI PNP)

## Must cover
- Labour Impact, Express Entry, Business Impact; monthly EOI draws; allocation; fees

## Current rules
**Official EOI draws page read via browser 2026-11-07 (page published 2026-10-16).**

### Streams
- **Labour Impact**: Skilled Worker, Critical Worker, International Graduate (job offer from a PEI employer)
- **Express Entry**: EE profile + PEI EOI
- **Business Impact**: Work Permit stream. **Zero business invitations in every 2026 draw** (H, official EOI page).

- Next draw: not announced on the official page (2026-11-07). Secondary trackers cite Nov 4 or Nov 19; check the official EOI page.
- **Fee: $300** non-refundable, payable only if selected to apply; EOI profile is free (official Skilled Workers in PEI page, 2026-01-13) (H). 2026 allocation: no official figure; secondary estimates conflict (1,025 / 1,340) (L).

## Recent changes (2025-2027)
- 2026: business stream invitations nil all year (H)

## Consultant pitfalls
- Oct 16, 2026 draw = 164 (official EOI page; total 1,483). Several trackers reported 163; cite the official page, not trackers.
- An EOI is not an application. Invited candidates have a short window to apply.

## Common refusal reasons
- Job offer/employer issues; experience not matching NOC; insufficient settlement funds; intent to reside doubts

## Re-verify before advising
- **Official PEI EOI draw page**: next draw results; 2026 allocation; Business Impact status
- Allocation estimate; fee change (currently $300, official 2026-01-13)
