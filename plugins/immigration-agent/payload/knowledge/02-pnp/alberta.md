# Alberta (AAIP)

## Must cover
- Opportunity, Express Entry (Accelerated Tech, Law Enforcement, Health Care, Priority Sectors), Rural Renewal, Tourism and Hospitality, entrepreneur streams
- WEOI points, allocation, fees, pauses

## Current rules

### Streams
- **Worker (4):**
  - Alberta Opportunity
  - Alberta Express Entry: Dedicated Health Care, Accelerated Tech, Law Enforcement pathways, priority-sector draws
  - Rural Renewal
  - Tourism and Hospitality (only for workers already in Alberta on an LMIA permit)
  - Rural Renewal rules, 31 designated communities, Rural Entrepreneur's 57 communities: see regional-community-streams.md
- **Entrepreneur (4):** Rural Entrepreneur, Graduate Entrepreneur, Farm, Foreign Graduate Entrepreneur.
- Worker streams require a **Worker EOI (WEOI)**, in place since 2024-09-30. Valid 1 year. Points grid: see below.
- Draws are "not regularly scheduled".

### Worker EOI points grid (PDF dated 2026-09-28; maximum 100) (H)
- Minimum CLB/NCLC 4 in all four skills to submit.
- **Education:** doctorate 12; master's 10; bachelor's 7; trades certificate 7; diploma/certificate 4; plus completed in Alberta 10, or elsewhere in Canada 6.
- **Language:** lowest skill counts. CLB 6+ 10 (French 8); CLB 5 8 (French 5); CLB 4 5 (French 3). Bilingual at 4+ in both: 3.
- **Experience:** total 12+ months 11; 6-11 months 7; under 6 months 3. In Canada 6+ months: Alberta 10, elsewhere 6.
- **Age:** 21-34 5; 35-49 4; 18-20 3; 50+ 3.
- **Family in Alberta** (parent, child, sibling; citizen or PR, 18+): 8.
- **Job offer:** permanent full-time Alberta offer 10; Rural Renewal endorsement, tourism and hospitality association employer, or law enforcement 6; location outside Calgary and Edmonton CMAs 5; regulated occupation with Alberta certification 10.
- WEOI pool 2026-11-04: 32,211 (Opportunity 20,603; priority sectors 4,581; Tourism and Hospitality 2,846; Accelerated Tech 1,883; Rural Renewal 1,283; Health 976; Law Enforcement 39).
- Federal spaces outside the 6,603: physicians 50 issued, Francophones 12 issued.

### Stream rules (read 2026-11-08) (H)
**Alberta Opportunity**
- Valid work permit at submission and assessment (not maintained or restoration status). Includes LMIA, listed exemptions, Alberta public-institution PGWP, some open permits. Refugee claimants and workers in other provinces are ineligible.
- Experience: 12 months full-time in current occupation in Alberta in last 18 months, **or** 24 months in Canada or abroad in last 30. PGWP: 6 months in Alberta in last 18.
- Language: CLB 5 (TEER 0-3); CLB 4 (TEER 4-5); 33102 needs CLB 7. Education: high school equivalent; ECA for foreign credentials.
- Full-time Alberta offer. Excludes part-time, casual, seasonal, contractors, owners, agency, home-based, virtual.
- Excluded NOCs: 00010, 40021, 40030, 40041, 60040*, 41100, 41220, 41221, 51111, 51122, 42200*, 42202*, 43100, 43109, 53121, 53122, 53124, 53200, 63101, 33100*, 44100, 44101, 64321, 55109, 65109, 65211, 65229, 65329, 75200, 85101, 85102, 85104, 85110, 85121.

**Job offer and employer rules (https://www.alberta.ca/job-offer-and-employer-requirements, read 2026-11-09) (H)**
- Employer: registered, place of business in Alberta, **2 complete fiscal years** of operation, demonstrated need (valid TFW/IMP permit or recruitment efforts). $400,000 gross revenue and 3 FTE employees; if not met, capped at 1 nominee at 2 years, 2 at 3 years, +1 per added year.
- Offer: bona fide, continuous, full-time 30+ h, 12+ months. Wage: provincial minimum **and** LMIA wage if applicable, **or** at least the lowest starting wage for the occupation across all industries in Alberta. Not the median.
- 2026 Alberta Opportunity draw minimums: 50 to 65 (Oct 30: 59; Oct 2: 54). Valid permit needed at submission and assessment; maintained status doesn't count.

**Alberta Express Entry** (all: active profile, CEC/FST/FSW, **CRS 300+**, intent to live in Alberta)
- Accelerated Tech: Alberta job in eligible tech occupation and NAICS industry.
- Law Enforcement: identified by Alberta Association of Chiefs of Police member; offer in 40040, 41310 or 42100.
- Priority sectors: construction, agriculture or aviation occupations.
- Dedicated Health Care: physicians, RNs, LPNs, nurse practitioners, physician assistants, OTs, physiotherapists, clinical social workers, psychologists; Alberta offer and regulator proof. Non-EE route only if the person cannot reach CRS 300; CLB 5.

**Entrepreneur**
- Rural Entrepreneur: see regional-community-streams.md.
- Graduate Entrepreneur: 2-yr Alberta public program; valid PGWP at EOI; CLB 7; 34% ownership; no minimum investment.
- Foreign Graduate Entrepreneur: non-Canadian degree in last 10 yrs with ECA; 6 months' experience; CLB 5; 34% (Calgary/Edmonton CMA) or 51% (regional); $100,000 or $50,000 invested; designated agency letter (Elite Global Group, Empowered Startups, Platform Calgary); listed sectors; settlement funds; grid 200.
- Farm: net worth $500,000; investment $500,000 in Alberta primary production; farm management proof; plan reviewed by Alberta Agriculture.

## Consultant pitfalls
- Most 2026 allocation is used (495 remaining; AEE priority sectors already over-issued). Expect few or no new nominations before 2027.
- Rural Renewal endorsement now expires after 1 year. Apply before expiry.
- In-Canada Rural Renewal applicants must keep valid work permits. Maintained status issues are a common risk.
- The WEOI fee is non-refundable and the EOI lasts 1 year. Don't file EOIs for clients who can't meet stream criteria.

## Common refusal reasons
- Job offer/employer criteria not met; NOC not eligible for the stream; work permit invalid; endorsement expired; insufficient language
